A Polygon Replaces Ambiguous Pictures

02 / 16
RepresentationA polygon is an ordered cycle v0, v1, ..., v(n-1) joined by noncrossing line segments.
  • Membership becomes a predicate.
  • Diagonals become segment tests.
  • Visibility becomes triangle coverage.
Stress that the boundary is a finite cyclic list of straight segments. That finite representation lets an algorithm ask crisp questions: is a query on the boundary, inside, or outside; which segments can be used as diagonals; and which vertices can see a point or a triangle.

Ray Crossing Is A Parity Test

03 / 16
Parity ruleA generic ray from a query point toggles inside/outside at each boundary crossing.
inside(q) iff crossings(q) is odd
Boundary cases are handled first; parity is the stable test away from the boundary.
Use the horizontal ray as a computational version of the Jordan curve intuition. Crossings toggle the state from outside to inside or back again. Boundary and vertex hits require conventions, so the clean statement is: away from the boundary, parity gives a stable inside-outside classifier.
legal splits

Diagonals Are Interior Chords

04 / 16
DefinitionA diagonal joins two nonadjacent vertices and lies in the polygonal region except at its endpoints.
  • Convex polygons accept every noncrossing vertex chord.
  • Nonconvex polygons require an interior test.
  • A legal diagonal splits one polygon into two smaller polygons.
A diagonal is not just any segment between nonadjacent vertices. It must stay inside the polygon except at its endpoints. This distinction is where nonconvexity first matters: one candidate chord can be legal while another visibly exits the region.
triangulation engine

Ears Make Induction Visible

05 / 16
Ear moveFind a triangle formed by three consecutive boundary vertices whose closing chord is a diagonal. Clip it, then repeat.
1local triangle
n-1smaller polygon
inductsame problem
The ear theorem turns triangulation from a global mystery into a local removal move. If a vertex has an interior diagonal between its neighbors, that triangle can be clipped away and the remaining boundary is a smaller polygon. Repeating this move produces a triangulation.

Every Triangulation Has The Same Ledger

06 / 16
nvertices and boundary edges
n - 3diagonals in any triangulation
n - 2triangles inside the polygon
For n = 9Any triangulation has 6 diagonals and 7 triangles, even if the diagonals look very different.
After triangulation, the numerical invariants no longer depend on the particular diagonal choices. The triangle count is n minus 2 and the diagonal count is n minus 3. Present these as a planar graph ledger, not as a property of one drawing.

Euler Explains The Counts

07 / 16
Two equations
3t = n + 2d
n - (n+d) + (t+1) = 2
t = n - 2,   d = n - 3
The outside face accounts for the +1 in Euler's formula.
Walk through the bookkeeping. There are n boundary edges, d diagonals, and t triangular faces inside the polygon. Counting triangle sides gives 3t equals n plus 2d, and Euler's formula for the disk gives the second equation. Solving yields t equals n minus 2 and d equals n minus 3.

Convex Polygons Reveal Catalan Growth

08 / 16
Catalan recurrence
T_n = sum T_{k+1} T_{n-k}
  • Mark one boundary edge.
  • Choose the third vertex of its triangle.
  • The remaining choices split independently.
In a convex polygon every noncrossing chord is legal, so the remaining difficulty is combinatorial. Choosing the triangle incident to a marked edge splits the polygon into independent smaller polygons. That split is exactly the Catalan recurrence.
outerplanar structure

Triangulations As Graphs

09 / 16
Graph viewpointThe triangulation graph has the polygon vertices, boundary edges, and diagonals. All vertices lie on the outer face.
Why it mattersTriangle adjacency gives an induction path for coloring, guarding, and local-to-global proofs.
Shift attention from the geometry of segments to the graph carried by the triangulation. Vertices are polygon corners and edges are boundary edges plus diagonals. This graph is maximal outerplanar, which is why local triangle information controls global guarding arguments.
art gallery theorem

Fisk Coloring Turns Triangles Into Guards

10 / 16
Fisk's strategyThree-color the triangulation so every triangle has one vertex of each color. Choose the smallest color class.
guards <= floor(n / 3)
Fisk's proof colors the triangulation graph with three colors so that every triangle receives all three colors. The smallest color class then has at most floor n over 3 vertices, and because every triangle has a vertex of that color, those guards see the whole polygon.
visibility covering

Why The Least Color Class Guards

11 / 16
Triangle visibilityA guard at a triangle vertex sees the whole triangle because triangles are convex.
  • Triangulation covers the polygon with closed triangles.
  • Every triangle contains a vertex of the selected color.
  • The selected guards cover the union.
Make the visibility argument geometric. A triangulation partitions the polygon into triangles. A guard placed at a vertex of a triangle sees that triangle because triangles are convex. If every triangle has a selected-color vertex, the union of visible triangles is the whole polygon.

The art-gallery theorem is a template, not a single universal number. Orthogonal galleries, exterior fortress guarding, and visibility restrictions change the combinatorial object being covered. Area Is A Complete 2D Dissection Invariant

13 / 16
Plane resultFor polygonal regions in the plane, equal area is enough for scissors congruence.
area(P) = area(Q) -> P ~ Q
Triangulation supplies the pieces; area bookkeeping supplies the invariant.
Introduce scissors congruence in the plane as an optimistic result. Polygonal pieces can be cut and rearranged, and equal area is the only obstruction for polygonal regions. The teaching point is that triangulation reduces arbitrary polygons to pieces that can be compared through area-preserving rearrangements.

A Triangle Can Become A Rectangle

14 / 16
Dissection roadmapReduce polygon -> triangles -> rectangles with equal area -> common rectangle.
A_triangle = (1/2) b h
Use the diagram as a proof roadmap rather than a recipe for physical cutting. A triangle can be decomposed into pieces that reassemble as a rectangle with the same base-height area. Once polygons have been triangulated, this move lets area bookkeeping drive dissection.
Dehn obstruction

Equal Volume Is Not Enough In 3D

15 / 16
3D obstructionScissors cuts in space preserve more than volume: edge lengths weighted by dihedral angle data also matter.
D(P) = sum l_e \otimes theta_e
Equal volume can coexist with incompatible angle ledgers.
The three-dimensional story breaks the plane analogy. Tetrahedral dissections preserve volume, but they also preserve an angle-weighted ledger now called a Dehn invariant. Two polyhedra can have equal volume and still fail this second test, so they cannot be scissors congruent.
