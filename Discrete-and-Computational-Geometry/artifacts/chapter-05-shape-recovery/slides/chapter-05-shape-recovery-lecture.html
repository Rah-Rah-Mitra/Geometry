Shape Recovery From Samples

How medial axes, local feature size, Delaunay structure, and alpha complexes turn scattered geometric evidence into a certified shape.

Frame shape recovery as an inference problem: the input is incomplete geometric evidence, usually samples, and the output is a shape with controlled topology. Skeletons

The Medial Axis Marks Where Boundary Evidence Competes

Definition

The medial axis of a region is the set of centers of maximal inscribed disks. A disk is maximal when no larger inscribed disk contains it.

lfs(p) = dist(p, medial axis)

Near sharp corners, narrow necks, or almost-touching boundary arcs, the medial axis moves close to the boundary. Those are the places where a reconstruction needs denser samples.

Medial axis03 / 16
Use the disk picture rather than a memorized definition. A medial-axis point is where at least two boundary features are equally close, so tiny perturbations can change which boundary points are paired. Local feature size is the distance from the boundary to this instability set.
Sampling Density

Local Feature Size Converts Geometry Into A Spacing Rule

Sampling Principle

A sample should be denser where the nearest competing feature is closer. Uniform spacing is wasteful in calm regions and unsafe near small features.

  • Large local feature size permits longer boundary steps.
  • Small local feature size demands tighter spacing and sharper certificates.
  • The condition is local, so adaptive sampling is natural.
Local feature size04 / 16
Students should leave this slide with a diagnostic habit: ask where the shape nearly touches itself or bends sharply, then ask whether the samples resolve that scale. The point is not to memorize a constant but to understand why any theorem must compare sample spacing with local feature size.
A Related But Different Skeleton

Straight Skeletons Track Polygon Wavefront Events

Interpretation

Move each polygon edge inward at the same speed. The straight skeleton records where moving edges collide, split, or vanish.

Do Not Conflate

The medial axis uses nearest boundary distance and can include curved branches. The straight skeleton uses straight-line offset events from polygon edges.

Straight skeleton05 / 16
This slide is included because the two skeletons are tempting to identify. Keep the contrast concrete: medial-axis disks are distance certificates, while straight-skeleton arcs are wavefront histories. Both expose structure, but they answer different algorithmic questions.
Finite Search Space

Delaunay Edges Are The Reconstruction Candidate Pool

The Voronoi diagram asks which sample is nearest. Its dual, the Delaunay triangulation, connects samples whose Voronoi cells touch.

Algorithmic Use

Instead of testing every pair of samples, reconstruction algorithms search a geometrically filtered graph.

Voronoi / Delaunay06 / 16
Point out the duality visually. Delaunay edges are not automatically the recovered curve, but they are a strong candidate pool because nearest-neighbor geometry has already removed many impossible chords. The reconstruction task becomes selecting the right subgraph.

What Can Go Wrong

Sparse Samples Create Plausible But Wrong Chords

Diagnostic

If spacing exceeds the local feature scale, the nearest Delaunay candidates may jump across a narrow gap.

  • Degree counts can still look reasonable.
  • Cycle rank may change even when the drawing looks smooth.
  • Failures concentrate near small local feature size.
Reconstruction failures08 / 16
This is the cautionary slide. A bad reconstruction can be visually convincing because human perception completes curves generously. Ask students to locate the narrow feature and explain why the chord is tempting to the algorithm but wrong for the underlying topology.
Growing Disks

Body(Alpha) Is A Monotone Geometric Proxy

Definition

For a sample set S, Body(alpha) is the union of closed disks of radius alpha centered at points of S.

alpha_1 <= alpha_2 implies Body(alpha_1) subset Body(alpha_2)

As alpha grows, components merge and holes may appear or fill. The geometry changes continuously; the next slide makes it finite.

Body(alpha)09 / 16
Make the monotonicity explicit: increasing alpha never removes points from the body. This gives a natural scale parameter. The price is that a union of disks is continuous geometry, so we need a combinatorial model that changes only at controlled thresholds.
Combinatorial Proxy

The Alpha Complex Keeps The Delaunay Pieces Supported At Scale Alpha

Threshold Rules

Edges enter when two radius-alpha disks have the required support. Triangles enter when their circumradius is at most alpha.

  • Every accepted edge or triangle comes from the Delaunay triangulation.
  • The complex grows as alpha grows.
  • The finite complex can preserve the homotopy type of the disk union under the right hypotheses.
Alpha complex10 / 16
The alpha complex is the bridge between continuous disk unions and finite computation. Avoid overloading students with all implementation cases; the essential idea is that Delaunay geometry supplies candidate simplices, and alpha decides which are active at a particular scale.

Finite Topology

For a graph, the cycle rank is E - V + C. Adding triangles can fill graph cycles, so the complex-level hole count is the more meaningful invariant.

  • Components should not increase as alpha grows.
  • Edges and triangles are monotone additions.
  • Wrong topology can reveal a wrong reconstruction even when the outline is plausible.

The table is not meant as a theorem statement; it models the habit of recording invariants. Stress that topology is a certificate layer. A recovered drawing can have the right local geometry while still having the wrong number of components or loops.
Why The Complex Can Stand In For The Body

Nerve Reasoning Explains The Alpha Complex

Proof Roadmap

When disk intersections behave well, a cover by disks has the same homotopy type as the nerve of the cover. The alpha complex is a Delaunay-efficient way to represent that nerve at scale.

  • Disks and their finite intersections are contractible.
  • The nerve records which subfamilies intersect.
  • Delaunay restrictions remove redundant simplices without changing the intended type under the hypotheses.
Nerve viewpoint13 / 16
This is a proof sketch slide, not a full algebraic topology lecture. The key message is that alpha complexes are not merely drawing conveniences. They are justified because the combinatorics of a good cover can preserve the homotopy type of the covered geometric body.
Implementation Mindset

Every Recovery Routine Needs A Small Audit Trail

Metric

Nearest-neighbor distances, edge lengths, and local scale estimates explain why candidate edges were accepted.

Combinatorial

Delaunay membership, degrees, components, and cycle rank make graph output reproducible.

Topological

Filtration events, filled triangles, and hole counts check whether the recovered object has the intended type.

good reconstruction = plausible geometry + recorded invariants + scale sensitivity
Audit trail14 / 16
Connect the lecture back to computational practice. A notebook or program should not only output a picture; it should save the quantities that justify the picture. This is the difference between a visualization and a reproducible geometric computation.
In-Class Exercise

Choose Alpha And Defend The Topology

Prompt

Pick a radius alpha that preserves one central hole. Which edges and triangles should enter, and what invariant would convince you?

  • Mark edges admitted by the radius choice.
  • Identify triangles that would fill the central loop.
  • Report components and a hole-count argument.
Exercise15 / 16
Have students work in pairs for a few minutes. The intended discussion is not about finding a perfect numeric alpha from the slide but about defending a scale choice with edge entries, triangle entries, and topology. Ask one group to argue for preserving the hole and another to argue when the hole should be filled.
Takeaways

Shape Recovery Works When Scale, Candidates, And Topology Agree

1. Skeletons Explain Sensitivity

The medial axis and local feature size identify where reconstruction is fragile.

2. Delaunay Structure Narrows Choices

Candidate edges come from geometry, not from arbitrary visual interpolation.

3. Alpha Complexes Certify Scale

A filtration records how components, loops, and filled regions change with alpha.

Closing Principle

Recovery is credible only when the drawing is accompanied by the assumptions and invariants that make it reproducible.
