Hermitian Structures On Bundles

Definition Layer

A Hermitian metric \(h\) on a complex vector bundle \(E\to X\) is a smoothly varying Hermitian inner product on each fiber \(E_x\).

\(h_x(u,v)\) is linear in one slot, conjugate-linear in the other, and positive definite.

fiberwisesmoothpositive

Induced Metrics

Duals, tensor products, exterior powers, and Hom bundles inherit metrics functorially.

Existence

Smooth partitions of unity glue local trivialization metrics into a global one.

Role

The metric is not decorative: it defines norms, orthogonality, adjoints, and integration pairings.

In the source span around PDF pages 176 to 182, Hermitian metrics are the first analytic structure placed on a bundle. Stress that the metric lives fiber by fiber but must vary smoothly. The partition-of-unity argument is the reason such metrics exist on smooth bundles, and the induced metrics on duals and tensor constructions are what make later adjoints and pairings canonical once h is chosen.

Forms With Bundle Values

Inner Products And Adjoints

\((\alpha,\beta)=\int_X \langle \alpha,\beta\rangle_{\omega,h}\,dV_\omega\)

  • The Hermitian metric \(h\) handles the \(E\)-component.
  • The Hermitian metric on \(X\) handles the form component.
  • The Hodge star packages the pointwise duality needed for formal adjoints.

Bundle-valued Dolbeault theory studies \(\bar\partial_E:A^{p,q}(X,E)\to A^{p,q+1}(X,E)\) and its adjoint \(\bar\partial_E^*\).

The notation A p q of X with values in E is the bridge from vector bundles to analysis. A bundle-valued form is locally a finite sum of ordinary p q forms times local bundle sections. Once X has a Hermitian metric and E has h, there is an L2 inner product. That inner product is what allows the Hodge star idea and formal adjoints to enter the Dolbeault complex.

Hodge Decomposition

Laplacian

\(\Delta_{\bar\partial}=\bar\partial_E\bar\partial_E^*+\bar\partial_E^*\bar\partial_E\) is elliptic on compact \(X\).

Representatives

Each Dolbeault cohomology class has a unique harmonic representative after choices of metrics.

Finite Dimension

Elliptic theory turns infinite-dimensional forms into finite-dimensional harmonic spaces.

Present Hodge decomposition as the analytic payoff of the metric choices. On a compact complex manifold, the bundle-valued Dolbeault Laplacian is elliptic, so the space of harmonic forms is finite-dimensional. The cohomology is not merely represented by arbitrary closed forms modulo exact forms; each class has a canonical harmonic representative once the metrics are fixed.

Serre Duality Pairing

Pairing Principle

\(H^{p,q}(X,E)\) pairs with a complementary Dolbeault group built from the dual bundle and canonical bundle.

The wedge product contracts \(E\) with \(E^\vee\), reaches top degree, then integrates over \(X\).

Serre duality is the cohomological mirror of the Hodge-star adjoint story: it converts a hard group into the dual of a complementary group.

For this lecture, avoid getting stuck in indexing conventions. The essential point from the source is that compactness and the Hermitian setup provide a nondegenerate pairing between Dolbeault cohomology with values in E and a complementary group involving the dual bundle and the canonical bundle. The product contracts bundle factors, produces a top-degree form, and integration gives the scalar pairing.

Connections As Leibniz Operators

Definition

\(\nabla(fs)=df\otimes s+f\nabla s\)

A connection differentiates sections of a bundle while remembering multiplication by functions.

first orderLeibnizbundle-aware

Affine Space

If \(\nabla_1,\nabla_0\) are connections, then \(\nabla_1-\nabla_0\in A^1(\operatorname{End}E)\).

Local Matrix

In a frame, \(\nabla=d+A\), with \(A\) a matrix of one-forms.

Complex Split

On complex manifolds, connection forms can be separated into \((1,0)\) and \((0,1)\) parts.

Around PDF pages 183 to 191, the chapter turns from metrics to differentiation. A connection is defined by the Leibniz rule, and this rule is the whole point: differentiating f times s produces a derivative of the scalar function plus a derivative of the section. The difference between two connections is tensorial, so it is an End E valued one-form rather than another differential operator.

Induced Connections And Second Fundamental Form

Functorial Rules

  • Direct sums: differentiate component by component.
  • Duals: use \(d\langle\lambda,s\rangle=\langle\nabla\lambda,s\rangle+\langle\lambda,\nabla s\rangle\).
  • Tensor products: apply the Leibniz rule to both factors.
  • Subbundles: project the ambient derivative into tangential and normal pieces.

The second fundamental form records exactly the part of the ambient derivative that leaves the subbundle.

After defining connections, the source checks that all standard bundle operations carry induced connections. This matters because characteristic classes and curvature computations must behave well under sums, duals, and tensor products. For a subbundle, the ambient derivative of a section does not usually remain inside the subbundle; the normal component is the second fundamental form, and it becomes important again in positivity statements for quotients and generated bundles.

The Chern Connection

Uniqueness Statement

For a holomorphic vector bundle with Hermitian metric \(h\), there is a unique connection \(\nabla\) whose \((0,1)\)-part is \(\bar\partial_E\) and which is compatible with \(h\).

\(\nabla=\nabla^{1,0}+\bar\partial_E\)

In a holomorphic frame, the connection matrix is determined by the metric matrix; this is why curvature can be computed from \(h\) locally.

The Chern connection is the central compatibility result of the connections section. A holomorphic bundle already has its dbar operator, and a Hermitian metric asks the connection to preserve fiber inner products. The theorem says these two requirements determine one connection. This is the connection whose curvature later carries both analytic positivity information and Chern-Weil topological information.

Atiyah Class As Obstruction

Obstruction Question

Can a holomorphic vector bundle admit a holomorphic connection?

A holomorphic connection would split the first jet sequence.

The Atiyah class measures the failure of such a splitting.

The obstruction viewpoint foreshadows Chern classes: curvature-like data can be read as a cohomology class attached to the bundle itself.

Keep the Atiyah class conceptual. The source introduces it as the obstruction to the existence of a holomorphic connection, equivalently as the obstruction to splitting the jet sequence holomorphically. The important lecture point is not to compute it here, but to recognize the pattern: a failure of a preferred differential structure becomes a cohomology class attached naturally to the bundle.

Curvature And Bianchi Identity

Local Formula

\(F_\nabla=\nabla^2\)

If \(\nabla=d+A\), then \(F_\nabla=dA+A\wedge A\).

Bianchi identity: \(\nabla F_\nabla=0\)

Curvature is tensorial: unlike the connection matrix, it transforms as an \(\operatorname{End}E\)-valued two-form.

In the curvature section, the square of the connection is no longer a first-order operator; it is tensorial and gives an End E valued two-form. In a local frame the formula is dA plus A wedge A. The Bianchi identity says the covariant derivative of curvature vanishes. This identity is the formal reason invariant polynomials in curvature will produce closed forms in Chern-Weil theory.

Chern Curvature And Positivity

Chern Curvature

For the Chern connection on a holomorphic Hermitian bundle, curvature has type \((1,1)\).

For a line bundle, local weights make curvature a complex Hessian.

Positivity asks that the resulting Hermitian form be positive in the relevant sense.

Warning: signs depend on the convention for \(F_\nabla\), the Chern curvature symbol, and factors of \(i\) or \(2\pi\). State the convention before comparing formulas.

The source then specializes curvature to the Chern connection. Its type is one one, which is exactly what makes positivity meaningful in complex geometry. For line bundles, curvature can be read from a local weight by a partial dbar expression. Warn students that sign conventions differ: the same geometric positivity may be written with different signs depending on how curvature and the factor of i are normalized.

Globally Generated Bundles

Evaluation Intuition

If global sections generate every fiber, a trivial bundle maps onto \(E\).

\(H^0(X,E)\otimes\mathcal O_X \twoheadrightarrow E\)

Quotient metrics and second fundamental form computations make semipositivity visible.

Use this as a geometric template: generation by flat sections upstairs descends to nonnegative curvature behavior downstairs.

The globally generated bundle result is best taught through the quotient picture. If global sections span each fiber, the bundle is a quotient of a trivial bundle. The trivial source has flat metric data, and the quotient metric inherits semipositivity after accounting for the second fundamental form. This is where the earlier subbundle discussion becomes a curvature computation rather than a side remark.

Chern-Weil Machine

Invariant polynomial \(P\) applied to curvature gives a closed differential form whose de Rham class is a characteristic class of \(E\).

Chern-Weil theory is the machine promised by the curvature section. Choose a connection, compute its curvature, feed that curvature into an invariant polynomial, and obtain a closed form. The closedness uses Bianchi and invariance. The more surprising statement is independence of the connection at the level of cohomology, so the resulting class belongs to the bundle rather than to the auxiliary connection.

Chern Classes In Practice

Characteristic Forms

  • Total Chern class: \(c(E)=1+c_1(E)+c_2(E)+\cdots\).
  • Chern character: \(\operatorname{ch}(E)\) turns sums into sums and tensor products into products.
  • Todd class: \(\operatorname{td}(E)\) is built from the same formal Chern-root calculus.
  • Tangent-bundle classes encode geometry of \(X\).

Hypersurfaces

Use exact sequences to express tangent and normal Chern classes.

Divisors

The line bundle of a divisor has first Chern class represented by that divisor class.

Instructor Exercise

Ask students to derive \(c(TY)\) for a smooth hypersurface \(Y\subset X\) from the normal sequence.

Close with practical uses from the final source span, PDF pages 203 to 215. The formal classes c k, the Chern character, and the Todd class are not just notation; they are computable through exact sequences and formal Chern-root algebra. For hypersurfaces, the tangent and normal sequence gives a direct calculation. For divisors, the key relation is that the first Chern class of O of D is the cohomology class represented by D.
