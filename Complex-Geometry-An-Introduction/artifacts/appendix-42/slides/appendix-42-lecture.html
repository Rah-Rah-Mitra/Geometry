Hermite-Einstein Condition

Curvature Becomes Scalar After Contraction

02 / 15
Definition 4.B.1
\[ i\Lambda_\omega F_\nabla=\lambda\,\operatorname{id}_E \]

The bundle metric \(h\) is Hermite-Einstein when the Chern curvature has scalar mean curvature with respect to the chosen Kahler metric on \(X\).

Dependence
  • The condition depends on the Kahler form \(\omega\), not only on the holomorphic bundle.
  • Flat bundles satisfy it with \(\lambda=0\).
  • Changing the metric on \(X\) can change existence.
Hermite-Einstein starts with \(E\), \(h\), and \(\omega\)printed 217
Emphasize that the full curvature is an endomorphism-valued two-form, so the equation is not saying the entire curvature matrix is scalar. It says that after taking the Kahler trace, the resulting endomorphism of each fiber is a constant multiple of the identity. This is why the condition is sensitive to the background Kahler metric on the base. Flat bundles give the first sanity check because the curvature term vanishes before any contraction is needed.
Trace And Primitive Curvature

HE Splits Curvature Into Scalar Trace Plus Primitive Noise

03 / 15
Equivalent Form
\[ iF_\nabla=\frac{\lambda}{n}\,\omega\otimes\operatorname{id}_E+F^\perp,\qquad \Lambda_\omega F^\perp=0 \]
\[ iF_\nabla\wedge\omega^{n-1} =\frac{\lambda}{n}\,\omega^n\otimes\operatorname{id}_E \]
  • The scalar part is measured by \(\Lambda_\omega\).
  • The primitive part is invisible to this contraction.
  • Taking trace turns the equation into a \(c_1(E)\) statement.
Primitive means killed by \(\Lambda_\omega\)printed 218
The key technical move is to separate what the contraction can see from what it cannot see. The scalar trace part is forced to be a multiple of the Kahler form, with the factor one over n coming from the Lefschetz commutator relation. The remaining matrix of two-forms is primitive, so it has zero Kahler trace. This decomposition prepares both the slope formula and the Chern-class inequalities, because trace and primitive terms contribute in different ways.
Slope

The Constant Is Topological Once \([\omega]\) Is Fixed

04 / 15
Definition 4.B.3
\[ \mu_\omega(E)= \frac{\int_X c_1(E)\wedge[\omega]^{n-1}} {\operatorname{rk}(E)} \]
For A Compact Kahler \(X\)
\[ \lambda=\frac{2\pi n\,\mu_\omega(E)} {\int_X \omega^n} \]

Constants reflect the appendix convention for \(iF_\nabla\) and \(c_1(E)\).

  • Different Hermite-Einstein metrics on the same bundle must share the same \(\lambda\).
  • The chosen Kahler class controls the slope and therefore the scalar.
Slope is the algebraic shadow of the curvature traceprinted 218
After the wedge identity, integrate over compact X. The left side represents the first Chern class, while the right side is rank times lambda times the volume. This proves that the scalar in the Hermite-Einstein equation cannot be tuned freely once the holomorphic bundle and Kahler class are fixed. Point out that this is the bridge to stability: slope is a cohomological number, but it will be used to compare subsheaves.
Line Bundles

Metric Rescaling Solves The HE Equation In Rank One

05 / 15
Lemma 4.B.4
\[ h' = e^f h,\qquad F_{\nabla'}=F_\nabla+\partial\bar\partial f \]

For a line bundle, \(i\Lambda_\omega F_\nabla\) is a real function. Choose \(f\) so its Laplacian removes the nonconstant part.

\[ i\Lambda_\omega F_{\nabla'}=\lambda \]
  • The harmonic representative of \(c_1(L)\) is realized by a Chern connection.
  • The proof uses Kahler identities to turn \(\partial\bar\partial f\) into a Laplacian term.
Rank one: the equation reduces to a scalar potential problemprinted 219
For line bundles, there is no trace-free endomorphism direction to manage. The contracted curvature is just a function on X. The proof rescales the metric by e to the f, which shifts curvature by a partial partial-bar term. Kahler identities identify the contraction of that shift with a Laplacian, so the nonconstant part can be absorbed. This is the first constructive existence result in the appendix and it foreshadows the later nonlinear volume-prescription argument.
Operations On HE Bundles

Tensor, Dual, And Direct Sum Behave By Slopes

06 / 15
Closure Rules
\[ E_1\otimes E_2,\quad E^\vee,\quad \operatorname{Hom}(E_1,E_2) \]

Natural induced metrics preserve the Hermite-Einstein condition.

Direct Sum Caveat
\[ E_1\oplus E_2\ \text{is HE} \iff \mu(E_1)=\mu(E_2) \]
Flat\(F_\nabla=0\), so \(\lambda=0\).
Line TwistThe key inequality is invariant under \(E\otimes L\).
Operations preserve HE only with the right scalar bookkeepingprinted 219
This slide gives students a small algebra of Hermite-Einstein bundles. Tensor products, duals, and Hom bundles carry induced metrics whose Chern curvatures combine in the expected way, so the contracted scalar terms combine as well. The direct sum is the important warning: block diagonal curvature can only become one scalar multiple of the identity if all summands have the same slope. That exact same condition reappears in the definition of polystability.
Bogomolov-Lubke

Hermite-Einstein Metrics Force A Chern-Class Inequality

07 / 15
Proposition 4.B.6
\[ \int_X\bigl(2r\,c_2(E)-(r-1)c_1(E)^2\bigr)\wedge\omega^{n-2}\ge 0 \]
  • Pass to \(G=\operatorname{End}(E)\), where \(c_1(G)=0\).
  • The induced connection is HE and has primitive curvature.
  • Hodge-Riemann turns primitive curvature into a nonpositive pointwise term; the displayed sign follows after translating Chern characters.
HE is analytic, but it leaves topological footprintsprinted 219-220
The inequality is one of the appendix's surprises: a metric equation produces a purely topological obstruction. The proof uses the endomorphism bundle because its first Chern class vanishes, and the induced curvature is primitive under the Hermite-Einstein hypothesis. Hodge-Riemann bilinear relations then give a pointwise sign for the second Chern character. Translate that statement back to E, and the combination two r c2 minus r minus one times c1 squared appears naturally.
Stability

Slope Tests Every Proper Subsheaf

08 / 15
Definition 4.B.8
\[ E\text{ stable}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \mu_\omega(\mathcal F)\lt\mu_\omega(E) \]

for every proper nonzero \(\mathcal O_X\)-subsheaf \(\mathcal F\subset E\).

What Must Be Tested
  • The notion depends on the Kahler class \([\omega]\).
  • In dimension at least three, arbitrary subsheaves are genuinely needed.
  • A stronger vector-bundle-only test uses line bundles \(L\subset\Lambda^s E\).
Stability is slope comparison inside \(E\)printed 220-221
Now the analytic scalar lambda is replaced by an algebraic comparison rule. Stability says no proper subsheaf is allowed to have slope as large as the whole bundle. It is important to say subsheaf, not just subbundle, because singular behavior in higher dimension can carry the destabilizing data. The line bundle criterion through exterior powers is useful for intuition, but the formal definition is the subsheaf one. This slide sets up the Donaldson-Uhlenbeck-Yau theorem.
Donaldson-Uhlenbeck-Yau

The Metric Equation Is Equivalent To Polystability

09 / 15
Theorem 4.B.9
\[ E\text{ admits HE}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad E\text{ is polystable} \]
Polystable
\[ E=\bigoplus_i E_i,\qquad E_i\text{ stable},\quad \mu(E_i)=\mu(E) \]

This is a Kobayashi-Hitchin correspondence: a PDE existence problem is decided by holomorphic slope data.

HE existence is algebraically decidable by polystabilityprinted 221
This is the central correspondence for vector bundles. One direction, Hermite-Einstein implies polystable, is conceptually closer to slope estimates from curvature. The converse is the deep analytic existence result due to Donaldson, Uhlenbeck, and Yau. Stress the payoff: one may prove the existence of a special metric without writing it down. Also connect polystability back to the direct sum warning, because the stable summands must have one common slope.
Kahler-Einstein Specialization

Set \(E=T_X\), But Do Not Choose An Unrelated Metric

10 / 15
Definition 4.B.11
\[ (X,g)\text{ is KE}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad i\Lambda_\omega F^{LC}=\lambda\,\operatorname{id}_{T_X} \]
Why Kahler Is Built In
  • The metric \(g\) on \(X\) induces the Hermitian metric on \(T_X\).
  • For Kahler metrics, the Chern and Levi-Civita connections coincide.
  • A separate HE metric on \(T_X\) is not automatically a KE metric on \(X\).
KE is the HE equation with geometric self-consistencyprinted 221-222
This is where the appendix changes from vector bundle theory to manifold geometry. In the Hermite-Einstein problem, the bundle metric is an independent choice. In the Kahler-Einstein problem for the tangent bundle, the Hermitian metric on the bundle must be the one induced by the Riemannian metric on the base. The Kahler condition matters because it identifies the Chern connection on the holomorphic tangent bundle with the Levi-Civita connection. Without that identification the equation would lose its geometric meaning.
Ricci Curvature

The Tangent-Bundle Trace Is The Einstein Equation

11 / 15
Corollary 4.B.13
\[ g\text{ Kahler-Einstein}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Ric}(X,g)=\lambda\,\omega \]
Chern Class Ray
\[ c_1(X)=\frac{\lambda}{2\pi}[\omega] \]

The primitive curvature piece is traceless, so the trace of the KE equation gives the Ricci form and the real first Chern class.

Trace of curvature equals Ricci; cohomology records \(c_1(X)\)printed 222-223
The Ricci equation is often the way Kahler-Einstein metrics are introduced. Here it arrives as the trace of the tangent-bundle Hermite-Einstein condition. Proposition 4.A.11 from the previous appendix supplies the bridge from contracted curvature to Ricci. Once Ricci equals lambda times the Kahler form, the first Chern class is represented by lambda over two pi times omega. That puts c1 of X on exactly one of three possible rays.
Sign Trichotomy

Kahler-Einstein Metrics Force \(c_1(X)\) Into One Of Three Cases

12 / 15
Positive
\[ c_1(X)\in\mathcal K_X,\qquad \lambda\gt 0 \]
Zero
\[ c_1(X)=0,\qquad \lambda=0 \]
Negative
\[ -c_1(X)\in\mathcal K_X,\qquad \lambda\lt 0 \]
\[ c_1(X)=\frac{\lambda}{2\pi}[\omega] \quad\Rightarrow\quad c_1(X)=0,\ c_1(X)\gt 0,\ \text{or}\ -c_1(X)\gt 0 \]
Corollary 4.B.14 and examples 4.B.16printed 223-224
Use this as the main geometric classification checkpoint. If a Kahler-Einstein metric exists, its Kahler form represents the sign direction of the first Chern class. Projective space with the Fubini-Study metric gives the positive model, flat complex tori give the zero model, and quotients of the complex ball give the negative model. The c1 equals zero case should be read as Ricci-flat, not as flat curvature in every direction.
Miyaoka-Yau

Kahler-Einstein Curvature Satisfies A Stronger Inequality

13 / 15
Proposition 4.B.17
\[ \int_X\bigl(2(n+1)c_2(X)-n\,c_1(X)^2\bigr) \wedge\omega^{n-2}\ge 0 \]
Surface Case
\[ 3c_2(X)\ge c_1(X)^2 \]

Equality points toward locally symmetric universal covers: projective space, affine space, or the ball.

Additional Kahler symmetries improve Bogomolov-Lubkeprinted 224-225
The tangent bundle already satisfies the Bogomolov-Lubke inequality when the metric is Kahler-Einstein, but Kahler curvature has extra symmetries. Those symmetries sharpen the Chern-class combination into the Miyaoka-Yau inequality. On a compact complex surface the expression reduces to the famous three c2 at least c1 squared bound. The equality statement is also geometrically meaningful: it signals locally symmetric models, so the inequality is not just a numerical test.
Calabi-Yau Theorem

Prescribe Volume In A Kahler Class, Then Control Ricci

14 / 15
Theorem 4.B.19
\[ \int_X e^f\omega_0^n=\int_X\omega_0^n \quad\Rightarrow\quad [\omega]=[\omega_0],\ \omega^n=e^f\omega_0^n \]

Existence is deep; uniqueness follows from a maximum-principle argument.

Ricci Consequence
\[ [\beta]=c_1(X)\quad\Rightarrow\quad \operatorname{Ric}(\omega)=2\pi\beta \]
When \(c_1(X)=0\)

Each Kahler class has a unique Ricci-flat representative. With a holomorphic volume form \(\Omega\), Ricci-flatness is detected by \(\omega^n=C\,\Omega\wedge\overline{\Omega}\).

Calabi-Yau converts a volume prescription into Ricci controlprinted 225-227
The Calabi-Yau theorem is the analytic engine behind the zero first Chern class case and the Ricci representative result. Give the theorem as a volume prescription: in a fixed Kahler class, a compatible positive volume form determines a unique Kahler metric. Lemma 4.B.20 explains how the Ricci form changes when the volume form changes by e to the f, namely by i partial partial-bar f. Combining these gives a metric whose Ricci form represents any chosen representative of c1.
Existence Landscape

End of Appendix 42: bundle HE and manifold KE are parallel existence storiesprinted 228-230
Close by putting the two halves of the appendix side by side. For vector bundles, Donaldson-Uhlenbeck-Yau says that Hermite-Einstein metrics are exactly polystable bundles. For manifolds, the Kahler-Einstein equation is more rigid because the bundle is the tangent bundle and the metric must be self-consistent. The zero c1 case is settled by Calabi-Yau, the negative c1 case by Aubin-Yau, and the positive case needs an additional stability condition. This gives students the conceptual landscape without pretending that all cases are equally constructive.
