Deformations Of Complex Structures

Two arcs: local Maurer-Cartan equations for almost complex structures, then families, Kodaira-Spencer maps, and Kuranishi theory.

Local Theory

Deform \(T^{0,1}\), impose bracket closure, solve \(\bar\partial\phi+[\phi,\phi]=0\).

Calabi-Yau Move

Use a volume form to turn brackets into exact terms and remove formal obstructions.

Family Theory

Package nearby fibres by a base space, the KS map, and complete or versal pullback properties.

Open with the scope of the chapter rather than a technical definition. The first arc treats complex structures on a fixed differentiable manifold as points in an infinite-dimensional space, then studies the integrability equations infinitesimally. The second arc changes viewpoint: a deformation is a family over a base, and local moduli are described by Kodaira-Spencer and Kuranishi theory.

Infinitesimal Strategy

The quotient slide is the conceptual setup for printed pages 255 to 256. The set of all almost complex structures is larger and better behaved than the integrable locus. Deforming \(T^{0,1}\) As A Graph

LOCAL COORDINATE FOR DEFORMATIONS
Graph Parameter
For small \(t\), a nearby antiholomorphic subbundle is encoded by \(\phi(t):T^{0,1}\to T^{1,0}\), so \(\phi(t)\in A^{0,1}(T_X)\).

Power Series

\(\phi(t)=\phi_1t+\phi_2t^2+\phi_3t^3+\cdots\)

The coefficients are the variables in the deformation problem.

This slide translates a moving complex structure into a form-valued tangent vector. For a small deformation, the new antiholomorphic tangent bundle can be written as the graph of a bundle map from the old T zero one into T one zero. The compactness assumption in the source keeps the smallness condition harmless.

Bracket Closure: Integrability As Residual Vanishing

INTEGRABILITY TEST
Closure Of The Antiholomorphic Distribution
The deformed almost complex structure is integrable exactly when \([T_t^{0,1},T_t^{0,1}]\subset T_t^{0,1}\).

Residual Interpretation

Compute the bracket, project away the graph direction, and ask for the quotient component to vanish.

Keep the geometric meaning visible before writing the Maurer-Cartan formula. Integrability means the antiholomorphic directions close under the Lie bracket. If the bracket of two graph sections has a component transverse to the graph, that component is the residual that obstructs integrability.

Maurer-Cartan Residual

LEMMA 6.1.2
Zero Set Equation
The graph deformation is integrable precisely when $$\bar\partial\phi(t)+[\phi(t),\phi(t)]=0.$$

Linear Plus Quadratic

The \(\bar\partial\)-term is the first-order part; the bracket is the nonlinear correction forced by closure.

Now give the formula. The bracket in A zero star of T X combines the Lie bracket of vector fields with wedge product in the form direction. The Maurer-Cartan residual is a nonlinear map from graph parameters to A zero two of T X, and the integrable deformations are its zero set.

At order \(k\), only lower coefficients create the known source term. Solving means asking whether that source is \(\bar\partial\)-exact.

This is the computational heart of the first section. The first equation says phi one is dbar closed. At every later order, the lower coefficients have already produced a bracket source. The next coefficient exists only if this source can be killed by applying dbar to something.

Kodaira-Spencer Class: Closed Forms Modulo Gauge

FIRST ORDER
Kodaira-Spencer Class
A one-parameter deformation has first term \(\phi_1\) with \(\bar\partial\phi_1=0\), defining \([\phi_1]\in H^1(X,T_X)\).

Gauge Directions

Infinitesimal diffeomorphisms change \(\phi_1\) by \(\bar\partial\)-exact terms, so first-order classes live in cohomology.

Connect the algebra to the quotient by diffeomorphisms. The first coefficient of the deformation is dbar closed, so it defines a Dolbeault cohomology class. A first-order deformation coming only from a one-parameter family of diffeomorphisms gives an exact class. Therefore H one of the tangent sheaf is the true first-order deformation space.

Obstruction In \(H^2\)

SECOND ORDER TEST
The First Possible Block
For \(v=[\phi_1]\), the order-two equation asks $$\bar\partial\phi_2=-[\phi_1,\phi_1].$$ If \([v,v]\ne0\in H^2(X,T_X)\), integration stops.

Geometric Meaning

A tangent vector to the would-be moduli space may fail to be the tangent of any actual curve of complex structures.

Emphasize that obstruction is not philosophical here; it is visible in the second recursive equation. The bracket of the first-order representative gives a closed A zero two of T X form. If its cohomology class in H two of T X is nonzero, no choice of phi two can solve the equation.

Calabi-Yau Contraction Ladder

CALABI-YAU INPUT
Holomorphic Volume Form
For compact Kahler \(X^n\) with \(K_X\simeq O_X\), choose \(\Omega\in H^0(X,K_X)\). Contraction gives \(\eta:\Lambda^pT_X\simeq\Omega_X^{n-p}\).

Why It Matters

The deformation complex can be compared with ordinary differential forms, where Hodge theory and the \(\partial\bar\partial\)-lemma are available.

The Calabi-Yau hypothesis enters through a concrete isomorphism, not through a metric at first. A nowhere vanishing holomorphic volume form converts polyvector fields into forms by contraction. This is the bridge that lets the Tian-Todorov argument translate bracket expressions into exact form statements.

\(\Delta\) Is Not The Laplacian

NOTATION WARNING
Historical Delta
The operator is $$\Delta=\eta^{-1}\partial\eta,$$ not a metric Laplacian. No metric is needed to define it.

Toy Identities

\(\Delta^2=0,\qquad \Delta\bar\partial=-\bar\partial\Delta.\)

The anti-commutation follows from \(\bar\partial\Omega=0\) and the usual sign rule.

Pause on the warning because the symbol Delta can mislead students. Here Delta is eta inverse composed with partial composed with eta, so it is a differential on polyvector-valued forms. It squares to zero and anti-commutes with dbar, but it is unrelated to the Laplacian until a metric is introduced later for Hodge choices.

Tian-Todorov Proof Move

LEMMA 6.1.9
Bracket As A Defect Of \(\Delta\)
For suitable degrees, \([\alpha,\beta]\) is the failure of \(\Delta\) to behave as a derivation of the wedge product.

Key Corollary

If \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) are \(\Delta\)-closed, then \([\alpha,\beta]\) is \(\Delta\)-exact; after applying \(\eta\), the bracket becomes exact form data.

State the proof move rather than the full coordinate proof. Tian-Todorov identifies the bracket with the failure of Delta to satisfy a Leibniz rule. When the inputs are Delta closed, this makes the bracket Delta exact. Combined with the Calabi-Yau contraction and the dbar partial lemma, this is what kills the obstruction terms.

Families Of Complex Manifolds

FAMILY VIEWPOINT
Deformation Of A Fibre
A smooth proper family \(\pi:\mathcal X\to S\) is studied near \(0\in S\) as a deformation of \(X=\mathcal X_0\).

Source Phenomena

  • All fibres isomorphic, but family not globally a product.
  • Fibres vary effectively, as with elliptic curves over \(\mathbb H\).
  • General fibres can converge to a different special fibre.
Shift from equations to families, following printed pages 268 to 270. The examples are meant to prevent a naive view of families: locally trivial, globally nontrivial, effectively parametrizing, and special-fiber behavior are different phenomena. Ehresmann says proper differentiable families are locally products, while Fischer-Grauert says a proper complex family with all fibres isomorphic is locally trivial holomorphically.

KS Map And Complex Spaces

KODAIRA-SPENCER MAP
Boundary Map
From \(0\to T_X\to T_{\mathcal X}|_X\to T_0S\otimes O_X\to0\), the boundary map sends base tangent vectors to first-order deformation classes.

Why Complex Spaces Enter

First-order deformations are naturally deformations over the double point, so nonreduced bases record infinitesimal directions.

Use the exact sequence to connect the family viewpoint back to H one of T X. A tangent vector on the base lifts locally to vector fields on the total space, and the obstruction to gluing those lifts is the Kodaira-Spencer class. Complex spaces enter because the double point is the clean base for first-order deformations.

Complete, Versal, Kuranishi

LOCAL MODULI LANGUAGE
Three Strengths
Complete means every nearby deformation is a pullback. Universal also makes the pullback map unique. Versal only requires uniqueness of the differential.

Criteria To Remember

  • Reduced base: completeness iff the KS map is surjective.
  • Kuranishi: every compact complex manifold has a versal deformation.
  • If \(H^2(X,T_X)=0\), deformations are unobstructed; Calabi-Yau manifolds are unobstructed by Tian-Todorov.

Projectivity Caution

Small deformations of projective manifolds need not remain projective unless the algebraic problem is modified, often by polarizing.

End by naming the local moduli theorem and the vocabulary students will see in applications. Complete means the family captures all nearby deformations by pullback, universal adds uniqueness, and versal keeps only first-order uniqueness. Kuranishi guarantees a versal family for any compact complex manifold, while unobstructedness means the base is smooth; H two vanishing and the Calabi-Yau Tian-Todorov result are the main criteria in this chapter.
