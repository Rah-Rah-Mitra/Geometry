1. Algebraic shell for forms

Differential Graded Algebras

Definition Pattern

A dga \(A=\bigoplus_{i\ge 0} A^i\) has a graded product and a degree \(+1\) map \(d\) with \(d^2=0\).

\(a\cdot b=(-1)^{ij} b\cdot a,\qquad d(a b)=d(a)b+(-1)^i a\,d(b)\)

Example. The smooth forms \((A^*(M),d)\) form a dga; its cohomology is de Rham cohomology with the wedge product.

DGA = differential graded algebraAppendix 31 / 15
Define a dga as the algebraic shell of the de Rham complex: graded pieces, graded commutative multiplication, and a differential satisfying the Leibniz rule. Students should see that cohomology is not merely a vector space here; the product descends because the differential behaves like a derivation.
2. Replace by cohomology-equivalent objects

Quasi-Isomorphisms and Equivalence

Quasi-Isomorphism

A dga map \(f:(A,d_A)\to(B,d_B)\) is a quasi-isomorphism when \(f^*:H^*(A)\to H^*(B)\) is an isomorphism.

Equivalence

Two dgas are equivalent if they are joined by a finite zigzag of quasi-isomorphisms. The arrows may point either way.

\( (A,d_A)\;\simeq_{\mathrm{qis}}\;(B,d_B) \)
Quasi-isomorphism preserves cohomology algebraAppendix 31 / 15
The key move is to compare dgas by the cohomology they induce, not by literal equality. A quasi-isomorphism can replace a complicated algebra by a simpler one without changing cohomology, and a zigzag of such maps is the equivalence relation used later in the definition of formality.
3. Sullivan's controlled replacement

Minimal Models: Free, Triangular, and Homotopical

Minimal DGA

\(M^0=k\), the positive part is freely generated by homogeneous \(x_i\), and \(d x_i\) uses only earlier generators.

Sullivan theorem. Every simply connected dga has a minimal model, unique up to isomorphism.

odd sphere: \(M=S^*(x),\ |x|=2n+1,\ dx=0\)
Minimality turns replacement into a triangular inductionAppendix 31 / 15
Minimal models are the controlled free replacements for simply connected dgas. Emphasize the triangular rule: each new generator has a differential made from earlier generators. This is why the model can create missing cohomology classes and then kill unwanted ones without losing the inductive grip.
4. The collapse statement

Formal Means Equivalent to Cohomology

Definition

A dga \((A,d)\) is formal if it is equivalent to a dga with zero differential. Equivalently, it is equivalent to \((H^*(A,d),0)\).

Strength. Formality does not say every closed form is harmonic or every product is simple. It says the entire dga has the same quasi-isomorphism type as its cohomology algebra.

\( (A^*(M),d)\quad \leadsto \quad (H^*(M,\mathbb{R}),0) \)
Formal manifold: its de Rham dga is formalAppendix 31 / 15
Formality says that the differential algebra carries no more real homotopy information than its cohomology ring with zero differential. Make the claim feel strong: the de Rham complex has infinitely many forms and a differential, while the target has only cohomology classes and multiplication.
5. The analytic engine

The \(dd^c\) Input on Compact \(K\ddot{a}hler\) Manifolds

Operators

\(d^c=-i(\partial-\bar{\partial})\), \((d^c)^2=0\), and \(dd^c=-d^c d\) up to the chosen convention.

Lemma Shape

If \(a\) is \(d\)-closed and \(d^c\)-exact on compact \(K\ddot{a}hler\) \(X\), then \(a=dd^c b\).

The source proves this from Hodge decomposition and Kahler identities; the deck uses it as the hinge for the two quasi-isomorphisms.

ddbar language and \(dd^c\) language carry the same role hereAppendix 31 / 15
Now bring in the compact Kahler hypothesis. The ddbar or ddc lemma converts a form that is closed for one operator and exact for the other into a double derivative. This is the analytic lever that will turn two natural algebra maps into quasi-isomorphisms.
6. First map in the proof

Inclusion of \(d^c\)-Closed Forms

\(i:(A^*(X)_c,d)\hookrightarrow(A^*(X),d)\)
Claim

For compact \(K\ddot{a}hler\) \(X\), this inclusion is a dga quasi-isomorphism.

Injective on cohomology. If a \(d^c\)-closed form is \(d\)-exact, the \(dd^c\) lemma makes it a \(d\)-boundary using a \(d^c\)-closed primitive.

Surjective on cohomology. Choose harmonic representatives; in the Kahler setting they are also closed for the auxiliary complex operators.

Corollary 3.A.24 in the source spanAppendix 31 / 15
The first map includes dc-closed forms into all forms. Injectivity uses the ddc lemma to show that a dc-closed exact form was already a boundary inside the smaller dga. Surjectivity uses harmonic representatives, since Kahler identities make harmonic forms closed for the auxiliary operator as well.
7. Second map in the proof

Projection to \(d^c\)-Cohomology

\(p:(A^*(X)_c,d)\longrightarrow (H^*_{d^c}(X),d)\)
Target

\(H^k_{d^c}(X)=\ker(d^c:A^k\to A^{k+1})/\operatorname{im}(d^c:A^{k-1}\to A^k)\).

The \(dd^c\) lemma again proves that \(p\) is a quasi-isomorphism, then forces the induced \(d\) on \(H^*_{d^c}(X)\) to vanish.

Corollaries 3.A.26 and 3.A.27Appendix 31 / 15
The second map projects dc-closed forms to dc cohomology. The same ddc lemma handles both directions: closed exact errors become internal boundaries, and a class whose d derivative vanishes in dc cohomology can be adjusted to a representative closed for both d and dc.
8. Main theorem of the appendix span

Compact \(K\ddot{a}hler\) Manifolds Are Formal

Input

Compact \(K\ddot{a}hler\) Hodge theory and the \(dd^c\) lemma.

Output

\((A^*(X),d)\) is equivalent to a dga with trivial differential.

Consequence

The cohomology ring controls the real homotopy data visible to the de Rham dga.

Proposition 3.A.28Appendix 31 / 15
Assemble the two quasi-isomorphisms into the proof. The de Rham dga is connected by a zigzag to dc cohomology, and the induced d on that cohomology is zero. Therefore a compact Kahler manifold is formal, so its real homotopy type is constrained by the cohomology algebra.
9. Higher product detector

Massey Triple Products

Setup

Let \(\alpha\in H^p\), \(\beta\in H^q\), \(\gamma\in H^r\), with \(\alpha\beta=0=\beta\gamma\).

Choose \(a\wedge b=df,\quad b\wedge c=dg\).
\(\langle\alpha,\beta,\gamma\rangle=\left[f\wedge c-(-1)^p a\wedge g\right]\)

The class is read modulo the usual products \(H^{p+q-1}H^r+H^pH^{q+r-1}\).

Massey products make sense for any dgaAppendix 31 / 15
Massey triple products measure a second-order failure after two ordinary products vanish. The choices of primitives create an indeterminacy quotient, but a nonzero class in that quotient is meaningful. Present it as a detector for information that the ordinary cohomology ring alone may miss.
10. Obstruction principle

Formal DGA Implies Trivial Massey Products

Naturality

DGA maps carry Massey products to Massey products, modulo the corresponding indeterminacy.

Formal Case

Compute in \((H^*(A),0)\); with zero differential the defining higher product has no nontrivial class.

Caution. Vanishing triple products alone is not a complete proof of formality, but a single nonzero one is a decisive obstruction.

Proposition 3.A.33Appendix 31 / 15
Naturality makes Massey products compatible with dga maps, so a formal dga lets us compute them inside its cohomology dga with zero differential. There, the defining primitives can be taken trivially and the products vanish. A nonzero Massey product is therefore an obstruction to formality.
11. A concrete nonformal manifold

Heisenberg Nilmanifold: A Massey Witness

Invariant Forms

\(\alpha=dx,\quad \beta=dz,\quad \delta=x\,dz-dy\)

\(d\alpha=0,\quad d\beta=0,\quad d\delta=\alpha\wedge\beta\)

Triple Product

\(\langle[\alpha],[\beta],[\beta]\rangle\) is represented by \(\delta\wedge\beta\), and this class is nonzero in the quotient.

Conclusion. The compact quotient \(G/\Gamma\) is not formal.

Example 3.A.34Appendix 31 / 15
The real Heisenberg nilmanifold supplies the concrete obstruction. Invariant one-forms alpha, beta, and delta satisfy d alpha equals d beta equals zero and d delta equals alpha beta. That relation makes the triple product alpha, beta, beta defined, and its delta beta representative is nonzero.
12. Why the obstruction matters

Iwasawa: Numerical Tests Are Not Enough

Kahler Consequence

Compact \(K\ddot{a}hler\) manifolds are formal, so a nonformal smooth manifold cannot support any \(K\ddot{a}hler\) complex structure.

The Iwasawa example passes the even-\(b_1\) numerical test, but the Massey product remembers differential algebra that cohomology dimensions do not see.

Teaching point. Formality can obstruct \(K\ddot{a}hler\) geometry even when Betti-number parity has no objection.

Iwasawa manifold as non-Kahler witnessAppendix 31 / 15
The complex Iwasawa manifold is the complex Heisenberg quotient analogue. Its first Betti number is even, so the simplest numerical Kahler obstruction does not rule it out. The Massey obstruction is finer: nonformality prevents any Kahler structure on the underlying differentiable manifold.

14. What to carry forward

Recap: Formality as a Three-Part Story

Algebra

Dgas encode forms, products, and differentials. Minimal models make that encoding homotopically manageable.

Kahler Input

The \(dd^c\) lemma produces two quasi-isomorphisms and a zero differential target.

Obstruction

Massey products detect nonformal differential algebra, ruling out Kahler structures in examples such as Iwasawa.
