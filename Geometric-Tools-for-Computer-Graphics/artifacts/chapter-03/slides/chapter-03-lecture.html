Vector Basics

Free Vectors Forget Where They Were Drawn

Definition Contract

A vector is a displacement with length and direction; its drawn base point is not part of its identity.

\[ u \sim u' \quad \text{when length and direction agree} \]
Type Discipline
  • \(u + v\), \(\alpha u\), and \(-u\) return vectors.
  • \(P + u\) returns a point; \(Q - P\) returns a vector.
  • \(P + Q\) and \(\alpha P\) need an affine interpretation before they are meaningful.
Invarianttranslation changes coordinates, not displacements
Stress that the head to tail diagram is not merely a drawing trick. It is the operational meaning of vector addition. A vector may be moved for construction because its identity is length and direction. Once a point is present, however, the operation has a type: point plus vector is a point, while point minus point is a vector.
Vector Spaces

Span Describes Reach; A Basis Describes Coordinates

Span

For vectors \(v_1,\ldots,v_n\), the span is every linear combination that can be built from them.

\[ \operatorname{span}\{v_i\}=\left\{\sum_i x_i v_i : x_i\in\mathbb{R}\right\} \]
Basis
  • Spanning means every target vector is reachable.
  • Independence means no listed vector is redundant.
  • For a basis, coordinate coefficients are unique.
Failure moderank loss makes coordinates nonunique or impossible
Move from the walking intuition to the algebraic abstraction. Span answers what can be reached by linear combinations. A basis adds the no redundancy condition, which is why coordinates become unique. In graphics code, the practical signal is rank: nearly parallel frame vectors or collapsed triangle edges make a coordinate solve unstable.
Orientation

Orientation Is A Sign Attached To An Ordered Basis

Signed Area Test
\[ \operatorname{orient}(u,v)=\det\begin{bmatrix}u_x & v_x\\ u_y & v_y\end{bmatrix} \]
  • Positive and negative signs distinguish the two ordered orientations.
  • Swapping \(u\) and \(v\) changes the sign.
  • A zero determinant means the ordered pair does not define a plane basis.
Convention Alert

The right hand rule is a convention that turns rotation order into a normal direction; code must state the convention it uses.

Notebook checkorientation changes sign when two vertices are swapped
Make the ambiguity explicit. A flat drawing alone cannot tell students which side of the plane is positive without an orientation convention. The determinant gives a computational sign for an ordered pair, and the right hand rule gives the corresponding three dimensional normal direction. The key programming habit is to document and test the order convention.
Frames

A Frame Gives Coordinates To Affine Geometry

Frame Equation

With origin \(O\) and basis matrix \(A=[a_1\ a_2\ \cdots]\), a point is reconstructed by

\[ P = O + A x_A. \]
Change Coordinates, Not The Point
\[ x_B = B^{-1}(O - O' + A x_A) \]

The object \(P\) is geometric; the coordinate tuple depends on the chosen frame.

Frame invariantreconstruction returns the same point after coordinate conversion
This is where students often mix up vectors and coordinates. A coordinate tuple is not itself the point; it is instructions for rebuilding the point from an origin and basis. Changing frames should change the tuple and preserve the reconstructed point. That distinction is exactly what camera spaces, object spaces, and world spaces rely on.

Affine Combination

A weighted sum of points is affine when the weights sum to one.

\[ \sum_i \lambda_i P_i,\qquad \sum_i \lambda_i=1 \]
Translation Test

After translating every \(P_i\) by the same vector \(t\), an affine combination translates by \(t\). If the weights do not sum to one, the expression depends on an arbitrary origin.

Notebook checkaffine combinations are invariant under translation
Keep the table visible long enough for students to see why affine geometry is not just linear algebra with prettier notation. Points cannot be added without an affine constraint because the result would depend on an origin that the geometry did not specify. The sum to one condition is the small algebraic rule that cancels that origin dependence.
Affine Transformations

Affine Maps Preserve Weighted Point Constructions

Affine Map

In coordinates, an affine map has a linear part and a translation.

\[ F(p)=Lp+t \]
Preservation Law
\[ F\!\left(\sum_i \lambda_i P_i\right)=\sum_i \lambda_i F(P_i),\quad \sum_i\lambda_i=1 \]
  • Translation, rotation, scale, and shear are affine.
  • Compositions of affine maps remain affine.
  • Linearity applies to displacement vectors, not raw points.
Graphics useobject, world, camera, and texture transforms are affine until projection enters
Connect this slide to the transformations that students already use in rendering pipelines. The map can move the origin, shear axes, rotate, or scale, but it still preserves affine combinations. That is why a transformed triangle can be obtained by transforming its vertices, and why points interpolated before or after an affine transform agree.
Euclidean Geometry

The Dot Product Turns Direction Into A Metric Query

Metric Formula
\[ u\cdot v=\|u\|\,\|v\|\cos\theta \]

The sign and magnitude answer whether two directions reinforce, oppose, or meet orthogonally.

Projection
\[ \operatorname{proj}_v(u)=\frac{u\cdot v}{v\cdot v}\,v \]
  • Closest-point routines use this scalar parameter before clamping.
  • Normals use the zero dot product condition to test perpendicularity.
Metric checkfor unit vectors, the dot product must stay in [-1, 1]
Present the dot product as the bridge from pure vector algebra to Euclidean measurement. The formula gives angles, projections, and perpendicularity tests with one scalar. In later distance chapters, this scalar often becomes the parameter of the closest point on a line, ray, segment, or face before domain constraints are applied.
Area And Normals

The Cross Product Couples Area With Orientation

Normal And Area
\[ u\times v \perp u,\qquad u\times v \perp v,\qquad \|u\times v\|=\|u\|\,\|v\|\sin\theta \]
Order Matters
\[ v\times u = -(u\times v) \]

Triangle winding, face normals, back-face culling, and signed area tests all depend on this convention.

Notebook Check

Assert \( (u\times v)\cdot u \approx 0\) and \( (u\times v)\cdot v \approx 0\).

Orientation conventionswitching vertex order reverses the normal
Use the cross product slide to tie algebra directly to mesh behavior. The vector length measures parallelogram area, while the direction records orientation. That direction is not optional in graphics: it controls face normals, winding order, lighting, culling, and several intersection tests. The perpendicular dot checks are simple and worth asserting.
Volume And Dependence

The Scalar Triple Product Measures Signed Volume

Determinant Form
\[ [u,v,w]=u\cdot(v\times w)=\det\begin{bmatrix}u&v&w\end{bmatrix} \]
Geometry
  • \(|[u,v,w]|\) is the parallelepiped volume.
  • The sign records orientation of the ordered triple.
  • A near-zero value warns that a tetrahedron or frame is nearly flat.
Tolerance

In production code, treat small determinants as diagnostic quantities, not as perfect Booleans.

Simplex bridgetetrahedron volume is one sixth of the absolute triple product
The triple product is the determinant story in geometric clothing. Its magnitude measures volume and its sign measures orientation. This is also the bridge to simplex degeneracy: a tetrahedron with very small signed volume is nearly coplanar, so barycentric solves and containment tests become sensitive to numerical noise.
Barycentric Coordinates

Weights Locate A Point Relative To A Simplex

Barycentric Form
\[ P=\lambda_0P_0+\cdots+\lambda_kP_k,\qquad \sum_{i=0}^{k}\lambda_i=1 \]

The weights are affine coordinates relative to the simplex vertices.

Triangle Test
  • All \(\lambda_i \ge 0\): point is inside or on the triangle.
  • One \(\lambda_i = 0\): point lies on the opposite edge.
  • Some \(\lambda_i < 0\): point is outside across that edge.
Notebook checkbarycentric weights sum to one
Emphasize that barycentric coordinates are affine coordinates, not just a triangle trick. They reconstruct the point from vertices with weights that sum to one. The sign pattern gives a containment test, and the same idea powers interpolation of normals, colors, texture coordinates, and many finite element quantities over a mesh.
Simplexes And Affine Independence

A Simplex Is A Nondegenerate Affine Coordinate System

Independence Matrix

For vertices \(P_0,\ldots,P_k\), build the edge matrix

\[ A=\begin{bmatrix}P_1-P_0 & \cdots & P_k-P_0\end{bmatrix}. \]

The simplex is nondegenerate when \(A\) has rank \(k\).

Solve For Weights
\[ P-P_0=A\begin{bmatrix}\lambda_1\\ \vdots\\ \lambda_k\end{bmatrix},\qquad \lambda_0=1-\sum_{i=1}^{k}\lambda_i \]
Degeneracy

Small rank margins mean the simplex is nearly collapsed; report the residual instead of trusting the weights blindly.

Affine independencesimplex coordinates fail exactly where the edge matrix loses rank
Use the tetrahedron to generalize the triangle story. A k simplex is built from k plus one affinely independent points, and the edge matrix turns barycentric recovery into a linear solve. The determinant or rank margin is not bookkeeping; it is the numerical health indicator for interpolation, collision queries, and mesh element quality.
Implementation Checklist

Turn Vector Algebra Into Assertions

Represent The Type

Keep point, vector, coordinate tuple, and frame vector roles explicit in names and data structures.

Carry The Convention

Record orientation order, handedness, coordinate frame, and transform direction where the data is created.

Measure A Residual

Check weight sums, perpendicularity, reconstruction error, determinant magnitude, and rank margin.

Report Degeneracy

Nearly parallel vectors, flat simplexes, and tiny denominators should be visible diagnostic states.

Applied Lab Prompt

Choose a triangle or tetrahedron from a graphics pipeline. Compute barycentric coordinates for a sample point, then translate the entire configuration and verify that the reconstructed point translates while the weights remain unchanged.

Chapter Takeaway

Vector algebra is the discipline of preserving geometric meaning while changing representations.

\[ \text{same geometry} \neq \text{same coordinates} \]
