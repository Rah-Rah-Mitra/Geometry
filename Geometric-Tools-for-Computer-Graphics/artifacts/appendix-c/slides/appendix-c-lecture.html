Triangles

One Triangle, Several Equivalent Measurements

Length and Area

\(2p=a+b+c\)
\(A=\frac{1}{2}\left|(V_2-V_1)\times(V_3-V_1)\right|\)

Equivalent Checks

\(A=\frac{ah_a}{2}\)
\(A=\sqrt{p(p-a)(p-b)(p-c)}\)

Use determinant area and Heron area as independent implementations.

triangle area formulas agree in final-sanity.jsonorientation may change sign, length does not
Treat the triangle as the basic executable object. The audience should see that side lengths, signed area, perimeter, and named centers all come from the same vertex data. Emphasize that changing vertex order affects orientation but should not change unsigned area or length checks.
Triangle centers

Centers Are Weighted Constructions

Centroid

\(C_g=\frac{V_1+V_2+V_3}{3}\)

Pure affine averaging; stable unless the vertices themselves are invalid.

Inradius and Incenter

\(r=\frac{A}{p}\)
\(C_r=\frac{aV_1+bV_2+cV_3}{a+b+c}\)

Degeneracy Warning

As \(A\) approaches zero, radius and circumcenter formulas lose geometric meaning or become numerically brittle.

center formulas encode conventionsnear-collinear triangles require explicit policy
Slow down on the center formulas because they are a useful debugging example. The incenter weights vertices by opposite side length, the centroid averages vertices, and the circumcenter is sensitive to near collinearity. The warning is that named centers are conventions plus degeneracy handling, not just decorative points.

Programming Use

Special cases are not separate code paths by default. They are test fixtures for the general formula implementation.

calibration before optimizationarea, radius, and altitude must be mutually consistent
Present right and equilateral triangles as calibration cases for general formulas. They give easy numbers for a renderer or geometry kernel, but the real lesson is that altitude, median, radius, and area formulas must agree when a special case is embedded inside a general triangle routine.

Convention check. Diagonal formulas require the included angle \(\theta\); base height formulas require a chosen parallel pair or altitude.

area by decompositionangle conventions are part of the input contract
Explain quadrilateral area as a decomposition problem. Squares, rectangles, parallelograms, rhombi, and trapezoids look like separate formula entries, yet most can be checked by splitting into triangles or by comparing base height, diagonals, and angle conventions. That unifies the lookup table.
Circles

Sectors and Segments Depend on Radian Measure

Full Circle

\(c=2\pi r,\qquad A=\pi r^2\)

Sector

\(s=\alpha r,\qquad A_{\mathrm{sector}}=\frac{\alpha r^2}{2}\)

Segment

\(x=2r\sin(\alpha/2)\)
\(A_{\mathrm{seg}}=\frac{r^2}{2}(\alpha-\sin\alpha)\)
notebook check: sector area scales linearly with angleradian angles make s = alpha r
Make radians nonnegotiable on this slide. Arc length and sector area are simple only because the angle is measured in radians. The segment formula then becomes a subtraction story: sector area minus the isosceles triangle determined by the chord.
Polyhedra

Extrusion, Tapering, and the One-Third Factor

PrimitiveFormulaMeaning
Box\(d=\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}\)3D Pythagorean check
Box\(S=2(ab+bc+ac)\)paired faces
Box\(V=abc\)three-axis product
Prism\(V=Bh\)base swept through height
Pyramid\(V=Bh/3\)similar cross sections shrink

Dimensional check. Surface terms must contain two lengths; volume terms must contain three.

volumes encode sweep typesurface area and volume must not share units
Use the polyhedron slide to distinguish extrusion from tapering. A box is a three-axis product, a prism is base area swept through height, and a pyramid carries the one-third factor because cross sections shrink quadratically with height. That is the conceptual check.
Round solids

Cylinders Sweep; Cones Taper

Cylinder

\(B=\pi r^2,\qquad A_{\mathrm{lat}}=2\pi rh\)
\(S=2\pi r(r+h),\qquad V=\pi r^2h\)

Cone

\(s=\sqrt{r^2+h^2}\)
\(S=\pi r(r+s),\qquad V=\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2h\)

The same base \(B\) produces different volume factors depending on whether the cross section stays constant or tapers to a point.

lateral area comes from unrollingvolume mirrors prism versus pyramid logic
For cylinders and cones, connect the formulas to unrolling and slicing rather than memorization. The lateral surface of a cylinder unfolds to a rectangle, the cone unfolds with slant height, and the volume factors follow the same sweep versus taper contrast as prisms and pyramids.
Spheres and caps

Spherical Segments Couple Height and Chord Radius

Full Sphere

\(S=4\pi r^2,\qquad V=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3\)

One-Base Segment

\(a^2=h(2r-h)\)
\(S_{\mathrm{cap}}=2\pi rh\)
\(V_{\mathrm{cap}}=\frac{\pi h^2}{3}(3r-h)\)

Domain check. A cap height must satisfy \(0\le h\le 2r\); outside that interval, the formula describes no spherical cap.

surface scales as length squaredvolume scales as length cubed
The sphere slide should emphasize powers of radius and cap geometry. Surface area scales like radius squared, volume like radius cubed, and a cap is controlled by height. The cap formulas are valuable because they couple a planar radius to a curved surface measurement.
Torus formulas and sampling

Analytic Formulas

\(S=4\pi^2Rr\)
\(V=2\pi^2Rr^2\)

Parametric Audit

\(X(u,v)=((R+r\cos v)\cos u,\ (R+r\cos v)\sin u,\ r\sin v)\)

Notebook check. A sampled torus mesh approximates the analytic surface area within the recorded tolerance.

two-radius convention: R major, r minorsampling converts a formula into an auditable residual
Bring in the torus from the notebook's computational treatment as a model for formula verification. The major radius moves the tube center, the minor radius sizes the tube, and the area element changes around the ring. Sampling gives a numerical audit of the analytic surface expression.
Dimensional analysis

Scale Laws Catch Formula Bugs Early

QuantityUnder \(x\mapsto \lambda x\)Examples
Length\(\lambda\)perimeter, arc length, diagonal, height
Area\(\lambda^2\)triangle area, circle area, surface area
Volume\(\lambda^3\)box, prism, cone, sphere, torus volume
Angleunchangedsector angle, triangle angles, diagonal angle

Fast rejection test. A proposed surface formula with three length factors is already suspect before numerical data is inspected.

scale is an invariant familyangles are dimensionless; measurements are not
This slide is the practical guardrail for the whole appendix. If a formula that claims to compute area scales like length or volume, the code is wrong even before a numerical test. Encourage students to use dimensional analysis as a cheap first-line invariant.

Takeaways

How to Use Appendix C in a Graphics Pipeline

Represent

Name the data: vertices, base area, radius, height, slant height, angle, or parameter grid.

Constrain

State the domain: positive lengths, radian angles, ordered vertices, nondegenerate height, or valid cap interval.

Measure

Choose the formula that matches the representation, not the one with the shortest expression.

Decompose

Split areas into triangles, sweep volumes through height, or taper cross sections when possible.

Scale

Check that lengths, areas, and volumes respond with powers one, two, and three.

Audit

Pair each formula with a residual, sampled approximation, or independent equivalent formula.
