Homogeneous Spaces

Lecture Recap

Portable Quotient Checklist

Orbit Set

Define the equivalence relation, but do not assume the result is a manifold.

Freeness

Trivial isotropy makes orbit dimensions and local models stable.

Properness

Compactness control separates orbit classes and prevents dense-pathology examples.

Adapted Charts

Local quotient coordinates are transverse coordinates to the orbits.

Coverings

Discrete free proper actions give evenly covered quotient maps.

Lie Applications

Closed normal subgroups and isotropy groups produce quotient Lie groups and homogeneous spaces.
