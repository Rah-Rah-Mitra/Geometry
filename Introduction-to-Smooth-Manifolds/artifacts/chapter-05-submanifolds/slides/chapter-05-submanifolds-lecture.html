Figure-Eight Sample

Minimum sampled speed \(0.69597\); sampled immersion test passes.

Parabola

Gradient

\(\nabla\rho(0,0,1)=(0,0,2)\), rank \(1\).

Tangent Test

Gradient dot tangent basis values are \(0.0\) and \(0.0\).

Mesh Residual

Check: local-slice-level-set.jsonRegularity turns an equation into a chart
The sphere slice is the clean regular level-set example. At the north pole, the gradient of rho has rank one, tangent basis vectors kill the gradient, and a local graph z equals square root of one minus x squared minus y squared gives a slice chart.

Restricting MapsTopology gates

Map Restrictions Need The Correct Topology On The Target

Restrict Domain

Automatic for maps \(F:M\to N\) restricted to immersed or embedded \(S\subset M\).

Embedded Codomain

Automatic by the subspace topology when the image lies in embedded \(S\).

Immersed Codomain

Requires continuity into the topology on \(S\).

Weakly Embedded

The needed restriction behavior is built into the definition.

Table: restriction-gates.csvTopology is part of smoothness
Map restriction is a topology gate, not just notation. Restricting the domain is automatic for immersed or embedded submanifolds, but restricting the codomain through an immersed submanifold needs an extra continuity hypothesis; embedded codomains get that gate from the subspace topology.
