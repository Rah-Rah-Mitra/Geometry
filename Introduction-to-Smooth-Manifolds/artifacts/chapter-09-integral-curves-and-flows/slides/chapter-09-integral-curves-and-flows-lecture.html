Definition

Integral Curves: Velocity Is The Field Value

Definition 9.1
Integral curve through a point

A smooth curve gamma:J -> M is an integral curve of V when every chart sees the ODE

gamma'(t) = V(gamma(t)),   gamma(0)=p.
Translation model: residual vector is (0,0).
Rotation model: residual vector is (0,0).
Inspection Target

Integral curves are not drawn as arbitrary streamlines: the residual check verifies the defining equation.

Use this slide to make the definition operational. Point to the translation field first: a horizontal line has exactly the velocity that the field prescribes. Then point to the rotation field: the circle is not just visually plausible; the notebook verifies that the derivative of the curve matches the vector field at each point. That residual-zero habit is the computational version of the definition.
From Curves To A Flow

Maximal Flow Domains Are Open, Not Usually Rectangular

Theorem Pattern
Maximal flow

The maximal integral curves assemble into Phi:D -> M, where D is open in R x M, Phi(0,p)=p, and the flow law holds whenever both sides are defined.

Model Check

For x' = x^2, the domain inequality is 1 - t x_0 > 0. The notebook checks both the flow law residual and the inverse residual as zero.

This is where students often expect a global R-action. Emphasize the picture on the left: even on a simple line, the admissible time interval depends on the starting point. The punctured-plane example adds a second failure mode: the vector field may be harmless, but the manifold can have a missing point that the trajectory reaches. The word maximal means no artificial truncation, not immortality.
Flowout Theorem

Flowouts Turn A Transverse Slice Into Coordinates

Construction
Start on a slice, then follow the flow

Given an initial submanifold S, define F(t,s)=Phi_t(s). If the vector field is transverse to S, the flowout becomes a local coordinate map.

F(t,s) = (s cos t, s sin t),   det DF = -s
Artifact Check

The determinant is nonzero for positive s, and the pushforward check partial_t F - W = 0 verifies the coordinate direction.

  • Time is one coordinate.
  • The initial slice supplies the remaining coordinates.
  • Near a regular point, the field becomes a coordinate vector field.
Treat the black segment as the initial data and the orange curves as time lines. The key geometric move is that flowing off the slice replaces an abstract vector field by one coordinate direction. In this model the determinant is minus s, so the positive part of the slice is regular. This is the local normal form idea made visible.
Manifolds With Boundary

At A Boundary, The Normal Component Decides What The Flow Can Do

Boundary Test
Not every vector field points into the manifold

In the half-plane model M={y >= 0}, the inward normal is n=(0,1). The sign of V dot n separates tangent, inward, and outward behavior.

Tangent: V dot n = 0; boundary coordinate is preserved.
Inward: V dot n = 1; one-sided collars form.
Outward: V dot n = -1; trajectories leave M.
Instructor cue: ordinary ODE existence must respect the allowed side.
This slide is a good place to slow down. On an interior point, small positive and negative times both make sense locally. At the boundary, the same ODE must also stay inside the half-space model. The tangent field moves along the boundary, the inward field creates a collar, and the outward field immediately exits the manifold. That sign test is the practical memory hook.
Comparing Vectors Along A Flow

The Lie Derivative Uses Pullback Before Differentiating

Definition 9.x
Compare at the same base point

Move by the V-flow, pull the nearby W vector back to the original point, and differentiate that comparison at time zero.

L_V W = [V,W]
1Flow the base point by Phi_t.
2Use the inverse differential to bring W_{Phi_t(p)} back to T_pM.
3The first-order change is the bracket. In the notebook model it equals (0,1).
The hard part of this definition is not the derivative; it is the comparison. Vectors based at different points do not live in the same tangent space, so the flow supplies the transport mechanism. The figure shows the nearby vector pulled back before the limit is taken. In the simple model with V equal to partial x and W equal to x partial y, the finite-difference limit and the bracket both give the vertical vector.
Lie Bracket Criterion

Two Local Motions Commute Exactly When The Bracket Vanishes

Criterion
Commuting local flows

Where both compositions are defined, the V-then-W square closes locally exactly when [V,W]=0.

Artifact Contrast

partial_x and partial_y give bracket (0,0). Replacing partial_y by x partial_y gives bracket (0,1) and endpoint gap (0,st).

The left panel is the mental model for coordinate vector fields: order does not matter, so the square closes. The right panel is more revealing because the horizontal move changes the size of the vertical field. The endpoint gap records the bracket as a second-order obstruction. This is why the bracket measures failure of flows to commute, not just an algebraic operation on formulas.
Time-Dependent Vector Fields

An Evolution Map Needs Both Start Time And End Time

Autonomous vs Time-Dependent
Elapsed time is no longer enough

For V(t,x)=x/t, the solution depends on the initial clock reading as well as the final clock reading.

Phi(t,t_0,x_0) = (t x_0)/t_0,   t,t_0 > 0
Cocycle Check

The notebook verifies the two-time composition law with residual zero.

Method Of Characteristics

First-Order PDEs Become Flowout Problems

Geometric Translation
Initial data plus characteristic flow

A first-order Cauchy problem asks for a graph swept out by the vector field determined by the PDE. Noncharacteristic initial data makes the projection locally regular.

Linear Model

The checked local solution is u=sqrt(x^2+y^2)+y, with PDE residual zero and noncharacteristic determinant s_pos.

Quasilinear Model

The graph-flowout solution is u=(x-y)/(1+y), valid where y>-1; the projection Jacobian is -t-1.

Local HTML artifact: quasilinear graph flowout
Connect this directly back to the flowout theorem. The initial curve is the slice, and the characteristic curves are the flow lines. In the linear model, the red curves carry the initial data into the plane where the contour colors show the solution. In the quasilinear model the real object is a graph in one higher dimension; regular projection is what lets that graph be read as a function of x and y.
Recap And Lab Prompts

What The Chapter Adds To The Manifold Toolkit

Takeaways
  • Integral curves are local ODE solutions with a geometric invariant: velocity equals field value.
  • Maximal flows assemble those curves, but domains can shrink through blow-up or escape.
  • Flowouts supply canonical coordinates, collars, and characteristic surfaces.
  • Lie brackets measure the first obstruction to commuting flows.
