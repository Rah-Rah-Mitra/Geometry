Stokes On The Rectangle

\(\int_{\partial R}\omega=\int_R d\omega=a b\).

Stokes theoremSlide 6 / 10
Present Stokes as the conceptual center of the chapter. On the rectangle, omega equals -y/2 dx plus x/2 dy, so d omega is dx wedge dy. The top and right boundary pieces each contribute a*b/2, and the boundary total equals the area integral a*b.

A Riemannian metric supplies a positive local volume density sqrt(det g). The sample density stays positive under orientation-preserving, orientation-reversing, and Mobius-loop transitions.

Orientation Sign Versus Density Sign

When Orientation Reverses

A top form records the sign of the chart transition. A density records the magnitude, so it stays positive on a nonorientable loop.

Lecture Recap

The Integration Toolkit

Measure

Top forms measure signed volume; dependent tangent vectors give zero.

Transform

Pullback inserts the Jacobian determinant, making coordinate changes intrinsic.

Assemble

Partitions of unity cut compact support into chart-supported integral pieces.

Boundary

Stokes identifies \(\int_M d\omega\) with \(\int_{\partial M}\omega\).

Corners

Boundary faces carry flux; lower-codimension corners handle sign bookkeeping.

Densities

Absolute-value transformation laws keep volume positive without orientation.
