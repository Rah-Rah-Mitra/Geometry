The First Obstruction

A Plane Field Can Twist Out Of Its Own Directions

Rank-Two Distribution

A distribution \(D\) assigns a subspace \(D_p \subset T_pM\) smoothly at each point.

\(X=\partial_x+y\partial_z,\quad Y=\partial_y\)
\([X,Y]=-\partial_z \notin \operatorname{span}(X,Y)\)
falsebracket lies in the span
Lie bracket testSlide 2 / 10
Use the first example as the chapter's warning light. The two vector fields X equals partial x plus y partial z and Y equals partial y span a rank-two plane field, but their bracket is minus partial z. That direction is not in the span, so a surface trying to follow the plane field is forced to move in a missing direction.
Annihilator Criterion

The Same Failure Appears In \(d\alpha\)

1-Form Test

If \(D=\ker\alpha\), then integrability forces \(d\alpha\) to vanish on vectors tangent to \(D\).

\(\alpha=dz-y\,dx\)
\(d\alpha=dx\wedge dy,\quad d\alpha(X,Y)=1\)
truecriterion detects the failure
Annihilating formsSlide 3 / 10
Translate the same obstruction into the annihilator language. The 1-form alpha equals dz minus y dx cuts out the proposed distribution, and d alpha equals dx wedge dy. Evaluating d alpha on the spanning fields gives one, so the annihilator does not close under exterior differentiation.

Bad Plane Field

Repair

Replace \(Y\) by \(\partial_y+x\partial_z\); now the bracket is zero and \(z-xy=c\) gives leaves.

Frobenius Checklist

How To Read A Distribution

1. Define \(D\)

Choose a smooth rank-\(k\) subbundle of \(TM\), often by spanning fields or annihilating forms.

2. Test Closure

Check \([X,Y]\in\Gamma(D)\) for tangent fields, or check the annihilator under exterior differentiation.

3. Flatten Locally

If the test passes, find coordinates where \(D\) is spanned by coordinate vector fields.

4. Read Plaques

Integral manifolds are local coordinate slices in the transverse variables.

5. Glue Leaves

Compatible plaques form maximal connected leaves that partition the manifold.

6. Apply

Lie subgroups and first-order PDE compatibility are Frobenius problems in disguise.
