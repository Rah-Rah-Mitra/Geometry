Obstruction

Closed nonexact formSlide 3 / 10
This is the first obstruction example. The angular form on the punctured plane is closed because d omega is zero, but its loop integral is 2*pi. If it were globally exact, Stokes would force the integral around a boundaryless loop to vanish, so the nonzero period detects the hole.

Mayer-Vietoris is the computation engine. Cover S^1 by two arcs with a two-component overlap: the local H1 groups vanish, but the mismatch of constants on the overlap has a one-dimensional cokernel. The rank check records rank alpha one, rank beta one, and H1 of the circle equal to one.

Lecture Recap

What De Rham Cohomology Measures

Complex

\(d^2=0\) turns differential forms into closed modulo exact quotients.

Periods

Nonzero integrals over cycles detect closed forms that are not exact.

Homotopy

The homotopy operator makes endpoint pullback differences exact.

Finite Models

Betti numbers count the quotient dimensions in small cochain complexes.

Covers

Mayer-Vietoris turns local data and overlap mismatch into global classes.

Degree

Top cohomology gives a smooth map a homotopy-invariant integer.

Final sanity checks cover angular-form closedness and period, homotopy operator identity, circle \(H^1\), Mayer-Vietoris ranks, top-degree cases, and agreement of degree definitions.
