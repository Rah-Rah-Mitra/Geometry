Complex Manifold: A Coherent Rotation Law

Definition 2.1 - complex structure

A complex structure is an atlas with holomorphic transition maps, equivalently an integrable tensor \(J:TM\to TM\) satisfying \(J^2=-I\).

The local tangent plane carries a distinguished quarter-turn. The global issue is whether all coordinate overlaps preserve it.

\(z=x+iy,\qquad J(\partial_x)=\partial_y,\quad J(\partial_y)=-\partial_x\)
Introduce the complex structure as a local rule for rotating tangent vectors by a quarter turn. The point is not just J squared equals minus the identity; it is the compatibility of this rule across overlapping charts. The small grid picture should make clear why holomorphic transition maps preserve the same rotation law.

Hermitian Metrics: Measuring Complex Directions

Definition 2.2 - Hermitian metric

A Riemannian metric is Hermitian when \(g(JX,JY)=g(X,Y)\). It defines the associated two-form

\(\omega(X,Y)=g(JX,Y)\).

Compatibility means that \(J\), \(g\), and \(\omega\) are three views of the same local geometry.

Move from complex structure to metric. A Hermitian metric is a Riemannian metric that does not notice when both input vectors are rotated by J. Stress the companion two-form omega. The form is not extra decoration; it packages the same metric data through omega of X and Y equals g of JX and Y.

The Kahler Condition: Closedness With Consequences

Definition 2.3 - Kahler metric

A Hermitian metric is Kahler when its associated form is closed:

\(d\omega=0\).
Equivalent consequences

\(\nabla J=0\), local Kahler potentials exist, and curvature respects complex bidegree.

This is the conceptual gate of the chapter. The Kahler condition is the closure of omega, and that single differential equation has many consequences: Levi-Civita preserves J, the metric is locally controlled by a potential, and curvature decomposes into complex types. Let students see it as a rigidity condition, not just a definition.

Local Potential: Geometry From One Function

Local formula

In holomorphic coordinates, a Kahler metric can be written locally as

\(g_{i\bar j}=\frac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial z^i\,\partial\bar z^j}\),

with \(\omega=i\,\partial\bar\partial \phi\).

The potential viewpoint is the entry point to elliptic equations in Kahler-Einstein theory.

Use the potential slide to make the local model computational. In a holomorphic coordinate chart, a real-valued potential phi produces the metric coefficients by mixed complex derivatives. This is the bridge from geometry to elliptic analysis: changing phi changes omega, Ricci, and ultimately the Einstein equation in a traceable way.

Ricci Form: Curvature As A (1,1)-Form

Curvature package

For a Kahler metric with Hermitian matrix \(g_{i\bar j}\), the Ricci form is locally

\(\rho=-i\,\partial\bar\partial \log \det(g_{i\bar j})\).
  • It is closed.
  • It has type \((1,1)\).
  • It records complex volume distortion.
Connect curvature to complex determinant data. The Ricci form is built from the log determinant of the Hermitian metric matrix, so it is sensitive to volume distortion in complex directions. The important teaching move is that Ricci is now a closed real (1,1)-form, not merely a symmetric tensor from Riemannian geometry.

First Chern Class: Topology Enters The Equation

Cohomological content

The Ricci form represents a fixed topological class:

\([\rho]=2\pi c_1(M)\).

Thus the sign and class of Ricci are not freely adjustable by local metric choices.

Explain why the first Chern class appears naturally rather than mysteriously. The Ricci form represents a cohomology class associated with the anticanonical line bundle, so curvature has become a topological witness. For Einstein problems, this means the sign and class of Ricci are constrained before solving any PDE.

The Kahler Dictionary

Complex

\(J^2=-I\), holomorphic charts, bidegree \((p,q)\).

Symplectic

\(\omega\) is closed and nondegenerate; cycles can be integrated.

Riemannian

\(g(X,Y)=\omega(X,JY)\), curvature and geodesic language.

Pause on the dictionary. On a Kahler manifold, real Riemannian data, complex holomorphic data, and symplectic form data describe the same object from different angles. The diagram should help students remember which operation lives in which language: J acts on tangent vectors, omega integrates over cycles, and curvature produces forms.

Hodge Theory In The Kahler Setting

Kahler identity package

On a compact Kahler manifold, the Laplacians built from \(d\), \(\partial\), and \(\bar\partial\) are tightly related.

\(\Delta_d = 2\Delta_{\partial}=2\Delta_{\bar\partial}\).

Harmonic representatives preserve complex bidegree and split de Rham cohomology into Hodge pieces.

Treat the Kahler identities as the mechanism behind the later Hodge statements. The key message is that the Laplacians associated with d, partial, and partial-bar are tightly related. This gives harmonic representatives that respect complex type, allowing cohomology to split into Hodge pieces under compactness assumptions.

Proof Roadmap: Why The Decomposition Persists

1. Closed form

\(d\omega=0\) forces commutation relations between Lefschetz operations and differential operators.

2. Laplacians align

The Kahler identities compare \(\Delta_d\), \(\Delta_\partial\), and \(\Delta_{\bar\partial}\).

3. Harmonic projection

Every de Rham class has a harmonic representative, and the representative splits by type.

4. Bidegree survives

The analytic minimizer remembers the complex grading, producing \(H^k=\oplus_{p+q=k}H^{p,q}\).

This slide is a proof roadmap rather than a full proof. Walk through the four moves: use closedness of omega, commute compatible operators, identify Laplacians up to constants, then take harmonic representatives. The payoff is that analytic minimizers remember complex bidegree, which is a major structural advantage.

Kahler-Einstein Equation

Equation
\(\rho=\lambda\,\omega\)

Equivalently, the Ricci tensor is a constant multiple of the metric.

Cohomology constraint

\(2\pi c_1(M)=\lambda[\omega]\). The class must match before a solution can exist.

Now connect back to Einstein metrics. In the Kahler setting, the Einstein equation asks the Ricci form to be proportional to the Kahler form. Because the Ricci form represents the first Chern class and omega represents a Kahler class, the equation is already a cohomological constraint before one discusses existence.

Calabi-Futaki Obstruction

Obstruction principle

Holomorphic vector fields can define a character that must vanish for the relevant Kahler-Einstein or constant scalar curvature problem.

\(\mathfrak{F}(X)=\int_M X(h)\,\omega^n\)

Here \(h\) is the normalized Ricci potential in the chosen Kahler class.

Use this as the obstruction slide. A holomorphic vector field can detect failure of a candidate Kahler class to support a constant scalar curvature or Kahler-Einstein metric. Do not overstate the theorem: present the Futaki character as a functional obstruction whose vanishing is necessary in the relevant positive-curvature existence problem.

Reference Example: Complex Projective Space

Fubini-Study metric

A standard local potential is

\(\phi=\log(1+|z|^2)\).

It yields a compact Kahler-Einstein model with positive first Chern class.

Use this example as the test case for the chapter's formulas.

The worked example is deliberately elementary: complex projective space with the Fubini-Study metric. It is the model where the potential, Kahler form, Ricci form, and Einstein constant align cleanly. The goal is to give students one stable reference example before they face less symmetric manifolds.

Warning: Similar Words, Different Conditions

Hermitian

Requires \(g(JX,JY)=g(X,Y)\). It does not by itself force \(d\omega=0\).

Symplectic

Requires a closed nondegenerate two-form. It may lack an integrable complex structure.

Kahler

Requires the compatible triple \((J,g,\omega)\) with \(d\omega=0\).

Use the warning to prevent a common collapse of concepts. Hermitian, symplectic, and Kahler are related but not interchangeable. A metric may respect J without producing a closed omega; a symplectic form may not come from an integrable complex structure. Kahler means the compatibilities are simultaneous.

Takeaways For The Einstein Story

  • Kahler geometry ties \(J\), \(g\), and \(\omega\) into one compatible package.
  • The Ricci form converts curvature into a representative of \(2\pi c_1(M)\).
  • Hodge theory becomes sharper because harmonic forms respect complex type.
  • Kahler-Einstein existence is constrained by both cohomology and holomorphic vector fields.
