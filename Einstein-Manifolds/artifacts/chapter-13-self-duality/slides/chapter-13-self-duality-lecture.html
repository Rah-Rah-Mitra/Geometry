Self-Duality

Four-dimensional curvature splits into two conformal halves. Twistor geometry turns the vanishing of one half into complex integrability.

Open the lecture by saying that self-duality is a four-dimensional accident that becomes a structural tool. The chapter is not just about a special curvature equation; it is about how orientation, conformal geometry, complex geometry, and Einstein metrics meet in dimension four.
Why dimension four is special

The middle degree has two equal halves

Linear algebra at one point
On an oriented Riemannian 4-space, the Hodge star sends Lambda^2 to itself and satisfies *^2 = 1.
Lambda^2 = Lambda^2_+ oplus Lambda^2_-,    dim Lambda^2_+ = dim Lambda^2_- = 3
  • The plus/minus labels require an orientation.
  • The splitting is pointwise, so it applies to every tangent space.
  • Curvature can now be read as a block operator on two-forms.
Hodge star2 / 17
Emphasize the dimension count before any theorem is stated. In four dimensions, two-forms have the same rank on both sides of the Hodge star, so the star operator has eigenspaces rather than merely moving forms to a different degree. This is the algebraic doorway into self-duality.
A basis-level view

Self-dual forms are paired coordinate planes

Inspection rule
A two-form is self-dual when every coordinate-plane component is matched with its Hodge partner using the orientation sign.
The split is metric-dependent but conformally stable on two-forms in four dimensions: changing g to e^{2f}g preserves the same plus/minus subbundles.
Lecture check
Reverse orientation and every box in the left column trades labels with its partner on the right.
Pointwise splitting3 / 17
Walk through the picture as a pointwise linear algebra statement on an oriented Euclidean four-space. The decomposition is not a choice of coordinates after the metric and orientation are fixed. The plus and minus halves are the two possible signs of the star action.
Curvature as an operator

The curvature tensor becomes a block matrix on Lambda^2_+ oplus Lambda^2_-

Curvature operator
The Riemann tensor defines a symmetric map R: Lambda^2 -> Lambda^2.
  • Diagonal blocks contain the Weyl halves plus scalar curvature.
  • Off-diagonal blocks encode traceless Ricci curvature.
  • The Einstein condition deletes the off-diagonal block.
Curvature decomposition4 / 17
Present the Riemann curvature tensor as a symmetric operator on two-forms. This block matrix is the organizing object for the lecture: scalar curvature, traceless Ricci curvature, and the two Weyl halves all have visible locations. Students should remember where each piece sits, not just its name.
Half-conformal flatness

Self-duality means one Weyl half vanishes

Definition
An oriented conformal four-manifold is self-dual when one block, conventionally W_-, vanishes. It is anti-self-dual when W_+ vanishes.
W = W_+ + W_-,    W_- = 0
Conformally flat metrics have both halves zero. Self-dual metrics allow the surviving half to carry nontrivial conformal curvature.
Weyl tensor5 / 17
Define half-conformal flatness through the vanishing of one Weyl block. The term self-dual is convention-dependent because reversing orientation swaps the labels plus and minus. The geometric content is the disappearance of one conformal curvature half.
Orientation is part of the data

Changing orientation swaps the names of the halves

Common mistake
Self-dual is not an absolute label attached to a bare metric. It is a statement about an oriented conformal structure.
  • If W_- = 0 for one orientation, then W_+ = 0 for the reverse orientation.
  • The underlying Riemannian metric has not changed.
  • Orientation enters twistor space through the choice of compatible complex structures.
Orientation dependence6 / 17
Use this slide to prevent a common error. A metric alone does not decide which Weyl half is called self-dual; the chosen orientation does. The underlying curvature may be the same while the plus and minus labels exchange.
Conformal geometry

The equation is stable under rescaling

g' = e^{2f} g    implies    W' = W
Conformal point
The vanishing statements W_+ = 0 or W_- = 0 belong to the conformal class, not to one preferred scale.
Einstein metrics are not conformally invariant in the same way. The chapter studies the productive overlap between a conformal curvature condition and the Einstein equation.
Conformal class7 / 17
Stress that the Weyl tensor is the conformal curvature, so self-duality is naturally a conformal condition. Rescaling the metric changes lengths and scalar quantities, but the vanishing of a Weyl half is preserved. This is why twistor methods can enter a chapter in an Einstein manifolds course.
Concept map

Three conditions that should not be merged

Conformally flat
W_+ = 0 and W_- = 0

The entire conformal curvature disappears. This is stronger than half-conformal flatness.

Half-conformally flat
W_+ = 0 or W_- = 0

One Weyl block vanishes. The other block may contain substantial geometry.

Einstein
Ric = lambda g

The traceless Ricci block vanishes. Weyl curvature can still be rich.

Examples and nonexamples8 / 17
This slide is a map rather than a classification theorem. The point is to separate three ideas that are easy to blur: conformal flatness, half-conformal flatness, and Einstein curvature. Some important examples sit at intersections, while other metrics satisfy only one part of the story.
Where Einstein enters

Einstein metrics remove the coupling between the two halves

Einstein equation
Ric = lambda g is equivalent to Ric0 = 0, so the curvature operator preserves Lambda^2_+ and Lambda^2_-.
  • Self-dual Einstein means: one Weyl half vanishes and the Ricci block is scalar.
  • The surviving Weyl half is where the local conformal geometry remains.
  • Scalar curvature then influences the twistor geometry built later.
Einstein block diagonalization9 / 17
The Einstein condition removes the off-diagonal traceless Ricci block in the curvature operator. That does not force either Weyl half to vanish, but it makes the block picture cleaner and turns self-dual Einstein metrics into a sharply constrained class. This is one reason the chapter belongs in the course.
Global accounting

Curvature halves leave topological traces

Chern-Weil memory
On a compact oriented four-manifold, curvature integrals recover the Euler characteristic and signature.
tau(M) = (1 / 12 pi^2) integral_M ( |W+|^2 - |W-|^2 ) dmu
chi(M) uses |W+|^2 + |W-|^2, Ric0, and s^2
Topology from curvature10 / 17
Introduce the formulas as global accounting identities rather than as local definitions. Curvature integrals remember Euler characteristic and signature, and the two Weyl halves contribute asymmetrically to the signature. The identities explain why self-duality has topological consequences.
Penrose construction, geometric input

Twistor space records compatible complex structures

Fiber over a point
For each x in M, take all orthogonal complex structures J on T_xM compatible with the orientation.
Z -> M,    fiber approximately S^2 approximately CP^1
The natural almost complex structure on Z is integrable exactly under the self-duality condition, up to the chosen orientation convention.
Twistor fibration11 / 17
Describe twistor space as the bundle of compatible complex structures on tangent spaces. Over each point of the oriented four-manifold sits a two-sphere of possible complex structures. The self-duality equation is precisely what makes the natural almost complex structure on this bundle integrable.
Penrose construction

Curvature vanishing becomes holomorphic incidence

Translation
Self-duality of the conformal structure is equivalent to integrability of the natural twistor almost complex structure.
  • Points of M correspond to rational curves in Z.
  • The real structure on Z remembers the original real manifold.
  • Conformal geometry becomes complex incidence geometry.
From self-dual geometry to complex geometry12 / 17
The Penrose construction turns a self-dual conformal four-manifold into complex geometry. Fibers become rational curves with controlled normal bundle, and real structure remembers the original real manifold. The important lecture move is that curvature information has become holomorphic incidence data.
Reverse construction

Suitable complex data reconstructs the conformal four-manifold

Input data
A complex threefold with a family of rational curves, the correct normal bundle, and a real structure can define a real four-dimensional moduli space.
curves in Z   ->   points of M
The conformal class on the recovered moduli space is read from which nearby rational curves intersect.
From complex geometry back to conformal geometry13 / 17
Now reverse the arrows carefully. Starting from suitable complex data, one recovers a real four-manifold as a moduli space of rational curves. The hypotheses are not decoration; they are what ensure that the recovered space has the right dimension, real form, and self-dual conformal structure.
Self-dual Einstein geometry

Einstein metrics add extra twistor structure

Application pattern
Start with an oriented self-dual conformal four-manifold. If a representative is Einstein, the twistor space carries additional geometric structure governed by scalar curvature.
  • Positive scalar curvature aligns with contact-type twistor geometry.
  • Zero scalar curvature is close to hyperkahler behavior.
  • Nonzero scalar curvature points toward quaternion-Kahler themes.
Einstein and twistor applications14 / 17
Explain that the Einstein condition adds extra structure to the twistor space, often visible through contact or symplectic data depending on the scalar curvature regime. This is where the chapter links forward to quaternion-Kahler geometry. The deck should leave students expecting the next chapter rather than treating twistor theory as isolated.
Warning slide

Three mistakes that distort the chapter

1
Not Ricci-flat
Self-dual controls a Weyl half. Ricci curvature lives in a different part of the curvature decomposition.
2
Not orientation-free
Reverse the orientation and the labels W_+ and W_- switch.
3
Not any complex threefold
Reverse Penrose reconstruction needs rational curves, normal-bundle control, and real structure.
A reliable reading strategy is to ask which block of the curvature matrix, which orientation, and which incidence condition is being used.
Conceptual guardrails15 / 17
Use this warning slide to catch three mistakes. First, self-dual is not the same as Ricci-flat. Second, changing orientation changes which half vanishes. Third, a formal-looking twistor space does not reconstruct the geometry unless the incidence and real-structure hypotheses are satisfied.

Lecture recap

Self-duality is the meeting point of three structures

Algebra

Lambda^2 = Lambda^2_+ oplus Lambda^2_- because four dimensions put two-forms in the middle degree.

Conformal curvature

W_+ = 0 or W_- = 0 is the half-flat condition; it survives conformal rescaling.

Twistors

The twistor fibration converts self-duality into complex integrability and incidence geometry.

Einstein + self-dual   ->   a rigid bridge toward quaternion-Kahler geometry
