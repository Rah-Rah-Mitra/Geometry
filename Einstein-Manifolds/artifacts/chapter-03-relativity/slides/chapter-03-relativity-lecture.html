Lorentzian Metrics Organize Causality

Local Model
g_p has signature (-,+,+,+).

Vectors split by the sign of g(v,v): timelike, null, or spacelike.

Inspection Target

A light cone is not a force field. It is the metric's zero set in each tangent space.

Metric signature replaces distance-only intuition3 / 16
Emphasize that the metric does two jobs at once: it measures proper time along timelike curves, and it identifies lightlike directions as the boundary of causal influence. This is the local vocabulary needed before the field equation can mean anything.

The Einstein Field Equation Packages Curvature

Field Equation
G_{ij} + Lambda g_{ij} = 8 pi T_{ij}

with G_{ij}=Ric_{ij} - (1/2) R g_{ij}.

Geometry

Einstein tensor is built from Ricci and scalar curvature.

Matter

Stress-energy records density, flux, and pressure.

Balance

Divergence-free structure matches conservation law.

The equation is a tensor identity, not a scalar slogan4 / 16
The field equation is best read term by term. The Einstein tensor is the divergence-free curvature package; the stress-energy tensor records matter and momentum; the constant sets the unit conversion; and the cosmological term lets the metric itself contribute a background energy scale.

Trace Reversal Connects Ricci to Matter

Original Form
Ric - (1/2) Rg = 8 pi T

Conservation is visible because the Einstein tensor has zero divergence.

Four-Dimensional Rewriting
Ric = 8 pi (T - (1/2)(tr T)g)

The Ricci tensor responds to a trace-adjusted matter tensor.

Vacuum with Lambda = 0 gives Ric = 05 / 16
Pause on the trace reversal. In four dimensions, the equation can be rewritten so Ricci curvature is directly driven by a modified stress tensor. This is the bridge from the abstract Einstein condition to the relativity convention used in the chapter.

Tidal Stresses Are Curvature Seen by Free Fall

Geodesic Deviation
nabla_u nabla_u xi = - R(xi,u)u

The measurable residue after choosing a freely falling frame is a curvature term.

Common Mistake

Vanishing Christoffel symbols at one event do not make curvature vanish near that event.

Local inertial coordinates remove first-order effects, not tidal data6 / 16
Tidal acceleration is the first honest curvature experiment. A freely falling observer can remove first derivatives of the metric along one worldline, but cannot remove the relative acceleration of nearby geodesics. That residual is the Riemann tensor being measured.

Curvature Normal Forms Separate Trace and Shear

Decomposition
Riemann = Weyl + Ricci traces

Normal forms are a disciplined way to choose an observer frame and read tensor blocks.

  • Ricci part is constrained by matter through the field equation.
  • Weyl part carries trace-free tidal information, including vacuum curvature.
  • Symmetry assumptions force many tensor entries to vanish.
Curvature can be nonzero in vacuum because Weyl need not vanish7 / 16
Explain the normal-form idea without overcomputing it. Symmetry and observer choice decompose curvature into pieces that can be read as tidal stretching, rotation, and constraint data. Ricci is the trace information; Weyl is the trace-free part that can propagate in vacuum.

Schwarzschild: A Rigid Exterior Vacuum Model

Metric Form
ds^2 = -(1-2M/r)dt^2 + (1-2M/r)^{-1}dr^2 + r^2 dOmega^2
Hypotheses

Vacuum, static, spherical symmetry.

Horizon Radius

r = 2M marks a chart transition.

Center

r = 0 is a curvature singularity.

Asymptotic End

Large r approaches flat spacetime.

The form is a theorem-level consequence of symmetry plus vacuum8 / 16
This slide positions Schwarzschild as the canonical exterior model. The assumptions are vacuum, static symmetry, and spherical symmetry. The lesson is that a small set of geometric hypotheses forces a very rigid metric with two special radii in the coordinates.

Coordinate Failure Is Not Curvature Blow-Up

Chart Diagnostic

The coefficient (1-2M/r)^{-1} blows up at r=2M.

This flags a poor coordinate chart at the horizon.

Invariant Diagnostic
R_{abcd}R^{abcd} = 48M^2/r^6

The invariant is finite at r=2M and blows up at r=0.

Curvature scalars help classify genuine singular behavior9 / 16
Walk through the three diagnostics. The horizon at r equals 2M is not detected by the Kretschmann scalar, while r equals zero is. This is the standard warning that a coordinate singularity and a curvature singularity are different kinds of failure.

Orbit Geometry Comes from an Effective Potential

Reduced Radial Equation
(dr/dtau)^2 + V_eff(r) = E^2

Circular orbits appear as critical points of the effective potential.

  • Energy and angular momentum reduce the geodesic equation.
  • Minima correspond to stable radial perturbations.
  • Maxima mark orbits that small perturbations can escape.
Qualitative dynamics is encoded in geodesic first integrals10 / 16
Use the effective potential picture to connect geodesic equations with orbital behavior. Circular orbits are critical points of the radial potential, and their stability changes as one moves inward. This is where geometry becomes qualitative dynamics.

Null Geodesics Bend Without Experiencing Proper Time

Weak-Field Scale
Delta phi approx 4M / b

Here b is the impact parameter measured at infinity.

Interpretation

The photon follows a null geodesic; the bending is a statement about spacetime geometry, not a Newtonian force applied to light.

Null means ds^2 = 0, not zero spatial motion11 / 16
Light bending can be taught as null geodesic bookkeeping. The impact parameter fixes the incoming line at infinity, while curvature changes the asymptotic outgoing direction. The formula shown is only the weak-field leading term, useful as an inspection scale.

Kruskal Coordinates Extend Through the Horizon

Extension Principle

Change coordinates so the metric coefficients are regular at the horizon.

UV = (1 - r/2M) exp(r/2M)

What Changes?

The chart sees both exterior regions and the black-hole and white-hole interiors.

Extension repairs r = 2M, not r = 012 / 16
Kruskal coordinates should be presented as a repair of the chart, not a removal of curvature. The horizon becomes a regular pair of null lines, and the extended diagram separates exterior regions, black-hole interior, and white-hole interior.

Conformal Diagrams Preserve the Causal Story

Reading Rule

Light rays run at 45 degrees, so causal influence is visible before metric distances are known.

  • Infinity is brought to a finite boundary.
  • Null lines remain null under conformal scaling.
  • Event horizons are global causal boundaries.
Causal compactification is qualitative, not an isometric picture13 / 16
Here the Penrose-style picture compresses infinite directions so causal structure can be seen at once. The diagonal light rays are the main invariant. The diagram is not a metric drawing; it preserves causal angles, not distances.

Singularity Theorems Are Global Focusing Arguments

Proof Skeleton
  1. Energy condition gives a Ricci sign along causal geodesics.
  2. Raychaudhuri equation forces expansion to decrease.
  3. A trapped surface makes focusing unavoidable.
  4. Global causality turns focusing into incompleteness.
The conclusion is geodesic incompleteness14 / 16
Singularity theorems are not formulas for where the singularity sits. They are global logic machines: energy condition, trapped surface, causal hypothesis, and geodesic incompleteness. The conclusion is about incomplete causal geodesics, not necessarily a visible point.

Same Ricci Tensor, Different Geometric Worlds

Riemannian Einstein Metric
Ric = lambda g
  • Positive definite metric.
  • Geodesics locally minimize distance.
  • Often studied through elliptic behavior and compactness.
Vacuum Spacetime
Ric = 0
  • Lorentzian metric with null directions.
  • Geodesics encode causal motion.
  • Evolution and global causality become central.

The course's unifying habit: ask what part of the geometry is controlled by Ricci curvature, and what remains in the trace-free curvature.

This chapter widens the Einstein condition into a spacetime equation15 / 16
This comparison helps students avoid a common misconception. Einstein metrics in Riemannian geometry and vacuum spacetime equations share Ricci language, but the signature changes the analytic behavior, the notion of length, and the interpretation of geodesics.

Chapter 3 Takeaways

Instructor Check

Ask students to explain the difference between a horizon, a coordinate singularity, and a curvature singularity using one invariant or diagram.

  • The Einstein tensor is the curvature side of a conservation-compatible equation.
  • Tidal effects expose curvature in local free-fall frames.
  • Schwarzschild separates chart extension from true curvature blow-up.
  • Singularity theorems convert focusing into geodesic incompleteness.
