Roadmap

From Quaternionic Frames To Twistor Geometry

Lecture Thesis
Quaternion-Kahler geometry preserves a quaternionic three-plane in the endomorphism bundle; the Levi-Civita connection rotates that three-plane and forces strong curvature identities.
  • Hyperkahler: three parallel complex structures, Ricci flat.
  • Quaternion-Kahler: preserved span, usually nonzero scalar curvature.
  • Twistor space: all compatible complex structures organized in one bundle.
Quaternionic structure as organizing data2 / 16
Use the map as the memory for the source span. Hyperkahler geometry is the flat reference case, while quaternion-Kahler geometry keeps an Sp(1) rotation in the holonomy. Twistor space translates that Riemannian data into complex geometry upstairs.
Local Algebra

One Tangent Space Carries A Quaternionic Sphere

Admissible Triple
On a real vector space V, a local quaternionic frame is a triple (I,J,K) with
I^2 = J^2 = K^2 = -1
IJ = K,   JK = I,   KI = J
JI = -K
aI + bJ + cK,   a^2+b^2+c^2=1
The structure is the oriented three-plane Q = span(I,J,K), not a globally preferred single complex structure.
The unit sphere in Q parameterizes compatible complex structures3 / 16
Pause on the algebra because later geometry transports this picture over the manifold. At one tangent space, I, J, and K behave like imaginary quaternion units. The structure preserves their three-plane, not one chosen complex structure.
Reference Case

Hyperkahler Means The Triple Is Parallel

Baseline Model
A hyperkahler metric has complex structures I,J,K that are parallel for the Levi-Civita connection and Kahler for the same metric.
nabla I = nabla J = nabla K = 0
  • Three closed two-forms: omega_I, omega_J, omega_K.
  • Holonomy reduces to Sp(m).
  • The metric is Einstein with Ric = 0.
Hyperkahler is the zero scalar curvature edge case4 / 16
Present hyperkahler manifolds as the rigid baseline: I, J, and K are each parallel and Kahler for the same metric. The three closed forms force Ricci flatness. This prepares the contrast with quaternion-Kahler rotation of the span.
Definition

Quaternion-Kahler Preserves The Span, Not The Frame

Working Definition
For real dimension 4m, m > 1, a Riemannian manifold is quaternion-Kahler when its holonomy lies in Sp(m)Sp(1).
Equivalent Local View
There is a rank-three subbundle Q subset End(TM) locally spanned by quaternionic triples, and nabla Q subset Q.
The connection may rotate I,J,K among themselves. This is exactly where the Sp(1) factor enters.
Dimension four is exceptional and treated separately later5 / 16
Emphasize the dimension condition and holonomy reduction. For dimension 4m with m greater than 1, the Levi-Civita connection preserves a rank-three subbundle locally spanned by quaternionic triples. This is weaker than a global hyperkahler triple but still forces Einstein behavior.
Holonomy

The Two Factors Have Different Jobs

Holonomy Inclusion
Hol(g) subset Sp(m)Sp(1) inside SO(4m).
  • Sp(m) respects quaternionic linear geometry in each tangent space.
  • Sp(1) rotates the admissible complex structures as a sphere.
  • Removing the Sp(1) curvature returns the hyperkahler intuition.
Common Mistake
Quaternion-Kahler does not usually mean there is a global complex structure making the manifold Kahler.
Holonomy controls which tensors remain parallel6 / 16
Use the diagram to separate the two holonomy factors. Sp(m) acts on quaternionic directions, while Sp(1) rotates the sphere of admissible complex structures. In the hyperkahler case the Sp(1) curvature disappears, which is why the scalar curvature is zero.
Curvature Payoff

The Quaternionic Constraint Forces Einstein Geometry

Structural Theorem
In dimension 4m with m > 1, a quaternion-Kahler metric is Einstein:
Ric = lambda g
The scalar curvature measures the Sp(1) curvature. Zero scalar curvature is the hyperkahler boundary case.
Einstein condition appears as a consequence of the reduced curvature algebra7 / 16
State the Einstein property as a consequence of the quaternionic curvature identities, not as an extra equation imposed by hand. The Ricci tensor has too little freedom to vary arbitrarily. Scalar curvature then distinguishes negative, zero, and positive cases.
Twistor Construction

Over Each Point Lives A Sphere Of Complex Structures

Twistor Space
Z = {I in Q_x : I^2 = -1} with projection pi: Z -> M.
  • Fiber is a Riemann sphere.
  • A point upstairs is a base point plus a compatible complex structure.
  • The Levi-Civita connection splits tangent directions into vertical and horizontal parts.
Twistor space records every admissible local complex structure at once8 / 16
Introduce twistor space as the bundle of compatible complex structures over the base. A point upstairs is a base point together with one admissible I from the quaternionic sphere. This records all local choices instead of committing to one.
Complex Geometry Upstairs

Horizontal Plus Vertical Directions Build The Twistor Complex Structure

Construction Rule
At a point (x,I) in Z, use the standard complex structure on the vertical sphere and use I itself on the horizontal lift of T_xM.
T_{(x,I)}Z = V \oplus H
J_Z|_V = J_{S^2}
J_Z|_H = I
N_{J_Z}=0
The integrability statement is where the quaternion-Kahler curvature identities do real work.
Twistor integrability turns quaternionic curvature into complex geometry9 / 16
Explain the horizontal and vertical directions separately. The fiber already has the complex structure of a Riemann sphere, while horizontal directions use the chosen I. Integrability is the compatibility statement where quaternion-Kahler curvature enters the construction.
Positive Case

Positive Scalar Curvature Makes Twistor Geometry Rigid

Positive Quaternion-Kahler
Compact positive examples sit at the meeting point of Einstein geometry, special holonomy, and complex contact geometry.
  • The twistor space is complex and carries a distinguished contact-type distribution.
  • Positivity supplies strong curvature and topology constraints.
  • Symmetric examples become the first atlas of models.
Positive scalar curvature is the compact, rigid side of the story10 / 16
For positive scalar curvature, keep the message focused on rigidity. The twistor space becomes a rich complex manifold with a contact-type distribution. This is the bridge from Riemannian holonomy to complex-geometric tools, not a separate classification lecture.

Exception

Dimension Four Does Not Follow The Same Template

Warning
The holonomy condition Sp(1)Sp(1)=SO(4) is too broad to define the intended geometry in dimension four.
Replacement Language
Four-dimensional quaternion-Kahler geometry is tied to oriented Einstein self-dual geometry, with convention-dependent orientation choices.
  • Use Weyl curvature splitting from the previous chapter.
  • Keep the Einstein condition visible.
  • Do not treat m=1 as a routine special case.
Chapter 13 language remains active in dimension four12 / 16
Dimension four is exceptional because the holonomy condition alone becomes too broad. The right language returns to oriented Einstein self-dual geometry, with conventions depending on orientation. This warning prevents students from applying the m greater than 1 template blindly.
Curvature Anatomy

Curvature Splits Into Scalar And Quaternionic Weyl Information

Schematic Formula
R = R_{scalar} + W_Q
  • The scalar term is controlled by the Einstein constant.
  • The quaternionic Weyl term contains the remaining compatible curvature.
  • Vanishing or symmetry assumptions can collapse the second term in model cases.
This decomposition explains why scalar sign is a recurring organizing invariant in the chapter.

2. Scalar Curvature
The Einstein constant records the visible Sp(1) curvature and separates hyperkahler from positive and negative quaternion-Kahler geometry.

The sphere bundle of compatible complex structures translates quaternionic Riemannian data into complex geometry upstairs.
