Printed pp. 145-186Connected Spaces
A space is connected when it cannot be split into two disjoint nonempty open sets. The union of connected sets sharing a point is connected, and the continuous image of a connected space is connected. Path connectedness is a stronger companion notion that implies connectedness but, as the topologist's sine curve shows, is not equivalent.

The connected subsets of the real line are exactly the intervals. From this the intermediate value theorem follows immediately: a continuous real-valued function on a connected domain takes every value between any two it attains. This recovers a familiar calculus theorem as a purely topological statement about connected images.

Compact Spaces

Definition

Every open cover has a finite subcover.

Stability

Closed subsets and finite products stay compact.

Cover

$X=\bigcup_{\alpha} U_\alpha \Rightarrow X=\bigcup_{i=1}^{n} U_{\alpha_i}$

In Hausdorff

Compact subsets are closed.

Printed pp. 145-186Compact Spaces
A space is compact when every open cover has a finite subcover. The continuous image of a compact space is compact, finite products of compact spaces are compact, and closed subsets of compact spaces are compact. Compactness in a Hausdorff space forces closedness, tightly coupling the two properties throughout analysis.

Printed pp. 145-186Compactness on the Line
The Heine-Borel theorem identifies the compact subsets of Euclidean space as precisely the closed and bounded ones. As a consequence a continuous real-valued function on a compact space attains its maximum and minimum, the extreme value theorem, and is uniformly continuous on a compact metric domain.

Printed pp. 145-186Limit Point and Local Compactness
Limit point compactness asks that every infinite subset have a limit point; in metric spaces it coincides with compactness and sequential compactness. Local compactness, where each point has a compact neighborhood, leads to the one-point compactification. These refinements prepare the separation and metrization theory of the next chapters.
