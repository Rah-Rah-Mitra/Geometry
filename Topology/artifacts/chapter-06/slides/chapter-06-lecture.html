Local Finiteness

Definition

Each point meets only finitely many sets of the family.

Refinement

Countably locally finite bases generalize countable ones.

Local finiteness

$\forall x\ \exists U\ni x:\ \#\{A: A\cap U\neq\varnothing\}<\infty$

Control

Manages covers without global countability.

Printed pp. 241-260Local Finiteness
A collection of subsets is locally finite when every point has a neighborhood meeting only finitely many of them. A basis that is countably locally finite, a union of countably many locally finite families, is the right notion for general metrization. Local finiteness controls how covers behave without requiring countability of the whole space.

The Nagata-Smirnov Theorem

Characterization

Metrizable iff regular with a countably locally finite basis.

Generality

No second-countability needed.

Criterion

metrizable $\iff$ regular $+$ $\sigma$-locally finite basis

Scope

Applies to nonseparable spaces.

Printed pp. 241-260The Nagata-Smirnov Theorem
The Nagata-Smirnov metrization theorem gives a complete characterization: a space is metrizable if and only if it is regular and has a basis that is countably locally finite. This removes the second-countability restriction of Urysohn and applies to spaces far larger than the separable ones, settling the metrization question in full generality.

Paracompactness

Definition

Every open cover has a locally finite open refinement.

Metric spaces

Every metrizable space is paracompact.

Normality

Paracompact Hausdorff spaces are normal.

Why it matters

The hypothesis for partitions of unity.

Printed pp. 241-260Paracompactness
A space is paracompact when every open cover has a locally finite open refinement. Every metrizable space is paracompact, and every paracompact Hausdorff space is normal. Paracompactness is the precise hypothesis under which partitions of unity exist, making it the foundational covering property for differential geometry.

Partitions of Unity

Definition

Continuous functions summing to one, subordinate to a cover.

Existence

Exactly on paracompact spaces.

Partition

$\sum_i \varphi_i = 1,\quad \operatorname{supp}\varphi_i \subseteq U_i$

Use

Glue local metrics or forms into global ones.

Printed pp. 241-260Partitions of Unity
A partition of unity subordinate to an open cover is a family of continuous functions summing to one, each supported within a cover element. They exist exactly on paracompact spaces and let one stitch together locally defined objects, such as metrics or differential forms, into global ones. This is their indispensable role on manifolds.
