Two compatible connectionsChapter 03

Dual Connections

Duality relation

$Z\langle X,Y\rangle=\langle\nabla_Z X,Y\rangle+\langle X,\nabla^*_Z Y\rangle$

Transport by $\nabla$ paired with transport by its dual $\nabla^*$ preserves inner products.

Levi-Civita as average

$\nabla^{(0)}=\tfrac12(\nabla+\nabla^*)$

Why relax

Estimation prefers flat affine structure over metric geodesics.

Printed pp. 51-80 · PDF 60-89Dual Connections
Define a pair of connections that are dual with respect to the metric: parallel transport by one preserves inner products when paired with transport by the other. The Levi-Civita connection is the average of the two. This relaxation of metric compatibility is exactly what statistical geometry requires, since estimation prefers flat affine structure to metric geodesics.
Two flat charts at onceChapter 03

Dually Flat Manifolds

Definition

$\nabla$ is flat in e-coordinates and $\nabla^*$ is flat in m-coordinates at the same time.

Realization

Exponential families are dually flat with the natural and expectation parameters.

Affine coordinates

$\theta\ (\text{e-flat}),\qquad \eta\ (\text{m-flat})$

Payoff

Two complementary affine pictures power the projection theorems.

Printed pp. 51-80 · PDF 60-89Dually Flat Manifolds
Introduce dually flat manifolds, where one connection is flat in the e-coordinates and its dual is flat in the m-coordinates simultaneously. Exponential families realize this structure exactly. Dual flatness gives two complementary affine pictures of the same model, and it is the geometric backbone for the divergence and projection theorems that follow.
Measuring separationChapter 03

Canonical Divergence

Bregman form

$D(p\,\|\,q)=\psi(\theta_p)+\varphi(\eta_q)-\theta_p^i\eta_{q,i}$

Built from the dual potentials; asymmetric and generally non-metric.

Exponential family

$D(p\,\|\,q)=\mathrm{KL}(p\,\|\,q)$

Use

Underlies maximum-likelihood fitting and information projection.

Printed pp. 51-80 · PDF 60-89Canonical Divergence
Define the canonical divergence from the pair of potentials through the Bregman construction. For exponential families this divergence is precisely the Kullback-Leibler divergence. It is generally asymmetric and non-metric, yet it measures separation between distributions and underlies maximum-likelihood estimation, model fitting, and the projection results of this chapter.
Orthogonal decompositionChapter 03

Generalized Pythagorean Theorem

Additivity

$D(p\,\|\,q)=D(p\,\|\,r)+D(r\,\|\,q)$

Holds when the e-geodesic $p\!-\!r$ meets the m-geodesic $r\!-\!q$ orthogonally.

Consequence

Information projection onto a model is well-defined and unique.

Inference

Gives clean geometric optimality proofs for estimators.

Printed pp. 51-80 · PDF 60-89Generalized Pythagorean Theorem
State the generalized Pythagorean theorem: when an e-geodesic and an m-geodesic meet orthogonally, the canonical divergences add just as squared lengths do in Euclidean space. This decomposition makes information projection well-defined and gives clean, geometric proofs of optimality for estimators and for fitting a model to data.
