Frequency-domain metricChapter 05

Spectral Fisher Metric

Metric integral

$g_{ij}=\dfrac{1}{4\pi}\!\int \partial_i\log S\,\partial_j\log S\,d\omega$

Compares log-spectra: the natural inner product for stationary signals.

Computable

The metric follows directly from power spectra.

Link

Connects information geometry to signal processing.

Printed pp. 115-132 · PDF 124-141Spectral Fisher Metric
Express the Fisher information metric for a stationary process through its spectral density. The metric becomes an integral over frequency comparing log-spectra, the natural inner product for stationary signals. This spectral form makes the geometry computable from power spectra and connects information geometry to classical signal processing and filter design.

The estimated system is the closest model in the information sense.

Model order

Recast system identification as projecting the observed spectrum onto the model manifold using the spectral divergence. The estimated system is the closest model in the information sense, and the Pythagorean theorem again decomposes the approximation error. Model order selection appears as choosing the dimension of the manifold onto which we project.
