Convex Analysis and Bregman

Bregman divergence

$B_\psi(x,y)=\psi(x)-\psi(y)-\langle\nabla\psi(y),x-y\rangle$

A convex potential and its dual generate the divergence; KL is one case.

Abstraction

The structure no longer needs probability at all.

Reach

Applies to optimization and online learning.

Printed pp. 167-180 · PDF 176-189Convex Analysis and Bregman
Revisit the dually flat structure purely through convex analysis. A convex potential and its Legendre dual generate Bregman divergences, of which Kullback-Leibler is one instance. This perspective frees the framework from probability, applying it to optimization, online learning, and any setting governed by a convex potential function.

The EM Algorithm

Two steps as projections

The E-step is an m-projection; the M-step is an e-projection.

Each projection cannot increase the divergence.

Monotonicity

$D_{t+1}\le D_t$

Consequence

Explains why likelihood climbs monotonically.

Printed pp. 167-180 · PDF 176-189The EM Algorithm
Reinterpret expectation-maximization geometrically as alternating projections between a data manifold and a model manifold. The E-step is an m-projection and the M-step is an e-projection, and each step cannot increase the divergence. This em-algorithm picture explains convergence and clarifies why the procedure climbs the likelihood monotonically.
A unifying familyChapter 08

Alpha-Connections

Interpolation

$\nabla^{(\alpha)}=\tfrac{1+\alpha}{2}\nabla^{(e)}+\tfrac{1-\alpha}{2}\nabla^{(m)}$

$\alpha=0$ recovers Levi-Civita; $\pm1$ the e- and m-connections.

Alpha-divergences

Include Kullback-Leibler and Hellinger as special cases.

Unification

One tunable spectrum behind every divergence seen.

Printed pp. 167-180 · PDF 176-189Alpha-Connections
Introduce the one-parameter family of alpha-connections interpolating between the exponential and mixture connections, with alpha equal to zero giving the Levi-Civita connection. The associated alpha-divergences include Kullback-Leibler and Hellinger as special cases. This family unifies the divergences and connections seen throughout the book into a single tunable spectrum.
