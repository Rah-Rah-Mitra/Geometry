Why Arrangements: Cell Partition Visual

Definition

An arrangement of lines is the planar subdivision induced by all input lines: vertices at intersections, edges along line pieces, and faces between them.

Why It Helps

Inside one cell, the sign pattern against every input line is fixed. A geometric query can often be reduced to locating the right cell or walking adjacent cells.

Proof Structure
charging boundary edges

Zone Theorem Proof Roadmap

Theorem Shape

For n lines, the zone complexity of one line h is linear; the chapter's explicit statement uses \(Z_n < 6n\).

Roadmap

  1. Split zone boundary into above-h and below-h chains.
  2. Charge each boundary segment to a nearby crossing or line endpoint at infinity.
  3. Show each input line receives only constant charge.
Original inline SVG: proof ledgerThe constant is less important than linearity.
This slide is a proof map, not a full proof. The important idea is to charge boundary edges of crossed cells to nearby crossings and to separate edges above and below the inserted line. Because a line can enter the zone only through controlled local events, the total charge grows linearly rather than quadratically.
Algorithmic Use
walk zone, split cells

Incremental Construction

State Machine
  1. Start with an empty subdivision.
  2. Insert one line h.
  3. Locate and walk the zone of h.
  4. Split crossed cells and repair adjacency.

Invariant

After k insertions, the subdivision is the arrangement of the first k lines, with cell adjacencies consistent along every edge.

Figure: incremental-zone-costs.pngEach insertion pays for a zone walk.
Translate the theorem into an algorithm. Maintain the current planar subdivision, insert the next line, walk its zone, split the crossed cells, and update adjacency along the walk. The theorem is doing the performance work: it says each insertion touches only linear complexity in the number of lines already present.

Point-Line Duality
incidence becomes level structure

Duality Setup

Dual Map

\((a,b)\quad \longleftrightarrow \quad y=ax-b\)

What Is Preserved

Incidence is preserved, and above-below tests are converted into above-below tests with a sign convention.

Why It Matters

Point-set questions become arrangement questions: search a level, find an intersection, or locate a cell.

Duality starts at printed p201 / PDF p210.Figure: duality-median-levels.png
Introduce duality as an incidence-preserving translation, not as algebra for its own sake. A point becomes a line, and the question of whether a primal point lies above a primal line turns into an above-below relation in the dual arrangement. This is the doorway from point problems to level searches.

Assumptions
where the ledger can break

Failure Modes And Assumptions

Combinatorial Degeneracy

Parallel lines, coincident lines, and triple intersections change vertex and edge counts. The algorithm needs a policy, not a hidden assumption.

Duality Conventions

Vertical lines and points at infinity need a projective or symbolic convention if the implementation must handle them uniformly.

Numerical Fragility

Nearly equal intersections can flip order along a zone walk, so exact or certified predicates matter for robust subdivisions.

Common Mistake

Using the simple formulas as runtime checks on nonsimple input without first perturbing or representing degeneracy.

Engineering Rule

State the input model, then make the subdivision data structure enforce that model.

Relevant checks: degeneracy and zone traversal.Assumptions are part of the algorithm.

These are not afterthoughts; they determine whether the combinatorial ledger matches the computed subdivision.
