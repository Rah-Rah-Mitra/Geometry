Primal And Dual Ledgers
Rockafellar uses convex f, concave g

The Theorem Pairs \(f-g\) With \(g^*-f^*\)

Primal Ledger

\[\alpha=\inf_x\{f(x)-g(x)\}\]

\(f\) is proper convex and \(g\) is proper concave on the same finite-dimensional space.

Dual Ledger

\[\beta=\sup_{p}\{g^*(p)-f^*(p)\}\]

The concave conjugate \(g^*\) uses the opposite inequality convention from the convex conjugate.

Common Translation

For \(f(x)+\phi(x)\), take \(g=-\phi\). Then the dual becomes \(\sup_p\{-\phi^*(-p)-f^*(p)\}\).

Effective primal domain: dom f intersect dom gEffective dual domain: dom g* intersect dom f*
Rockafellar writes the theorem with a convex function f and a concave function g, so the primal object is f minus g. Translate this carefully for students who expect f plus phi notation: choosing g as minus a convex penalty or indicator recovers the familiar sum form.
Weak Duality Is One Inequality Chain
gap is always nonnegative

Fenchel Inequalities Put Every Dual Value Below Every Primal Value

Inequality Chain

\[ f(x)+f^*(p)\ge \langle x,p\rangle \ge g(x)+g^*(p) \]

\[ f(x)-g(x)\ge g^*(p)-f^*(p). \]

Gap Function

\[ \Gamma(x,p)=f(x)-g(x)-g^*(p)+f^*(p)\ge 0. \]

Equality Case

The gap vanishes exactly when the same pairing \(\langle x,p\rangle\) is tight for both conjugacy inequalities.

Weak duality: \(\alpha\ge\beta\)Exact duality asks when the gap can be zero
Spend time on the inequality chain because it is the whole proof skeleton. Fenchel's inequality for f gives the pairing below f plus f star, while the concave version for g places the same pairing above g plus g star. Subtracting produces a nonnegative gap.
Relative-Interior Hypotheses
exactness plus attainment

Qualification Tells Which Optimizer Is Guaranteed

Fenchel Duality

If either condition holds,

\[ \inf_x(f-g)=\sup_p(g^*-f^*). \]

Primal ri overlap

\(\operatorname{ri}(\operatorname{dom} f)\cap\operatorname{ri}(\operatorname{dom} g)\ne\varnothing\) gives dual attainment.

Dual ri overlap

If \(f,g\) are closed and dual relative interiors meet, primal attainment follows.

Both sides

When both conditions hold, the two values are finite and optimizers are available on both ledgers.

Polyhedral data

For polyhedral \(f\) or \(g\), ordinary domains can replace the corresponding relative interiors.

Qualification prevents a separating certificate from escaping into a vertical limitAttainment direction matters
The theorem is not only an equality statement; it also says where an optimizer is guaranteed to appear. A primal relative-interior overlap gives dual attainment, while a dual relative-interior overlap, together with closedness, gives primal attainment. Polyhedral data reduce the need for relative interiors.
Proof Geometry
epigraph versus shifted hypograph

The Dual Vector Is The Slope Of A Separator

Set The Optimal Shift

Let \(\alpha=\inf(f-g)\). The shifted hypograph of \(g+\alpha\) sits below the epigraph of \(f\).

Why Nonvertical?

A vertical separator would separate the projected domains, contradicting the primal relative-interior overlap.

Read The Certificate

\[ f(x)\ge h(x)\ge g(x)+\alpha \]

The affine graph supplies \(p\), and those two support inequalities bound \(f^*(p)\) and \(g^*(p)\).

The separator is the constructive part of the proofIts slope is the dual optimizer
Describe the proof as a geometric certificate extraction. The epigraph of f and a shifted hypograph of g cannot cross at the optimal shift. The relative-interior condition rules out a vertical separator, so the separator is the graph of an affine function whose slope becomes the dual optimizer.
Theorem 31.2
linear map A

Introduce \(A\) By Perturbing The Argument Of \(g\)

Perturbed Bifunction

\[ (F_u)(x)=f(x)-g(Ax+u) \]

The unperturbed primal value is \(p(0)=\inf_x F_0(x)\).

Adjoint Ledger

\[ (F^*_{x^*})(u^*)=g^*(u^*)-f^*(A^*u^*+x^*). \]

\[ \sup_{u^*}\{g^*(u^*)-f^*(A^*u^*)\}. \]

Strong Consistency

The primal program is strongly consistent exactly when some \(x\in\operatorname{ri}(\operatorname{dom}f)\) has \(Ax\in\operatorname{ri}(\operatorname{dom}g)\).

Dual objective: \(g^*(u^*)-f^*(A^*u^*)\)Identity \(A=I\) recovers Theorem 31.1
Now move from the identity case to the linear map A. The perturbation u translates the argument of g, so the value p of u records how the primal optimum changes under a right-hand-side shift. The adjoint A star is the bookkeeping device that moves dual slopes back to x-space.
Value Function View
support at u=0

The Dual Vector Supports The Perturbation Value At The Origin

Perturbation Value

\[ p(u)=\inf_x\{f(x)-g(Ax+u)\}. \]

No Gap Interpretation

Exact duality means the closed lower support of \(p\) at \(u=0\) reaches the actual value \(p(0)\).

Failure Mode

If \(p\) has a closure jump at the origin or no usable supporting slope, the dual ledger can under-report the primal value.

Uniqueness Cue

When \(p\) is differentiable at \(0\), the dual vector is unique and is read from the local slope.

Duality is local information about a value functionSection 31 connects this to the general program theory of Section 30
Use the graph of p near the origin as the operational meaning of the dual variable. A dual vector is a supporting slope for the value function at zero. A jump or missing support at zero is the visual version of a duality gap or a failed attainment statement.
Theorem 31.3
equality certificate

Kuhn-Tucker Conditions Are Matched Fenchel Equalities

Certificate Pair

\[ A^*u^*\in \partial f(x),\qquad Ax\in \partial g^*(u^*). \]

Primal Equality

\[ f(x)+f^*(A^*u^*)=\langle x,A^*u^*\rangle. \]

Dual Equality

\[ g(Ax)+g^*(u^*)=\langle Ax,u^*\rangle. \]

Both equalities together give \(f(x)-g(Ax)=g^*(u^*)-f^*(A^*u^*)\).

The KKT relations are necessary and sufficient for simultaneous primal-dual optimalityClosedness lets subdifferentials invert cleanly
State the conditions as matched equality cases in Fenchel's inequality. The relation A star u star belongs to the subdifferential of f at x, and Ax belongs to the subdifferential of g star at u star. Together they certify both primal and dual optimality, not merely stationarity.
Executable Check
quadratic plus indicator

A Boundary Minimizer Has A Nonzero Dual Certificate

Primal Model

\[ f(x)=\tfrac12(x+1)^2,\qquad g=-\delta_{[0,\infty)}. \]

\[ \inf_x\{f(x)-g(x)\}=f(0)=\tfrac12. \]

Dual Model

\[ f^*(p)=\tfrac12p^2-p,\qquad g^*(p)= \begin{cases}0,&p\ge0,\\-\infty,&p<0,\end{cases} \]

\[ \sup_{p\ge0}\{p-\tfrac12p^2\}=\tfrac12 \quad \text{at }p=1. \]

Audit Result

The primal point \(x=0\) and dual point \(p=1\) have zero Fenchel gap.

Notebook invariant: primal value equals dual support valueBoundary optimum makes the multiplier visible
This is the notebook's finite check in a concrete one-dimensional form. The unconstrained quadratic would minimize at minus one, but the indicator half-line forces the boundary point x equals zero. The dual parabola peaks at p equals one, and both ledgers return the same value one half.
Theorem 31.4
cone constraints

Cone Constraints Turn Fenchel Duality Into Complementarity

Cone Version

\[ \inf_{x\in K} f(x)=-\inf_{x^*\in K^*} f^*(x^*) \]

Here \(K^*=\{x^*:\langle x^*,x\rangle\le0\ \text{for all }x\in K\}\).

Certificate

\[ x\in K,\quad x^*\in K^*,\quad x^*\in\partial f(x),\quad \langle x,x^*\rangle=0. \]

Subspace Version

Taking \(K\) as a subspace gives the familiar dual restriction \(x^*\in L^\perp\).

Indicators convert constraints into support functions and polar conesComplementarity is Fenchel equality in cone clothing
The cone corollary should feel like a specialization, not a new theorem from nowhere. Replacing g by a negative indicator of a cone turns the dual variable into a member of the negative polar cone. Equality is then the familiar complementarity picture: primal feasible, dual feasible, orthogonal contact, and subgradient support.
Theorem 31.5
Moreau decomposition

The Quadratic Kernel Splits Every Vector Across A Subgradient Graph

Moreau Identity

\[ (f\square w)(z)+(f^*\square w)(z)=w(z),\qquad w(z)=\tfrac12\|z\|^2. \]

Unique Split

\[ z=x+x^*,\qquad x^*\in\partial f(x). \]

The two minimizers are unique because the quadratic term is strictly convex.

Proximation

The component \(x\) is \(\operatorname{prox}(z\mid f)\), and \(\operatorname{prox}(z\mid f^*)=z-\operatorname{prox}(z\mid f)\).

Moreau: exact duality plus strict convexity gives a unique decompositionProx maps are continuous contractions
Moreau's theorem is the section's geometric payoff. Adding the quadratic w makes the two infima finite and uniquely attained, so every vector z splits into x plus x star with x star in the subdifferential of f at x. For an indicator function this is nearest-point projection and polar residual.
Recap
audit a Fenchel duality claim

Four Checks Make The Theorem Usable

1. Ledger

Identify \(f-g\) and \(g^*-f^*\), including any sign conversion from convex penalties.

2. Qualification

Check the appropriate relative-interior condition before claiming equality or attainment.

3. Certificate

Look for Fenchel equality or KKT relations, not merely a plausible optimizer.

4. Perturbation

Read dual variables as supporting slopes of the value function at \(u=0\).

The section's durable invariant: every accepted primal-dual pair must make the Fenchel gap vanish.
