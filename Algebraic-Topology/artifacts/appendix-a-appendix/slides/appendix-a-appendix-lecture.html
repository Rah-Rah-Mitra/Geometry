CW Data

A characteristic map \(\Phi_\alpha:D_\alpha^n\to X\) attaches the boundary to \(X^{n-1}\) and sends \(\operatorname{int} D_\alpha^n\) homeomorphically onto an open cell \(e_\alpha^n\).

Keep Separate

  • The cell is only the interior image.

Weak Topology And Boundary Check

Weak Topology Test

A subset of \(X\) is closed exactly when its pullback along every characteristic map is closed in the corresponding disk. This is the topology that lets cellwise tests assemble into a statement about \(X\).

Two-boundary matrix
\(B_2=\begin{bmatrix}1\\[2pt]1\end{bmatrix}\)

The two-cell boundary is assembled from both one-cells.

Chain sanity
\(B_1B_2=\begin{bmatrix}0\\[2pt]0\end{bmatrix}\)

\(\chi=2-2+1=1\), matching the saved finite model.

Cellwise Continuity Scaffold

Continuity Scaffold

A map \(f:X\to Y\) from a CW complex is continuous when every restriction to a closed cell is continuous and the restrictions agree on overlaps.

Homotopy Version

For \(F:X\times I\to Y\), check each \(\overline e_\alpha^n\times I\). Compact generation of \(X\times I\) supplies the topology.

Product CW Versus Product Topology

Theorem A.6 orientation

CW Product

Product cells \(e_\alpha^m\times e_\beta^n\) generate a CW topology from product characteristic maps.

This topology can be finer than the ordinary product topology.

Compactly Generated Product

The natural CW structure lives on \((X\times Y)_c\), the topology generated by compact subsets.

Compact subsets project into finite subcomplexes, so cellwise checks become finite.

Ordinary Product

The distinction disappears if one factor is locally compact, or if both CW complexes have countably many cells.

These are the common algebraic-topology cases where no extra repair is visible.

Practical Rule

Before checking a homotopy or product construction, ask whether compact generation, local compactness, or countability is supplying the topology you intend to use.

Source orientation: products of CW complexes and compactly generated topologyPrepares the Dowker wedge witness
The distinction is subtle but important. The product cells always give a CW structure on the compactly generated product, while the ordinary product topology can be too coarse. In common cases, such as one locally compact factor or both factors countable, the topologies agree.

Domination And Mapping Telescopes

CW homotopy type

Open interaction. The toy telescope stacks mapping cylinders of a cellular endomap.

Domination

\[ Y \xrightarrow{i} X \xrightarrow{r} Y,\qquad r i\simeq \operatorname{id}_Y. \] If \(X\) is CW, the telescope of a cellular model for \(i r\) gives \(Y\) a CW homotopy type.

Mechanism

Replace the composite by a cellular map on \(X\), then stack its mapping cylinders so the telescope has CW cells.

Compact-Open Path Tubes

Compact-Open Subbasis

\[ M(K,U)=\{f:Y\to X\mid f(K)\subset U\}. \] Finite intersections of these sets control map-space neighborhoods.

Metric Case

If \(Y\) is compact and \(X\) is metric, the compact-open topology agrees with the sup metric topology.

Evaluation and currying use local compactness of the source variable. Quotient products use local compactness of the parameter space.
