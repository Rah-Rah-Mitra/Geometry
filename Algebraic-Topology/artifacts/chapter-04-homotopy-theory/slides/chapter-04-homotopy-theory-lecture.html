Definition · Based Cubes

Higher Homotopy Groups Use Cubes With Collapsed Boundary

Definition

For \(n\ge 1\), an element of \(\pi_n(X,x_0)\) is represented by a based map

\( \pi_n(X,x_0)=[(I^n,\partial I^n),(X,x_0)]. \)

Group Law

Concatenate cubes in one coordinate. Homotopies preserve the boundary condition, so the product is defined on classes.

Basepoint Role

The entire boundary lands at \(x_0\), so every pasted face is already compatible.

Boundary collapse makes concatenation legal.\(\pi_1\) is the path-loop case.
Introduce higher homotopy groups through the based cube model. A class is a map from the n-cube with the entire boundary collapsed to the basepoint. Concatenation in one coordinate gives the product, and the boundary condition is what makes pasting well defined. Pause to stress that basepoints are bookkeeping, not decoration.
Eckmann-Hilton · Two Products Become One

Interchange Forces Commutativity For \(n\ge 2\)

Proposition

For \(n\ge 2\), the group \(\pi_n(X,x_0)\) is abelian.

Interchange Skeleton

\(x\star y=(x\cdot e)\star(e\cdot y)\)

\(\quad=(x\star e)\cdot(e\star y)=x\cdot y\)

and similarly \(x\star y=y\cdot x\).

Check

The local JSON records two products collapsing to one and the common product being commutative.

Artifact: ../figures/pi-n-eckmann-hilton.pngchecks/pi-n-eckmann-hilton.json
Use the Eckmann-Hilton figure to explain why the group law changes character in dimension at least two. There are two independent concatenation directions, and the interchange law forces the two products to agree and commute. This is the first major structural simplification: higher homotopy groups are abelian.
Relative Groups · Exactness Begins

Relative Homotopy Records Boundary Obstructions

Pair Data

\(\pi_n(X,A,x_0)\) measures maps of \(n\)-cubes whose selected boundary faces land in \(A\), modulo homotopies of pairs.

Exact Segment

\(\cdots\to \pi_n(A)\to\pi_n(X)\to\pi_n(X,A)\xrightarrow{\partial}\pi_{n-1}(A)\to\cdots\)

Rank Check

For the disk-sphere pair model, the boundary is an isomorphism and the image/kernel ranks match one.

Artifact: ../figures/relative-homotopy-les.pngExactness turns deformation into algebra.
Treat the relative long exact sequence as the first usable control panel. A pair keeps track of maps of cubes whose boundary lands partly in a subspace, and the boundary homomorphism records the obstruction left on that subspace. The disk-sphere check anchors the diagram in a rank-one example.

Proof Mechanism · Cell Compression

Cellular Approximation Compresses Maps Skeleton By Skeleton

Cellular Approximation

A map between CW complexes can be deformed so that \(X^k\) lands in \(Y^k\) for every skeleton \(k\).

Compression Lemma

If relative homotopy obstructions vanish in the relevant range, cells can be pushed into the smaller subspace.

Whitehead Use

Repeat the local compression over all cells to build a homotopy inverse.

Cellular approximation is a deformation theorem.Compression is the obstruction-free local move.
Build cellular approximation as a compression picture. Maps can be deformed so each skeleton lands in the corresponding skeleton, and vanishing relative groups let cells be pushed into a smaller target. This slide is the proof mechanism behind Whitehead, not a separate decorative lemma.
Range Theorems · Excision And Suspension

Freudenthal Gives A Visible Stable Range

Freudenthal Range

If \(X\) is \(n\)-connected, then suspension \(\pi_i(X)\to\pi_{i+1}(\Sigma X)\) is an isomorphism for \(i\lt 2n+1\), and a surjection for \(i=2n+1\).

Range Theorems · Homotopy To Homology

Hurewicz Builds The First Reliable Bridge

Hurewicz Principle

If \(X\) is \((n-1)\)-connected with \(n\ge2\), then \(H_i(X)=0\) for \(i

Why It Matters

Homology is usually easier to compute. Hurewicz tells us when it sees the first genuine homotopy signal.

Do Not Overread

It identifies the first visible group in a range; it does not turn all homotopy into homology.

Artifact: ../figures/hurewicz-bridge.pngRange theorem, not universal formula.
Connect Hurewicz to the same computational problem as Freudenthal. Homotopy is hard, homology is computable, and under connectivity assumptions the first nonzero homotopy group maps isomorphically to homology. Ask students to notice that both range theorems give partial windows, not total formulas.
Fibrations · Homotopy Lifting

Fibrations Are Lifting Machines

Homotopy Lifting Property

Given an initial lift and a homotopy downstairs, a fibration \(p:E\to B\) supplies a compatible lifted homotopy upstairs.

Exact Sequence Source

The fiber \(F\to E\to B\) yields a long exact sequence because lifting failure is measured by homotopy classes in \(F\).

Bundle Link

Fiber bundles are familiar examples, but fibration language is flexible enough for homotopy theory.

Fibration language organizes long exact sequences.The fiber stores lifting defects.
Present fibrations as lifting machines. The homotopy lifting property turns a homotopy downstairs into a lifted homotopy upstairs after an initial lift is chosen. This is why fibrations generate long exact sequences: lifting failures are organized as homotopy classes in the fiber.

Representability · Cohomology As Maps

Eilenberg-Mac Lane Spaces Represent Cohomology

Representability

For CW complexes, cohomology classes are homotopy classes of maps into Eilenberg-Mac Lane spaces:

\(H^n(X;G)\cong [X,K(G,n)].\)

Why It Belongs Here

Cohomology becomes a homotopy classification problem, so it can control Postnikov towers and stable operations.

Memory Hook

\(K(G,n)\) has one intended homotopy group: \(G\) in degree \(n\), and no lower noise.

Bridge to Postnikov towers and Steenrod operations.CW hypotheses remain visible.
Move from cohomology as a group to cohomology as maps into a target space. For CW complexes, degree n cohomology with coefficients G is represented by homotopy classes of maps to K of G and n. This bridge is the reason Postnikov stages and cohomology operations can speak to each other.
Postnikov Systems · Homotopy With Twisting Data

A Postnikov Tower Stores Groups Plus \(k\)-Invariants

Stage

The \(n\)-th stage keeps \(\pi_i(X)\) for \(i\le n\) and kills higher homotopy.

Fiber

\(K(\pi_n X,n)\) is the layer added at degree \(n\).

Twist

A \(k\)-invariant is the cohomology class telling how the next layer is attached.

Check

Fiber labels sampled: \(K(\pi_2,2)\), \(K(\pi_3,3)\), \(K(\pi_4,4)\), \(K(\pi_5,5)\).

Artifact: ../html/postnikov-k-invariant-tower.htmlchecks/postnikov-obstruction-checks.json
Explain a Postnikov tower as a controlled loss of information. Each stage keeps homotopy groups only up to a certain degree, with fiber K of pi n and n. The k-invariants are the missing twisting data; they are why listing the groups alone usually does not determine the space.

Stability · Suspension Packages Data

The Stable Viewpoint Turns Repeated Suspension Into Structure

Spectrum

A spectrum packages spaces \(E_n\) with compatible suspension data \(\Sigma E_n\to E_{n+1}\).

Stable Splitting

Suspension can split a construction into a wedge of simpler pieces after unstable attachments are neutralized.

Why Stable

Some patterns are invisible in a single dimension but become functorial after repeated suspension.

Artifact: ../figures/stable-splittings-dold-thom.pngStability is a change of viewpoint, not a loss of rigor.
Shift to the stable viewpoint by describing suspension as a packaging operation. Stable splittings simplify spaces after suspension, and spectra record compatible suspension data across all degrees. The purpose is not to abandon unstable homotopy, but to expose patterns hidden by low-dimensional accidents.
Symmetric Products · Homology As Homotopy

Dold-Thom Converts Homology Into Homotopy

Dold-Thom

For connected CW complexes, the infinite symmetric product satisfies

\( \pi_i SP(X)\cong H_i(X;\mathbb Z)\quad (i\ge 1). \)

Sphere Check

For \(X=S^n\), the notebook records \(SP(X)\) with weak type \(K(\mathbb Z,n)\) and \(\pi_n=\mathbb Z\).

Conceptual Payoff

Homology is represented by a homotopy type, not merely by a chain computation.
