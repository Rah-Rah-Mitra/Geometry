Local Models

Pseudogroups Say Which Coordinate Changes Count

Working Definition

A differentiability class is encoded by a family of local transformations closed under restriction, composition, inverse, and gluing.

Atlas Test

Two charts are compatible when the overlap map phi_j phi_i^{-1} belongs to the chosen pseudogroup.

compatibility is a rule on overlaps, not on isolated charts
Pseudogroup viewpointcompatibility, maximal atlases, smooth structures
Emphasize that the pseudogroup is a policy for admissible coordinate changes. A chart by itself is only a local measurement device. The manifold structure appears when every overlap measurement respects the same policy, so calculations made in one chart translate coherently to another.
Differentiable Manifolds

Maps Are Smooth When Coordinates Make Them Smooth

Manifold

A Hausdorff space with a compatible atlas of local coordinates, usually taken smooth in this course.

Smooth Map

Test f: M → N in charts: psi f phi^{-1} must be smooth on coordinate domains.

Differential

At a point, the map linearizes to f_*: T_pM → T_{f(p)}N.

Coordinate independencesmooth maps, differentials, local rank
After the definition, immediately move to the testing procedure. A smooth map between abstract spaces is checked by ordinary calculus after choosing charts. The differential is the first invariant object that survives the chart translation and lets later chapters compare tangent directions.
Submanifolds

Embedded Pieces And Plane Fields Are Local Geometry In Motion

Submanifold Lens

Immersed and embedded submanifolds separate local tangent behavior from global topology.

Distribution

A rule p ↦ S_p ⊂ T_pM assigns allowable tangent directions at each point.

Integrability Question

When do these directions form actual submanifolds rather than only infinitesimal instructions?

Submanifold intuitionimmersions, embeddings, integral manifolds
Treat a distribution as a field of permitted tangent directions. The visual should make the integrability question concrete: a plane field is only infinitesimal data until curves and higher dimensional leaves fit through it. This prepares students for bundle language and connection horizontality.
Vector Fields

Derivations Generate Motion, Brackets Measure Noncommutation

Field As Operator

A vector field differentiates functions: X(f) is again a smooth function.

Flow

The local one-parameter family phi_t moves points along integral curves of X.

Bracket

[X,Y] records the first-order failure of flowing by X and Y in opposite orders.

Local transformationsderivations of functions and Lie brackets
Invite students to read a vector field in two equivalent ways: as arrows telling particles how to move, and as a derivation telling functions how to change. The bracket then becomes less mysterious, because it compares two tiny flow experiments performed in different orders.
Tensor Algebras

The Tensor Product Linearizes Multilinear Dependence

Universal Task

Replace bilinear maps U x V → W by ordinary linear maps out of U ⊗ V.

Why It Matters

The construction lets component formulas, contractions, and derivations live in one algebraic habitat.

Bilinear data → one linear object
Algebraic coretensor products, basis rules, universal property
Do not let the universal property float as a slogan. Explain that tensors earn their keep by turning many-input linear behavior into one-input linear behavior. This is why later coordinate transformations can be tracked systematically instead of rebuilt for every multilinear expression.
Components And Invariance

Instructor Warning

Components are not the tensor; they are the tensor viewed through a chosen basis.

Tensor spacescontravariant, covariant, mixed, contraction
This slide is a checkpoint against a common misconception. The superscripts and subscripts are not decorative indices; they identify which slots accept vectors, which slots accept covectors, and how the coordinate expression must change so the underlying multilinear object stays fixed.
Tensor Fields

A Tensor Field Is Smooth Multilinear Algebra Over Every Point

Pointwise

At each p, a tensor lives in a tensor space over T_pM.

Smoothness

In local coordinates, the component functions vary smoothly.

Operational View

Covariant tensor fields eat vector fields and return smooth functions.

Section 3 bridgefield of tensors, multilinear interpretation
Stress the transition from linear algebra at one vector space to geometry over a manifold. Smoothness is the extra condition that prevents the assignment from being arbitrary pointwise algebra. This prepares the class to recognize metrics, forms, and later connections as structured fields.
Exterior Algebra

Differential Forms Measure Oriented Infinitesimal Content

Alternating Rule

An r-form changes sign when two vector slots are exchanged.

Exterior Product

The wedge product builds higher-degree measurements from lower-degree ones.

Pullback

Maps transport forms backward, making integration on parameter domains possible.

Formsskew-symmetry, wedge product, pullback
Make forms visual by treating them as oriented measuring devices. A one-form measures directed motion through level families, while a two-form measures oriented area. The algebraic sign change then has a geometric reason: reversing the order reverses the orientation of the measurement.
Lie Differentiation

Differentiate A Field By Transporting It Along A Flow

Lie Derivative

L_X K compares a tensor field with its pullback along the flow generated by X.

Cartan Toolkit

For forms, L_X = i_X d + d i_X links flow change, contraction, and exterior derivative.

Interpretation

Invariance means the field is unchanged by the motion generated by the vector field.

Tensor calculusLie derivative, interior product, exterior derivative
Explain that the Lie derivative avoids choosing a connection. It uses only the flow of a vector field to move data back to the original point for comparison. This makes it the natural derivative for symmetry questions before covariant differentiation has been introduced.
Lie Groups

A Lie Group Is Simultaneously A Smooth Space And A Group

Definition Pattern

The group operations are smooth, so algebraic multiplication can be differentiated.

Examples

GL(n,R), O(n), matrix subgroups, and transformation groups acting on manifolds.

Infinitesimal Shadow

Vector fields generated by group motion foreshadow Lie algebras and later connection forms.

Lie groupssmooth multiplication, examples, transformation groups
Tie Lie groups back to flows. A vector field gives a local one-parameter motion, while a Lie group organizes many such motions into a smooth algebraic object. Matrix groups are the simplest laboratory because multiplication is familiar and differentiability can be checked directly.
Actions And Homogeneous Spaces

Group Actions Turn Symmetry Into Geometry On A Manifold

Action

A smooth rule M x G → M sends points to points while respecting group multiplication.

Quotient Idea

When a closed subgroup H sits in G, the quotient G/H often carries a manifold structure.

Geometric Payoff

Many model spaces are best read as homogeneous spaces, not as coordinate sets.

Symmetry sectionactions, stabilizers, quotients, homogeneous spaces
Use the orbit picture to connect algebra to geometry. Moving a point by all group elements creates a geometric subset, while the stabilizer explains which elements were redundant. The quotient viewpoint packages this redundancy and becomes essential for principal bundles and homogeneous examples.
Fibre Bundles

A Principal Bundle Is Locally A Product But Globally Twisted

Principal Bundle

P(M,G,pi) has a base M, total space P, and right action by G along each fibre.

Local Triviality

Over small sets U, the bundle looks like U x G.

Transition Functions

On overlaps, maps g_{ij}: U_i cap U_j → G describe the twisting.

Principal bundlesfibres, right actions, transition functions
The central idea is that local triviality is deliberately weaker than global triviality. Students should see the transition functions as the bookkeeping that remembers how local product pictures disagree on overlaps. This is the language that connection theory will use immediately in Chapter 2.
Associated Bundles

Representations Turn Principal Data Into Familiar Geometric Bundles

Construction

If G acts on a standard fibre F, the associated bundle has local fibre F and transition action by G.

Frame Bundle

A point is a basis of a tangent space.

Tensor Bundle

Fibre is a tensor space built from the tangent space.

frames + representation = vector and tensor bundles
Examplesframe bundle, tangent bundle, tensor bundle
Present associated bundles as a translation device. The principal bundle carries the abstract frame-changing symmetry, and a representation tells that symmetry how to act on a chosen model fibre. This turns the same transition data into tangent, cotangent, and tensor bundles.
Sections And Reductions

Geometric Structures Often Appear As Choices Inside A Bundle

Section

A section chooses one point in each fibre, smoothly over the base.

Reduction

Reducing the structure group means finding a subbundle with a smaller group that still describes the geometry.

Metric Example

A Riemannian metric can be read through the reduction of the frame bundle to orthonormal frames.

Structures as reductionssections, subbundles, orthonormal frames
This is a good moment to show why bundle language is economical. A metric is not only a formula for lengths; it also selects frames adapted to that formula. The reduction viewpoint makes the relationship between local choices and global compatibility explicit.
Induced Bundles And Coverings

Pullback And Covering Constructions Test The Gluing Rules

Induced Bundle

A smooth map f: N → M pulls a bundle over M back to one over N.

Covering View

Covering manifolds are local diffeomorphisms with discrete fibres, a clean example of local product behavior.

Diagnostic

If a construction behaves correctly under pullback, it is usually coordinate-free in the right sense.

Final bundle constructionspullback bundles and covering manifolds
Use this slide as a bridge from definitions to habits. Pullbacks ask whether a construction travels along maps, and coverings show how global shape can change while every small neighborhood still looks ordinary. These examples sharpen the meaning of local versus global.
