Open the lecture by telling students that transformations are not decorative symmetries added after geometry is built. In this chapter they are tests for the connection itself. A map, a vector field, or a local germ earns geometric meaning by preserving parallel transport, curvature data, metric data, or analytic normal-coordinate information.

Affine Maps Preserve Parallelism

section 1

Definition Lens

An affine map is a smooth map whose differential respects covariant differentiation.

Operational Test

If vector fields are related by f, then the derived field and the torsion or curvature expressions are related as well.

Teaching Cue

Tell students to watch the transported vector, not only the point. Affineness is a promise about the rule for comparison along curves.

Frame affine mappings as maps that respect the connection before they respect any metric. A good classroom move is to draw one curve and one transported vector, then apply the map. If the transported image agrees with transport in the target connection, the map is passing the chapter's main preservation test.

The Frame-Bundle Test

connection forms

Bundle Criterion

The induced map on frames must preserve the solder form θ and the connection form ω.

θ records base velocity ω records frame rotation both must be stable

Why this is useful: it reduces a downstairs geometric condition to preservation of canonical forms upstairs.

What it catches: a diffeomorphism may move points smoothly while still distorting the comparison rule between frames.

This slide connects Chapter VI back to the principal-bundle language established earlier. The point is not to make students memorize two forms. The point is that the solder form and connection form together encode the entire affine structure, so preserving both is a compact and testable version of preserving the connection.

The Affine Transformation Group

finite-dimensional control

1

A transformation is accepted only if it preserves the connection data.

2

The induced action on the frame bundle preserves a rigid system of forms.

3

The resulting symmetry set becomes a Lie group under the natural topology.

4

Local flow calculations can therefore be read as Lie algebra calculations.

Stress that the Lie group statement is a structural payoff. It means the symmetries are not an arbitrary collection of maps. Once the connection is fixed, the accepted transformations have enough rigidity that one-parameter subgroups and their vector-field generators become the right language for the rest of the chapter.

Infinitesimal Affine Transformations

section 2

Local Definition

A vector field X is infinitesimally affine when its local flow preserves the connection wherever the flow is defined.

Lifted View

The natural lift to the frame bundle commutes with standard horizontal fields.

Common Misread

Being a vector field is not enough. The flow must preserve the rule for differentiating other vector fields.

Introduce infinitesimal affine transformations as the derivative version of the preceding group. Students should see that the vector field is being judged by its flow. If the flow keeps the connection stable to first order, then the lifted field upstairs commutes with the standard horizontal directions that encode parallel displacement.

Four Faces Of The Same Condition

Proposition 2.2

Operator Form

The commutator of Lie differentiation with covariant differentiation is controlled by the bracket with X.

Instructor Prompt

Ask which face is easiest to verify in a concrete example. Flow language is geometric, while the operator identity is usually the calculation surface.

Treat the equivalence as a translation table. The same condition can be read geometrically from flows, upstairs from the frame bundle, algebraically from an operator identity, or diagnostically from induced behavior on torsion and curvature. This is a good moment to ask students which formulation they would use for a computation.

Completeness Turns Local Flow Into Global Flow

Theorem 2.4

Main Message

If the connection is complete, an infinitesimal affine transformation integrates to a global one-parameter group.

A

Lift the vector field to the frame bundle and use its commutation with horizontal fields.

B

Reach arbitrary frames by horizontal and vertical moves supplied by completeness.

C

Extend the local flow step by step without changing the affine condition.

The proof idea is worth making visual. Completeness is not a decorative hypothesis; it prevents the flow from running out of manifold before the affine condition can be propagated. The frame-bundle argument uses horizontal accessibility and vertical changes of frame to extend the local generator to a full one-parameter transformation group.

Isometries Are Orthogonal Affine Maps

section 3

Riemannian Extra Condition

An isometry preserves the metric, so it preserves the Levi-Civita connection as an affine transformation.

Group Consequence

For finite-dimensional connected manifolds, the isometry group is a Lie group with the expected infinitesimal algebra.

Mnemonic

Affine asks whether parallelism survives. Isometry asks that parallelism and measuring rods survive together.

Now specialize from connections to Riemannian geometry. The orthonormal frame bundle gives a clean picture: an isometry cannot just move frames; it must move orthonormal frames to orthonormal frames. The Levi-Civita connection turns this metric preservation into affine preservation, so the earlier machinery becomes available.

Killing Fields And The Skew Test

infinitesimal isometry

Killing Criterion

X is infinitesimally isometric when the metric does not change along its flow.

Equivalently, the covariant derivative of X is skew with respect to g.

Calculus surface: verify the symmetric part of the covariant derivative vanishes.

Geometry surface: local distances are unchanged to first order along the flow of X.

Present Killing fields as infinitesimal isometries, not as mysterious vector fields with a name attached. The skew condition is the key computational test: a skew endomorphism rotates or shears within the metric conventions without changing inner products to first order. This prepares the holonomy and Ricci restrictions that follow.

Holonomy Reads The Infinitesimal Part

section 4

Auxiliary Tensor

AX measures the difference between Lie motion by X and covariant motion along X.

1

Flow by X around the base point and compare with parallel displacement.

2

The mismatch lands in the normalizer of the holonomy algebra.

3

On compact Riemannian manifolds, stronger holonomy membership appears for isometries.

This slide is about where the infinitesimal data can live. The tensor A sub X compares two ways of moving: by the flow of X and by the connection. Holonomy records what parallel transport can produce, so the chapter uses normalizer and compactness arguments to restrict the allowable mismatch.

Ricci Tensor As A Trace Constraint

section 5

Definition In Words

Fix X and Y. Send V to R(V, X)Y, then take the trace over V.

Why It Matters Here

For infinitesimal affine or isometric fields, Ricci identities become sign and vanishing tests.

Instructor Cue

Call Ricci a census of curvature responses rather than a new independent object.

Define Ricci carefully but visually. The chapter needs Ricci because a trace of curvature can be integrated or sign-tested more directly than the full curvature tensor. In lecture, emphasize that Ricci summarizes how the curvature operator responds across all directions, which makes it effective for controlling infinitesimal symmetries.

Negative Ricci Removes Continuous Isometries

Bochner-style consequence

Rigidity Statement

On a connected Riemannian manifold with appropriate completeness and sign hypotheses, negative Ricci curvature forces infinitesimal isometries to vanish.

Conceptual Reason

A Killing field cannot both preserve all metric data and spend energy against a strictly negative Ricci trace.

Group Consequence

On compact spaces, the identity component of the isometry group can collapse, making the full isometry group finite.

Do not present this as a magic theorem about negativity. Explain the mechanism: Killing fields preserve the metric, so analytic identities convert their derivative data and the Ricci trace into a nonnegative quantity that cannot coexist with strict negative Ricci unless the field is zero. Then connect the infinitesimal result to finiteness of isometry groups on compact manifolds.

The extension section is a local-to-global machine. The key idea is that analytic maps which agree in enough initial data cannot fork silently. Along a chain of neighborhoods, the local affine isomorphism continues, and simple connectivity prevents different chains from creating incompatible answers after going around a loop.

The Equivalence Problem In Normal Coordinates

section 7

Problem

When do two connections look the same near chosen base frames?

Strategy

Expand torsion and curvature in normal coordinates and compare the resulting jets.

Data To Compare

Initial frame, torsion, curvature, and the covariant derivatives that the normal-coordinate recursion exposes.

The equivalence problem asks for a practical recognition rule. Normal coordinates turn a connection into a controlled collection of coefficients near a base point. The chapter shows that torsion, curvature, and their derivative data can be organized so that matching the right jets produces a local affine equivalence.

Parallel Torsion And Curvature Produce Models

invariant by parallelism

Recognition Rule

If torsion and curvature are parallel, normal-coordinate data becomes analytic and repeatable.

Model Payoff

Complete simply connected spaces with matching invariant data can be identified by a unique affine isomorphism.

What Can Fail

Without completeness or simple connectivity, local matching may stop early or return with monodromy.

This slide gathers the final equivalence results into one picture. Parallel torsion and curvature mean the invariants do not drift under parallel displacement, so normal-coordinate expansions become stable. With completeness and simple connectivity, the local affine equivalence can be pushed to a global model identification.

Lab Surface: Perturb One Hypothesis

from notebook to lecture

Keep The Map

Preserve the point map but change the connection. The same diffeomorphism may stop being affine because transported vectors no longer match.

Keep The Field

Preserve the vector field but change the metric. A flow that was Killing can acquire a symmetric derivative part.

Keep The Ricci Sign

Remove compactness or completeness. The sign argument may lose the integration or extension step needed for global rigidity.

Keep The Jets

Remove simple connectivity. Local continuations can still agree nearby but fail to produce a single global transformation.

Discussion Prompt

Which theorem in the chapter fails first when the selected hypothesis is removed, and what geometric object records that failure?

Use the lab slide to connect the lecture back to the notebook's perturbation mindset. Students should practice changing one assumption at a time and predicting which invariant breaks. The aim is not to solve exercises from the source, but to make the hypotheses feel like active controls in a geometric experiment.

3

Close by returning to the preservation hierarchy. A general diffeomorphism is too loose for this chapter. Affine transformations preserve the connection, isometries preserve the metric and hence the Levi-Civita connection, and infinitesimal versions expose the same ideas through vector fields. The final lesson is that local data can become global symmetry only under explicit rigidity hypotheses.
