Algebraic Preliminaries

From Real Tangent Spaces To Complex Directions

Definition Pattern

A complex structure on a real vector space is an endomorphism \(J\) satisfying \(J^2=-I\).

Basis Consequence

Every real basis adapted to \(J\) appears as paired vectors \(e_a, Je_a\). The real dimension is therefore even.

Group View

Changing adapted bases leads to the complex-linear frame group \(GL(n,\mathbb C)\) inside \(GL(2n,\mathbb R)\).

Stress that a complex structure on a real vector space is not extra notation for coordinates. It is an operator J with square minus the identity, so every admissible basis arrives in pairs. The diagram should make the pairing visible before any complex coordinate symbols are introduced.
Complexification

Type Decomposition: (1,0) And (0,1)

Algebraic Split

\(T_xM \otimes \mathbb C = T_x^{1,0}M \oplus T_x^{0,1}M\)

Why It Matters
  • Vector fields acquire a type.
  • Forms acquire bidegrees \((p,q)\).
  • Integrability becomes a bracket closure test.
Common Trap

The split lives after complexifying. It does not remove the underlying real tangent space.

Use the split into type (1,0) and type (0,1) directions as the first major payoff of the algebra. The audience should see that complexification doubles the room, while the eigenvalue test separates holomorphic and antiholomorphic motion into complementary lanes.
Almost Complex Manifolds

Almost Complex Structures: Algebra Becomes Field

Pointwise Data

A smooth tensor field \(J:TM\to TM\) with \(J^2=-I\) equips every tangent space with complex-linear algebra.

Immediate Geometry

Such a manifold has even real dimension and a natural orientation, because every local adapted frame is ordered by pairs.

Not Yet A Complex Manifold

The field \(J\) may fail to come from holomorphic coordinate charts.

Move from algebra at one tangent space to a smooth field of operators on the whole manifold. Almost complex means the pointwise algebra is available everywhere; it does not yet guarantee holomorphic charts. That distinction is the hinge for the next integrability slide.
Integrability

Integrability: When J Comes From Holomorphic Charts

Obstruction

\(N_J(X,Y)=[JX,JY]-J[JX,Y]-J[X,JY]-[X,Y]\)

Geometric Reading

Vanishing torsion means the type \((1,0)\) fields close under bracket.

Complex Manifold

When \(J\) is induced by holomorphic coordinates, the obstruction vanishes automatically.

Present integrability as the difference between a formal rotation rule and coordinates that genuinely behave holomorphically. The Nijenhuis torsion is the obstruction detector. When it vanishes, brackets of type (1,0) fields stay in the expected type and the local complex language becomes honest.
Differential Forms

Forms Split Into Bidegrees

Bidegree

\(\Lambda^k T^*M\otimes\mathbb C=\bigoplus_{p+q=k}\Lambda^{p,q}\)

Complex Case

The exterior derivative separates into \(d=\partial+\bar\partial\) by type.

Inspection Prompt

Given a displayed form, mark how many \(dz\) and \(d\bar z\) factors it contains.

Forms inherit the same type bookkeeping as tangent vectors, and this is where the chapter begins to feel computational. Explain that d can be inspected by its type-changing pieces. On a complex manifold, the clean partial and conjugate-partial split becomes a practical diagnostic.
Complex Linear Frames

Connections That Preserve J

Compatibility

\(\nabla J=0\)

Bundle Language

The connection form takes values in the complex-linear Lie algebra \(\mathfrak{gl}(n,\mathbb C)\).

Consequences

Torsion and curvature commute with the type decomposition, giving identities unavailable to arbitrary affine connections.

A connection in this chapter is useful only if it knows about J. The slide should connect the bundle of complex linear frames with the equation parallel J equals zero. Once a connection preserves J, torsion and curvature are forced to satisfy complex-linear identities.
Hermitian Metrics

Hermitian Metrics And The Fundamental Form

Metric Compatibility

\(g(JX,JY)=g(X,Y)\)

Fundamental Two-Form

\(\Omega(X,Y)=g(X,JY)\)

Frame Reduction

Orthonormal complex frames reduce the structure group from \(GL(n,\mathbb C)\) to \(U(n)\).

Emphasize that a Hermitian metric is a Riemannian metric that treats J as an isometry. The fundamental two-form packages the same data in skew form. This pairing is the bridge from metric geometry to the exterior-form tests that define the Kaehler condition.
Kaehler Metrics

Kaehler Condition: Three Equivalent Lenses

Connection Lens

\(\nabla J=0\)

The Levi-Civita connection transports complex directions into complex directions.

Form Lens

\(d\Omega=0\)

The fundamental form behaves like a closed symplectic form.

Chart Lens

\(N_J=0\)

The almost complex structure is compatible with holomorphic coordinates.

This is the central compression slide for Kaehler geometry. The audience should leave with three interchangeable lenses: the Levi-Civita connection preserves J, the fundamental form is closed, and the complex structure is integrable with the compatible metric. Each lens supports a different computation.
Local Coordinate Systems

Local Coordinates: Reading The Metric Matrix

Hermitian Matrix

\(ds^2=\sum g_{\alpha\bar\beta}\, dz^\alpha d\bar z^\beta\)

Kaehler Symmetry

\(\partial_\gamma g_{\alpha\bar\beta}=\partial_\alpha g_{\gamma\bar\beta}\)

Local Potential Heuristic

Many examples can be read from a scalar potential by \(g_{\alpha\bar\beta}=\partial_\alpha\partial_{\bar\beta}K\).

Local coordinates are not merely notation here; they are the practical place where a Kaehler metric becomes a Hermitian matrix of functions. Point out how symmetry and derivative identities reduce the number of independent coefficients and make curvature formulas readable.
Curvature Components

Curvature In Complex Notation

Type Restriction

Curvature respects the complex structure, so the active components carry balanced holomorphic and antiholomorphic indices.

Trace Object

\(\rho(X,Y)=\operatorname{tr}\{Z\mapsto J R(X,Y)Z\}\)

Reading Rule

Curvature formulas become coordinate diagnostics for Ricci flatness, constant holomorphic curvature, and holonomy reduction.

Curvature in complex notation has fewer live components than an arbitrary Riemannian curvature tensor. Use the block diagram to show why Kaehler symmetries remove many mixed terms. The Ricci form is then a trace of the curvature, not a mysterious new object.
Examples

The Example Zoo: Flat, Projective, Ball

Complex Euclidean Space

Constant coefficients, zero curvature, and the simplest Hermitian form.

Projective Space

Fubini-Study geometry packages lines through the origin as points.

Complex Ball

The invariant metric bends geodesic behavior toward the boundary.

Other chapter models include complex tori, Grassmannians, Hopf-type manifolds, and induced Kaehler structures on suitable submanifolds.
Keep the examples as models, not as a catalogue to memorize. Flat complex space, projective space with positive curvature, the ball with negative curvature, tori, and Grassmannians show how the same Kaehler grammar appears in different global settings.
Holomorphic Sectional Curvature

Curvature Of A J-Invariant Plane

Definition

\(H(X)=\dfrac{R(X,JX,JX,X)}{\lVert X\rVert^4}\)

Why Selective?

Only planes stable under \(J\) are tested, so the curvature question respects complex directions.

Kaehler Payoff

Under Kaehler symmetries, holomorphic sectional curvature can determine the full curvature model in constant cases.

Holomorphic sectional curvature is the sectional curvature of a J-invariant two-plane. It is more selective than ordinary sectional curvature, but in the Kaehler setting it carries enough information to organize the full curvature tensor under the chapter's symmetry assumptions.

Product Geometry

De Rham Splitting In The Kaehler World

Parallel Factor Logic

If the Riemannian connection preserves \(J\), then parallel subbundles are tested for \(J\)-invariance.

Kaehler Product

\(M \simeq M_1 \times \cdots \times M_r\)

Holonomy Reading

Irreducible factors correspond to pieces on which the holonomy representation does not split further.

De Rham decomposition gains extra structure in the Kaehler category because parallel transport respects J. Riemannian factors can be paired with complex factors, and non-degeneracy becomes a statement about which parallel pieces remain. This links holonomy, product geometry, and complex coordinates.
Complex Submanifolds

Submanifolds Inherit The Curvature Test

Complex Compatibility

If \(M\subset M'\) is complex, tangent and normal spaces are \(J\)-invariant.

Gauss Reading

\(H_M \le H_{M'}\) when the correction is nonnegative in the chosen convention.

Use Case

Non-positive ambient holomorphic sectional curvature descends to complex submanifolds.

For complex submanifolds, the normal space is also J-invariant, so the second fundamental form satisfies complex compatibility identities. The Gauss equation then explains why ambient holomorphic sectional curvature controls the submanifold's corresponding curvature, with a visible correction term.
Hermitian Vector Bundles

Hermitian Vector Bundles And The Chern Connection

Uniqueness

There is a unique connection compatible with the Hermitian metric and the holomorphic bundle structure.

Local Form

\(\theta = H^{-1}\partial H,\qquad F=\bar\partial(H^{-1}\partial H)\)

Bridge Forward

Its curvature is the local engine behind Chern forms and later characteristic-class formulas.

Close the theory arc with Hermitian vector bundles. The Chern connection is the unique connection compatible with the Hermitian metric and holomorphic structure. Its curvature supplies the local form formulas behind Ricci-type quantities and later characteristic-class constructions.
