Small Surgical Hands: Actuation And Feedback

Miniaturization Moves Dexterity Into Transmission Geometry

Design tension
  • Small wrists and tools limit local actuators near the end effector.
  • Tendons, cables, or compliant links transmit motion from elsewhere.
  • Feedback must recover contact information through the transmission.

The hand is small, but the configuration map is still high-dimensional.

Microrobotics: Scale Laws Break Rigid-Body Intuition

Scaling Down Preserves Shape But Not Dominant Physics

Scale-law check

A \sim L^2, \qquad V \sim L^3, \qquad A/V \sim L^{-1}

  • Kinematic drawings can look similar across scale.
  • Surface, volume, inertia, and actuation do not scale together.
  • Rigid-body intuition must be rechecked against dimensionless ratios.
