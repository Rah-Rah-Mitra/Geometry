Core Data Structure · Cochain Complex

The Exterior Derivative Makes Forms Into Cohomology

Definition

The de Rham complex of a smooth manifold \(M\) is

\(\Omega^0(M) \xrightarrow{d} \Omega^1(M) \xrightarrow{d} \cdots \xrightarrow{d} \Omega^n(M)\), with \(d^2=0\).

Closed Versus Exact

  • Closed: \(d\omega=0\), so the form passes the boundary test.
  • Exact: \(\omega=d\eta\), so the form is a derivative of lower-degree data.
  • Cohomology: \(H^k_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)=\ker d/\operatorname{im}d\).
Chapter object: the cochain complex of forms.Cohomology measures closed forms not accounted for by potentials.
Present the de Rham complex as the central data structure of the chapter. A differential form is not just an expression to integrate; it is a cochain whose coboundary is the exterior derivative. The equation d squared equals zero is the reason closed forms modulo exact forms produce cohomology, and the diagram keeps degrees visible.
Local Exactness · Ordinary Poincare Lemma

On A Contractible Patch, Closed Forms Have Potentials

Local Theorem

If \(U\subset \mathbb{R}^n\) is star-shaped, then \(H^k_{\mathrm{dR}}(U)=0\) for \(k>0\), and \(H^0_{\mathrm{dR}}(U)\) records locally constant functions.

Why It Matters

De Rham cohomology is locally trivial in positive degree. Nontrivial classes are created by how patches overlap, not by mysterious local behavior.

Proof Intuition

A contraction produces a homotopy operator \(K\) satisfying \(dK+Kd = I-\varepsilon\), where \(\varepsilon\) remembers the constant part.

Local exactness is the chapter's starting engine.Positive-degree local classes vanish.
Use the local slide to lower the intimidation level. On a star-shaped or contractible coordinate patch, positive-degree closed forms are exact because a homotopy operator contracts the patch to a point. The point is not the formula itself; the point is that de Rham cohomology is locally boring, so all interesting classes come from gluing.
Gluing Machine · Mayer-Vietoris

Exactness Measures Whether Local Forms Glue

Short Exact Sequence Of Complexes
\(\Omega^\bullet(M) \to \Omega^\bullet(U)\oplus\Omega^\bullet(V) \to \Omega^\bullet(U\cap V)\)

The last map sends \((\alpha,\beta)\) to \(\alpha|_{U\cap V}-\beta|_{U\cap V}\).

Interpretation

  • Restriction is easy: any global form has compatible local pieces.
  • Gluing is the test: local forms must agree on the overlap.
  • The connecting homomorphism records the obstruction class.
Exact sequence: global form, local pieces, overlap discrepancy.Long exact cohomology sequence follows.
Frame Mayer-Vietoris as exact linear algebra for forms on an open cover. A global form restricts to two pieces, and the difference of those restrictions on the overlap measures whether local data glue. This short exact sequence of complexes is the machine that later produces long exact sequences in cohomology.
First Computation · Cohomology From An Overlap

A One-Class Can Be Born From Two Contractible Arcs

Cover Data

Let \(S^1=U\cup V\), where \(U\) and \(V\) are slightly enlarged arcs. Each piece is contractible.

Overlap Data

\(U\cap V\) has two components. A mismatch that is constant on one component and different on the other cannot be removed globally.

Outcome

\(H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}(S^1)\cong \mathbb{R}\), detected by integrating around the circle.

Two simple patches can create a nontrivial global class.Mayer-Vietoris explains the birth of H^1.
Run the circle example as a concept check rather than as a computation. Two arcs have trivial first cohomology, but their overlap has two components, so a locally constant discrepancy can survive as a global one-class. This is the first concrete sign that cohomology records the way local patches are assembled.
Orientation · Coherent Signs For Top Forms

Integration Requires A Consistent Choice Of Local Volume Sign

Orientation

An orientation is a compatible choice of positive ordered bases in tangent spaces, or equivalently compatible signs for local volume forms.

Sign Sensitivity

Changing orientation changes \(\int_M \omega\) by a sign for top-degree forms. That is why orientability appears before Poincare duality.

Instructor Check

Ask whether the symbol \(dx_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx_n\) is a volume form by itself. The answer depends on the chosen orientation.

Orientation is the sign infrastructure for integration.No coherent sign, no ordinary fundamental class.
Orientation should be taught as the sign convention that makes integration coherent. A top-degree form can be integrated only after compatible local volume signs have been selected. Reversing orientation reverses the sign of the integral, which is why orientability becomes a structural hypothesis in duality statements.
Integration · Stokes As Compatibility

The Boundary Of A Chain Is Dual To The Derivative Of A Form

Stokes Formula
\(\displaystyle \int_M d\omega = \int_{\partial M}\omega\)

The exterior derivative and boundary operator are adjoint under integration.

Cohomology Consequence

Exact forms integrate to zero on cycles. Closed forms therefore define functionals on homology classes.

Lecture Language

Integration is not an extra operation after cohomology; it is the map that lets cohomology read chains.

Stokes is the compatibility law behind de Rham's theorem.Exact forms vanish on cycles.
Make Stokes the compatibility law between the two worlds. Chains have a boundary operator and forms have the exterior derivative; integration turns those operations into adjoints. This single formula explains why exact forms integrate to zero on cycles and why closed forms define cohomology functionals.
Compact Support · The Top-Degree Exception

Compactly Supported Forms Remember Total Integral

Compact-Support Poincare Lemma

For \(\mathbb{R}^n\), compactly supported cohomology vanishes except in degree \(n\), where integration gives an isomorphism \(H^n_c(\mathbb{R}^n)\cong \mathbb{R}\).

Contrast

Ordinary Poincare lemma says a contractible patch has no positive-degree cohomology. Compact support changes the boundary condition at infinity.

Seed For Later

The top compact-support class is the local model for oriented integration, Thom classes, and Poincare duality.

Compact support changes the local answer.Top-degree integral survives.
Separate ordinary and compact-support Poincare lemmas clearly. Ordinary local exactness kills positive-degree classes on Euclidean space, while compact support leaves a top-degree class measured by total integral. That top compact-support class is the local seed from which orientation, Thom classes, and Poincare duality grow.
Duality · Pairing Ordinary And Compact-Support Classes

Poincare Duality Turns Integration Into A Perfect Test

Poincare Duality Shape

For an oriented \(n\)-manifold, integration gives a nondegenerate pairing

\(H^k(M)\times H^{n-k}_c(M)\to \mathbb{R}\), \(([\alpha],[\beta])\mapsto\int_M\alpha\wedge\beta\).

Read The Formula

A \(k\)-class is detected by complementary compactly supported forms. Compactness makes the integral finite on noncompact manifolds.

Duality is integration upgraded to a theorem.Compact support provides the complementary test space.
Introduce Poincare duality as a perfect pairing, not just as a dimension count. A compactly supported form of complementary degree can test an ordinary cohomology class by wedging and integrating. The visual pairing reminds students that duality is a global statement assembled from local integration data.
Bundles · Thom Class As Fiberwise Unit

A Thom Class Integrates To One On Every Fiber

Thom Class

For an oriented rank \(r\) vector bundle \(\pi:E\to M\), the Thom class \(u\in H^r_c(E)\) restricts on each fiber to the compact-support generator whose integral is \(1\).

Local Model

Over a trivializing chart, the class looks like the compactly supported top class on \(\mathbb{R}^r\).

Global Point

Orientation lets those fiberwise generators glue coherently across the base.

Thom class: compact vertical normalization.Fiber integral equals one.
Define the Thom class by its behavior along each fiber. In an oriented rank r vector bundle, the Thom class is a compactly supported vertical r-class whose integral over every fiber is one. This slide should make clear why compact support is built into the definition: the class lives near the zero section.
Thom Isomorphism · Bundle-Level Poincare Lemma

Cup With The Thom Class Moves Cohomology Upstairs

Thom Isomorphism

The map \(a\mapsto \pi^*a\wedge u\) identifies \(H^k(M)\) with \(H_c^{k+r}(E)\) for an oriented rank \(r\) bundle.

Euler Class

Pulling the Thom class back along the zero section gives \(e(E)=s^*u\), a class on the base that measures self-intersection or obstruction.

Proof Memory

Local trivial bundles reduce to compact-support Poincare; Mayer-Vietoris glues the local isomorphisms.

Thom isomorphism packages compact-support local cohomology over a base.Euler class is the zero-section trace.
Use the Thom isomorphism as the bundle-level version of the compact-support Poincare lemma. Pull a class from the base, multiply by the Thom class, and the result is a compactly supported class upstairs. The Euler class then appears by restricting the Thom class back along the zero section, turning a tubular construction into a characteristic obstruction.
Proof Strategy · Local-To-Global

Mayer-Vietoris Glues The Local Theorems Into Global Ones

1. Localize

Choose coordinate balls, collars, or trivial bundle charts.

2. Prove Locally

Use ordinary or compact-support Poincare lemmas.

3. Compare

Write restriction maps and overlap discrepancies.

4. Exactness

Use Mayer-Vietoris long exact sequences.

5. Induct

Grow the cover while preserving isomorphisms.

6. Interpret

Translate the result into integration or duality.

The chapter's proofs reuse one recognizable pattern.Local exactness plus exact gluing.
Show the proof strategy rather than every proof detail. Local Poincare lemmas prove the statement on coordinate balls or trivial bundles, Mayer-Vietoris glues across covers, and the five-lemma style exactness step transports isomorphisms. This is the chapter's recurring proof grammar, and students should recognize it before seeing it again.
Twisting · Nonorientable Manifolds

When Signs Cannot Be Chosen Globally, Record The Twist

What Breaks

Local orientations exist, but continuation around a loop can return with the opposite sign. There is no ordinary global top form with coherent sign.

Repair

Use the orientation local system. It stores the sign changes, allowing integration and duality statements to be written with twisted coefficients.

Conceptual Takeaway

Nonorientability does not destroy de Rham theory; it tells the theory which coefficient system must carry the signs.

Twisted coefficients remember the missing orientation.Local signs still glue after the twist is recorded.
Treat the nonorientable case as an adjustment to coefficients, not as a failure of the theory. Local orientations still exist, but signs change when transported around certain loops. The orientation local system records those sign changes, so integration and duality continue after the coefficient system remembers the twisting.

Recap · Choose The Right Machine

De Rham Theory Converts Geometry Into Exact Sequences And Pairings

Complex

Forms with \(d^2=0\) give cohomology \(H^\bullet_{\mathrm{dR}}\).

Local

Poincare lemmas say local positive-degree data are exact.

Gluing

Mayer-Vietoris turns overlap disagreement into long exact sequences.

Integration

Orientation and Stokes connect forms to chains and cycles.

Big Picture

Duality comes from combining local compact-support generators with global integration.

Exit Prompt

For a new manifold or bundle, decide first: local exactness, overlap gluing, orientation signs, compact support, or fiber integration?

The point of the overview is not to memorize a chain of named theorems, but to see how local exactness, gluing, signs, compact support, and fiber integration cooperate. End with the message that de Rham theory converts geometry into computable exact sequences and pairings.
