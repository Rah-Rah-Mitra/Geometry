Good Covers
local exactness becomes combinatorics

Good Covers Make Columns Exact

A good cover turns each nonempty finite intersection into a contractible laboratory. Local de Rham classes disappear, leaving the nerve to carry global topology.

Good-Cover Input

Every nonempty \(U_{i_0\cdots i_p}\) has trivial higher de Rham cohomology.

Column Consequence

The augmented vertical complexes are exact away from degree zero.

Global Consequence

The bottom Cech complex of the nerve retains the global obstruction data.

Teaching Translation

Replace an infinite sheaf-of-forms problem by finite boundary ranks on a simplicial complex.

Good cover = local Poincare lemma available on every intersectionCech cohomology reads the overlap pattern
Emphasize why good covers are more than a pleasant hypothesis. If every finite intersection is contractible or empty, the Poincare lemma kills local higher cohomology. The hard-looking sheaf of forms then contributes exact columns, so the remaining information is forced into the combinatorics of overlaps.

Circle Nerve

3 vertices, 3 edges, no filled face gives one \(H^1\) generator.

Sphere Nerve

Boundary of a tetrahedron gives one \(H^2\) generator and no \(H^1\).

Inspection Target

The finite examples keep the abstract comparison honest. A three-set circle cover has a triangular nerve without its face, so one one-dimensional cycle survives. A sphere good cover can be modeled by the boundary of a tetrahedron, where the surviving class is two-dimensional instead of one-dimensional.
Constructive Bridge
from Cech cocycle to closed form

Collating Turns Cech Data Into A Form

Operator Idea

Partition-of-unity homotopies lower Cech degree until only a global form remains.

Discrete Input

A Cech cocycle records mismatch data on overlaps.

Form Output

The collated form is closed and has the same cohomology class.

Circle Test

The model one-form integrates to one, so its period sees the generator of \(H^1(S^1)\).

Collating is the constructive inverse directionDiscrete overlap data becomes a de Rham representative
Now move from comparison to construction. A partition of unity supplies an operator that lowers Cech degree. Repeating that move converts a Cech cocycle into an ordinary closed form. For the circle, the discrete one-cocycle becomes a global one-form whose period detects the same generator.
Presheaves
local constancy is not global constancy

Presheaves Add Monodromy To The Same Cover Story

Local System On A Circle

\(H^0=\ker(P-I)\), and \(H^1=\operatorname{coker}(P-I)\).

Failure Mode

Local fibers may be isomorphic while a loop returns by a nontrivial automorphism.

Chapter Role

Refinements and homotopies make Cech cohomology independent of a chosen cover.

Takeaway

The nerve sees both incidence data and coefficient transport around loops.

Locally constant presheaf: restrictions are local isomorphismsGlobal sections are fixed by loop transport
This slide should prevent the common shortcut that locally constant means globally constant. A locally constant presheaf has isomorphic local fibers, but a loop in the nerve may return by a nontrivial automorphism. The cohomology on a circle is controlled by fixed vectors and cokernels of the monodromy minus identity.

Introduce sphere bundles as the same double-complex machine with a new geometric input. Local top cohomology classes of fibers must agree on overlaps before one can even ask for a global angular class. Orientability is the compatibility condition that lets the tic-tac-toe descent begin.

Euler Class As The Last Unclosed Edge

Obstruction View

The failed final Cech closure defines \(e(E)\in H^n(M)\).

If It Vanishes

An angular class can be adjusted to become closed.

If It Persists

The residual cocycle measures the impossibility of a global closed angular representative.

Geometric Meaning

Zeros of sections and local degrees become visible representatives of the same obstruction.

Euler class is a terminal obstruction in the same proof machineVanishing is a closed angular-form condition
Frame the Euler class as an obstruction, not just a named invariant. The attempted diagonal extension of local fiber-volume data nearly closes the board. The last horizontal Cech term that refuses to vanish is a cocycle on the base, and its de Rham image is the Euler class.
Measuring Euler

Local Degree Measures The Obstruction

Sphere Check

\(\langle e(TS^2),[S^2]\rangle = 2 = \chi(S^2)\).

Local Picture

Remove a small disk around the singularity and read the boundary map into the fiber circle.

Obstruction Picture

A nonzero local degree means no global nonvanishing tangent field exists.

Notebook Model

The boundary map \(z\mapsto z^2\) winds twice and records the Euler number.

Concrete representative: isolated zero of a tangent vector fieldEuler number equals local degree sum
Use the unit tangent bundle of the sphere as the concrete measuring device. A vector field with an isolated singularity has a boundary map into the fiber circle. In the notebook model that boundary map winds twice, so the Euler number is two, matching the Euler characteristic of the sphere.

Duality
Thom class pulled back

Poincare Duality As A Pullback Story

Pullback

\(s_0^*(\mathrm{Thom}(E))=e(E)\).

Thom Form

A compactly supported form near the zero section represents the fiber orientation class.

Poincare Dual

A transverse section's zero locus pairs with test classes as the Euler class.

Chapter Thread

Duality is again a local-to-global statement controlled by exactness and support.

Zero-section pullback returns Euler dataCompact support keeps the representative localized
Connect the compact-support construction to intersections. A Thom form lives near the zero section and represents the class dual to that section. Pulling it back by the zero section gives the Euler class, while pulling it against a transverse section records the zero locus as a Poincare dual cycle.
Proof Pattern
the chapter's recurring algorithm

The Tic-Tac-Toe Argument Template

1. Start

Place a cocycle in a corner or along an edge of the board.

2. Solve

Use exactness to fill the next square in one direction.

3. Alternate

Switch direction and apply the total-complex sign convention.

4. Stop

Landing on an edge proves a comparison or exposes an obstruction.

What Changes Between Applications

Rows, columns, coefficients, and support conditions change; the board logic stays recognizable.

Comparison theorem: tic-tac-toe proves isomorphismEuler class: tic-tac-toe detects failure to close
Summarize the proof method in operational language. Start with a cocycle in one corner, use exactness to solve one edge at a time, alternate directions with the total-complex sign convention, and watch where the process terminates. In easy cases it proves an isomorphism; in sphere-bundle cases it exposes an obstruction.
