Manifolds
charts are local, smoothness is checked on overlaps

Smooth Structure Is Compatible Local Data

Atlas compatibility
\(x_j\circ x_i^{-1}:x_i(U_i\cap U_j)\to x_j(U_i\cap U_j)\)

is smooth wherever two coordinate descriptions overlap.

Coordinate-independent smoothness

A function is smooth on \(M\) exactly when every local coordinate representative is smooth.

Why this matters

Tensor fields, maps, and later connections can be specified locally and checked globally.

Emphasize that a manifold is not merely a set with coordinates. The important condition is compatibility on overlaps, because that lets local calculations glue into globally meaningful smooth functions, vector fields, tensors, and later partitions of unity.

Tangent Spaces
arrows are recorded by what they do to functions

Tangent Vectors Are Derivations

Definition

A tangent vector at \(p\) is a linear map \(D:C^\infty(M)\to \mathbb{R}\) satisfying

\(D(fg)=f(p)Dg+g(p)Df.\)

In coordinates, \(D=\sum_i a^i \partial_i|_p\).

Frame tangent vectors as operators before drawing arrows. The derivation viewpoint explains why the same vector can be recognized in every chart: it differentiates smooth functions and obeys the product rule, while coordinates only provide one basis for recording it.
Tensor Algebra
exterior calculus prepares invariant formulas

Forms And Pullbacks Preserve Alternation

Exterior derivative
\(d(df)=0,\qquad d(\alpha\wedge\beta)=d\alpha\wedge\beta+(-1)^k\alpha\wedge d\beta.\)
Pullback
\((F^\ast \omega)_p(v_1,\ldots,v_k)=\omega_{F(p)}(dF_pv_1,\ldots,dF_pv_k).\)
Cartan identity
\(L_X\alpha=i_Xd\alpha+d(i_X\alpha)\)
The point of this slide is algebraic hygiene. Forms measure oriented infinitesimal content, exterior differentiation records antisymmetric change, and pullback moves forms backward through a map so integration and orientation can be discussed on the source.
Smooth Maps
global deformation, local linear evidence

Mappings Are Measured By Their Differentials

Differential
\(dF_p:T_pM\to T_{F(p)}N\)

is the best linear approximation of \(F\) at \(p\).

Regularity evidence

Stable rank decides whether a map locally behaves like a projection, inclusion, or diffeomorphism.

Jacobian sign

For equal dimensions, the determinant records local area scaling and orientation.

Make the differential the central inspection tool. A smooth map can bend a grid globally, but at one point it is read by a linear map on tangent spaces. Rank, kernel, image, and Jacobian sign are the local evidence for regularity.

Affine Connections
comparison across tangent spaces is extra structure

A Connection Chooses How To Differentiate

Covariant derivative
\(\nabla_XY\)

is the derivative of \(Y\) along \(X\) after a rule for comparing nearby tangent spaces has been chosen.

Torsion
\(T(X,Y)=\nabla_XY-\nabla_YX-[X,Y]\)
Metric rule
\(Xg(Y,Z)=g(\nabla_XY,Z)+g(Y,\nabla_XZ)\)
Levi-Civita

unique torsion-free, metric-compatible connection.

Stress that ordinary derivatives of vector fields subtract vectors living in different tangent spaces. A connection supplies the comparison rule. Torsion and metric compatibility are then extra properties of that rule, with the Levi-Civita connection occupying the Riemannian sweet spot.
Parallel Transport
zero covariant derivative along a curve

Parallelism And Geodesics Come From One Rule

Parallel field
\( \nabla_{\dot\gamma}V=0 \)

The vector field changes only as the connection requires.

Geodesic
\( \nabla_{\dot\gamma}\dot\gamma=0 \)

The curve transports its own velocity.

Coordinate form
\( \ddot x^k+\Gamma^k_{ij}\dot x^i\dot x^j=0 \)
Parallel transport and geodesics should be presented as two uses of the same covariant derivative. A transported vector has zero derivative along the curve, while a geodesic transports its own velocity. The equation is local, but the geometric meaning is intrinsic.
Exponential Map
initial velocity becomes endpoint

The Exponential Map Packages Geodesic Initial Data

Definition
\( \exp_p(v)=\gamma_v(1) \)

where \( \gamma_v(0)=p \) and \( \dot\gamma_v(0)=v \).

Near \(0\in T_pM\), this gives normal coordinates and turns radial lines into geodesics.

The local question is whether \(\exp_p\) is a diffeomorphism near zero; the global question is how curvature changes this behavior.
Describe the exponential map as a controlled conversion from tangent vectors to nearby manifold points. Near zero it gives normal coordinates, and within a convex normal neighborhood it makes short geodesics unique. Later curvature controls how far this picture remains reliable.
Structural Equations
coframes convert geometry into differential forms

Moving Frames Expose Torsion And Curvature

First structural equation
\(d\omega^i=-\omega^i_j\wedge\omega^j+\Omega^i\)

torsion appears as the obstruction to symmetric soldering.

Second structural equation
\(d\omega^i_j=-\omega^i_k\wedge\omega^k_j+\Omega^i_j\)

curvature is the obstruction inside the connection forms.

This is where the chapter's tensor notation becomes compact. A coframe and connection forms encode differentiation without permanent coordinates. Cartan's first structural equation records torsion, and the second records curvature as the failure of connection forms to close flatly.
Riemann Curvature
infinitesimal holonomy around a small loop

Curvature Measures Failed Return

Curvature operator
\(R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-\nabla_Y\nabla_XZ-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z.\)

It measures the noncommutativity of covariant differentiation.

Sectional curvature is the curvature seen by one two-plane; isometry invariants are built from how these measurements transform.
Use the loop diagram to give curvature a tactile meaning. If a vector is parallel transported around a tiny loop and fails to return exactly to itself, the leading error is governed by the curvature tensor on the two-plane spanned by the loop directions.
Comparison Sign Check
radial Jacobi factors and disk areas

Constant Curvature Controls Radial Spread

Radial factor
\(J_K(r)=S_K(r)/r\)

where \(S_K(r)=\sin(\sqrt K r)/\sqrt K\), \(r\), or \(\sinh(\sqrt{-K}r)/\sqrt{-K}\).

Positive

short circles and disks are smaller than Euclidean comparison.

Flat

radial separation grows linearly with radius.

Negative

radial separation and disk area expand faster than Euclidean comparison.

The comparison formulas give students a quantitative sign check. Positive curvature squeezes radial circles and small disk area compared with Euclidean space, while negative curvature expands them. This is the computational bridge to the notebook's curvature-sign lab.
