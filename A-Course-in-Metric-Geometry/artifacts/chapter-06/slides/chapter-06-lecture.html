Metric In Coordinates

The Matrix \(g\) Tells The Coordinate Grid How To Measure

Local Formula

\(ds^2 = \sum g_{ij}(x)\,dx^i dx^j\)

Coordinates label points; the metric decides lengths and angles of coordinate directions.

Emphasize that the coordinate formula is not the geometry itself. The matrix g records how the coordinate grid should be measured at each point. The conformal model is useful because one scalar factor changes lengths while keeping angles visually recognizable.
Christoffel Correction

Ordinary Derivatives Forget That The Basis Moves

Conformal Pattern

\(\Gamma^x_{xx}=\phi_x,\quad \Gamma^x_{xy}=\phi_y,\quad \Gamma^x_{yy}=-\phi_x\)

\(\Gamma^y_{xx}=-\phi_y,\quad \Gamma^y_{xy}=\phi_x,\quad \Gamma^y_{yy}=\phi_y\)

all Christoffel residuals = 0
Source orientation: coordinate correction terms in Section 6.1Check: curvature-symbolic-checks.json
The main conceptual point is that ordinary componentwise differentiation sees the moving coordinate frame as fixed. Christoffel symbols record how the frame changes. In the conformal test, every listed residual vanishes, so the symbolic computation agrees with the theoretical connection formula.

Covariant Derivative

The Levi-Civita Derivative Is The Product-Rule-Preserving Replacement

Coordinate Rule

\((\nabla_i V)^k=\partial_i V^k+\Gamma^k_{ij}V^j\)

One term differentiates components. The other differentiates the frame implicitly carried by the coordinates.

Levi-Civita Conditions

  • Linear over functions in the lower slot.
  • Satisfies the Leibniz rule in the vector slot.
  • Compatible with the metric: \(\nabla g=0\).
  • Torsion-free: \(\nabla_XY-\nabla_YX=[X,Y]\).
Source orientation: Section 6.2 Covariant DerivativeCoverage: symbolic conformal pattern checked
Explain the covariant derivative as the replacement for naive differentiation that respects the metric and the product rule. It is not an extra structure being chosen arbitrarily here; the Levi-Civita connection is the unique torsion-free metric-compatible correction.
Geodesic Versus Normal Curvature

One Curve, Two Measurements Of Turning

Intrinsic / Extrinsic Split

Geodesic curvature is the part of acceleration tangent to the surface.

Normal curvature is the part seen by the ambient normal.

A surface geodesic has zero geodesic curvature even when it bends in space.

Source orientation: Section 6.3 Geodesic and Gaussian CurvaturesArtifact support: curvature-coordinate-and-wavefronts.png
Separate intrinsic and extrinsic language carefully. Geodesic curvature is the tangential failure of a curve to be a surface geodesic. Normal curvature is what an ambient observer sees in the surface normal direction. The same drawn curve can carry both measurements.
Distance Circles And \(S_k(r)\)

The Function \(S_k\) Measures Polar Spreading

\(k=1\)

\(S_1(r)=\sin r\)

spreading slows and reconverges.

\(k=0\)

\(S_0(r)=r\)

Euclidean linear spreading.

\(k=-1\)

\(S_{-1}(r)=\sinh r\)

spreading accelerates outward.

Polar model metric: \(ds^2=dr^2+S_k(r)^2\,d\theta^2\), with \(S_k''+kS_k=0\).

Gaussian Curvature As Metric Distortion

Curvature Appears In The First Non-Flat Terms

Normal-Coordinate Reading

Small circles and nearby radial geodesics reveal whether the metric behaves above or below the Euclidean model.

positive \(K\): geodesics focus
negative \(K\): geodesics spread

Source orientation: Section 6.4 Geometric Meaning of Gaussian CurvatureCheck: \(S_k''+kS_k=0\)
The local meaning of Gaussian curvature appears in how small metric circles and normal-coordinate distances deviate from flat expectations. Positive curvature makes nearby radial geodesics reconverge; negative curvature makes them separate faster. This is the bridge from formulas to comparison.
Jacobi / Rauch Roadmap

Curvature Bounds Order Variations Of Geodesics

Jacobi Equation

\(J'' + R(J,\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma = 0\)

A Jacobi field differentiates a family of geodesics, so its norm measures infinitesimal separation.

Rauch Comparison

  • Upper curvature bounds allow more spreading.
  • Lower curvature bounds restrict spreading.
  • Normal-coordinate triangles inherit the ordering.
Source orientation: Section 6.5 Comparison TheoremsValidation: model functions solve checked Jacobi equations
Present Jacobi fields as derivatives of a family of geodesics, not as mysterious vector fields. Rauch comparison says curvature bounds order the growth of these fields. That statement is the engine behind the later local and global triangle comparison results.

Break-A-Hypothesis Lab

Which Assumption Makes The Conclusion Fail?

Remove One Condition

  • Metric compatibility: lengths drift under transport.
  • Torsion-free connection: geodesic equations no longer match the Levi-Civita model.
  • Curvature bound: Jacobi comparison loses its ordering.
  • Completeness: global comparison can stop at the edge of the space.

Instructor Prompt

Given a claimed triangle comparison, ask which local calculation certifies it and which global condition allows it to persist.

formula check + hypothesis check = theorem check

This lab-style slide asks students to diagnose which assumption has been removed. If smoothness, metric compatibility, curvature bounds, or completeness disappears, the comparison conclusion can fail or become meaningless. The goal is to make theorem hypotheses feel operational.
