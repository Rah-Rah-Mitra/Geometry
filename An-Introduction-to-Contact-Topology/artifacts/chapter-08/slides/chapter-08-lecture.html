The Existence Theorem

Paraphrased Theorem 8.0.6
Theorem Pattern

For a closed simply connected five-manifold, the obstruction \(W_3(M)\) is the gate to almost-contact data; once the gate is open, contact surgery realizes the needed topological pieces.

Lecture Reading

The proof is a coordinated bookkeeping problem: choose \(c_1\), split the manifold, and tune framings so the contact boundary has the prescribed almost-contact class.

Key issue: existence is topological before it becomes geometric. Chapter 8 route map

Dimension Five Contact Condition

The Local Test Still Has One Line

Positive coorientation
Definition

A positive cooriented contact structure on \(M^5\) is \(\xi=\ker\alpha\) with

\(\alpha\wedge(d\alpha)^2>0.\)

What This Encodes

  • \(\xi\) is a rank-four bundle.
  • \(d\alpha|_{\xi}\) is symplectic at every point.
  • The orientation is fixed by the top-degree form.

Every global theorem below must first produce formal data compatible with this local nondegeneracy condition.

Formula: \(\xi=\ker\alpha\), \(\alpha\wedge(d\alpha)^2>0\) Local model before classification
Use this slide to re-anchor the analytic definition. In dimension five, d alpha restricts to a symplectic form on a four-plane distribution, and the Reeb line supplies the missing direction. The wedge condition is not decoration: it is the local nondegeneracy test that every later surgery model must preserve near the handles.
Warning

The \(W_3\) Gate Comes First

Almost contact reduction
Gate

The obstruction to an almost-contact structure is

\(W_3(M)=\beta w_2(M)\in H^3(M;\mathbb Z).\)

Reduction

Almost contact means the tangent bundle structure group reduces as

\(SO(5)\longrightarrow U(2)\times 1.\)

Equivalently, \(w_2(M)\) must be the mod-two reduction of some integral class.

No \(W_3=0\), no almost-contact starting point. Obstruction theory gate
Pause on the warning because it prevents a common overstatement. A stable or formal-looking plane field is not enough; the tangent bundle must reduce from SO(5) to U(2) times a line. The obstruction is the integral Bockstein of w2, so the first question is whether w2 has an integral lift.
Bockstein And Parity

The Ladder Behind \(W_3=\beta w_2\)

Integral lift test
Exact Sequence

\(\mathbb Z \xrightarrow{\times 2}\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\rho_2}\mathbb Z/2\)

Interpretation

\(\beta w_2=0\) exactly when \(w_2\) has an integral lift \(c\). Any two lifts differ by \(2u\).

This is why \(c_1\) can vary by even classes while its reduction mod two stays fixed.

Parity controls both almost-contact classes and later framings. Bockstein ledger
Walk through the exact-sequence ladder slowly. Reduction mod two sends an integral class c to w2, and the Bockstein measures the failure of such a lift to exist. When beta of w2 vanishes, the possible lifts form an affine family shifted by twice integral cohomology classes, which foreshadows the c1 classification.

The Fiber

Why \(F_5\) Is Manageable

Obstruction theory collapses
Fiber

\(F_5=SO(5)/U(2)\cong SO(6)/U(3)\cong\mathbb{CP}^3.\)

Consequence In Dimension Five

  • The relevant obstruction-theory information is degree-two information.
  • Degree two is exactly where \(c_1\) lives.
  • This is why the previous slide is more than notation.
Mental Model

Almost-contact choices are maps into a manageable homogeneous fiber; \(c_1\) is the coordinate students should keep in view.

\(\mathbb{CP}^3\) keeps the five-dimensional obstruction story low-dimensional. Fiber calculation
Explain why obstruction theory does not explode in dimension five. The fiber SO(5) over U(2) is CP3, equivalently SO(6) over U(3). Up to the dimension of the manifold, its relevant homotopy is concentrated in degree two, so the obstruction-theory story collapses to a first Chern class calculation.
Stable Almost-Complex Twist

The Factor Of Two Keeps Returning

Twist formula
Stable Twist

\(c_1(\eta_u)=c_1(\eta_0)+2u.\)

Why It Matters

  • The \(w_2\) shadow is unchanged.
  • The integral class is still adjustable.
  • Surgery framings will implement this arithmetic geometrically.
Proof Memory

The coefficient-two shift is the same parity constraint seen in the Bockstein sequence.

Twisting adjusts \(c_1\), but never its parity. Stable almost-complex bookkeeping
Use the formula as the algebraic hinge between formal data and surgery choices. Once a base stable complex structure is chosen, twisting by u changes c1 by twice u. That factor of two is the same parity phenomenon from the Bockstein ladder, and it will reappear when a framing integer is read by contact surgery.

Parity distinguishes them
Classification Anchor

Framing Warning

Pictures of base and fiber look similar. The distinguishing datum lives in the parity of the clutching or surgery framing.

Contact Reading

Contact surgery must not merely create \(H_2\); it must create the correct parity type.

Framing parity is the bridge between handle data and bundle type. Free pieces
This slide turns a classification fact into a memory device. There are two oriented S3-bundles over S2 in the simply connected list: the product and the twisted bundle. They have the same visible base-and-fiber shape, so the distinguishing datum is a parity signal carried by the framing or by the associated w2 behavior.
Structure Theorem Overview

Read \(H_2\) As A Routing Table

Simply connected \(5\)-manifolds
Decomposition Language

\(w_2=0:\ M\cong \#^r(S^2\times S^3)\#M_t.\)

\(w_2\ne0:\ M\cong \#^{r-1}(S^2\times S^3)\#X_\infty\#M_t.\)

What The Contact Proof Must Do

  • Realize the free summands.
  • Route the nonzero \(w_2\) case through \(X_\infty\).
  • Use \(M_t\) with \(H_2(M_t)\cong G\) and \(w_2(M_t)=0\).
The classification theorem supplies the list of blocks; surgery supplies contact representatives. Structure theorem overview
Give students the large-scale decomposition before discussing individual constructions. A simply connected five-manifold with W3 equal to zero has two cases: if w2 vanishes, the free part is copies of S2 times S3; if w2 is nonzero, one free summand is replaced by X_infty. The torsion block M_t has H2 equal to G, w2 zero, and is obtained smoothly from S5 by surgery on a link of two-spheres.

Torsion pieces are finite blocks, not a different obstruction theory. \(M_t\) family

Framing Parity Lab

An Integer Framing Has A Mod-Two Shadow

\(\pi_1(U(1))\cong\mathbb Z\)
Lab Rule

A framing integer in \(\pi_1(U(1))\cong\mathbb Z\) contributes to Euler-class and contact \(c_1\) bookkeeping.

Even

Same mod-two type as the product route.

Odd

Switches the parity signal to the twisted route.

Do Not Forget

The integer is richer than its parity, but the two \(S^3\)-bundle types see the parity first.

Framing parity is the practical calculation behind the two bundle outcomes. Parity laboratory
Make the integer framing feel concrete. The framing group pi1 of U(1) is Z, so rotating once changes an integer, but the bundle type only sees its parity while c1 changes by even increments. This is the short laboratory calculation that explains why even and odd framings route to the two S3-bundles over S2.
