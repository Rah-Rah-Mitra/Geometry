Existence Move

Martinet Construction: Make The Boundary Match

solid torus model
Martinet Existence Anchor

Every closed orientable 3-manifold admits a contact structure.

Local Torus Form

$$\alpha = h_1(r)\,d\theta + h_2(r)\,d\phi$$

The radial curve \((h_1,h_2)\) encodes the contact planes on nested tori.

Lecture Point

Global existence is achieved by reducing the gluing problem to controlled boundary characteristic data.

Construction side of the chapter Local contact inequality becomes a gluing device
Describe Martinet's construction as a global assembly theorem. A contact form on a solid torus is controlled by the curve r maps to h one of r and h two of r, while boundary data is tuned so that pieces can be glued. The point of this slide is the design pattern: a local contact inequality is converted into a boundary-matching tool for a closed three-manifold.

The first difference between plane fields is visible in Euler classes.

Regular Values

Preimage links record how the plane field winds; \(d_3\) is defined after \(d_2=0\) and a 2-skeleton homotopy has been chosen, so it may depend on that choice.

Why It Matters

Plane-field data is the flexible classification coordinate Contactness adds nonintegrability on top of homotopy
The chapter does not treat contact structures as isolated forms. A cooriented plane field gives a Gauss map into the two-sphere, and regular values have preimage links carrying obstruction data. The Euler-class relation gives the first comparison between plane fields. The later Lutz move is powerful because it can alter contact geometry while keeping deliberate control over these homotopy coordinates.

Full Twist Effect

The Main Fork

Tight Versus Overtwisted

flexible vs rigid

Overtwisted Side

Tight Side

Chapter Split

Overtwisted contact structures are flexible; tight contact structures are rigid enough for numerical bounds and classification theorems.

This distinction drives the remaining surface theory Same local contact condition, different global behavior
Separate the two worlds before introducing the surface technology. Overtwisted means that a particular disk appears, and once it appears, flexible classification takes over. Tight means no such disk is present, which makes the theory rigid enough to support inequalities and uniqueness theorems. This is the chapter's main fork: the same local contact condition has two very different global behaviors.

The elimination lemma is a surface-cleaning move. Overtwisted Classification: Plane Fields Become Contact Classes

weak homotopy equivalence
Classification Anchor

$$i_\Delta:\Xi_{\mathrm{ot}}(M,\Delta)\to \mathrm{Distr}(M,\Delta)$$

is a weak homotopy equivalence.

Instructor Translation

Once the overtwisted disk is fixed, homotopy of plane fields is enough to classify up to contact isotopy in the flexible world.

Flexible side: contact classification follows plane-field homotopy This is the theorem that full Lutz twists prepare
This is the main flexible classification anchor. Fix an overtwisted disk Delta and compare the space of overtwisted contact structures with the space of plane fields. The inclusion is a weak homotopy equivalence, so plane-field homotopy data controls contact isotopy after the overtwisted witness is present. Contrast this with tight classification, where the same plane-field information is not enough.
Tight-Side Reconstruction

Tomography: Read \(S^2\times[-1,1]\) By Convex Slices

boundary foliations matter
Tomography Principle

Study a tight structure by a one-parameter family of convex surfaces and the changes in their dividing sets.

Boundary Data

For \(S^2\times[-1,1]\), boundary characteristic foliations and compatible transitions can determine the tight structure.

Contrast

On the tight side, classification is reconstructed from controlled surface slices, not only from plane-field homotopy.

Tomography turns 3-dimensional uniqueness into a controlled movie Dividing sets are the frames of the movie
Tomography is the tight-side habit of studying a three-manifold by slicing it with convex surfaces. In a product S two times an interval, one watches how dividing sets and boundary foliations evolve across the slices. The strong message is that tight structures can be forced by boundary data and controlled transitions, which is very different from overtwisted flexibility.
Classification Consequences

Tight Classification And Prime Decomposition

unique tight anchors
Tight Classification Anchors

Unique positive cooriented tight structures up to isotopy on \(S^3\), \(\mathbb{R}^3\), and \(S^2\times S^1\); unique \(D^3\) relative to its boundary foliation.

Prime Decomposition Role

Connected-sum behavior lets tight classification results pass through prime pieces, with convex spheres doing the separating work.

Final Takeaway

Construction gives abundance, dividing sets compress surface data, and tightness decides when numerical and uniqueness statements survive.
