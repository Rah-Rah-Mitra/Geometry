Aligning configurations and the geometry of shape

Landmark Configurations

Configuration matrix

$k$ landmarks in $m$ dimensions stacked as a $k\times m$ matrix $X$.

Nuisance group

Translation, scale, and rotation carry no shape information.

Same shape

$X$ and $Y$ share a shape when one maps to the other under those three.

Shape equivalence

$Y = \beta\,(X - \mathbf{1}_k t^{\top})\,Q$

Goal

Quotient out $\beta>0$, $t\in\mathbb{R}^m$, and $Q\in O(m)$ to isolate shape.

From points to shapeLandmark Configurations
A configuration is a matrix of labelled landmark coordinates. Two such matrices describe the same shape when one becomes the other after rotation, translation, and rescaling. Our task is to quotient out exactly those three nuisance transformations so that the residual difference measures genuine shape, not pose or size.

The Orthogonal Procrustes Problem

Objective

Rotate $B$ onto $A$ with least squared Frobenius error.

Constraint

$Q$ lives on the orthogonal group $O(m)$, so $Q^{\top}Q = I$.

Problem

$\min_{Q^{\top}Q=I}\; \lVert A - BQ \rVert_F^{2}$

Reading

A constrained least squares fit; the constraint forbids stretching, only turning.

Constrained least squares on $O(m)$The Orthogonal Procrustes Problem
Here is the central optimization. Holding both configurations fixed, we seek the orthogonal matrix that rotates B onto A with least squared error. Because the rotation is constrained to the orthogonal group, this is a constrained least squares problem whose closed form solution is the payoff of the next slide.
Statistics on Special ManifoldsChapter 09

Solving by the SVD of $B^{\top}A$

Reduce to a trace

$\lVert A-BQ\rVert_F^{2} = c - 2\,\mathrm{tr}\!\big(Q^{\top}B^{\top}A\big)$

Optimal rotation

$B^{\top}A = U\Sigma V^{\top}\;\Rightarrow\; \hat{Q} = U V^{\top}$

Trace maximization gives a closed formSVD Solution
Expand the Frobenius objective; the only term that depends on the rotation is the trace of Q transpose times B transpose A. Maximizing that trace over orthogonal matrices is solved by the singular value decomposition of B transpose A. The optimal rotation is simply U times V transpose, a remarkably clean result.

Step 1: Remove Translation

Center each cloud

Subtract the centroid so both configurations share the origin.

Centering matrix

$C = I_k - \tfrac{1}{k}\mathbf{1}_k\mathbf{1}_k^{\top}$ projects out the mean.

Centered configuration

$X_c = C X,\qquad \mathbf{1}_k^{\top}X_c = 0$

Consequence

After centering, the optimal translation is exactly zero.

Translation decouples firstRemoving Translation
Translation is removed first because it decouples cleanly from rotation and scale. Centering each configuration at its centroid, equivalently multiplying by the centering matrix, sends both clouds to a common origin. After centering, the optimal translation is automatically zero, so the remaining work concerns only rotation and scale.

Step 2: Remove Scale

Centroid size

The Frobenius norm $\lVert X_c\rVert_F$ of the centered cloud measures size.

Normalize

Divide by centroid size to land on the pre-shape sphere.

Pre-shape

$Z = \dfrac{X_c}{\lVert X_c\rVert_F},\qquad \lVert Z\rVert_F = 1$

Remaining freedom

Only rotation now separates two pre-shapes.

Scale normalized by centroid sizeRemoving Scale
With configurations centered, size is captured by the centroid size, the Frobenius norm of the centered matrix. Dividing by centroid size places every configuration on the unit pre-shape sphere. Scale is now normalized, and only rotation separates two pre-shapes, which is exactly the orthogonal Procrustes problem we already solved.

Ordinary Procrustes Analysis

One onto one

Match a single configuration to a fixed target.

Recipe

Center, scale, then rotate by the SVD of the cross product.

Optimal scale

$\hat{\beta} = \dfrac{\sum_i \sigma_i}{\lVert B_c\rVert_F^{2}}$ from the singular values.

Best matched copy

$\hat{B} = \hat{\beta}\,B_c\,\hat{Q},\quad \hat{Q}=UV^{\top}$

Residual

$\lVert A_c - \hat{B}\rVert_F$ is the ordinary Procrustes residual.

Center, scale, rotateOrdinary Procrustes Analysis
Ordinary Procrustes analysis aligns one configuration onto a fixed target by composing the three steps: center, scale, then rotate by the singular value decomposition. The optimal scaling factor is the ratio of the relevant singular value sum to the squared norm. The result is the best matched copy and its residual.

Generalized Procrustes Analysis

Many configurations

No single target is privileged when aligning a whole sample.

Iterate

Align all to the current mean, recompute the mean, repeat.

Iterate to a consensusGeneralized Procrustes Analysis
When many configurations must be aligned simultaneously, no single target is privileged. Generalized Procrustes analysis iterates: align every configuration to the current mean, recompute the mean, and repeat until the mean stabilizes. This alternating scheme decreases the total residual sum of squares monotonically and converges to a consensus shape.

The Pre-Shape Sphere

After centering

Configurations live in a $(k-1)m$-dimensional subspace.

After scaling

Unit norm confines them to a sphere in that subspace.

Still left

Rotation, whose orbits are the fibers over each shape.

Sphere modulo rotationThe Pre-Shape Sphere
Centering removes translation and leaves a subspace; normalizing by centroid size lands us on a sphere within that subspace, the pre-shape sphere. Points still differ by rotation, so shape is the quotient of this sphere by the rotation group. The fibers of that quotient are the rotation orbits.

Kendall's Shape Space

Quotient

Shape space is the pre-shape sphere modulo the rotation group.

A point is an orbit

Each shape gathers all rotated pre-shapes into one class.

Shape space

$\Sigma_m^k = S^{\,(k-1)m-1} \,/\, SO(m)$

Geometry

The quotient inherits a Riemannian metric; shape space is a curved manifold.

Pre-shapes modulo rotationKendall's Shape Space
Kendall's shape space is the pre-shape sphere modulo rotation. Each shape is an orbit, a whole circle or higher dimensional fiber of rotated pre-shapes collapsed to one point. This quotient carries a natural Riemannian metric, making shape space a curved manifold rather than a flat Euclidean parameter set.

Procrustes Distance

Geodesic separation

The distance between two shapes on Kendall's space.

From singular values

It is the angle set by the singular values of the cross product.

Full vs partial

Full uses optimal scaling; partial fixes unit size.

Procrustes distance

$\rho(Z_1,Z_2) = \arccos\!\Big(\textstyle\sum_i \sigma_i\Big)$

Meaning

Residual shape difference after optimal alignment.

Angle between aligned pre-shapesProcrustes Distance
Procrustes distance measures the geodesic separation of two shapes on Kendall's space. Operationally it is the angle between optimally aligned pre-shapes, computed from the singular values of the cross product matrix. Full Procrustes distance uses optimal scaling; partial Procrustes uses fixed unit size. Both quantify residual shape difference after alignment.
