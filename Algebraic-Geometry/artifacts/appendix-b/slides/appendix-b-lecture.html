Local Analytic Spaces From Equations

Polynomial equations over \( \mathbb C \) become holomorphic equations, but the sheaf remembers quotients, nilpotents, and gluing.

Local Model

For an affine presentation \(A=\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(f_1,\ldots,f_q)\), the associated analytic space uses the same equations as holomorphic functions.

Warning

The Analytification Functor

Functoriality sends algebraic objects to analytic ones, while comparison theorems decide how much information comes back.

\(X \longmapsto X^h,\qquad \mathcal F \longmapsto \mathcal F^h\)
Functoriality

A morphism \(f:X\to Y\) induces a holomorphic map \(f^h:X^h\to Y^h\). Coherent algebraic sheaves acquire coherent analytic sheaves.

Interpretation

The functor supplies analytic shadows. Equivalence, full faithfulness, and cohomology comparison are additional claims.

GAGA In The Projective Case

For projective \(X/\mathbb C\), coherent algebraic sheaves and coherent analytic sheaves carry the same information.

GAGA Comparison

\(\operatorname{Coh}(X)\simeq \operatorname{Coh}(X^h)\), and for every coherent \(\mathcal F\), \(H^i(X,\mathcal F)\simeq H^i(X^h,\mathcal F^h)\).

1. Projective Space

Compute and compare cohomology on \(\mathbb P^n_\mathbb C\).

2. Resolutions

Resolve coherent sheaves using sums of twists.

3. Closed \(X\)

Transfer the comparison from \(\mathbb P^n\) to \(X\).

Load-Bearing Hypothesis

Projectivity supplies the finite cohomological control that makes the analytic category remember the algebraic one.

Grounded in section 2, printed pp. 440-441 / PDF pp. 455-456. No converse is asserted without the stated hypotheses.
State GAGA as a projective theorem and then give the proof pipeline at the level of strategy. Projective space cohomology is computed first, resolutions transfer the result to coherent sheaves, and closed subvarieties inherit the comparison. Repeat that projectivity is doing real work here.
Chow Algebraization
Compact analytic subspaces inside projective space

Chow's Projective Algebraization

In complex projective space, compact analytic geometry is forced to acquire homogeneous algebraic equations.

Chow's Theorem

Every compact analytic subspace of \(\mathbb P^n_\mathbb C\) is algebraic.

This is a reverse bridge: analytic data inside a projective ambient space is not merely comparable to algebraic data; it is algebraizable.

Section 3
When is a compact complex manifold algebraic?

Compact Complex Manifolds

In dimension one the story is special; in higher dimension the names track genuinely different hypotheses.

Taxonomy To Keep Separate
compact complex algebraic projective Moishezon
  • Projective algebraic manifolds are compact complex manifolds.
  • Moishezon means enough meromorphic functions.
  • Kahler and Hodge conditions add differential and integral constraints.
Meromorphic Function Test

\(\operatorname{trdeg}_{\mathbb C} K(X)\leq \dim X\). A compact complex manifold is Moishezon when equality holds.

Dimension One

Compact Riemann surfaces are algebraic. The appendix then warns that higher-dimensional compact complex manifolds need stronger tests.

Use this taxonomy slide to slow down vocabulary. Compact, analytic, algebraic, projective, and Moishezon are not synonyms in higher dimension. The inequality for the field of meromorphic functions gives a numerical test, and dimension one is flagged as special rather than representative of all dimensions.
Riemann Existence
Finite covers compare algebraic and analytic topology

Finite Covers And Riemann Existence

For normal finite-type \(X/\mathbb C\), a normal finite analytic cover of \(X^h\) is algebraized by a finite morphism over \(X\).

Comparison Principle

Finite algebraic covers of normal finite-type \(X\) and normal finite analytic covers of \(X^h\) match under analytification.

This comparison is one route to the algebraic fundamental group: finite covers become a category whose analytic and algebraic descriptions agree.
Section 3 context: compact complex manifolds and algebraicity criteria. Finite covers connect the analytic topology to algebraic covers.

Moishezon Failure Modes
