Section 1: Sheaves

Sheaves Glue Local Data

Notebook artifact: compatible restrictions on an overlap have one glued section.

Definition
Sheaf Axiom

If \(U=\bigcup U_i\) and sections \(s_i\in F(U_i)\) agree on \(U_i\cap U_j\), then there is a unique \(s\in F(U)\) restricting to every \(s_i\).

Use In Schemes

Regular functions, modules, invertible sheaves, and differentials are all controlled by restriction maps and overlap compatibility.

Invariant: compatible restrictions glue uniquelyArtifact: sheaf-gluing-compatible-sections.png
Treat the notebook image as a gluing board rather than a topological picture. The key teaching point is that local sections are not independent pieces: after restriction to overlaps they must agree, and the sheaf axiom says the compatible family has a unique glued section.
Section 1: Local Tests

Stalks Are Local Tests

\(F_x=\varinjlim_{x\in U}F(U)\)
Stalkwise Test

A morphism \(\varphi:F\to G\) of sheaves is an isomorphism exactly when every induced map \(\varphi_x:F_x\to G_x\) is an isomorphism.

Reading Habit

Translate a global sheaf claim into a germ-by-germ local check, then glue the answer back.

Original asset: stalk-local-test.svgMechanism: germs at a point
A stalk is the algebraic microscope at a point. Explain that germs forget behavior away from the point, then stress the practical theorem: many sheaf questions can be checked stalk by stalk, especially whether a morphism of sheaves is an isomorphism.
Section 2: Schemes

Spec A: Prime Ideals As Points

Finite teaching model: arrows run from generic information toward closed information.

Affine Scheme

\(\operatorname{Spec} A\) is the space of prime ideals of \(A\), equipped with the Zariski topology and the structure sheaf.

Specialization

For primes \(\mathfrak p\subseteq \mathfrak q\), the point \(\mathfrak q\) lies in the closure of \(\mathfrak p\). Generic points specialize to more closed points.

Why It Matters

Irreducible components are represented by actual points, not only by subsets named from outside the space.

Invariant: prime inclusion reverses geometric specializationArtifact: spec-specialization-poset.png
Read the specialization poset from generic primes toward closed primes. The finite model is not a full spectrum, but it makes the reversal visible: inclusion of prime ideals becomes geometric specialization, and irreducible components acquire generic points.
Section 2: Affine Open Bookkeeping

Principal Opens Localize Rings

Principal Open

\(D(f)=\{\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}A:f\notin\mathfrak p\}\) is the affine open where \(f\) becomes invertible.

\(D(f)\cap D(g)=D(fg)\)
\(\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}A}(D(f))=A_f\)

Restriction Is Localization

Passing from \(D(f)\) to \(D(fg)\) means inverting one more element. The topology and the algebra move together.

Notebook Lab

The interactive artifact tracks localized generators and compatibility on overlaps.

affine-open-gluing-lab.html
Artifact link: html/affine-open-gluing-lab.htmlCheck: restriction composition model
This is the local algebra engine of affine schemes. Principal opens are simultaneously topological neighborhoods and ring localizations, so intersections and restrictions can be computed with the formulas D(f) cap D(g) equals D(fg) and O(D(f)) equals A_f.
Section 2: Schemes Beyond One Chart

A Scheme Is Glued From Affines

Scheme

A scheme is a locally ringed space admitting an open cover by affine schemes \(\operatorname{Spec}A_i\).

Cocycle Condition

Overlap isomorphisms must agree on triple overlaps: changing charts along two routes gives the same identification.

Instructor Cue

Every later global object is checked chartwise, compared on overlaps, and then glued.

Original asset: affine-atlas-cocycle.svgGrammar: affine chart plus overlap agreement
Move from one affine chart to an atlas. The diagram emphasizes that a scheme is not just a union of affine pieces: the overlap identifications must agree on triple overlaps, which is the cocycle condition that makes the glued object independent of chart order.
Section 2: Affine Morphisms

Morphisms Reverse Rings

Function Pullback

A map \(X\to Y\) pulls regular functions on \(Y\) back to \(X\).

Prime Preimage

A prime \(\mathfrak q\subset B\) maps to \(\alpha^{-1}(\mathfrak q)\subset A\).

Affine Formula

\(\operatorname{Hom}(\operatorname{Spec}B,\operatorname{Spec}A)=\operatorname{Hom}(A,B)\).

Original asset: morphism-rings-reverse.svgContravariance is structural
Make contravariance concrete. A geometric morphism from Spec B to Spec A is encoded by pulling functions back from A to B, and the point map sends a prime of B to its preimage in A. This is the affine prototype for morphisms of schemes.
Section 3: First Properties

Reduced, Irreducible, Integral

Use the left panel as the warning: nilpotent thickness changes functions without adding ordinary points.

Reduced

No nonzero nilpotents in the structure sheaf. The point set may be unchanged while the functions change.

Irreducible

The underlying topological space has one generic component.

Integral

Integral \(=\) reduced \(+\) irreducible. On an affine chart, \(\operatorname{Spec}A\) is integral exactly when \(A\) is a domain.

Notebook check: epsilon squared is zero in dual numbersArtifact: nilpotent-formal-neighborhood-tower.png
Use the nilpotent image to separate point sets from function rings. Reduced removes nilpotents, irreducible asks for one generic component, and integral combines both; on an affine chart this corresponds to a domain condition. This is the first property checkpoint.
Section 3: Constructions

Subschemes, Products, Fibers

Closed Subscheme

An ideal \(I\subset A\) gives \(\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\to\operatorname{Spec}A\). The quotient records scheme structure, not just a subset.

Fiber Product

\(X\times_S Y\) is the universal scheme mapping to \(X\) and \(Y\) with equal composites to \(S\).

Fiber

The fiber over \(s\in S\) is \(X_s=X\times_S\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)\).

Original asset: subschemes-fibers.svgConstruction habit: quotient and pullback
The left half explains closed subschemes as quotient rings rather than simply subsets. The right half gives the pullback square habit: fibers and products are universal ways to compare families over a base. Keep the universal property in view.
Section 4: Separated Morphisms

Separatedness Is Diagonal Control

Criterion

A morphism \(f:X\to S\) is separated when the diagonal \(\Delta:X\to X\times_S X\) is a closed immersion.

Failure Mode

The affine line with doubled origin behaves as if a generic point has two incompatible special limits.

Meaning

Separatedness is the uniqueness part of a good limiting theory.

Original asset: diagonal-separatedness.svgDiagnostic: diagonal closed immersion
Separatedness is the scheme-theoretic version of uniqueness of limits. The doubled-origin picture shows what fails when two specializations are forced to share a generic part, while the closed diagonal criterion gives the clean structural test.
Section 4: Proper Morphisms

Properness Is Complete Lifting

Valuative Shape

For a DVR \(R\) with fraction field \(K\), a generic map \(\operatorname{Spec}K\to X\) over \(Y\) should extend across \(\operatorname{Spec}R\).

Existence Versus Uniqueness

Properness supplies existence of the lift. Separatedness supplies uniqueness of the lift.

Geometry

Think of proper morphisms as compact-like maps in algebraic geometry.

Original asset: valuative-criterion.svgLimit test over a DVR
The valuative criterion packages compactness-like behavior for schemes. Separatedness gives uniqueness of a lift, while properness adds existence: a map defined generically over a discrete valuation ring extends over the special point. The picture is a lifting test, not a computation.
Section 5: Sheaves Of Modules

Modules Become Sheaves

Tilde Construction

For an \(A\)-module \(M\), \(\widetilde M\) is the associated sheaf of \(\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}A}\)-modules.

\(\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}A,\widetilde M)=M\)
\(\Gamma(D(f),\widetilde M)=M_f\)
Locality

Quasi-coherent sheaves are modules glued with localization formulas.

Original asset: module-sheaf-restriction.svgAffine formula: global sections recover M
Explain quasi-coherent sheaves by starting with a familiar module. On Spec A, the tilde construction has global sections M and restricts to M_f on D(f), so the sheaf records the same algebra chart by chart.

Section 7: Projective Morphisms

Projective Morphisms From Sections

Construction

Sections \(s_0,\ldots,s_n\) of an invertible sheaf \(\mathcal L\) define \(x\mapsto [s_0(x):\cdots:s_n(x)]\) where not all sections vanish.

Projective Affine Chart

For a graded ring \(S\), \(D_+(f)=\operatorname{Spec}S_{(f)}\) gives the affine chart where the homogeneous element \(f\) is nonzero.

Base Point

If all \(s_i\) vanish at \(x\), there is no projective coordinate at \(x\).

Original asset: projective-sections-map.svgMap defined by sections, checked chartwise
Sections of an invertible sheaf become homogeneous coordinates for a map to projective space. The warning is just as important as the construction: if all chosen sections vanish at a point, the projective coordinate is undefined.
Section 8: Differentials

Differentials Are Universal Derivations

Universal Property

\(\operatorname{Der}_A(B,M)=\operatorname{Hom}_B(\Omega_{B/A},M)\). The module of differentials represents derivations.

Notebook Check

For \(f=y^2-x^3\), \(df=2y\,dy-3x^2\,dx\). At \((1,1)\), the relation is \(-3\,dx+2\,dy=0\).

Interpretation

Differentials translate infinitesimal motion into a sheaf-theoretic object.

Notebook sanity check: -3*dx + 2*dyOriginal asset: differentials-cusp.svg
Use the universal property as the definition that makes every derivation factor through differentials. The cusp calculation is a local sanity check: differentiating y squared minus x cubed gives the relation minus three dx plus two dy at the point (1,1).
Section 9: Formal Schemes

Formal Schemes Keep Every Thickening

The right panel becomes the main object: a tower of infinitesimal neighborhoods.

Completion Along Y

If \(Y\subset X\) is cut out by an ideal sheaf \(\mathcal I\), the formal completion remembers all thickenings \(Y_n\).

\(\widehat X_Y=\varprojlim Y_n\)
\(Y_n=(Y,\mathcal O_X/\mathcal I^{n+1})\)
Point Set Warning

The ordinary points can stay fixed while the function algebra keeps deeper nilpotent layers.

Artifact: nilpotent-formal-neighborhood-tower.pngInvariant: nilpotents vanish on points while remaining in rings
Return to the nilpotent tower and now read it as a limit object. A formal completion does not add new ordinary points; it records every infinitesimal neighborhood of the closed subscheme at once. The tower is the geometry.
