Smooth Maps Between Manifolds

Local condition

A map is smooth when its representatives in charts are infinitely differentiable.

Diffeomorphisms

Smooth bijections with smooth inverse are the isomorphisms of the whole theory.

Chart definition

$\psi \circ f \circ \varphi^{-1} \in C^{\infty}$

for charts $\varphi$ on $M$, $\psi$ on $N$.

Why review it

Only to fix notation and to set up the harder case of corners next.

Printed pp. 574-582Smooth Maps Between Manifolds
Recall the chart definition: a map is smooth when its coordinate representatives are infinitely differentiable. The appendix reviews this only to fix notation and to prepare the harder corner case. Emphasise that smoothness is a local, chart-checkable condition and that diffeomorphisms are the isomorphisms organising the whole theory.

Manifolds with Corners

Local model

$[0,\infty)^k \times \mathbb{R}^{n-k}$

Depth $k$ = number of vanishing coordinates.

Strata

Interior, codimension-one faces, codimension-two corners, and so on by depth.

Printed pp. 574-582Manifolds with Corners
The genuinely new object is a manifold with corners, modelled locally on a quadrant. Walk the index of strata: interior, codimension-one faces, codimension-two corners. The depth of a point counts how many coordinates vanish. This stratified picture is exactly what compactified moduli spaces of holomorphic curves carry.

Why Corners Arise

Products force them

The product of two manifolds with boundary has a corner along $\partial M \times \partial N$.

Boundary of a product

$\partial(M\times N)=(\partial M\times N)\cup(M\times\partial N)$

Moduli spaces

Compactified moduli of pseudoholomorphic curves degenerate by bubbling; the strata fit together as corners.

Takeaway

Corners are not pathology — they are the natural boundary structure of glued spaces.

Printed pp. 574-582Why Corners Arise
Corners are not pathology; they are forced. A product of two manifolds with boundary already has a corner along the product of the two boundaries. Moduli spaces of pseudoholomorphic curves degenerate by bubbling, and the strata fit together as corners. Motivate the abstraction by these two unavoidable sources.

The Standard Smooth Bump

Germ

$\beta(x)=\begin{cases}e^{-1/x}, & x>0\\[2pt] 0, & x\le 0\end{cases}$

Smooth, not analytic

Flat to infinite order at $0$: every derivative vanishes, so no Taylor series can recover it.

Printed pp. 574-582The Standard Smooth Bump
Everything constructive flows from one function: exp(-1/x) on the positive axis, zero elsewhere. It is smooth but not analytic, flat to infinite order at the origin. From it we cut, paste, and interpolate. Have students verify that every derivative vanishes at zero, killing any hope of a Taylor series.

Bump Functions and Cutoffs

Build a plateau

Combine and rescale copies of $\beta$ to get a function equal to $1$ on a ball, $0$ outside a larger ball.

Cutoff $\chi$

$\chi\equiv 1$ on $B_r,\quad \operatorname{supp}\chi\subset B_R$

Printed pp. 574-582Bump Functions and Cutoffs
Stretching the basic germ gives a plateau: a smooth function equal to one on a ball and zero outside a larger ball. These cutoffs let us localise any construction. Underline that compact support plus the value-one core is the engine behind every gluing and extension argument that follows.

Partitions of Unity

Local to global

Bumps subordinate to a locally finite cover let us average local data into one global object.

Defining property

$\sum_\alpha \rho_\alpha \equiv 1,\quad \operatorname{supp}\rho_\alpha\subset U_\alpha$

What it glues

Metrics, connections, almost complex structures, and sections all assemble this way.

Most reused tool

Flag every later existence proof that secretly invokes a partition of unity.

Printed pp. 574-582Partitions of Unity
Partitions of unity turn local data into global objects. Subordinate to a locally finite cover, the bump functions sum to one, so locally defined metrics, connections, or sections average into a global one. This is the single most reused tool in the appendix; flag every later existence proof that secretly invokes it.

Extension Lemmas

Whitney flavour

A smooth map or section defined near a closed set $A$ extends smoothly to all of $M$.

Statement

$f\in C^\infty(\mathcal U\supset A)\ \Rightarrow\ \tilde f\in C^\infty(M),\ \tilde f|_A=f$

Proof idea

Glue a local extension to the original with a cutoff $\chi$ supported near $A$.

Where it is spent

Promotes germ-level data to global maps in Moser and neighbourhood theorems.

Printed pp. 574-582Extension Lemmas
The extension results say a smooth map or section defined near a closed set extends smoothly to the whole manifold, in the Whitney spirit. The proof glues a local extension to the original using a cutoff. Point out that this is how neighbourhood theorems promote germ-level data to honest global maps.
