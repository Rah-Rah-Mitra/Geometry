Stationarity Turns a Waveform Into a Covariance Object

Lag Covariance

$$\gamma(k)=\operatorname{Cov}(X_t,X_{t+k})$$

Spectral Density

$$S(\omega)=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}\gamma(k)e^{-i k\omega}$$

Geometric Move

The process is represented by a structured point, not one observed sample path.

Covariance ValidityPositive semidefinite matrices

Not Every Smooth Lag Sequence Is a Valid Process

Toeplitz Covariance

$$\Gamma_n=(\gamma(i-j))_{i,j=1}^n \succeq 0$$

Failure Mode

A hand-drawn autocovariance can violate positive semidefiniteness.

Chapter Check

The autocovariance matrix generated by the notebook passes the PSD check.

Covariance validity is a geometric constraintPSD mirrors spectral positivity
Autocovariance positive semidefiniteness is a quiet but essential check. A sequence of lag covariances is not valid merely because it looks smooth; every finite Toeplitz covariance matrix must be positive semidefinite. This is the finite-dimensional shadow of spectral nonnegativity.
