The Whittle Fisher Metric

Frequency domain

Likelihood becomes a spectral integral.

Inner product

It scores which moves are short.

Whittle metric

$g_{jk}=\dfrac{1}{4\pi}\displaystyle\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}\partial_j \log S\,\partial_k \log S\;d\omega$

Closed form

Log-spectrum derivatives integrated over $\omega$.

Printed pp. 215-227The Whittle Fisher Metric
The Whittle approximation turns the Gaussian likelihood into a frequency-domain integral, giving a clean closed form for the Fisher metric. Two parameter perturbations are compared by integrating their log-spectrum derivatives against frequency. This integral is the inner product that decides which moves on the manifold are short or expensive.
