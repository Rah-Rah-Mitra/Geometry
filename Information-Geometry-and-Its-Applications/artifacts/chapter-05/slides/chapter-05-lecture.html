Derivative Operator

Local linearizationArrow, derivation, or score
Treat tangent vectors as three equivalent interfaces. Students can picture an arrow, compute a directional derivative, or read a score random variable in a statistical model. The deck should insist that these are representations of the same local linear object rather than separate definitions.

Coordinate FootprintSphere example

The Same Motion Has Different Coordinate Cost

Unit sphere

\(ds^2 = d\theta^2 + \sin^2(\theta)\,d\phi^2\)

Latitude Matters

Longitude motion is cheap near the poles and costly near the equator.

Lesson

Coordinates are bookkeeping; the metric tells the actual geometric cost.

Metric coefficients have meaningSpherical coordinates
The sphere formula is a useful warning against coordinate intuition. The expression d theta squared plus sine squared theta d phi squared says that longitude motion shrinks near the poles. That is not a defect of coordinates; it is precisely what a metric records.
Affine ConnectionsComparing tangent spaces

A Connection Corrects Basis Motion

Covariant derivative

\((\nabla_i V)^k=\partial_i V^k+\Gamma^k_{ij}V^j\)

Problem

Tangent vectors at different points belong to different tangent spaces.

Correction

The Christoffel symbols record how the coordinate basis changes.

Metric measures; connection transportsChristoffel symbols
Introduce affine connections as the answer to a practical problem: tangent vectors at different points do not automatically live in the same vector space. The Christoffel symbols are not mysterious corrections; they record how the coordinate basis itself changes while we move.

CurvatureLoop test

Curvature Is Failed Return After a Small Loop

Operational meaning

transport around a loop -> compare returned vector

Tensor Package

The Riemann-Christoffel tensor records the infinitesimal loop residual.

Derivative Test

Curvature also measures failure of covariant derivatives to commute.

Artifact: curvature_loop.pngCurvature as a detected residual
Curvature should be presented as an operational failure, not as a tensor to memorize. Move a vector around a small loop; if it returns changed, curvature has been detected. The Riemann-Christoffel tensor packages that loop experiment and also measures noncommuting covariant derivatives.

Cylinder diagnosticEmbedding curvature is not intrinsic curvature
The submanifold slide separates two kinds of bending. A cylinder curves in ambient space but has zero intrinsic curvature, so a traveler on the surface can miss the embedding curvature. In information geometry this distinction matters whenever a model is embedded in a larger distribution space.
Closing DiagnosticWhat structure do you need?

Measure, Compare, Transport, Then Test Curvature

Metric

Use it when the question is length, angle, or Fisher cost.

Connection

Use it when vectors must be compared across points.

Curvature

Use it when the result depends on the path of comparison.

Instructor prompt

For each calculation, ask: is this measuring, transporting, or detecting path dependence?

End of Chapter 5Differential geometry as a working toolkit
Close by asking students to classify a calculation by its required structure. A metric is enough to measure, but a connection is needed to transport, and curvature appears only after a path comparison. That diagnostic prevents the chapter's vocabulary from becoming a list of disconnected objects.
