The nuisance-fiber picture is deliberately low-dimensional. Fixing the target coordinate leaves a fiber of nuisance values, and the Neyman-Scott setting keeps adding new fibers as the sample grows. More data therefore do not automatically mean the target direction becomes easier to isolate.

Estimating functions are geometric objects, not merely algebraic tricks. A valid rule has mean zero at the target, but an efficient rule also removes nuisance components. The figure shows the target score decomposed into nuisance projection plus the horizontal residual used for inference.

Likelihood Must Be Inspected When Nuisance Directions Move

Fixed Dimension

Classical likelihood intuition is often stable when nuisance dimension stays controlled.

Growing Dimension

Adding observations can also add nuisance directions, breaking usual variance intuition.

Functional Nuisance

Semiparametric models replace finite nuisance vectors with tangent spaces of functions.

Repair

Project away nuisance components and use the residual target direction.

Estimating Functions

Encode orthogonality and scale accounting directly in the rule.

Diagnostic Habit
