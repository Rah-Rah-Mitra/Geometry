Ancillary FibersThe estimator as foliation

An Estimator Is a Local Foliation Near the Model

Fiber A(u)

All observations assigned to the same estimate lie on one fiber.

Consistency

The fiber for u passes through the model point.

First-Order Efficiency

The fiber is orthogonal to the model tangent at that point.

Artifact: ancillary_fiber_foliations.pngEstimator equals geometry of fibers
Ancillary fibers are not an optional embellishment; they are the estimator. Every nearby ambient observation is assigned to a model point by lying on some fiber. Consistency asks fibers to pass through the model, while first-order efficiency asks them to meet the model orthogonally.

The Schur Complement Quantifies Fiber Tilt

Local precision

\(\bar g_{uu}=g_{uu}-g_{uv}g_{vv}^{-1}g_{vu}\)

Orthogonal Fibers

The off-diagonal block vanishes and precision is not lost.

Tilted Fibers

Normal noise leaks into the reported parameter and inflates variance.

Artifact: first_order_schur_covariance_check.pngAngle becomes variance
The Schur-complement slide turns the fiber angle into a covariance calculation. The local coordinates split error into parameter and fiber directions. If the off-diagonal metric block vanishes, the nuisance fiber does not inflate variance; tilted fibers reduce precision and produce a visible penalty.
