Positive MeasuresFlat before normalization

The Flat Coordinate Hidden in Positive Measures

Alpha representation

\(h_\alpha(m)_i = m_i^{(1-\alpha)/2}\), with a logarithmic limit at \(\alpha=1\)

Positive Cone

Components can scale freely, so the power representation supplies affine coordinates.

Simplex Constraint

The condition \(\sum_i p_i=1\) is not generally affine in those coordinates.

Key idea: remove normalization firstPower coordinates
Stress the distinction between positive measures and probabilities. The power coordinate h alpha straightens the cone because every component can scale freely. The probability simplex imposes a sum constraint, so the same straight line in representation space usually returns as a curved path after normalization.

Escort GeometryPower reweighting

Escort Distributions Reweight the Same Point

Escort transform

\(\mathrm{Esc}_q(p)_i = \dfrac{p_i^q}{\sum_j p_j^q}\)

q > 1

Large coordinates are amplified and dominate the displayed distribution.

q < 1

Small coordinates receive more relative mass.

Interactive artifact: escort_distribution_flow.htmlCoordinates, not decoration
Use escorts as a concrete reweighting experiment. For q greater than one, large coordinates become even more visible; for q less than one, small coordinates gain relative weight. The geometric point is that this is a coordinate system, not merely a heuristic smoothing trick.

Positive-Definite MatricesMatrix-valued positive measures

From Positive Coordinates to Log-Det Divergence

Stein/log-det form

\(D(P:Q)=\operatorname{tr}(P Q^{-1})-\log\det(P Q^{-1})-n\)

Nonnegative

Zero exactly when \(P=Q\).

Congruence Invariant

Stable under \(P\mapsto APA^T\), \(Q\mapsto AQA^T\).

Matrix divergenceInvariance under congruence
Move carefully from diagonal positive measures to full positive-definite matrices. Eigenvalues behave like positive masses, but orientations now matter too. The Stein or log-det divergence is useful because it respects congruence transformations, the matrix analogue of changing coordinates without changing the geometry.
