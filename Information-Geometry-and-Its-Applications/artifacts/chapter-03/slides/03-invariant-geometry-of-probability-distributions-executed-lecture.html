Coarse Graining Is the Basic Invariance Test

Monotonicity Demand

$$D(Pp:Pq)\leq D(p:q)$$

Map

\(P\) sums fine outcomes into reported cells.

Meaning

Reporting less detail should not inflate distinguishability.

Finite alphabets make the map visibleCoarse graining before formulas
Use coarse graining as the simplest concrete operation. A partition matrix sends fine probabilities to coarse probabilities by summing within blocks. The important inequality is conceptual: a lossy observation can make two distributions harder to distinguish, but it should never make them more distinguishable.
Invariant DivergencesConvexity drives monotonicity

\(f\)-Divergences Are Built for This Test

Decomposable Form

$$D_f(p:q)=\sum_i p_i f\!\left({q_i\over p_i}\right),\qquad f(1)=0$$

Jensen Step

A merged likelihood ratio is a weighted average of fine ratios.

Observed Gaps

KL, dual KL, Hellinger squared, and Pearson \(\chi^2\) all decrease in the check.

Decomposable plus invariantJensen's inequality in statistical clothing
The f-divergence slide explains why convexity is the engine. Inside a merged block, the new likelihood ratio is an average of the old ratios. Jensen's inequality then gives monotonicity, and the plotted gaps show the same decrease for several standard divergence choices.
Equality CaseSufficient statistics

Equality Means the Discarded Detail Was Parameter-Free

Sufficient Case

Within each coarse block, the conditional distribution does not depend on the model parameter.

Broken Case

A hidden conditional shift has the same coarse report but a positive fine-level divergence gap.

Diagnostic

zero residual \( \Leftrightarrow \) no parameter information was thrown away

Equality is more informative than inequalitySufficiency as geometry preservation
Equality is the statistical signal hidden inside the monotonicity theorem. If the conditional distribution within each reported block is parameter-free, the statistic is sufficient, so the coarse observation loses no parameter information. The artifact contrasts that exact case with a deliberately broken conditional structure.

The Fisher Metric Survives Sufficient Embedding

Quadratic Form

$$g_p(v,v)=\sum_i {v_i^2\over p_i},\qquad \sum_i v_i=0$$

Embedding

Split \(q_j\) into \(r_{ij}q_j\) using fixed conditional weights.

Cancellation

The tangent vector splits in the same proportions, preserving Fisher length.

Chentsov-style uniqueness begins hereMetric selected by invariance
Now move from global divergences to the infinitesimal metric. The Fisher quadratic form is invariant under sufficient embeddings because both masses and tangent vectors split in the same fixed proportions. The displayed residual records the numerical version of that cancellation.

The Square-Root Coordinates Flatten the Metric

Coordinate Change

$$\xi_i=2\sqrt{m_i},\qquad ds^2=\sum_i {dm_i^2\over m_i}=\sum_i d\xi_i^2$$

Probability Simplex

The constraint \(\sum_i m_i=1\) becomes a spherical section of radius \(2\).

Interpretation

Hellinger geometry is the Euclidean shadow of the invariant metric.

Positive cone to sphereHellinger geometry as memory
The square-root picture gives students a geometric memory for the Fisher metric. On positive measures, the coordinate change xi equals two square roots of mass turns the metric into ordinary Euclidean length. The probability simplex becomes a spherical section, explaining the Hellinger intuition.

Common MisreadingsSeparate maps, models, and metrics

Three Things Invariance Does Not Mean

Not Model Projection

Coarse graining maps observation alphabets; it is not the same as projecting onto a parametric family.

Not Invertibility

A sufficient statistic can be many-to-one when discarded conditionals are parameter-free.

Not Euclidean Distance

Raw coordinate distance depends on how finely the sample space was represented.

Correct View

Distinguishability should contract under data loss.

Equality View

Exact preservation diagnoses sufficiency.

Local View

The Fisher metric is the surviving second-order geometry.

Maps between data resolutionsInvariance selects, it does not decorate
Pause on misconceptions because they are easy here. Coarse graining is not projection onto a model submanifold, sufficiency is not invertibility, and Euclidean distance on raw probability coordinates is not invariant under observational resolution. The chapter is selecting geometry by behavior under maps.
