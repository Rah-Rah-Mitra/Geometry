Mixture Coordinates

Probabilities themselves, so m-geodesics are ordinary line segments.

Exponential Coordinates

Same Metric

Artifact: dual_connections_simplex.pngFlatness depends on coordinates
The simplex picture should make the main contrast visible. Mixture coordinates are ordinary probabilities, so mixture geodesics look like line segments. Exponential coordinates are log-odds before normalization, so the corresponding geodesics curve when drawn back on the same probability simplex.

Compatibility, not duplicationDual affine connections
Emphasize that dual connections are paired through one metric. The lecture is not introducing two unrelated geometries. One connection is natural for averaging probabilities, the other for multiplying and renormalizing likelihood factors, and compatibility is what lets inner products differentiate coherently.

A Legendre Gap Measures Separation

Dually flat chart

\(D(P,Q)=\psi(\theta_P)+\phi(\eta_Q)-\theta_P\cdot\eta_Q\)

Potentials

\(\eta=\nabla\psi(\theta)\) and \(\theta=\nabla\phi(\eta)\) translate between coordinates.

Asymmetry

The orientation matters, even when a familiar KL quantity appears.

Interactive: canonical_divergence_surface.htmlConvex analysis as geometry
Present canonical divergence as a convex gap that knows both coordinate systems at once. The formula combines theta for one point and eta for the other through Legendre duality. Students should inspect asymmetry and nonnegativity rather than trying to remember only a KL label.

The mixed-coordinate slide is where projection geometry becomes visible. Holding one expectation coordinate fixed and another natural coordinate fixed creates dual flat sheets. When they meet orthogonally, the canonical divergence decomposes in the same spirit as a Pythagorean identity.

Use k-cuts as the bridge from geometry to modeled interaction. Replacing a coupled joint distribution by a factorized family is a projection to a restricted manifold. The divergence from the original to the cut model measures interaction structure that the chosen factorization cannot express.

Integrated Information as Predictive Loss Under Cuts

Full Mechanism

A coupled two-node binary Markov transition model.

Cut Mechanism

Each next node is predicted from its own present node only.

Score

Average divergence over present states measures lost predictive structure.

Artifact: integrated_information_lab.pngTransparent projection motif
The integrated-information lab should be described as a transparent motif, not as a complete theory of consciousness. A coupled transition kernel is compared with cut kernels; if every cut predicts worse, the residual divergence records irreducible predictive structure across the partition.
Pitfalls and ChecksDo not trust labels alone

Four Questions Before Interpreting a Number

Metric?

Which inner product pairs the two connections?

Connection?

Which coordinate system is flat for this calculation?

Orientation?

Is the divergence \(D(P,Q)\) or \(D(Q,P)\)?

Family?

Which restricted submanifold defines the projection?

Orthogonal?

Does the dual Pythagorean identity actually apply?

Residual?

What invariant or numerical check confirms the interpretation?

Active diagnostic slideOrientation matters
Make the pitfalls active. Ask students to identify the metric, the two connections, the orientation of divergence, and the projection family before interpreting a number. Most errors in this chapter come from swapping orientation or expecting Euclidean symmetry in a dually flat setting.
