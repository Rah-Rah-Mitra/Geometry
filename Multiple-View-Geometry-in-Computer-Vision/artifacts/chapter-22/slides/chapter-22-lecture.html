Degenerate Configurations

When structure and motion cannot be uniquely recovered

Critical configuration

Point and camera arrangements that leave an alternative model consistent with the same images.

Diagnosis

Read off a rank, a nullspace, a surface incidence, or a homography fit.

Noise Is Not Degeneracy

Noisy data

A unique model exists and is merely perturbed; better optimisation recovers it.

Degenerate data

A whole family of models fits the images exactly. No optimiser can choose.

Remedy

Detect the condition, then route to a smaller well-posed model.

Ambiguity

$$\{M_\theta : \theta\in\mathbb{R}\}\ \text{all reproject the data}$$

Map of cases

Planar scene, pure rotation, ruled quadric, resection pencil, three-view quadrics.

Printed pp. 533-560What Degeneracy Means
Stress the distinction between a noisy estimate and a degenerate one. With noise a unique model exists but is perturbed; with degeneracy a one-parameter or larger family of models fits the data exactly. The remedy is not better optimisation but detecting the condition and routing to a smaller, well-posed model.

A Homography Explains the Correspondences

Plane transfer

$$\mathbf{x}' \simeq H\,\mathbf{x}$$

Pencil of F

$$F = [\mathbf{e}']_\times H \ \text{solves}\ \mathbf{x}'^\top F \mathbf{x}=0$$

Free epipole

Any $\mathbf{e}'$ works, so the second camera is not pinned down.

Printed pp. 533-560Homography Explains the Data
Make the algebra explicit. When points lie on a plane the correspondences satisfy x' equals H x, and any F of the form skew-e times H solves the epipolar constraint, giving a pencil of valid fundamental matrices. The epipole is free, so the second camera is not recovered. This motivates the homography-versus-F decision.

Pure Rotation As Critical Motion

Zero baseline

The camera centre is unchanged, so there is no parallax to triangulate.

Warp survives

Images relate by the rotation homography $H = K R K^{-1}$.

Essential matrix

$$E = [\mathbf{t}]_\times R = 0 \ \text{when}\ \mathbf{t}=\mathbf{0}$$

Printed pp. 533-560Pure Rotation
Cover the second motion degeneracy: zero baseline. With a fixed camera centre the images differ only by the calibration-conjugated rotation homography, and there is no parallax to triangulate. The essential matrix collapses because the translation is null. A spatial, non-planar cloud still degenerates, showing the cause is motion, not scene shape.

Critical Surfaces: The Ruled Quadric

Two-view theorem

$$\mathbf{X}^\top Q\,\mathbf{X}=0,\quad C,\,C'\in Q$$

Ambiguity locus

Both centres and all points on a ruled quadric leave the reconstruction ambiguous.

Horopter

A twisted cubic through both centres: points imaged identically.

Printed pp. 533-560Critical Surfaces and the Horopter
Introduce the two-view critical surface theorem: ambiguity arises when both camera centres and all scene points lie on a ruled quadric. The horopter, a twisted cubic through both centres, is the locus of points imaged identically. Emphasise that incidence with this quadric is the hidden geometric reason behind the ambiguity, not coincidence.

Resectioning Degeneracy

Camera pencil

$$P_\theta = P + \theta\,P_{\text{alt}}$$

Rank three

Right nullspace is a single camera centre.

Rank drop

Nullspace becomes a line or plane and joins the critical set.

Classical case

The centre with the points lying on a twisted cubic through it.

Printed pp. 533-560Resectioning Degeneracy
Turn to single-camera resection. Ambiguity is organised by the pencil P-theta equals P plus theta P-alt. When the pencil keeps rank three the nullspace is a single centre; when rank drops the nullspace becomes a line or plane joining the critical set. The classical case is the centre with points on a twisted cubic through it.

Rank Drop In The Pencil

Sweep $\theta$

The smallest singular value of $P_\theta$ dips to zero where the shared block cancels.

At the exception

$$\mathrm{rank}\,3\to 1,\qquad \mathrm{null}\,1\to 3$$

Meaning

The dimension jump is the linear component added to the critical set.

Printed pp. 533-560Rank Drop in the Pencil
Read the numerical proof. As theta sweeps, the smallest singular value of P-theta dips to zero at the exceptional value where the shared block cancels; rank falls from three to one and nullity rises to three. That dimension jump is exactly the linear component the chapter adds to the resection critical set.

Carlsson-Weinshall Duality

Duality map

$$\Gamma(X,Y,Z,T)=(YZT,\ ZTX,\ TXY,\ XYZ)$$

Reciprocation

Away from coordinate planes $\Gamma$ acts like coordinate reciprocation up to scale.

Points and cameras swap

The map exchanges point and camera roles.

Lesson

A line statement dualises into a twisted-cubic statement.

Printed pp. 533-560Carlsson-Weinshall Duality
Sketch the duality map Gamma sending each homogeneous coordinate to the product of the other three. Away from coordinate planes it acts like reciprocation, exchanging points and cameras. The lesson is that a simple line statement about points and centres dualises into a twisted-cubic statement, unifying resection and reconstruction degeneracies under one map.

Detecting Degeneracy: H vs F

RANSAC is not enough

It can fit a confident but meaningless $F$ on planar or rotational data.

Fit both models

Estimate $H$ and $F$ on the same matches and compare.

GRIC model score

$$\sum \rho(e_i^2) + \lambda_1 d\,n + \lambda_2 k$$

Decision

Pick $H$ when the plane explanation wins; avoid a spurious projective reconstruction.

Printed pp. 533-560Detecting Degeneracy: H vs F
Bring in model selection. RANSAC alone can fit a confident but meaningless F on planar or rotational data, so compare a homography against the fundamental matrix on the same matches. GRIC scores each model with a penalty for parameters and structure dimension, selecting H when the plane explanation wins and avoiding a spurious projective reconstruction.

A Preflight Diagnostic Scorecard

Eight-point nullity

More than one null direction flags a plane.

Planarity ratio

Smallest over largest singular value of centred 3D points.

Baseline length

Near zero flags pure or near-pure rotation.

H transfer vs Sampson

Compare homography transfer error against the Sampson error of $F$.

Three-view quadric rank

Dependent pairwise quadrics leave an intersection curve, not finite points.

Goal

$$\text{route each sequence to the well-posed model}$$

Printed pp. 533-560Diagnostic Scorecard
Close with a preflight checklist an implementation should run before trusting a reconstruction: eight-point nullity, planarity ratio, baseline length, homography transfer versus Sampson error, and three-view quadric dependence. The aim is not to reject planar or rotational data but to route each sequence to the correctly posed model.

Printed pp. 533-560Summary
Recap the unifying thread: degeneracy is non-uniqueness diagnosed through rank, nullspace, and surface incidence.
