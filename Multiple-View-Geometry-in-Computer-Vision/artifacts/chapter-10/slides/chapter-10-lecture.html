Retrieving a Camera Pair from $F$

Canonical pair

$$P = [\,I \mid 0\,]$$

$$P' = [\,[e']_\times F \mid e'\,]$$

The epipole

$e'$ is the left null vector of $F$: $\;F^{\!\top} e' = 0$.

One frame of many

This pair is consistent with $F$ but is not the original Euclidean rig; it is a chosen projective coordinate frame.

Gauge fixed

Setting $P=[I\mid 0]$ removes the obvious freedom; what remains is a single $\mathbb{P}^3$ projectivity.

Printed pp. 262-278Retrieving Cameras from F
Explain the canonical choice that breaks the gauge freedom: fix the first camera as identity, then read the second directly off the fundamental matrix using the epipole. Point out that this is one particular projective frame among infinitely many, chosen for convenience, not the true rig that produced the photographs.

Triangulation: Intersecting Two Rays

Linear system

$$A X = 0,\quad A=\begin{bmatrix} u\,p_3^{\!\top}-p_1^{\!\top}\\ v\,p_3^{\!\top}-p_2^{\!\top}\\ u'p_3'^{\!\top}-p_1'^{\!\top}\\ v'p_3'^{\!\top}-p_2'^{\!\top}\end{bmatrix}$$

Solve

Smallest singular vector of $A$; off the baseline $\operatorname{rank} A = 3$, so $X$ is unique.

Printed pp. 262-278Triangulation of a 3D Point
Describe triangulation as intersecting two back-projected rays that share an epipolar plane. Build the four-row homogeneous system from the two projection equations and solve by its smallest singular vector. Note that away from the baseline the rays meet in exactly one point, giving a rank-three measurement matrix.

The Baseline Exception

The degeneracy

A point on the baseline projects to the two epipoles. Both rays lie along the baseline and coincide.

Rank drops

$$\operatorname{rank} A : 3 \;\longrightarrow\; 2$$

Highlight the single failure mode of triangulation. A world point lying on the baseline projects to the two epipoles, so both rays collapse onto the baseline itself and lose a rank. Connect this degeneracy to the notebook check where the measurement matrix drops from rank three to rank two.

Projective Reconstruction Theorem

The invariance

$$X \mapsto H X,\qquad P \mapsto P H^{-1}$$

$$ (P H^{-1})(H X) = P X $$

Consequence

Any nonsingular $4\times 4$ $H$ leaves every image point unchanged.

What is fixed

Incidence and the images themselves.

What is lost

Angles, parallelism, length ratios in the recovered 3D cloud are meaningless.

Printed pp. 262-278The Projective Ambiguity
State the reconstruction theorem precisely. Replacing every point by H times the point, and every camera by the camera times H inverse, leaves all image measurements untouched. Therefore two views fix the scene only up to a four-by-four projective transformation, and angles or parallelism in the recovered cloud are meaningless.

Affine Upgrade: Locating $\pi_\infty$

Upgrade homography

$$H_\pi = \begin{bmatrix} I & 0 \\ \pi^{\!\top} \end{bmatrix},\quad H_\pi^{-\top}\pi_\infty = \begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}$$

Exact identity

Verified symbolically: the chosen plane maps to the canonical $(0,0,0,1)^{\!\top}$.

What is restored

Parallel scene directions become parallel again; midpoints and ratios along lines are meaningful.

What is not

Right angles need not yet be correct; that waits for the metric rung.

Metric Upgrade: the Absolute Conic

Image of absolute conic

$$\omega = (K K^{\!\top})^{-1}$$

$$A A^{\!\top} = (M^{\!\top}\!\omega\,M)^{-1}$$

Rectify

Cholesky factor $A$ supplies $H$; right angles and length ratios return.

Calibrated route

With $K$ known, $E = K'^{\!\top} F K$ gives the essential matrix.

Four-fold $(R,t)$

$E$ decomposes into four pose candidates; cheirality (points in front of both cameras) selects the true one.

Direct Upgrade from Ground Control

The shortcut

Known world points fix the missing $4\times 4$ homography directly, bypassing the strata.

Counting equations

$$5 \text{ controls} \times 3 = 15 \ge 15$$

Each non-coplanar correspondence gives three independent equations.

Estimate $H$

Solve $X^{\text{world}}_i = H\, X^{\text{proj}}_i$ from the controls.

Validate

Held-out reconstructed points must align with the Euclidean scene to certify the fit.

Printed pp. 262-278Direct Upgrade from Ground Control
Contrast the stratified route with the direct one. Five non-coplanar control points with known world coordinates fix the missing four-by-four homography outright, since each correspondence gives three equations. Held-out points then certify the fit. This is the practical shortcut when survey data or a known calibration object is available.

Synthesis and Outlook

Projective for free

Two views recover cameras and structure up to a $\mathbb{P}^3$ projectivity.

Affine costs one object

Identify $\pi_\infty$ to restore parallelism.

Metric costs one more

Identify $\Omega_\infty$, via $K$ and $E$, to restore the similarity class.

Baseline caveat

Triangulation fails only for points on the baseline.

Forward

Real images carry noise: motivating robust estimation and bundle adjustment in later chapters.
