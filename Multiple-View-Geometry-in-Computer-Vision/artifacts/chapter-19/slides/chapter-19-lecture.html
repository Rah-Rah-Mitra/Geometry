The Absolute Conic $\Omega_\infty$ and Its Image

Definition

$\Omega_\infty$ is the imaginary point conic on $\pi_\infty$, fixed by every similarity transform.

Image of the absolute conic

$\omega = (KK^\top)^{-1}, \qquad \omega^* = KK^\top$

Why it calibrates

Recover $K$ from $\omega$ by Cholesky factorization; a moving rigid camera always images the same $\Omega_\infty$.

Define the absolute conic as the fixed imaginary conic on the plane at infinity, invariant under similarity transforms. Its image omega equals the inverse of K K transpose, so once omega is found the calibration matrix follows by Cholesky. Emphasize that a moving rigid camera always sees the same absolute conic.

The Absolute Dual Quadric $Q^*_\infty$

A single packaged unknown

$Q^*_\infty = \begin{bmatrix} I_3 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$ (metric frame)

Rank and shape

$4\times4$, symmetric, positive semidefinite, rank $3$. One zero eigenvalue.

Two unknowns in one

Null vector $Q^*_\infty\,\pi_\infty = 0$ gives the plane; the $3$-block carries $\Omega_\infty$.

Printed pp. 458-501The Absolute Dual Quadric
Introduce the dual absolute quadric as the single four by four rank-three object that packages both unknowns. Its null vector is the plane at infinity and its non-trivial part is the absolute conic. This is the fixed object every auto-calibration method solves for; everything else is bookkeeping around it.

The Auto-Calibration Equation

Dual quadric projects to the DIAC

$$P_i\,Q^*_\infty\,P_i^\top \;\simeq\; \omega_i^* \;=\; K_iK_i^\top$$

The trick

One shared 3D quadric; each view's assumed $K_i$ becomes equations on its entries.

Constant intrinsics

If $K_i=K$, every $\omega_i^*$ is equal, sharply over-constraining $Q^*_\infty$.

Printed pp. 458-501The Projection Equation
Derive the central identity: a camera projects the dual quadric to the dual image of the absolute conic, P times Q-star times P transpose proportional to K K transpose. Make clear this single equation turns assumptions about the internals into constraints on one shared 3D quadric across all views.

Recovering the Rectifying Homography $H$

Factorize

Eigendecompose $Q^*_\infty$. The leading block factors as $KK^\top$; the null vector is $\pi_\infty$.

Block form of H

$H = \begin{bmatrix} K & 0 \\ -p^\top K & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad p = \pi_\infty$

Apply

Map every camera $P_i\!\to\!P_iH$ and point $X\!\to\!H^{-1}X$ to land in a metric frame.

Assumptions

Linear rows

The Kruppa Equations

Two-view only

Need just the fundamental matrix $F$ and epipole $e'$; no explicit projective reconstruction.

Constant-K proportionality

$$[e']_\times\,\omega^*\,[e']_\times \;\simeq\; F\,\omega^*\,F^\top$$

Caveat

Weaker than a known infinite homography; it omits much multiview $Q^*_\infty$ structure.

Kruppa as Epipolar Tangency

Geometric picture

Two epipolar planes are tangent to $\Omega_\infty$; they meet each image in tangent epipolar lines.

Why dual

Tangency is a line condition, so the algebra is naturally written with the dual conic $\omega^*$.

Reading

Stratified Upgrade via $H_\infty$

Once $\pi_\infty$ is known

The affine-to-metric step is linear: transfer the IAC between views.

IAC transfer

$\omega_i = H_\infty^{-\top}\,\omega\,H_\infty^{-1}, \quad H_\infty = A - a\,p^\top$

Rotating camera

The inter-image homography of a camera rotating about its centre is already an $H_\infty$.

A rotating camera's inter-image homography is already an infinite homography, so the same equation calibrates it.

Critical Motions: Degeneracy as Extra Nullity

The warning

Self-calibration fails when motion is too restricted: unobservable degrees of freedom remain.

Examples

Single rotation axis, planar rig motion, pure translation, turntable sequences.

Diagnostic

Inspect the constraint singular spectrum; extra small singular values flag the ambiguity.

Printed pp. 458-501Critical Motions and Degeneracy
Drive home the practical warning. Auto-calibration fails when motion is too restricted: a single rotation axis, planar rig motion, pure translation, or turntable sequences leave unobservable directions. Show these as extra nullity in the constraint spectrum, and recommend checking conditioning before trusting any recovered calibration.
