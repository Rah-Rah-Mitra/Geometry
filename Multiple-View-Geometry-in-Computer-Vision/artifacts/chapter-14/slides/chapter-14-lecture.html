The Affine Camera

Definition

An affine camera has a final projection row of $(0,0,0,1)$, removing the perspective division.

Linear in the world

Image coordinates become affine functions of world coordinates.

When it is valid

Distant objects and long lenses: scene depth small relative to its distance.

Affine projection matrix

$$P_A=\begin{bmatrix}m_{11}&m_{12}&m_{13}&t_1\\m_{21}&m_{22}&m_{23}&t_2\\0&0&0&1\end{bmatrix}$$

Consequence

$\mathbf{x}=M\,\mathbf{X}+\mathbf{t}$: no depth-dependent scaling, so parallel world lines stay parallel.

Stress that it is an excellent model for distant, shallow scenes viewed through long lenses where depth variation is small.

The Parallel-Projection Limit

The affine camera is the limit of a perspective camera for a distant, shallow scene

Perspective

Rays pass through a finite centre; depth changes scale, and the epipole is a finite point.

Affine limit

Push the centre to infinity: rays become a parallel bundle and the epipole becomes an ideal point.

Approximation quality

The model is accurate when relief is small compared with viewing distance.

Parallel Epipolar Lines

Projective pencil

Epipolar lines converge to a finite epipole $\mathbf{e}$ where the baseline meets the image.

Affine pencil

The epipole is at infinity, so every epipolar line in an image is parallel, sharing one normal.

Signature

Parallel lines are the visible fingerprint of an affine pair.

The Affine Fundamental Matrix $F_A$

Sparse form

$$F_A=\begin{bmatrix}0&0&a\\0&0&b\\c&d&e\end{bmatrix}$$

Zero upper block

The vanishing $2\times2$ block is exactly what sends both epipoles to infinity.

Correspondence constraint

$$a\,x' + b\,y' + c\,x + d\,y + e = 0$$

One linear equation

Each match is linear in the four measured coordinates $(x',y',x,y)$.

Drives everything

This linearity makes estimation and correction pure linear algebra.

The correspondence constraint reduces to one linear equation in x prime, y prime, x, y, the fact that drives every later algorithm.

Degrees of Freedom and Epipoles

Four DoF

Five entries up to overall scale leave four degrees of freedom, against seven for general $F$.

Automatic rank two

The structure guarantees $\det F_A=0$ without any extra constraint.

Epipoles are ideal points

$$\mathbf{e}'=(-d,\,c,\,0)^{\!T},\qquad \mathbf{e}=(-b,\,a,\,0)^{\!T}$$

Read off directly

The trailing zero marks an ideal point: each epipole lies on the line at infinity.

Line normals

$(a,b)$ orients lines in the second image; $(c,d)$ orients them in the first.

Estimation Is Hyperplane Fitting

Lift each match

Stack each correspondence as a point $X_i=(x'_i,y'_i,x_i,y_i)$ in a four-dimensional measurement space.

Hyperplane

$$(a,b,c,d)\cdot X + e = 0$$

Normal and offset

The normal yields $(a,b,c,d)$; the offset yields $e$.

Fitting F_A is fitting that hyperplane. The normal vector gives a, b, c, d and the offset gives e. This is the chapter's key algorithmic simplification.

The Gold Standard Is Closed Form

Quadratic error

The affine reprojection error is exactly quadratic, so no iterative refinement is required.

Recipe

Centre the four-dimensional data, take the SVD, and read the smallest singular vector as the hyperplane normal.

Orthogonal regression

Total least squares delivers the maximum-likelihood $F_A$ directly.

Closed-form estimate

$$\min_{\lVert n\rVert=1}\sum_i \big(n^{\!T}(X_i-\bar X)\big)^2$$

Solution

$$n=\text{smallest singular vector},\quad e=-\,n^{\!T}\bar X$$

Parallax and the Minimal Case

Plane transfer

Three coplanar points fix a plane-induced affine transfer $H_A$ between the views.

Fourth point

Its parallax, the departure from $H_A$, sets the epipolar-line direction.

Degeneracy

As relief tends to zero, parallax vanishes and the estimate becomes ill-conditioned.

Triangulation as Exact Sampson Correction

Projective case

Sampson correction is only a first-order approximation to geometric correction.

Affine case

The constraint is a genuine hyperplane, so the correction is exact, not approximate.

Then triangulate

The corrected pair satisfies the epipolar equation exactly and feeds affine triangulation.

Minimal correction

$\hat X$ is the closest point to the noisy measurement that exactly satisfies the constraint.

Affine Reconstruction and Bas-Relief

Up to an affine map

Two affine views reconstruct the scene up to a $3\mathrm{D}$ affine transformation once a basis is fixed.

Basis

Four non-coplanar points define an affine frame; ratios along parallel lines survive.

Factorization link

Stacking many views yields a measurement matrix of rank at most four, factored by SVD.

Bas-relief ambiguity

$$\Delta x = \Delta Z\,\sin\rho = \text{const}$$

Cannot separate

The product of depth and turn is fixed, but neither factor alone is recoverable from two affine views.

But the bas-relief ambiguity means the product of depth and turn is fixed while neither factor is, so depth and rotation magnitude cannot be separated.
