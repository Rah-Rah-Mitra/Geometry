Tensors Are Multilinear Slot Machines

Definition Pattern

T: (V^*)^r x V^s -> R

A tensor is multilinear in every input slot.

Examples

  • A metric has type (0,2).
  • A vector field has type (1,0).
  • Curvature is often read as type (1,3) or (0,4).
Tensor type is slot bookkeepingSlide 3 / 10
Emphasize that the type of a tensor is bookkeeping with consequences. Covariant slots eat vectors, contravariant slots eat covectors, and multilinearity is the rule that makes local computations meaningful. The slide deliberately separates slots before mentioning coordinates, because coordinates should serve the object rather than define it.

Coordinate Change Is the Stress Test

T'^{i_1...i_r}_{j_1...j_s} = (A^{-1})^{i_1}_{a_1} ... (A^{-1})^{i_r}_{a_r} A^{b_1}_{j_1} ... A^{b_s}_{j_s} T^{a_1...a_r}_{b_1...b_s}

Object

The tensor is the invariant multilinear rule, independent of the chart used to record it.

Components

The array of numbers changes because basis vectors and covectors change.

Failure Mode

An arbitrary array is not a tensor unless its components obey the transformation law.

From One Vector Space to a Tensor Bundle

Bundle View

T^r_s M = union over p in M of T^r_s(T_pM)

Tensor Field

A smooth section chooses one tensor in each fiber, compatibly with smooth coordinate changes.

Later Use

Metrics, curvature, Ricci curvature, and volume elements are all tensor fields or are built from them.

Pointwise algebra becomes geometry by smooth variationSlide 5 / 10
Move from linear algebra at one point to fields over a manifold. A tensor bundle is a smooth assignment of the appropriate tensor space over every point. The critical phrase is smooth assignment; without that, we only have unrelated algebraic fibers and no geometric object to differentiate or integrate.

Differential Forms Add Antisymmetry

Alternating Tensors

omega(v,w) = -omega(w,v)

A repeated input forces an alternating form to vanish.

Wedge Product

The operation alpha wedge beta builds higher-degree oriented measurements from lower-degree forms.

Integration Bridge

Top-degree forms are the tensorial objects that can be integrated over oriented manifolds.

Antisymmetry records orientation and degeneracySlide 6 / 10
Forms are tensors with a built-in orientation detector. The wedge product is not just notation for multiplication; it records signed area, volume, and higher dimensional oriented measurement. This prepares students for Stokes type statements later because boundaries and signs must be encoded before integration begins.

Densities Integrate Without Choosing Orientation

Top Form

omega' = det(Dx/Dy) omega

Orientation reversal changes the sign of a top-degree form, so integration requires a chosen orientation.

Density

mu' = |det(Dx/Dy)| mu

The absolute Jacobian keeps measured volume nonnegative and works on nonorientable manifolds.

Forms remember sign; densities remember sizeSlide 7 / 10
Contrast forms and densities carefully. A top form is naturally integrated once an orientation is chosen, while a density transforms with absolute Jacobian weight and therefore avoids sign changes under orientation reversal. This distinction matters whenever the manifold is not oriented or when volume should remain positive.
