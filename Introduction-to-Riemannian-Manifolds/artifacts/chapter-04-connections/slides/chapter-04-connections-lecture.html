Why Naive Partial Derivatives Are Not Enough

The Comparison Problem

For a vector field X on M, the expression (X_q - X_p)/t requires subtracting elements of different vector spaces T_qM and T_pM.

What Charts Give

Coordinate partials \partial_i X^j are well defined inside a chart, but they do not transform as a tensor under chart changes.

Resolution

Add structure: a connection specifies how nearby tangent spaces are linked, so directional differentiation becomes intrinsic.

The motivating obstructionSlide 2 / 10
Stress that the difficulty is not technical fussiness. Tangent vectors at distinct points genuinely live in distinct vector spaces, so the difference quotient that defines a directional derivative in Euclidean space has no meaning. Show students that the naive coordinate expression for the derivative changes nontensorially under a chart transition: a quadratic correction term appears. This forces the introduction of an additional structure that supplies the missing linear data.

Axioms for a Connection on a Vector Bundle

Definition

A connection on a smooth vector bundle E \to M is a map \nabla : \Gamma(TM) \times \Gamma(E) \to \Gamma(E), written (X, s) \mapsto \nabla_X s.

Three Axioms

  • Linear in X over C^\infty(M): \nabla_{fX} s = f \nabla_X s.
  • R-linear in s: \nabla_X (a s_1 + b s_2) = a \nabla_X s_1 + b \nabla_X s_2.
  • Leibniz in s: \nabla_X (f s) = (Xf)\, s + f\, \nabla_X s.

Tensoriality vs. Leibniz

The directional slot is tensorial (depends on X_p only). The section slot obeys a product rule: \nabla_X s depends on s in a neighborhood of p.

Special Case

When E = TM, the connection is called an affine or linear connection and differentiates vector fields against vector fields.

The defining structureSlide 3 / 10
Walk through each axiom with intent. The first axiom says the directional slot is point-evaluated, so the result depends only on the vector at a point, not on the field. The third axiom is what distinguishes a connection from a tensor: it has a derivation-like Leibniz rule in the section variable, which is exactly what forces the connection to involve actual differentiation rather than pointwise algebra. Make explicit that these three rules are the complete contract: anything obeying them is a connection.

Covariant Derivative in Coordinates

\(\nabla_{\partial_i} \partial_j = \Gamma^k_{ij}\, \partial_k \qquad \nabla_X Y = \left( X^i \partial_i Y^k + \Gamma^k_{ij} X^i Y^j \right) \partial_k\)

Christoffel Symbols

The n^3 smooth functions \Gamma^k_{ij} encode the connection in a chart. They are not the components of a tensor.

Two Pieces

The coordinate partial term plus the Christoffel correction: the correction restores tensoriality after change of chart.

Failure Mode

Setting \Gamma^k_{ij} \equiv 0 in one chart fixes a connection there, but it will not stay zero in other charts.

Reading Habit

Read \nabla_X Y as a corrected partial: the first term is what you would write in Euclidean space; the second term pays the geometric tax for living on a manifold.

Local formula and bookkeepingSlide 4 / 10
Drive home that the Christoffel symbols are the connection's coordinate avatar, but they are not a tensor. Their transformation law has an inhomogeneous term, and this inhomogeneity is exactly what cancels the bad part of the partial derivative. Encourage students to internalize the two-term formula as a paid correction: ordinary partial plus geometric tax.

Existence and Nonuniqueness of Connections

Existence

Every smooth vector bundle E \to M admits a connection. Construction: choose connections on trivializing patches and glue with a partition of unity.

Nonuniqueness

The difference of two connections \nabla^1 - \nabla^2 is C^\infty(M)-bilinear in both slots, so it is a tensor field of type (1,2).

\(\text{Connections on } E \;=\; \nabla^0 \;+\; \Gamma\!\left(T^*M \otimes \mathrm{End}(E)\right)\)

Affine Space Structure

The set of connections is an affine space over the vector space of endomorphism-valued one-forms. Choosing a metric will later pin down a unique preferred connection.

Preview

On a Riemannian manifold, requiring metric compatibility and zero torsion picks out the Levi-Civita connection (Chapter 5).

Affine space of connectionsSlide 5 / 10
Explain why nonuniqueness is structurally informative. Because two connections differ by a tensor, the space of connections is an affine space rather than a vector space. This sets up the Levi-Civita uniqueness theorem in the next chapter as an honest selection problem: among an infinite-dimensional family, the metric and torsion-free conditions pick out one. Mention the partition-of-unity construction briefly to ground existence in standard manifold technology.

Geodesics: Curves with Zero Acceleration

Geodesic Equation

D_t \dot\gamma = \nabla_{\dot\gamma} \dot\gamma = 0

Local ODE

\ddot\gamma^k + \Gamma^k_{ij}(\gamma)\, \dot\gamma^i \dot\gamma^j = 0

Existence

Given p \in M and v \in T_pM, ODE theory gives a unique maximal geodesic with \gamma(0)=p, \dot\gamma(0)=v.

Acceleration measured by the connectionSlide 6 / 10
Position the geodesic as the simplest motion compatible with the connection: a curve whose velocity does not change in the sense the connection measures. Translate the geometric condition into the second-order ODE and emphasize the quadratic-in-velocity term: this is where the Christoffel symbols enter the dynamics. The local existence theorem is a direct consequence of standard ODE theory and should be reassuring rather than mysterious.

Parallel Transport Along a Curve

Parallel Field

A vector field V along \gamma:[a,b] \to M is parallel if D_t V = 0.

Transport Operator

For each v \in T_{\gamma(a)}M there is a unique parallel V with V(a)=v. This defines a linear isomorphism P_\gamma : T_{\gamma(a)}M \to T_{\gamma(b)}M.

Geodesic Restatement

A curve is a geodesic iff its own velocity is parallel along it.

Linear identification of tangent spaces along a curveSlide 7 / 10
Parallel transport is the operational payoff of the connection. Given a curve, the connection furnishes a canonical linear isomorphism between the tangent spaces at its endpoints. Emphasize the words canonical and curve-dependent in the same breath: the isomorphism exists, but it usually depends on the path chosen. The illustration on the sphere previews holonomy, which is the next slide.

Holonomy: Curvature Detected by Loops

Holonomy Group

For a loop \gamma based at p, parallel transport gives P_\gamma \in GL(T_pM). The set of all such maps is the holonomy group \mathrm{Hol}_p(\nabla).

Flat vs. Curved

  • If P_\gamma = \mathrm{id} for every contractible loop, the connection is flat.
  • Nontrivial holonomy around small loops measures curvature.

Forward Pointer

The curvature tensor (Chapter 7) is the infinitesimal generator of holonomy around small loops.

Loops measure what tangent spaces forgetSlide 8 / 10
Holonomy is the slogan version of curvature: a vector parallel-transported around a closed loop need not return to itself. On the sphere, transport around a geodesic triangle rotates the starting vector by an angle equal to the spherical excess. Use this image to motivate the curvature tensor in a later chapter: it is the linearization of holonomy at the level of infinitesimal loops.

Pullback Connection and Derivatives Along Curves

Setup

Let F : N \to M be smooth and let \nabla be a connection on E \to M. Sections of E along F are sections of the pullback bundle F^*E \to N.

Existence and Uniqueness

There is a unique connection F^*\nabla on F^*E such that (F^*\nabla)_X (s \circ F) = \nabla_{dF(X)} s \circ F for every section s of E.

\(D_t V \;=\; (\gamma^* \nabla)_{\partial_t} V \qquad \text{for } V \in \Gamma(\gamma^* TM)\)

Why It Matters

The covariant derivative D_t used in the geodesic equation and parallel transport is exactly the pullback connection on a curve.

Special Cases

Vector fields along a curve, variations of geodesics, and Jacobi fields all live in pulled-back bundles.

Connections travel along mapsSlide 9 / 10
The pullback connection is the technical device that makes “derivative along a curve” precise. Without it, the expression D_t V is a local kludge; with it, the same expression sits inside a clean bundle-theoretic framework. Mention that the same machinery will be used to differentiate variation fields when deriving the first and second variation formulas, so it pays to recognize it now.
