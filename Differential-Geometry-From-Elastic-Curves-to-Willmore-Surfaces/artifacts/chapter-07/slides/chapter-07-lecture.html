Forms As Measuring Devices

Why Ordinary Density Is Not Enough

Scalar Density

A function gives an amount at a point. It must borrow a coordinate area element before it can be integrated.

ordinary sample: \(f(x)\,\Delta u\,\Delta v\)

Differential Form

A form eats tangent vectors. The sign and size are determined by the ordered directions supplied to it.

oriented sample: \(\omega_p(v_1,v_2)\)
Integration must track direction, not only amount.02 / 14
Motivate differential forms by contrasting a scalar density with an oriented measuring device. A scalar can tell us how much material sits at a point, but a form also knows which tangent directions were fed into it and how the orientation of those directions affects the sign.

Alternation

Two-Forms Measure Oriented Area

Definition

A two-form \(\omega_p\) at \(p\) is a bilinear alternating function of two tangent vectors.

\(\omega_p(v,w)=-\omega_p(w,v)\)

Geometric Reading

Magnitude records area-like content; sign records whether the ordered pair agrees with orientation.

Degenerate Case

If \(v\) and \(w\) point along one line, the measured parallelogram collapses and the value is zero.

Alternation is the algebraic shadow of orientation.04 / 14
Use this slide to define the operational meaning of a two-form. It takes an ordered pair of tangent vectors and returns a signed area-like value. Reversing the order flips the sign, and feeding in dependent vectors gives zero, so the form reacts exactly to the oriented parallelogram.
Pullback

Pullback Moves The Measurement

Chart Map

Let \(\Phi:U\subset\mathbb{R}^2\to M\). Coordinate basis vectors become surface tangent vectors \( \Phi_u,\Phi_v \).

Pullback Rule

\((\Phi^\ast\omega)_{(u,v)}(a,b)=\omega_{\Phi(u,v)}(d\Phi(a),d\Phi(b))\)

Teaching Cue

The form is evaluated after the tangent vectors have been transported by the derivative.

Pullback is the coordinate-safe way to compute a geometric measurement.05 / 14
Explain pullback as the act of moving the measuring rule to the parameter square. We do not push little rectangles blindly onto the surface; instead, the surface map differentiates coordinate vectors, and the original form evaluates those transported tangent vectors.
Surface Integration

Surface Integrals Use The Chart But Do Not Belong To It

\(\displaystyle \int_{\Phi(U)}\omega=\int_U \omega_{\Phi(u,v)}(\Phi_u,\Phi_v)\,du\,dv\)

For The Area Form

The special two-form induced by the metric gives the familiar density \( \sqrt{EG-F^2} \).

\(\displaystyle dA=\sqrt{EG-F^2}\,du\wedge dv\)

Coordinate Warning

A denser grid in the parameter plane is not automatically a larger geometric area.

The formula uses coordinates to remove coordinate dependence.06 / 14
The chart supplies parameters and a Jacobian-like area signal, but the integral is intended to describe the geometric patch. The displayed formula is a compact way of saying that the two-form eats the two coordinate tangent vectors before the ordinary du dv integration happens.
Line Integrals

One-Forms Measure Tangent Motion

Curve Measurement

\(\displaystyle \int_\gamma\alpha=\int_a^b \alpha_{\gamma(t)}(\gamma'(t))\,dt\)

A one-form pairs with the curve velocity at every instant.

Reparametrization Check

Changing speed changes \( \gamma' \) and \( dt \) in compensating ways.

Orientation Check

Reversing the curve direction reverses the sign of the integral.

One-forms measure how a curve moves through a field of covectors.07 / 14
Shift from surfaces to curves. A one-form does not merely count length; it pairs with the curve velocity. Reparametrization changes the speed and time variable together, so the measured line integral remains the same when the orientation is preserved.
Orientation

Boundary Orientation Is Part Of The Data

Induced Boundary Direction

The oriented surface determines a direction on each boundary component.

Region With A Hole

The outer boundary and inner boundary must run in opposite senses.

\(\displaystyle \partial(\hbox{annulus})=C_{\rm outer}-C_{\rm inner}\)
A sign error in the boundary can make a true theorem look false.08 / 14
Stress that the direction on a boundary is not a sketching preference. Once the surface orientation is chosen, the induced boundary orientation is fixed. For a region with a hole, the outer loop and inner loop run in opposite senses because the oriented surface must stay on the same side of the moving boundary tangent.
Exterior Derivative

Exterior Derivative Detects Local Circulation

Small-Loop Test

Walk around a tiny oriented rectangle and divide the accumulated one-form measurement by its area.

Result

The limiting density is a two-form \(d\alpha\).

\(\displaystyle d(P\,dx+Q\,dy)=(Q_x-P_y)\,dx\wedge dy\)

Interpretation

It records infinitesimal circulation, not just a partial derivative table.

The derivative of a form is defined by what it measures.09 / 14
Introduce the exterior derivative through a small-loop test. If a one-form accumulates nonzero circulation around tiny rectangles, the normalized limiting density is a two-form. This is the local interior quantity that Stokes will integrate. Emphasize that the sign depends on the same orientation convention used for the tiny boundary loop.
The Balance Law

Stokes' Theorem

Theorem

\(\displaystyle \int_{\partial M}\alpha=\int_M d\alpha\)

Here \(M\) is an oriented surface with oriented boundary, and \(\alpha\) is a smooth one-form near \(M\).

Boundary Side

Measure the one-form while walking around the induced boundary.

Interior Side

Accumulate the exterior derivative over the oriented surface.

Stokes turns a global boundary measurement into an interior accounting identity.10 / 14
State the theorem as a balance law. The left side is the measurement made by walking around the boundary, and the right side is the accumulated local circulation over the oriented surface. The theorem is powerful because every coordinate and triangulation used to compute either side is auxiliary.
Worked Example

Disk Example

Angular One-Form

\(\displaystyle \alpha=\frac12(-y\,dx+x\,dy)\)

Exterior Derivative

\(\displaystyle d\alpha=dx\wedge dy\)

Stokes Computation

\(\displaystyle \int_{\partial D}\alpha=\pi=\int_D dx\wedge dy\)
The sign works because the circle inherits the positive boundary orientation.11 / 14
Work the disk example slowly because it fixes the sign convention. The one-form one half times minus y dx plus x dy measures angular circulation. Its exterior derivative is the standard area form, so the boundary integral around the positively oriented unit circle equals the area of the disk.
Boundary Components

Annulus Sign Check

Stokes On An Annulus

\(\displaystyle \int_{\partial A}\alpha=\int_{C_R}\alpha-\int_{C_r}\alpha=\pi(R^2-r^2)\)

What Can Go Wrong

If both loops are traversed the same way, the missing disk is added instead of subtracted.

\(+\pi R^2\)outer contribution
\(-\pi r^2\)inner contribution
Holes are subtracted by orientation, not by a separate bookkeeping trick.12 / 14
Use the annulus to make the hole orientation concrete. The exterior derivative integrates over the material annulus. The outer boundary contributes positively with the usual counterclockwise direction, while the inner boundary contributes with the opposite orientation, subtracting the missing disk.

Summary

Takeaways

Forms

Forms are tangent-data measuring devices with orientation-sensitive signs.

Pullback

Pullback translates a geometric measurement into coordinates without making the measurement coordinate-owned.

Orientation

Boundary direction is induced by the surface, and holes inherit the opposite sign.

Stokes

The exterior derivative accounts for exactly what the boundary integral accumulates.
