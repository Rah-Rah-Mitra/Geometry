Curve Prototype

Elastic curves teach the course how to think variationally

\(E(\gamma)=\int \kappa^2\,ds\)

Prototype pattern

Define a bending signal, build an energy, vary it, then check the equation computationally.

Curvesthe first variational laboratory
Remind learners that elastic curves are the first complete variational laboratory. Curvature has a visible meaning, an energy, an Euler-Lagrange equation, and concrete reconstruction checks. This prototype later reappears whenever a surface problem is reduced to a one-dimensional curvature signal.
Surface Layer

Surfaces add area, direction pairs, and intrinsic curvature

Metric

Measures lengths, angles, and the area element.

Shape operator

Records how the normal changes across tangent directions.

Gauss curvature

Connects local bending with total topological information.

Surfacesmetric, area, shape, curvature
Surfaces add two-dimensional bookkeeping. The metric supplies area, the shape operator supplies principal curvatures, and Gauss-Bonnet turns total Gaussian curvature into topology. This is the hinge where local differential data starts carrying global topological meaning.
Invariant Lane

Topology marks the quantities smooth motion cannot erase

Orientation

Controls signs in integration and boundary conventions.

Genus

Organizes closed surfaces into deformation classes.

Euler characteristic

Appears inside total Gaussian curvature through Gauss-Bonnet.

\( \int_\Sigma K\,dA = 2\pi\chi(\Sigma) \)

Reading habit

Before comparing curvature energies, ask which topological class is being held fixed.

Topologywhat remains under smooth deformation
This slide should feel like a lock on deformation. Genus, orientation, and Euler characteristic are not numerical decorations; they decide which curvature integrals can change under smooth motion. When a family deforms smoothly, these quantities tell students what should stay fixed in the notebook checks.
Recombination

Willmore geometry is where the course threads meet

\(W=\int H^2\,dA\)

New organizing principle

Conformal transformations become tests, not distractions.

Willmorethreads recombine
Willmore geometry is the reunion point. It uses curvature energy like elastic curves, surface area and mean curvature like surface theory, topology through Gauss-Bonnet, and conformal invariance as a new organizing principle. The slide should make the final chapter feel inevitable rather than isolated.
