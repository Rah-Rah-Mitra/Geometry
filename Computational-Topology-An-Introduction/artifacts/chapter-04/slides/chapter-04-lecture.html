IV.1 / Homology groups

From Simplices To Chain Groups

Choosing an ordering of simplices turns p-chains into coordinate vectors over \(\mathbb F_2\).

Definition

A p-chain is a finite sum \(c=\sum_i a_i\sigma_i\), with \(a_i\in\mathbb F_2\). The p-chains form a vector space \(C_p(K)\).

Computational Reading

  • Basis vector = one simplex.
  • Coefficient 1 = selected; coefficient 0 = absent.
  • Dimension \(n_p=\dim C_p\) counts p-simplices.
n0 = 3 n1 = 3 n2 = 1
simplices become basis vectorschains are inspectable finite data
Make the algebra concrete by naming the basis elements. A p-chain is a finite formal sum of p-simplices, and over F2 the coefficients act like on and off switches. The important move is that geometry has become a coordinate vector.
Chains add by mod two arithmetic

Addition Cancels Shared Pieces

Symmetric Difference

For mod two chains, \(a+b\) keeps exactly the simplices that appear in one chain but not both.

Why This Matters

Cancellation is not a visual trick. It is the arithmetic that makes shared internal faces vanish in a combined chain.

edge \(e\): \(1+1\equiv 0 \pmod 2\)
addition = XOR of simplex indicatorsshared pieces cancel exactly
Use the paired triangles to show why F2 addition is natural for computation. When two chains share an edge, that edge appears twice and disappears. This is the same cancellation that makes symmetric difference and mod two arithmetic agree.
The operator that drops dimension

Boundary Maps

Boundary Homomorphism

The map \(\partial_p:C_p\to C_{p-1}\) sends each p-simplex to the sum of its codimension-one faces, then extends linearly.

Linear Extension

If \(c=\sigma_1+\sigma_2+\sigma_3\), then \(\partial c=\partial\sigma_1+\partial\sigma_2+\partial\sigma_3\).

C2 -> C1 C1 -> C0 C0 -> 0
boundary maps are homomorphismsone formula covers every chain
Present the boundary operator as the machine that drops dimension. Vertices are the boundary of an edge, edges are the boundary of a triangle, and the linear extension is what lets one assemble complicated chains from simple columns.
Fundamental lemma of homology

Why \(\partial\partial = 0\)

Fundamental Lemma

For every p, \(\partial_p\partial_{p+1}=0\). Therefore every p-boundary is a p-cycle.

GF(2) Check

D_p @ D_{p+1} % 2 == 0

triangle check: trueall vertices cancel twice
faces of faces appear in pairsboundaries land inside cycles
Slow down on this lemma because it licenses the quotient definition later. Each lower-dimensional face of a simplex appears exactly twice in the boundary of the boundary. Over F2 those paired appearances cancel, so every boundary is automatically a cycle.
The quotient where holes live

Cycles, Boundaries, Homology

Definitions

\(Z_p=\ker \partial_p\), \(B_p=\operatorname{im}\partial_{p+1}\), and \(H_p=Z_p/B_p\).

Interpretation

  • Cycle: closes up.
  • Boundary: closes because it surrounds a higher chain.
  • Homology class: a surviving cycle modulo filled-in cycles.
\(B_p\subseteq Z_p\) because \(\partial\partial=0\)
homology is cycles modulo boundariesquotients remove explained cycles
Distinguish the two subgroups before taking the quotient. Cycles are chains whose boundary has vanished, while boundaries are cycles already explained by a higher-dimensional chain. Homology keeps the cycles left over after those explained ones are identified.

Maps of complexes become maps of homology

Chain Maps Preserve Boundaries

Chain Map

A sequence \(f_p:C_p(K)\to C_p(L)\) is a chain map when \(\partial f_p=f_{p-1}\partial\) for every p.

Consequence

Cycles map to cycles, boundaries map to boundaries, and \(f\) induces a well-defined map \(H_p(K)\to H_p(L)\).

commuting square homology functor boundary-compatible
structure-preserving maps survive quotientingchain level controls homology level
Explain functoriality as a compatibility rule. A map of complexes induces linear maps on chains, and the square says mapping then taking boundary agrees with taking boundary then mapping. Without this commutation, homology classes would not be transported reliably.
IV.2 / Matrix reduction begins

Boundary Matrices

Matrix Convention

The p-th boundary matrix \(D_p\) has \((p-1)\)-simplices as rows and p-simplices as columns.

Surface Check

D1 @ D2 % 2 == [[0],[0],[0]]

truethree zero rows
entry \(D_p[i,j]=1\) iff face \(i\subset\sigma_j\)
incidence data becomes linear algebramatrix product verifies chain-complex law
Turn the boundary operator into a board of zeros and ones. Rows name lower-dimensional simplices, columns name higher-dimensional simplices, and a one records incidence. The product check D1 times D2 equals zero is the matrix version of boundary squared zero.
Column operations over \(\mathbb F_2\)

Reduction Worked Example

circle graph D1 e01 e12 e20 v0 1 0 1 v1 1 1 0 v2 0 1 1

Rank Formula

\(\beta_0=3-\operatorname{rank}D_0-\operatorname{rank}D_1=1\), and \(\beta_1=3-\operatorname{rank}D_1-\operatorname{rank}D_2=1\).

row/column operations preserve rank circle: (1,1,0)
rank survives elementary operationsone free column = one loop class
Walk through the small circle example as a rank computation. Column operations over F2 preserve rank, and the reduced D1 matrix has two pivots. With three edges and three vertices, the formula gives one connected component and one independent loop.

Euler-Poincare

For a finite simplicial complex, $$\chi(K)=\sum_{p\ge 0}(-1)^p n_p=\sum_{p\ge 0}(-1)^p\beta_p.$$

Why It Works

Cycle and boundary ranks cancel in adjacent dimensions when the alternating sum is expanded.

same chain complex, two alternating tracesuse after every computed Betti vector
Use Euler-Poincare as an audit trail. Alternating simplex counts and alternating Betti counts agree because ranks cancel across the chain complex. It is not a complete invariant, but it is an excellent consistency check for a homology computation.
IV.3 / Chains modulo a subcomplex

Relative Homology Of A Pair

Relative Chain Group

\(C_p(K,K_0)=C_p(K)/C_p(K_0)\). The same boundary descends because \(K_0\) is a subcomplex.

Relative Cycle

A chain c may satisfy \(\partial c\neq 0\) in K, but become a relative cycle when \(\partial c\) is carried by \(K_0\).

relative quotient check true collapse reference part
relative homology measures what remains after K0new classes can appear relative to a pair
Describe relative chains as chains of K with the subcomplex K0 treated as invisible. Boundaries are allowed to land in K0, so a chain that was not an absolute cycle can become a relative cycle. This is the algebra of collapsing the reference part of the space.
Relative computations ignore removable interior clutter

Excision As Local Replacement

Excision Motif

If two pairs keep the same outside data, their relative homology can agree after removing a safely contained part.

Matrix Reading

Order simplices in \(K_0\) first. The lower-right incidence block controls the quotient complex.

relative ranks matchsame complement block
excision is a local replacement theoremrelative block carries the answer
Present excision as permission to replace local clutter without changing the relative answer. Once the same complement is kept, the lower-right incidence block carries the relative computation. The slide's check names that the relative quotient ranks match.
IV.3-IV.4 / The image-kernel rule

Exactness: Image Equals Kernel

Exact At V

For \(U\xrightarrow{f}V\xrightarrow{g}W\), exactness at V means \(\operatorname{im}f=\ker g\).

Toy Sequence Check

rank(im f) == dim(ker g) and g @ f == 0.

im = ker truecomposite zero true

Common Misread

The condition \(g\circ f=0\) is necessary, but exactness also says all elements killed by g came from f.

exactness is a local diagnostic at a termdies next = born before
Exactness should sound operational: everything that dies at the next map was born from the previous map. The toy sequence verifies image equals kernel at the middle term. This is stronger than merely saying the composite is zero.
The pair sequence organizes absolute and relative data

Long Exact Sequence And Connecting Map

Exact Sequence Of A Pair

\(\cdots\to H_p(K_0)\to H_p(K)\to H_p(K,K_0)\xrightarrow{D}H_{p-1}(K_0)\to\cdots\)

Connecting Map

Choose a representative relative cycle c. Its boundary lies in \(K_0\), and \(D[c]=[\partial c]\) one dimension lower.

exact at every stationconnecting map well-defined
relative classes reveal absolute boundary dataexactness constrains all neighboring groups
Focus on the connecting map as the non-obvious arrow. A relative cycle may have a boundary inside K0, and that boundary becomes a lower-dimensional class in K0. The long exact sequence organizes the absolute and relative groups into one diagnostic line.
