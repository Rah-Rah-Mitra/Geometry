Why Duality

One Translation, Four Uses

1

Detect cycles. Cochains are tests on chains, so topology can be seen through evaluations.

2

Reverse incidence. Coboundaries and dual blocks replace faces by cofaces.

3

Pair complements. Poincare and Lefschetz duality compare complementary dimensions.

4

Compute quickly. Alexander duality turns complement connectivity into simplex classification.

Key question: what is preserved by each translation?Slides 3-17 follow the translations in order.
Use this as the motivation map. Duality is not one theorem but a repeating translation: cycles become tests, tests become complementary blocks, complementary blocks become intersections, and intersections become fast classification rules. V.1 Cohomology

Cochains

Definition

A $p$-cochain is a homomorphism

$$\varphi:C_p(K)\to \mathbb Z_2,\qquad C^p=\mathrm{Hom}(C_p,\mathbb Z_2).$$

Evaluation is written like a pairing: $\langle \varphi,c\rangle$.

field = Z2 evaluation = parity
Inspection target: cochains are functions on chains, not new geometric cells yet.Asset: cochain-complex.json
Emphasize that a cochain is an observer of chains. Over Z2 it reports parity, so the evaluation can be read as whether the selected simplices meet the tested chain an odd number of times. This simple parity viewpoint is the seed of the later intersection pairing.
Dual To Boundary

Coboundary

Operational Identity

$$\langle \delta\varphi,c\rangle=\langle\varphi,\partial c\rangle$$
  • $\partial:C_p\to C_{p-1}$ lowers dimension by faces.
  • $\delta:C^{p-1}\to C^p$ raises dimension by cofaces.
  • Because $\partial\partial=0$, we also get $\delta\delta=0$.
delta_0 = boundary_1^T delta_1 delta_0 = 0
Source move: boundary maps dualize into coboundary maps.Computational check: delta delta = 0.
Make the coboundary feel operational. A boundary lowers dimension by taking faces, while the coboundary raises dimension by asking which cofaces see the chosen simplex. The displayed identity is the whole mechanism: evaluating before a boundary equals evaluating after a coboundary.
Cocycle Modulo Coboundary

Cohomology Groups

Definition Pattern

$$Z^p=\ker(\delta:C^p\to C^{p+1}),\qquad B^p=\mathrm{im}(\delta:C^{p-1}\to C^p).$$
$$H^p(K)=Z^p/B^p.$$

Cocycles are tests that vanish on boundaries. Coboundaries are tests produced by lower-dimensional potentials.

Reduced cohomology adjusts degree 0 in parallel with reduced homology.Core quotient: $H^p=Z^p/B^p$.
Treat this slide as the mirror of the homology definition. Cocycles are cochains whose coboundary vanishes, coboundaries are those already produced from one dimension below, and the quotient records the tests that detect genuine topology rather than a local potential.

Hypothesis For Dual Blocks

Combinatorial Manifolds

Local Test

A triangulated $d$-manifold is combinatorial when links have sphere-like local topology:

$$\mathrm{Lk}(\sigma)\simeq S^{d-\dim\sigma-1}.$$

This is the condition that makes the dual block of a $p$-simplex look like a $q$-cell with $p+q=d$.

Use the hypothesis to make dual cells topologically honest.Dimension rule: p + q = d.
Before stating Poincare duality, slow down on the manifold hypothesis. The link condition guarantees that local neighborhoods behave like balls and spheres, so dual blocks have the expected complementary dimensions. Without that local control, the dual block proof can lose its geometric meaning.
Barycentric Subdivision

Dual Blocks

Construction

  • Subdivide $K$ by barycenters of simplices.
  • The dual block $\widehat{\sigma}$ is built from subdivided simplices in which $\sigma$ is the smallest face.
  • If $\sigma<\tau$, then $\widehat{\tau}$ lies in the boundary of $\widehat{\sigma}$.
$$\dim\sigma+\dim\widehat{\sigma}=d.$$

Original SVG asset: primal incidence in blue, dual blocks in teal.

Dual blocks reverse face/coface incidence.Asset: dual-block-torus.svg
Use the diagram as a construction recipe. Barycentric subdivision supplies a vertex for every simplex, and the dual block of sigma consists of subdivided simplices where sigma is the smallest face in the chain. Face relations reverse, which is why the boundary of a dual block is built from cofaces.
First Form

Poincare Duality, First Form

Theorem Shape

For a compact combinatorial $d$-manifold and $p+q=d$:

$$H^p(M)\cong H_q(M).$$

Over $\mathbb Z_2$, the Universal Coefficient Theorem also gives rank symmetry for homology.

phi_q(hat_sigma) = sigma_star commuting square verified on basis blocks
Asset: poincare-proof-graph.jsonProof idea: dual block chains are cochains in disguise.
Present the proof as a commuting square rather than as a mysterious isomorphism. Map each dual block to the cochain that selects the corresponding primal simplex. Both routes around the square select exactly the cofaces of sigma, so the chain-level correspondence descends to homology and cohomology.
Geometric Pairing

Intersection Numbers

Modulo 2 Count

For cycles in complementary dimensions $p+q=d$, define

$$c\cdot d=\#(c\cap d)\pmod 2.$$

Changing representatives by boundaries changes intersections in pairs, so the parity descends to homology classes.

Intersection theory gives the geometric form of duality.Orientations are avoided here because coefficients are Z2.
Intersection is the geometric reading of the same duality map. In complementary dimensions, count intersection points modulo two. The important point is well-definedness: if either cycle is changed by a boundary, intersections are created or removed in pairs, so the parity is unchanged.
Second Form

Perfect Pairing

Poincare Duality, Second Form

$$\#:H_p(M)\times H_q(M)\to\mathbb Z_2,\qquad p+q=d$$

The intersection pairing is perfect: each nonzero class is detected by some complementary class.

$$H_q(M)\cong \mathrm{Hom}(H_p(M),\mathbb Z_2).$$

Matrix Witness

A square pairing matrix is perfect over Z2 exactly when it is invertible.

det 0110 = 1 mod 2

det 0111 = 1 mod 2

Asset: intersection-pairings.jsonPerfect pairing = no invisible homology class.
A perfect pairing means no nonzero class is invisible to all complementary classes. Algebraically, the pairing identifies one homology group with the dual vector space of the other. This is the second form of Poincare duality and the bridge from geometry back to linear algebra.

This comparison prevents a common misconception. The torus and Klein bottle have the same Z2 Betti numbers, but their intersection forms differ. On the Klein bottle, the one-sided curve has self-intersection one after push-off, so the pairing records more than just ranks.
Manifolds With Boundary

Lefschetz Duality

Boundary-Corrected Duality

For compact combinatorial $d$-manifolds with boundary:

$$H^p(M,\partial M)\cong H_q(M),\qquad H^p(M)\cong H_q(M,\partial M).$$

The pairing form is perfect:

$$H_p(M)\times H_q(M,\partial M)\to\mathbb Z_2.$$

Relative classes may end on the boundary; absolute complementary classes still count intersections.

Asset: lefschetz-half-torus.svgThe ball example needs relative top homology to recover duality.
Explain Lefschetz duality as the boundary-corrected version of Poincare duality. Absolute classes pair with relative classes because complementary chains may now have endpoints or boundaries on the boundary of the manifold. The half-torus picture makes the relative generator visible.
Complementary Subcomplexes

Alexander Duality

Theorem Shape

Let $K$ triangulate $S^d$, let $N\subseteq K$, and let $X$ be the complementary subcomplex in the dual block decomposition.

$$\widetilde H_p(N)\cong \widetilde H_{d-p-1}(X).$$
circle in S2 tilde beta_1(N)=1 tilde beta_0(X)=1
Asset: alexander-complement.jsonReduced groups make the component count behave correctly.
Alexander duality shifts attention from a manifold to its complement in a sphere. A p-dimensional reduced homology class in N corresponds to a complementary class in the dual block complex X of dimension d minus p minus one. The toy circle in S2 shows the dimension shift.

Now turn the theorems into an algorithm. Adding one simplex either creates a new class in its own dimension or kills a class one dimension lower. Once a simplex has been classified as positive or negative, the Betti update is constant time.
Fast Classification In S3

S3 Classification

Vertices

First vertex is negative because it removes the empty reduced class. Later vertices are positive.

Edges

Negative when endpoints lie in different components; otherwise positive. Maintain components with union-find.

Tetrahedra

In a triangulated $S^3$, the last tetrahedron is positive and all earlier tetrahedra are negative.

Triangles

Scan backward through dual blocks. A triangle is read through the connectivity of its dual edge.

Runtime after classification: one Betti update per simplex.Union-find contributes inverse-Ackermann practical cost.
For complexes in S3, the low and high dimensional classifications become cheap. Vertices and edges are handled with ordinary union-find on components, while tetrahedra and triangles are handled from the complementary dual perspective. Alexander duality justifies reading triangles through reverse dual edges.
Recap

Recap / Instructor Prompt

What Students Should Be Able To Trace

  • Boundary incidence dualizes to coboundary incidence.
  • Cohomology records cocycles modulo coboundaries.
  • Dual blocks convert cochains into complementary chains.
  • Intersection pairings are perfect on compact combinatorial manifolds.
  • Lefschetz adds relative groups; Alexander studies complements.
  • Positive/negative simplex classification drives prefix Betti updates.

Instructor Prompt

Pick one simplex $\sigma$ in a triangulated 3-manifold. Explain how it appears as:

  1. a basis chain,
  2. a basis cochain $\sigma^*$,
  3. a dual block $\widehat{\sigma}$,
  4. a possible intersection witness, and
  5. a positive or negative update in a filtration.
Where does the argument use compactness, the link condition, or the sphere complement?
