Area and defect

Area Must Be Rebuilt When Rectangles Disappear

Euclidean Habit

Rectangles make area feel like base times height, and triangles inherit half of that formula.

Hyperbolic Obstruction

The needed rectangle theory is not available, so area must be characterized by behavior rather than a familiar construction.

Operational Axioms

Congruent triangles have equal area, and cutting a triangle into two pieces adds the two areas.

Area survives as a rule for triangles before it becomes a formula.
concept: invariance plus additivityno copied figures
This slide turns area into a definition problem. In Euclidean geometry, rectangles provide the base times height story, but hyperbolic geometry lacks the rectangle supply needed for that proof. Ask students to notice that invariance and additivity are still sensible even when the old construction disappears.
Defect becomes area

The Missing Angle Measures the Triangle

Formula

\(K(T)=\pi-A-B-C\)

With curvature normalized to \(-1\), the area \(K(T)\) is the triangle defect in radians.

Bound

Since the angle sum is positive, a finite hyperbolic triangle has area below \(\pi\).

Teaching point. A larger triangle can have a larger defect, but not an arbitrarily large triangle area.

defect is an invariant, not a drawing artifactunits: radians
Treat defect as the chapter's first major compression. A triangle whose angle sum is below pi has a positive missing amount, and in curvature minus one that missing amount is area. The bound is not just a curiosity; it explains why hyperbolic triangles have a maximum possible area.
Angle of parallelism

A Distance Gauge Disguised as a Direction

Limiting Parallel

From a point at distance \(d\) from a line, the boundary between crossing and noncrossing rays makes angle \(\Pi(d)\).

Bolyai-Lobachevsky Formula

\(\Pi(d)=2\arctan(e^{-d})\)

Large distance narrows the limiting angle; near the line the angle approaches a right angle.

The same geometry appears as a ray fan and as a decaying function.
notebook lab: angle-of-parallelism decayfunction view
The angle of parallelism should be read as a distance-to-direction converter. Moving a point farther from a line narrows the limiting parallel angle. Have students inspect the fan first, then read the graph as the same geometric statement expressed by a decaying function.
Function bridge

\(\Pi(d)\) Links Circular and Hyperbolic Functions

Angle Formula

\(\Pi(d)=2\arctan(e^{-d})\)

\(\sin \Pi(d)=\operatorname{sech} d\)

\(\cos \Pi(d)=\tanh d\)

\(\tan \Pi(d)=\operatorname{csch} d\)

\(\Pi(d^*)=\frac{\pi}{2}-\Pi(d)\)

The function \(\Pi\) lets limiting-parallel geometry speak in analytic identities.
The analytic bridge keeps the limiting-parallel origin visible.
KaTeX formulas rendered in browserSTIX math fallback declared
Here the class sees why the formula for Pi is more than a named function. Its sine, cosine, and tangent connect circular language to hyperbolic functions. Emphasize that these identities allow synthetic limiting parallels to talk to analytic expressions without erasing their geometric origin.
Cycles

Boundary Contact Classifies Hyperbolic Curves

Circle

Euclidean circle wholly inside the disk.

Horocycle

Circle internally tangent to the ideal boundary.

Equidistant Curve

Circle cutting the boundary nonorthogonally.

Hypercycle

Curve at fixed distance from a geodesic.

In the disk, Euclidean appearance is less important than boundary behavior.
classification target: relation to ideal circlechapter vocabulary
Cycles are where the Poincare disk becomes a sorting machine. A Euclidean circle inside the disk may represent a hyperbolic circle, a horocycle, an equidistant curve, or a hypercycle depending on its boundary relation. The important habit is to classify by incidence with the ideal boundary, not by Euclidean appearance alone.
The pseudosphere

A Faithful Patch Is Not the Whole Plane

Local Model

A horocyclic sector can be represented isometrically on a surface of revolution.

Global Warning

No smooth complete surface in Euclidean three-space captures the entire hyperbolic plane without obstruction.

Teaching Use

The pseudosphere makes negative curvature tangible while preserving the difference between local and complete models.

surface intuitionlocal isometry, global boundary
Use the pseudosphere slide to separate local faithfulness from global completion. The surface gives an isometric patch of the hyperbolic plane, not the whole plane. This is a good moment to remind students that a beautiful physical model can be mathematically honest and still have a boundary.
Hyperbolic functions

\(\sinh\) and \(\cosh\) Are the Algebra of the Hyperbola

Core Identity

\(\cosh^2 u-\sinh^2 u=1\)

Parametric Meaning

The branch \(x=\cosh u,\ y=\sinh u\) traces \(x^2-y^2=1\), using \(u\) as a hyperbolic angle parameter.

Why It Matters

Hyperbolic trigonometry uses these functions where Euclidean formulas use circular sine and cosine.

The hyperbola supplies the algebraic functions used by side lengths.
analytic bridgenegative curvature dialect
The hyperbolic functions are introduced as the algebraic dialect of negative curvature. Point out the hyperbola identity before displaying formulas. Students should see sinh and cosh as measurement tools with geometric meaning, not as unrelated functions imported from calculus for convenience.
Hyperbolic right triangles

Right-Triangle Trigonometry Changes the Side Functions

Standard Right Triangle

\(\sin A=\frac{\sinh a}{\sinh c}\quad \cos A=\frac{\tanh b}{\tanh c}\)

\(\cosh c=\cosh a\cosh b\)

For very small sides, the first terms recover familiar Euclidean approximations such as \(c^2\approx a^2+b^2\).

side lengths use sinh, cosh, tanhlocal Euclidean limit
This slide is the first trigonometric payoff. In a right hyperbolic triangle, side lengths enter through sinh, cosh, and tanh. The local Euclidean limit matters pedagogically because it explains why ordinary trigonometry remains a good approximation for tiny triangles while failing globally.
All triangles

The Laws of Sines and Cosines Survive in Hyperbolic Form

Law of Cosines

\(\cosh c=\cosh a\cosh b-\sinh a\sinh b\cos C\)

Law of Sines

\(\frac{\sin A}{\sinh a}=\frac{\sin B}{\sinh b}=\frac{\sin C}{\sinh c}\)

These formulas are analogous to Euclidean ones, but the length side is filtered through hyperbolic functions.

The familiar side-angle grammar remains, but the metric is different.
general triangle formulascurvature normalized to k=1
General triangle laws show that the measurement language is not restricted to right triangles. The formulas resemble Euclidean laws, but the side lengths pass through hyperbolic functions. Ask students to compare the two columns and identify which pieces survive only as infinitesimal approximations.
Lambert quadrilaterals

Complementary Segments Package Parallel Angles

Complement Rule

\(\Pi(x)+\Pi(x^*)=\frac{\pi}{2}\)

Distance Translation

\(\sinh x^*=\operatorname{csch} x\)

One segment determines the other, so a right-angle configuration can encode a limiting-parallel fact.

Complementary segment relations turn a quadrilateral into a formula device.
right angles as measuring structureparallel angle complement
Lambert quadrilaterals make the complement relation visible. The paired segment x and its complementary partner x star are linked by their angles of parallelism. This slide is useful for slowing down the algebra and letting students see why a right-angle framework can encode distance information.
Coordinate dictionaries

Different Coordinates Reveal Different Invariants

Poincare

Good for angles: geodesics are diameters or boundary-orthogonal arcs.

Klein

Good for incidence: geodesics appear as chords and betweenness is visually direct.

Later Systems

Beltrami, Lobachevsky, and Weierstrass coordinates package algebraic tests for lines and distance.

Model changes are translations of meaning, not changes of geometry.
angle, incidence, and algebraic distance viewscoordinate literacy
Coordinate systems are not competing definitions of the plane; they are dictionaries. Poincare coordinates favor angles, Klein coordinates favor chords, and the later Beltrami, Lobachevsky, and Weierstrass systems package distance and line tests differently. Stress that each coordinate choice reveals one invariant while hiding another.
Circles and triangle data

Hyperbolic Circle Measurements Encode Triangle Angles

Bolyai-Style Ratio

\(O_a:O_b:O_c=\sin A:\sin B:\sin C\)

Area Reminder

\(K=\pi-A-B-C\)

Global circle data, side data, and angle data become mutually readable through the hyperbolic trigonometric system.

circle measurementstriangle diagnostics
Circle formulas extend the trigonometric story to global triangle data. Bolyai's sine relation can be read as a statement about the circumferences of comparison circles. Keep the discussion at the level of invariant meaning: the formulas make side and angle data communicate through hyperbolic radius.

Instructor-ready activity

Seminar Prompt: Compute One Diagnostic, Explain One Meaning

Group Tasks

  • Compute its defect and interpret the area.
  • Choose a distance \(d\) and compare \(\Pi(d)\) with a limiting ray sketch.
  • Classify one disk circle by boundary contact.

Report Rule
