Application 1 - Chemistry

Chemistry: Molecules As Typed E(3) Graphs

Symmetry

Atom relabeling is a graph permutation. Coordinates transform under translations and rotations in E(3).

Task constraints

Property prediction usually wants invariant scalars; conformer generation or force prediction wants equivariant coordinates or vectors.

Sanity check

D(X) = D(RX + t)

Molecular graphs plus coordinatesE(3) and permutations
Emphasize that a molecule is not merely a graph and not merely coordinates. Atom types, bonds, distances, angles, and conformers all matter. A property predictor should ignore rigid motion and atom ordering, while an equivariant generator should move coordinates consistently.
Application 2 - Proteins

Proteins: Sequence, Contact, And Frame Geometry

Representations
  • Residue sequence for local biochemical context.
  • Contact graph for folded spatial neighborhoods.
  • Frames and gauges for directional interactions.
Constraint

Predicted structures should rotate and translate with the protein, while labels such as function or binding class should be invariant.

Sequence is not the whole geometryGauge choices must not leak labels
Present proteins as multi-scale geometric objects. Sequence order carries local biochemical context, contact graphs express folded neighborhoods, and residue frames make orientation-dependent interactions visible. The modeling challenge is to use all three without treating arbitrary frame choices as biological signals.
Application 3 - Recommenders

Recommenders: Bipartite Symmetry With Asymmetric Goals

Symmetry

Users may be permuted among users and items among items, but swapping the two node types changes the problem.

Task

Predict and rank likely edges, often with implicit feedback and sparse observations rather than clean labels.

Constraint pressure

Cold start, popularity bias, temporal drift, and exposure bias determine whether a geometric prior is useful.

Bipartite graphPermutation within type, not across type
Point out the subtlety in recommendation systems. User and item identities can be permuted within their own types, but the two sides are not interchangeable. The task is a ranked edge prediction problem constrained by sparsity, feedback bias, and changing catalogs.
Application 4 - Traffic

Traffic: Road Graphs Carry Time

Domain

A road network with sensor readings or edge speeds attached at each time step.

Symmetry and stability

Node labels are arbitrary; short time shifts suggest shared filters; small sensor gaps should not break the forecast.

Task constraint

ETA and congestion prediction are latency-sensitive, so useful locality is both mathematical and operational.

Spatiotemporal graphLocal predictions under missing data
Explain that traffic forecasting combines a fixed spatial graph with moving temporal signals. The road network restricts message passing, time shifts suggest shared temporal filters, and the task constraint is operational: predictions must be local, timely, and robust to missing sensors.
Applications 5 and 6 - Vision and games

Vision And Games: Structured Grids, Different Tasks

Vision: grid signal

Local filters and translation sharing are useful, but viewpoint, scale, and object geometry decide the readout.

Games: state and legal moves

Board symmetries can share evidence, but action legality and strategic dynamics define the output.

Same grid habit, different task behaviorInvariant score versus equivariant action map
Compare the two grid-like domains carefully. Images often ask for translation-stable classification or dense equivariant maps, while games ask for legal actions under board symmetries and strategic state transitions. Similar locality does not mean identical readouts or constraints.
Application 7 - Language and audio

Language And Audio: Order, Causality, And Soft Graphs

Soft graph view

Attention creates edges from content, but the sequence still carries position, order, and sometimes causality.

Audio view

Waveforms and spectrograms carry shift, scale, and phase structure; stable features often matter more than exact alignment.

Warning

A permutation-invariant set model can destroy syntax, rhythm, or causal information that the task needs.

Sequence is a geometryAttention is not permission to ignore order
Use language and audio to separate sequence geometry from grid geometry. Tokens and samples have order, attention builds a data-dependent graph, and many tasks impose causality. Translation-like shifts may matter for audio, while word position can change meaning.
Application 8 - Healthcare

Healthcare: Heterogeneous Graphs Under Constraints

Domain

Patients, visits, diagnoses, procedures, labs, medications, clinicians, and devices form typed temporal relations.

Symmetry

Codes can be permuted within a vocabulary, but type, time, and causal order cannot be ignored.

Task constraints

Risk scores and recommendations need calibration, uncertainty, privacy boundaries, and robustness to sparse records.

Typed temporal graphGeometry plus governance constraints
Keep the healthcare slide grounded in task constraints rather than hype. Patient histories, diagnoses, procedures, labs, and medications form heterogeneous temporal graphs. Useful models must respect type structure, irregular sampling, privacy boundaries, missingness, and calibrated uncertainty.
Application 9 - Physics

Physics: Particles, Fields, And Conservation

Strict invariances

Translations, rotations, permutations, and physical frame choices are usually not data augmentation conveniences; they are part of the law.

Task outputs

Scalar energies are invariant, vector forces are equivariant, and field updates must respect coordinate changes.

Extra constraints

Conservation of energy, momentum, charge, or mass may impose structure that symmetry alone does not express.

Particles and fieldsConservation narrows the admissible maps
Frame physical prediction as the strictest symmetry test in the deck. Particles and fields live in coordinate systems chosen by observers, so rotations, translations, permutations, and sometimes Lorentz structure are not optional details. Conservation laws add constraints beyond equivariance.
Application 10 - VR and AR

VR And AR: Pose Graphs In A Moving World

Symmetry

World and device coordinates are linked by SE(3) transformations; anchors should remain consistent as the user moves.

Task constraints

Low latency, temporal smoothing, occlusion handling, and stable surface maps determine whether geometry feels correct.

Failure mode

A small pose inconsistency becomes visible as sliding objects, incorrect depth, or misregistered hands.

Online pose and mesh estimationSE(3) consistency under latency
Describe VR and AR as online geometry under latency. The system estimates cameras, hands, surfaces, and anchors while the user moves. SE(3) consistency, temporal filtering, occlusion reasoning, and mesh updates matter because visual errors become embodied errors.

What these checks mean

They are finite, inspectable claims that the notebook construction obeys the advertised symmetry or structural assumption.

What they do not mean

They do not prove a benchmark result. They make a small domain transformation concrete enough to debug.

Failure modes

Common Symmetry Mismatches

Diagnostic question

After a transformation, should the answer stay fixed, move with the input, change smoothly, or change completely?

Implementation question

Is the behavior built into the layer, learned from data augmentation, or merely hoped for after training?

Validation question

Can the notebook create a small transformed example where the expected residual is visible and numerical?

Geometry should constrain behaviorNot every symmetry is desirable
Warn that geometric language can become decorative when the task is not specified. Invariance can erase important orientation, equivariance can be wasted on scalar decisions, locality can miss long-range constraints, and augmentation can hide rather than fix a wrong representation.
Recap

Takeaways

One blueprint, many domains

Chemistry, proteins, recommenders, traffic, vision, games, language/audio, healthcare, physics, and VR/AR differ because their domain objects and tasks differ.

The enduring habit

Translate every architecture claim into an executable claim about transformations, output behavior, and a residual that can be checked.

Lecture close

The application atlas is not a catalog; it is a method for asking the right geometric questions before training begins.
