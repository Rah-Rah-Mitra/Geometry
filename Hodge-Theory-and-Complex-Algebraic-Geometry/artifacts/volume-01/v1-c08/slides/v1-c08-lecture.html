Hypercohomology

Cohomology of a Complex Requires a Total Complex

Definition

For a complex of sheaves \(\mathcal K^\bullet\), \(\mathbb H^n(X,\mathcal K^\bullet)\) is computed by resolving \(\mathcal K^\bullet\), forming the total complex, then taking degree \(n\) cohomology.

Trap

Do not take sheaf cohomology term by term and stop. The total differential combines the resolution direction and the complex direction.

Stress that hypercohomology is not an exotic replacement for cohomology. It is the natural way to remember both directions of a resolution: the sheaf direction and the complex direction. The total complex is the central object, and ordinary cohomology appears only after the two directions have been honestly combined.
Holomorphic de Rham complex

A Small Complex Resolves a Constant Sheaf

Comparison statement

\(0 \to \mathbb C_X \to \mathcal O_X \xrightarrow{d} \Omega_X^1 \xrightarrow{d} \Omega_X^2 \to \cdots\)

On a complex manifold, this complex computes \(H^\bullet(X,\mathbb C)\) through hypercohomology.

Why it matters

The Hodge filtration begins by truncating form degree: \(F^p\Omega_X^\bullet = \Omega_X^{\ge p}\).

Frame the holomorphic de Rham theorem as a comparison statement. The complex of holomorphic forms is much smaller than the complex of smooth forms, yet on a complex manifold it resolves the constant sheaf. The slide should make students ask which analytic lemma makes that small complex large enough.
Filtered complexes

A Filtration Is a Controlled Loss of Resolution

Data

A filtered complex is a complex \(K^\bullet\) with subcomplexes \(F^pK^\bullet\) nested in \(p\).

Reading rule

The spectral sequence first sees the graded pieces \(\operatorname{Gr}_F^p K^\bullet\), then tries to reconstruct filtered cohomology.

The filtration slide is the first place where Hodge bookkeeping becomes visible. Emphasize that a filtration does not merely hide information; it creates successive quotients that are easier to read. The associated graded object is the shadow seen by a spectral sequence, not automatically the whole cohomology group.
Spectral sequence pages

Every Page Is a Provisional Survivor Ledger

Page recursion

\(E_{r+1}^{p,q} = H(E_r^{p,q}, d_r)\)

Interpretation

An \(E_1\) class is a candidate. It becomes a stable graded piece only after all relevant page differentials have been accounted for.

Walk slowly through the page diagram. Students often memorize the bidegree of the differential but miss the interpretation: each page is a provisional ledger of surviving classes. A dot on E1 is only a candidate; a later arrow can identify it as a boundary or move information to a different bidegree.

Logarithmic complexes

Open Varieties Need Boundary-Aware Forms

Local model

\(\Omega_{\bar X}^1(\log D)\) is generated by \(dz_i/z_i\) along divisor coordinates and by \(dz_j\) away from the boundary.

Purpose

Logarithmic forms compute cohomology of the open manifold while keeping compactification data visible.

Introduce logarithmic forms as the controlled way to let poles appear along a normal-crossings boundary. The goal is not to compute residues in detail, but to show why open varieties require a boundary-aware complex. The logarithmic complex records forms on the compactification while remembering how they meet the divisor.
Mixed Hodge bookkeeping

Do Not Confuse the Two Filtrations

Hodge filtration \(F^\bullet\)

Decreasing filtration from form degree.

\(F^pH^n \supset F^{p+1}H^n\)

Question: how many holomorphic degrees survive?

Weight filtration \(W_\bullet\)

Increasing filtration from boundary depth and singularity data.

\(W_{m-1}H^n \subset W_mH^n\)

Question: how much boundary complexity is present?

Lecture warning

Mixed Hodge structure is not a single refined Hodge decomposition. It is a vector space with compatible filtrations whose graded pieces carry pure Hodge structures.

The two-filtration slide should separate Hodge and weight data with discipline. The Hodge filtration is decreasing and comes from form degree. The weight filtration is increasing and comes from boundary depth. Mixed Hodge theory is powerful precisely because these two ledgers coexist without being the same ledger.

Common misreadings

Three Questions Before Trusting a Page

Is this term final?

An \(E_1\) or \(E_2\) entry may still support or receive a differential.

Which degree?

The cohomological target uses total degree \(n=p+q\), not a row or column alone.

Which filtration?

Hodge and weight filtrations answer different questions and have opposite indexing habits.

Instructor prompt

Given one visible differential on an \(E_1\) grid, predict which class dies, which class survives, and which associated graded piece changes.

Use the pitfalls slide as an active diagnostic. Ask students which visible object is provisional, which degree is total, and which filtration is being used. This converts common mistakes into quick checks they can apply when reading later proofs involving filtered complexes, logarithmic forms, or mixed Hodge structures.
