Orientation

The Compact Kahler Manifold As a Meeting Point

What the Hypothesis Buys

  • Forms split by type: \(\Omega^k=\oplus_{p+q=k}\Omega^{p,q}\).
  • The operator \(d=\partial+\bar\partial\) becomes compatible with metric adjoints.
  • The Kahler identities align analytic harmonicity with complex type.
  • The Kahler class acts by Lefschetz operators \(L\) and \(\Lambda\).
Chapter 0Orientation
The opening conceptual move is to treat Kahler geometry as a compatibility condition rather than as a decorative metric assumption. The same manifold carries a complex structure, a symplectic form, and a Riemannian metric, and the closedness of the Kahler form is what later turns formal decompositions into theorems about cohomology.
Preliminaries

Local Analytic Data Feeds Global Cohomology

Analytic Side

  • Several complex variables: propagation and the local \(\bar\partial\)-problem.
  • Complex manifolds: atlases, tangent splitting, integrability.
  • Kahler metrics: Hermitian data plus closed fundamental form.

Cohomological Side

  • Sheaves measure failure of local data to glue globally.
  • Fine and acyclic resolutions turn sheaf cohomology into complexes.
  • Dolbeault and de Rham comparisons become calculational bridges.
Chapters 1-4Preliminaries
Frame the preliminary chapters as infrastructure with a purpose. The local theory of holomorphic functions and the dbar equation is not a detour; it is what lets the Dolbeault complex resolve holomorphic sheaves. Sheaf cohomology then records global obstructions in a language that can be compared with differential forms.
Hodge Theorem

Harmonic Representatives Turn Analysis Into Cohomology

Core Analytic Statement

On a compact Riemannian manifold, each de Rham class has a unique harmonic representative: \(H^k(X,\mathbb{R})\cong \mathcal{H}^k(X)\).

The operator \(\Delta=dd^\ast+d^\ast d\) supplies the finite-dimensional kernel.

  • For lecture: pause on uniqueness, because it is the bridge from infinite-dimensional forms to finite-dimensional topology.
  • For computation: finite matrix Laplacians model the same kernel/image bookkeeping.
Chapter 5Harmonic forms and cohomology
This slide is the analytic pivot of the volume. Stress that harmonic forms are not merely convenient representatives; they make cohomology visible as a kernel of an elliptic operator. Once cohomology classes have canonical representatives, later algebraic and complex decompositions can be studied through operators on forms.
Kahler Identities

Type Decomposition Survives Passing to Cohomology

Compact Kahler Consequences

  • \(\Delta_d=2\Delta_{\partial}=2\Delta_{\bar\partial}\), so harmonic forms split by \((p,q)\).
  • \(H^k(X,\mathbb{C})=\oplus_{p+q=k}H^{p,q}(X)\).
  • Conjugation exchanges \(H^{p,q}\) and \(H^{q,p}\).
  • Hard Lefschetz organizes cohomology around powers of \([\omega]\).
Chapter 6The case of Kahler manifolds
Now the Kahler condition pays off. The Laplacians associated to d, partial, and dbar become proportional, so harmonic representatives can be chosen with pure complex type. The Hodge diamond should be presented as a bookkeeping device for actual subspaces, not merely as a table of numbers.
Pure Hodge Structures

Abstract the Linear Algebra Produced by Geometry

Data

A weight \(k\) Hodge structure is a rational vector space \(V_{\mathbb{Q}}\) with \(V_{\mathbb{C}}=\oplus_{p+q=k}V^{p,q}\) and \(\overline{V^{p,q}}=V^{q,p}\).

Rigidity

A polarisation \(Q\) imposes bilinear positivity and orthogonality conditions, turning a decomposition into controlled metric geometry.

Chapter 7Hodge structures and polarisations
This is where the course changes viewpoint from manifolds to the linear algebra they produce. Make clear that a Hodge structure remembers a rational lattice, a complex decomposition, and conjugation symmetry. The polarisation is not optional decoration; it is the positivity package that makes period domains and rigidity statements possible.
Filtered Complexes

Spectral Sequences Organize What a Filtration Reveals

Lecture Emphasis

  • A filtration \(F^\bullet C\) gives associated graded data before the whole cohomology is known.
  • The holomorphic de Rham complex links sheaf cohomology to de Rham cohomology.
  • Degeneration statements explain when early-page data already determines the answer.
Chapter 8Holomorphic de Rham complexes
Students often experience spectral sequences as notation before meaning. Use the grid as a disciplined accounting device: a filtered complex first exposes graded pieces, then the differentials measure which preliminary classes fail to survive. In this volume, that machinery prepares both Hodge filtrations and later mixed or refined invariants.
Families

Topology Can Stay Fixed While Hodge Data Moves

Deformation Vocabulary

  • A smooth proper family gives locally constant cohomology.
  • The complex structures on fibers vary over the base.
  • The Kodaira-Spencer class records the first-order change of complex structure.
  • The Gauss-Manin connection differentiates flat cohomology classes.
Chapter 9Families and deformations
The important contrast is between local topological triviality and nontrivial variation of complex structure. A family can carry the same underlying cohomology groups from fiber to fiber, while the subspaces making up the Hodge filtration rotate. This contrast motivates the period map of the next chapter.
Variations of Hodge Structure

The Period Map Is Constrained by Horizontality

Griffiths Transversality

\(\nabla F^p \subseteq F^{p-1}\otimes\Omega^1_B\)

The Hodge filtration may move, but its derivative is forced to lower the filtration by at most one step.

Chapter 10Variations of Hodge structure
Present the period map as the global visualization of moving Hodge filtrations. It does not wander freely in a flag variety: the Gauss-Manin connection and holomorphicity impose the horizontal tangent condition. This is the first major instance where differential geometry restricts the possible motion of algebraic or complex-geometric data.
Cycles and Classes

Algebraic Cycles Land on the Diagonal

Cycle-Class Question

  • A codimension \(k\) algebraic cycle determines a cohomology class.
  • For smooth projective geometry, that class has type \((k,k)\).
  • The Hodge conjecture asks whether rational diagonal classes come from algebraic cycles.
  • Correspondences act as controlled operators on cohomology.
Chapter 11Hodge classes
The final part returns from abstract cohomology to geometry. Algebraic cycles are subvarieties or formal combinations of subvarieties, and their cohomology classes occupy the diagonal Hodge components. The lecture goal is to make the Hodge conjecture feel like a precise visibility question about which diagonal classes geometry can actually produce.
Secondary Invariants

When the Primary Class Vanishes, Periods May Remain

Deligne-Beilinson and Abel-Jacobi

  • Homologically trivial cycles have zero primary cohomology class.
  • Integration over a bounding chain can still define a period modulo a lattice.
  • The Abel-Jacobi map packages that secondary information into a complex torus.
Common Misread

Vanishing in ordinary cohomology does not mean the cycle has become geometrically invisible.

Chapter 12Deligne-Beilinson cohomology and Abel-Jacobi
This slide closes the volume by showing why the story cannot stop with ordinary cohomology. A cycle can have a zero primary class and still carry a period invariant. The Abel-Jacobi construction should be described as a secondary measurement, sensitive to how a bounding chain integrates against Hodge-theoretic forms modulo periods.
