Five Pieces Of The Kahler Package

Identities

Kahler commutators connect \(\partial\), \(\bar\partial\), \(L\), \(\Lambda\), and adjoints.

Laplacians

The de Rham and Dolbeault Laplacians have the same harmonic kernel up to constants.

Decomposition

Harmonic forms split by bidegree, giving \(H^k(X,\mathbb C)=\oplus_{p+q=k}H^{p,q}(X)\).

Lefschetz

\(L(\alpha)=\omega\wedge\alpha\) and \(\Lambda=L^\ast\) form weight strings with \(H=[\Lambda,L]\).

Primitive

Primitive classes are the lowest pieces of those strings before repeated raising by \(L\).

Index

The Hodge index form has a controlled signature, not ordinary positive definiteness.

Core Mechanism

The Kahler Identities Tie Operators Together

Operator Pattern

The identities have the schematic form

\([\Lambda,\partial] \sim \bar\partial^\ast,\quad [\Lambda,\bar\partial] \sim \partial^\ast\)

and their adjoint companions.

The exact constants depend on convention; the structural fact is that lowering by \(\Lambda\) converts a Dolbeault differential into the opposite adjoint.

Present the identities as a translation device. The formulas are often remembered as signs and constants, but the pedagogical point is stronger: the Kahler metric makes the Dolbeault differentials and Lefschetz lowering operator interact so tightly that adjoints become commutators.
Analytic Consequence

One Harmonic Theory, Several Names

Laplacian Comparison
\(\Delta_d = 2\Delta_{\partial}=2\Delta_{\bar\partial}\)

on a compact Kahler manifold, after using the compatible adjoints and standard normalization.

de Rham

\(\ker \Delta_d\)

harmonic representatives for singular cohomology with complex coefficients

Dolbeault

\(\ker \Delta_{\bar\partial}\)

harmonic representatives that preserve bidegree \((p,q)\)

Payoff

\(H^k(X,\mathbb C)=\bigoplus H^{p,q}(X)\)

type splitting becomes a theorem about the same finite-dimensional kernel

This is the bridge from differential operators to topology. Stress that compactness gives Hodge theory, while the Kahler identities identify which Laplacian is being used. Without this comparison, a de Rham class need not have a harmonic representative that visibly decomposes by complex type.

Algebraic Consequence

The Kahler Class Builds Lefschetz Strings

Lefschetz Decomposition

\(H^k(X)=\bigoplus_r L^r P^{k-2r}(X)\)

Primitive Condition

A class is primitive when it is killed by the appropriate next power of \(L\), equivalently when it begins a finite Lefschetz string.

Move slowly through the string picture. The operator L raises degree by wedging with the Kahler form, while Lambda lowers and H records the weight. Primitive classes are not minor exceptions; they are the seeds from which the entire Lefschetz decomposition is rebuilt.
Finite Model Check

The Commutator \(H=[\Lambda,L]\) Records Weights

sl2 Pattern
\([H,L]=2L,\quad [H,\Lambda]=-2\Lambda,\quad [\Lambda,L]=H\)

These relations turn powers of \(L\) into structured strings rather than unrelated wedge products.

Notebook Diagnostics

  • \(H=[\Lambda,L]\) has the expected toy weights.
  • Boundary-square norm is \(0.0\).
  • Smallest model Laplacian eigenvalue is \(1.0\).
  • Fourier unitarity residual is approximately \(3.16\cdot 10^{-16}\).
The finite checks are not offered as proofs of the theorems. They are a debugging environment for the same bookkeeping. When students see the commutator relations hold in a small exterior algebra model, the abstract Lefschetz decomposition becomes a controlled representation-theoretic pattern.
Signature Warning

The Hodge Index Form Is Controlled, Not Positive

Common Misread

The intersection form is not simply a positive inner product. On primitive middle-degree classes, the sign is governed by the Hodge-Riemann bilinear relations.

Surface Memory

For a projective surface, the Neron-Severi intersection pairing has one positive direction, represented by the ample Kahler class, and negative directions transverse to it.

The Hodge index theorem is a good place to pause and prevent a misleading intuition. Kahler metrics produce positive norms on forms, but the intersection pairing has its own signature. The primitive decomposition tells us exactly where the signs live.

Takeaways

What Compact Kahler Geometry Buys

Analytic Control

Kahler identities compare Laplacians and let harmonic forms carry complex type.

Cohomology Splitting

De Rham cohomology decomposes as a direct sum of Hodge bidegrees.

Lefschetz Algebra

\(L\), \(\Lambda\), and \(H\) organize classes into finite \(\mathfrak{sl}_2\) strings.

Primitive Pieces

Primitive classes are the irreducible starting points for Lefschetz decomposition.

Signature Data

The Hodge index theorem records a precise sign pattern, not naive positivity.
