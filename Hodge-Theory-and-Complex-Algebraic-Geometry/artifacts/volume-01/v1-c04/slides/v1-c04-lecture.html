Sheaves And Cohomology

A local-to-global machine for Hodge theory: sheaves encode compatible local data, while cohomology measures the exactness lost after taking global sections.

Lecture Thesis

Sheaf cohomology is the controlled bookkeeping of global gluing failure. A resolution turns that failure into ordinary cohomology of a complex.

Persistent Trap

Exactness on stalks does not mean exactness after applying global sections. The functor \(\Gamma(X,-)\) is left-exact, not generally exact.

Use this slide as the contract for the hour. The sequence matters: first define what local data means, then understand how exactness behaves, then introduce resolutions as a replacement device. The payoff is that familiar differential complexes compute abstract sheaf cohomology once their terms are acyclic for global sections.

The Sheaf Axiom: Uniqueness Plus Gluing

Definition

A presheaf assigns groups \(F(U)\) and restriction maps. It is a sheaf when compatible local sections on every open cover glue uniquely.

Equalizer View

\(F(U) \to \prod_i F(U_i) \rightrightarrows \prod_{i,j} F(U_i \cap U_j)\)

The image consists exactly of compatible families.

Separate the two halves of the axiom. Uniqueness says a section is determined locally, while existence says compatible local pieces actually glue. The equalizer picture is useful because it already resembles the beginning of a Cech complex: restrictions to overlaps record whether a local family is globally meaningful.

Examples That Set The Hodge-Theoretic Vocabulary

Constant Sheaf

Sections are locally constant functions with values in a fixed group. Disconnected opens remember one value per connected component.

Structure Sheaf

\(C^0\), \(C^\infty\), or \(\mathcal O_X\) assigns functions of the chosen regularity. The regularity is part of the sheaf.

Vector Bundles

A vector bundle of class \(A\) corresponds to a locally free sheaf of \(A\)-modules, with rank equal to the local free rank.

Stress that the same underlying bundle can produce different sheaves when the allowed regularity changes. A differentiable section sheaf and a holomorphic section sheaf answer different gluing questions. This distinction is essential later, because Dolbeault theory computes cohomology of holomorphic sections using a resolution by smoother objects.

Stalks Turn Local Questions Into Pointwise Algebra

Stalk

The stalk \(F_x\) is the direct limit of sections over neighborhoods of \(x\). Its elements are germs.

Exactness Test

A sequence of sheaves is exact exactly when the induced sequence on every stalk is exact.

The stalk is the microscope of sheaf theory. It discards all behavior that disappears after shrinking around a point, so it is ideal for testing local exactness. Warn students that stalk exactness is powerful but not the end of the story: global sections can still fail to preserve surjectivity.

Kernels Behave Immediately; Images Need Sheafification

Kernel

For a morphism \(\varphi:F\to G\), the assignment \(U \mapsto \ker(\varphi_U)\) is already a sheaf.

Image And Cokernel

The presheaf image or quotient may fail the gluing axiom. The sheaf-theoretic image is obtained by sheafifying the presheaf image.

This slide is a good place to slow down. Students often expect every construction to be performed open set by open set. Kernels work that way, but images and quotients can lose the gluing property. Sheafification repairs this, and that repair is what makes sheaves of abelian groups form an abelian category.

Global Sections Are Left-Exact, Not Exact

Left-Exact Functor

If \(0\to F'\to F\to F''\) is exact, then

\(0\to \Gamma(X,F')\to \Gamma(X,F)\to \Gamma(X,F'')\)

is exact at the first two terms.

Where Cohomology Enters

The last map need not be onto. \(H^1(X,F')\) records the first obstruction to lifting a global section of \(F''\).

Frame cohomology as a diagnostic rather than a mysterious new object. When a local or quotient-level solution cannot be lifted globally, the obstruction has a home in the next derived functor. The connecting map in the long exact sequence is the mechanism that turns failed surjectivity into a cohomology class.

Resolutions Replace A Sheaf By A Computable Complex

Resolution

An exact complex \(0\to F\to K^0\to K^1\to\cdots\) encodes \(F\) inside objects better suited to a functor.

Instructor Check

The notebook's toy boundary matrices verify \(d^2=0\), rank-nullity consistency, and the expected vanishing in an acyclic ladder.

The resolution is the central computational move of the chapter. It replaces one sheaf by a complex whose individual terms are easier for the chosen functor. Students should hear that exactness is imposed before applying global sections; after applying global sections, the remaining cohomology is precisely the information we want to measure.

Derived Functors Measure The Defect Of Exactness

General Pattern

For a left-exact functor \(F:C\to D\), choose an injective resolution \(M\to I^\bullet\), apply \(F\), and define

\(R^iF(M)=H^i(F(I^\bullet))\).

Independence

Different injective resolutions give canonically isomorphic derived objects, so the notation records an invariant of \(M\), not of a chosen ladder.

Keep the category-theoretic statement concrete. The existence of enough injectives guarantees a universal way to define the right derived functors, but most geometric calculations avoid constructing injectives explicitly. The reason this abstraction matters is that it proves the answer is independent of the resolution used to compute it.

Acyclic Resolutions Are The Workhorse

Acyclic Replacement Principle

If \(0\to F\to A^0\to A^1\to\cdots\) is a resolution and \(H^q(X,A^p)=0\) for \(q>0\), then \(H^i(X,F)\) is the cohomology of \(\Gamma(X,A^\bullet)\).

Geometric Sources
  • Flasque sheaves: restrictions are surjective.
  • Fine sheaves: partitions of unity kill higher cohomology.
  • Cech-acyclic covers: intersections have vanishing higher cohomology.
This is the slide where the abstract apparatus becomes practical. Injective resolutions are foundational, but acyclic resolutions are what the geometer actually computes with. Fine sheaves are especially important on smooth manifolds because partitions of unity make the sheaves of differential forms acyclic for global sections.

The de Rham Theorem As A Sheaf-Cohomology Statement

Smooth Resolution

\(0\to \mathbb C_X\to \mathcal A_X^0 \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal A_X^1 \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal A_X^2 \to \cdots\)

The Poincare lemma gives local exactness; fine sheaves give acyclicity.

Conclusion

\(H^i(X,\mathbb C_X) \cong H^i_{\mathrm{dR}}(X,\mathbb C)\)

Sheaf cohomology of the constant sheaf is computed by the global de Rham complex.

Present de Rham comparison as the model success story for the chapter. The constant sheaf is not itself easy to compute globally, but it admits a resolution by smooth forms. Local exactness comes from the Poincare lemma, and acyclicity comes from fineness, so abstract sheaf cohomology becomes the familiar de Rham quotient.

Dolbeault Cohomology Uses The Same Template

Holomorphic Bundle \(E\)

\(0\to \mathcal O(E)\to \mathcal A^{0,0}(E)\xrightarrow{\bar\partial}\mathcal A^{0,1}(E)\xrightarrow{\bar\partial}\cdots\)

The Dolbeault lemma supplies local exactness; smooth \((0,q)\)-form sheaves are fine.

Cohomological Payoff

\(H^q(X,\mathcal O(E)) \cong H^q_{\bar\partial}(X,E)\)

Use the parallel with de Rham theory deliberately. The input sheaf is now holomorphic sections, but the resolution uses smooth forms with values in the bundle. This is the conceptual bridge to complex geometry: holomorphic data is studied through a fine resolution whose global cohomology is the Dolbeault complex.

Cech Cohomology Makes \(H^1\) Visible

1-Cocycle

Transition data on pairwise intersections satisfies a compatibility law on triple intersections.

1-Coboundary

A cocycle is trivial when it comes from changing local trivializations, \(g_{ij}=h_i h_j^{-1}\) in multiplicative notation.

Interpretation

\(H^1\) classifies gluing data modulo local changes of coordinates in several important sheaves.

This is the most concrete view of first cohomology. Pairwise transition functions are easy to draw and easy to test on triple overlaps. The quotient by coboundaries expresses the idea that changing local frames should not change the glued object. Later vector bundles and torsors use exactly this language.

What \(H^1\) Parametrizes

Line Bundles

\(H^1(X,\mathcal O_X^*)\) records holomorphic line bundles through transition functions modulo local unit changes.

Affine Gluing

For a sheaf of groups acting on local models, \(H^1\) records objects locally isomorphic to the model.

Additive Obstructions

For abelian sheaves, \(H^1(X,F)\) is the quotient of closed one-cochains by changes of local zero-cochains.

Make the quotient interpretation explicit. A cocycle is not yet a geometric object up to isomorphism, because local descriptions can be changed. Coboundaries encode those harmless changes. The cohomology class is what survives, so the abstract quotient has a direct moduli interpretation in line bundles and related gluing problems.

Finite Checks Behind The Notebook Model

Boundary Squares To Zero

\(d_1d_0=0\)

The composition of consecutive Cech boundary maps vanishes, so cohomology is a quotient of cycles by boundaries.

Rank-Nullity Ledger

\(\dim C^i = \operatorname{rank} d_i + \dim\ker d_i\)

Small matrices keep the quotient dimensions honest.

Takeaways For The Next Unit

Sheaves

They organize local sections and make gluing a structural axiom rather than an informal operation.

Cohomology

It measures how global sections fail to preserve exactness, via derived functors.

Resolutions

Acyclic resolutions convert sheaf cohomology into ordinary cohomology of global complexes.

Hodge-Theoretic Bridge

de Rham and Dolbeault theorems become canonical comparisons between sheaf cohomology and differential-form complexes.

Close by connecting the chapter forward. The next Hodge-theoretic constructions depend on treating cohomology groups as functorial invariants rather than ad hoc quotients. Students should leave knowing that differential forms compute sheaf cohomology only because local exactness and acyclicity have been carefully verified.
