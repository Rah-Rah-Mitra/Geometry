Pure Hodge Structure: The Portable Object

Definition Pattern

A pure integral Hodge structure of weight \(k\) is a free abelian group \(V_{\mathbb Z}\) with

\(V_{\mathbb C}=\bigoplus_{p+q=k} V^{p,q}, \qquad \overline{V^{p,q}}=V^{q,p}.\)

The rational version replaces \(V_{\mathbb Z}\) by \(V_{\mathbb Q}\).

What Must Be Remembered?

  • The lattice is not decoration: it remembers periods and arithmetic constraints.
  • The weight fixes the diagonal \(p+q=k\) where the summands live.
  • Conjugation ties the two sides of the Hodge diamond together.
  • The dimensions \(h^{p,q}\) are invariants, not the full structure.
Stress that the definition is deliberately austere. It does not mention a manifold, a metric, or differential forms once the cohomology group has been converted into linear algebra. This is the conceptual gain: the structure can be compared, transported, dualized, and placed in categories without carrying the original geometry at every step.

From Decomposition To Filtration

Strictness Target

For a morphism of Hodge structures, the filtration is the interface:

\(\phi(F^p V_{\mathbb C}) \subseteq F^{p+r}W_{\mathbb C}.\)

The strictness statement says images, kernels, and cokernels inherit the expected filtered structure when the morphism respects type.

This is the moment to explain why filtrations are not merely an alternative notation. Direct summands are visually clear, but filtrations behave better in families and under maps. When proving exactness statements, one checks how a map meets each filtered level, and strictness prevents hidden dimension loss at the associated graded level.

Polarisation Adds Orthogonality And Sign

Polarised Hodge Structure

A bilinear form \(Q\) polarises the weight \(k\) structure when it has the correct symmetry, pairs only complementary bidegrees, and satisfies the Hodge-Riemann sign condition.

\(i^{p-q}(-1)^{k(k-1)/2} Q(v,\overline v) > 0\)

The positivity is read on the appropriate nonzero \(V^{p,q}\) component, and in geometry it is refined by primitive decomposition.

Three Tests

  1. Rationality: the form is defined on \(V_{\mathbb Q}\) or \(V_{\mathbb Z}\).
  2. Bidegree: \(Q(V^{p,q},V^{p',q'})=0\) unless \(p'=k-p\) and \(q'=k-q\).
  3. Positivity: the signed Hermitian form has the required definite behavior.
Do not let the formula become a wall of symbols. Its job is to say that an intersection form, after the correct complex phase and sign correction, behaves like a positive Hermitian metric on each Hodge piece. That is why polarisation is rigidity data, not just another pairing written beside the decomposition.

Polarised Manifolds Become Projective

Geometric Input

  • A compact complex manifold \(X\).
  • An integral Kahler class \([\omega]\in H^2(X,\mathbb Z)\cap H^{1,1}(X)\).
  • A holomorphic line bundle whose first Chern class represents the class.
Projectivity Bridge

The chapter uses the line-bundle criterion and Kodaira embedding as the bridge from polarised Kahler geometry to smooth projective varieties.

The practical conclusion is: an integral positive class gives enough holomorphic sections to embed \(X\) into projective space.

For the lecture, treat this bridge as a structural theorem: the polarising class is not merely a cohomology class; it supplies the projective model in which algebraic geometry can operate.

Connect the abstract definition back to geometry here. The class of the Kahler form is doing two jobs: it defines Lefschetz operators and pairings on cohomology, and when integral and positive it comes from a line bundle. Kodaira embedding then turns positivity of a line bundle into projective coordinates, which explains the chapter title's algebraic-geometric force.

Example: Projective Space Has Tate-Type Cohomology

Model Computation

\(H^{2k}(\mathbb P^n,\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z\cdot H^k,\quad H^{2k+1}=0.\)

The Hodge structure is concentrated on the diagonal. The hyperplane class \(H\) supplies the generator and the polarising Lefschetz operator.

Projective space is the sanity check example because nothing exotic is hiding in the Hodge diamond. Every nonzero class lies on the diagonal, generated by powers of the hyperplane class. Use it to calibrate the meaning of weight, type, and polarising class before moving to abelian varieties or weight two structures.

Weight One Structures And Abelian Varieties

Complex Torus From Hodge Data

A weight one structure has \(V_{\mathbb C}=V^{1,0}\oplus V^{0,1}\). Its lattice and \(V^{1,0}\) determine a complex torus.

\(T = V_{\mathbb C}/(F^1V_{\mathbb C}+V_{\mathbb Z})\)

Polarisation Test

  • The alternating form is integral on the lattice.
  • The associated Hermitian form is positive.
  • When the condition holds, the torus is projective, hence an abelian variety.

Notebook lab: assemble a toy period matrix and test the Riemann bilinear relation by changing one entry at a time.

This slide is a good place to slow down and distinguish a complex torus from an abelian variety. The Hodge structure determines the torus, but projectivity requires a polarisation satisfying the Riemann bilinear relation. In the notebook lab, the first failed positivity or integrality check usually reveals exactly which hypothesis was doing the work.

Weight Two: Period Domains In One Picture

K3-Type Template

When \(h^{2,0}=1\), the structure is determined by the line \(H^{2,0}=\mathbb C\sigma\).

The polarising form cuts out the conditions \(Q(\sigma,\sigma)=0\) and \(Q(\sigma,\overline{\sigma})>0\).

Present this as the first glimpse of period-domain geometry. A whole Hodge decomposition of weight two, under the one-dimensional \(H^{2,0}\) assumption, can be encoded by a line satisfying a quadratic equation and a positivity inequality. This prepares students for later variation of Hodge structure without yet needing the full general theory.

Common Failure Mode

A correct topological degree is not enough. The Hodge bidegree and Tate shift must be tracked simultaneously.

\(H^k(\widetilde X)\cong H^k(X)\oplus \bigoplus_i H^{k-2i}(Y)(-i)\)

This is the slide for preventing the most expensive bookkeeping error in the chapter. Pullbacks are type preserving, but Gysin and blowup formulas include degree and Tate shifts. Ask students to say out loud where the new classes come from, what their cohomological degree is, and how their Hodge type is shifted.

What To Carry Forward

Structure

A pure Hodge structure is a rational or integral lattice plus a weight-compatible complex decomposition.

Polarisation

A polarisation is the pairing that turns Lefschetz decomposition and Hodge-Riemann positivity into rigid linear algebra.

Examples

Projective space, abelian varieties, and K3-type weight two structures show how much geometry the linear data can remember.

Instructor Prompt

Given a filtered vector space with the right dimensions, identify the extra data needed before calling it a polarised Hodge structure.

Close by returning to the difference between labels and data. A Hodge diamond gives dimensions, a Hodge structure gives a lattice and decomposition, and a polarised Hodge structure gives a compatible pairing with positivity. That distinction is the foundation for the later chapters on variations, period maps, cycles, and infinitesimal invariants.
