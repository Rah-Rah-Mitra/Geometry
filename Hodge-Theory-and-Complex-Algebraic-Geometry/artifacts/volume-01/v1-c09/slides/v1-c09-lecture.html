Lecture Route

Concept map02 / 12
Frame the lecture as a translation exercise. The same family is viewed first as a smooth fibration, then as a first-order deformation class, and finally as a cohomology bundle with a flat connection. This prevents a common confusion: nearby fibers are diffeomorphic, but not usually biholomorphic.

Families Are Proper Holomorphic Submersions

Definition

A family of compact complex manifolds is a proper holomorphic submersion \(\varphi:X\to B\). The fiber over \(t\) is \(X_t=\varphi^{-1}(t)\).

  • Submersion gives smooth fibers of constant dimension.
  • Properness keeps the fibers compact and supports Ehresmann triviality.
  • A base point \(0\in B\) turns the family into deformations of \(X_0\).
Proper + submersive + holomorphic03 / 12
Use this slide to pin down what data is actually present. The total space is complex, the base is complex, and the map is holomorphic. Properness and submersion are not decorative hypotheses: together they make the family locally stable as a differentiable fibration while still allowing the complex structures to vary.

Ehresmann: Topology Does Not Move Locally

Theorem

If \(\varphi:X\to B\) is a proper differentiable submersion and \(B\) is contractible, then \(X\) is diffeomorphic over \(B\) to \(X_0\times B\).

T=(T_0,\varphi):X \xrightarrow{\sim} X_0\times B
Important Distinction

The trivialization is smooth. It does not say the fibers are holomorphically equivalent.

Local product structure04 / 12
The proof idea is useful even if we do not reproduce every detail: first build a retraction near the reference fiber, then use properness to keep control and integrate lifted vector fields when a global contractible base is needed. The conceptual payoff is that all nearby cohomology groups can be compared through diffeomorphisms.

A Smooth Trivialization With Holomorphic Slices

Refinement

After shrinking \(B\), one can choose \(T=(T_0,\varphi)\) so the fibers of \(T_0\) are complex submanifolds of \(X\).

Interpretation

The fixed differentiable manifold \(X_0\) carries a holomorphically varying family of complex structures \(I_t\).

Holomorphic variation after smooth identification05 / 12
This refinement is the bridge from topology to deformation theory. The family is not being trivialized holomorphically, but the chosen transverse slices are complex. That is just enough structure to talk about a holomorphic parameter dependence of complex structures on the fixed smooth fiber.

The Kodaira-Spencer Map

Exact Sequence Along \(X=X_0\)

\(0\to T_X\to T_{\mathcal X}|_X\to \varphi^*T_B|_X\to 0\)

Connecting Homomorphism

\(\rho:T_{B,0}=H^0(X,\varphi^*T_B|_X)\to H^1(X,T_X)\)

The value \(\rho(u)\) is the first-order class of the deformation in the base direction \(u\).

Extension class as deformation derivative06 / 12
Explain that the map is built by a standard connecting homomorphism. A base tangent vector lifts locally to vector fields on the total space, but the differences of local lifts lie in the vertical tangent bundle. Those differences form the cohomology class measuring how the complex fiber changes to first order.

First-Order Deformations Are Cech Cocyles

Local Model

\(\mathcal O_{X_\varepsilon}|_{U_i}\cong \mathcal O_{U_i}[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2)\)

Change of chart\(\theta_{ij}(f)=f+\varepsilon\chi_{ij}(f)\)
Ring condition\(\chi_{ij}\) is a derivation, hence a holomorphic vector field.
Cocycle condition\(\chi_{ij}+\chi_{jk}+\chi_{ki}=0\)
Change of trivializationadds a coboundary, so the class lies in \(H^1(X,T_X)\).
Infinitesimal gluing data07 / 12
This is the most concrete algebraic description of the first-order class. Local first-order products always exist, but their transition automorphisms contain derivations. The cocycle condition comes from composing changes of chart on triple overlaps, and changing local trivializations only changes the representative by a coboundary.

Analytic View: Differentiate The Complex Structure

Beltrami-Type Parameter

Vectors of type \((0,1)\) for \(I_t\) have the form \(u-\alpha_t(u)\), where \(\alpha_t\in A^{0,1}(T_X)\).

Kodaira-Spencer In Dolbeault Form

\(u\mapsto d_u(\alpha_t)|_{t=0}\) is \(\bar\partial\)-closed and represents \(\rho(u)\in H^1(X,T_X)\).

Dolbeault representative of the same class08 / 12
Use the diagram to make the analytic model tangible. A nearby complex structure can be encoded by how its anti-holomorphic tangent directions tilt relative to the original splitting. Differentiating that tilt in a base direction gives a Dolbeault representative of the same Kodaira-Spencer class defined by the extension sequence.

Local Systems And Flat Connections Are Equivalent

Local System

A sheaf locally isomorphic to a constant vector space, with constant transition matrices.

Associated Connection

For \(\sigma=\sum_i a_i e_i\), set \(\nabla\sigma=\sum_i e_i\otimes da_i\). Constant transition matrices make this intrinsic.

Sheaf language and connection language09 / 12
Emphasize why both languages are kept. Local systems are the natural way to say that cohomology is locally constant as a sheaf, while flat connections are the natural way to differentiate sections algebraically. Flatness is the condition that local parallel sections exist and are determined by one fiber value.

The Gauss-Manin Connection

Cohomology Local System

\(\mathbb H_A^k=R^k\pi_*A\), with stalk at \(b\) canonically \(H^k(X_b,A)\).

Gauss-Manin

The flat connection on the associated bundle \(H^k\to B\) is denoted \(\nabla\).

Cartan-Lie Formula

\(\nabla\omega|_0(u)= [\,\iota_v(d\Omega)|_{X_0}\,]\), where \(\varphi_*v=u\).

Flat transport of cohomology10 / 12
The Gauss-Manin connection is the intrinsic version of identifying cohomology through local Ehresmann trivializations. The Cartan-Lie formula gives a practical derivative: extend a family of closed fiber forms to a total-space form, differentiate by contracting the exterior derivative with a lift of the base tangent vector, and then take cohomology class.

Semicontinuity Forces Constant Hodge Numbers Near Kahler Fibers

Upper Semicontinuity

\(b\mapsto \dim H^q(X_b,F|_{X_b})\) is upper semicontinuous for holomorphic vector bundles \(F\).

Apply To Relative Forms

\(h^{p,q}(X_b)=\dim H^q(X_b,\Omega^p_{X_b})\) cannot jump up near \(0\).

Kahler Anchor

Betti numbers are locally constant by Ehresmann. For a Kahler reference fiber, Hodge decomposition makes their sum already minimal.

Upper semicontinuity plus fixed Betti numbers11 / 12
The proof uses either algebraic resolutions in the projective case or elliptic operator theory in the analytic case. Once upper semicontinuity is known for the Hodge numbers, the Kahler fiber pins down the total Betti number. A chain of inequalities begins and ends at the same Betti number, so every inequality must be equality.

Small Deformations Of Kahler Manifolds Remain Kahler

Stability Theorem

If \(X_0\) is Kahler in a family \(\varphi:X\to B\), then \(X_b\) is Kahler for all \(b\) sufficiently near \(0\).

Step 1

Constancy of \(h^{p,q}\) and smooth variation of harmonic spaces move a \((1,1)\)-class to nearby fibers.

Step 2

Degeneration at \(E_1\) supplies corrections that make the moved form closed.

Step 3

Positivity is open, so a nearby real closed \((1,1)\)-form remains Kahler.

From deformation theory to Hodge variation12 / 12
Conclude by tying the theorem back to the opening route. Ehresmann keeps the differentiable topology fixed, Kodaira-Spencer measures the first-order complex motion, and Gauss-Manin supplies the flat cohomological background. The stability result is the chapter payoff: in a sufficiently small family, Kahler geometry survives deformation.
