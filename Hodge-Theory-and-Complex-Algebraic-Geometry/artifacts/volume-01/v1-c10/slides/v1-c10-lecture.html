Variations of Hodge Structure

How a fixed cohomology lattice carries a moving Hodge filtration, and why the period map is forced to move horizontally inside a constrained flag domain.

Object

A flat local system with filtrations on its complexification.

Constraint

Polarization and Hodge numbers cut out the period domain.

Motion

The period map records the filtration and satisfies Griffiths transversality.

Open by separating the three ledgers that will be used throughout the lecture: the fixed topological cohomology, the moving Hodge filtration, and the constraints imposed by polarization. The main promise is that a family of varieties becomes a holomorphic curve in a period domain, but only along horizontal directions.

Lecture Route: From Family To Horizontal Period Map

Construction Ledger

  • Start with a smooth proper family \(f:X\to S\).
  • Transport \(H^k(X_s,\mathbb{Z})\) by the Gauss-Manin connection.
  • Package \(F^p_s=\bigoplus_{a\ge p}H^{a,k-a}(X_s)\) as a filtration.
  • Send \(s\) to the flag \(F^\bullet_s\).
Use this slide as the map of dependencies. A variation is not merely a moving vector space; after flat trivialization the vector space is fixed and the filtration moves. The period map is therefore a map from the base into flags, and the geometric content is encoded by which flags are allowed.

Definition: Polarized Variation Of Hodge Structure

Data On A Complex Base \(S\)

  • A local system \(H_{\mathbb{Z}}\) with flat connection on \(H=H_{\mathbb{Z}}\otimes\mathcal{O}_S\).
  • A decreasing holomorphic filtration \(H=F^0\supset F^1\supset\cdots\supset F^{k+1}=0\).
  • Fiberwise Hodge decompositions recovered by \(H^{p,q}_s=F^p_s\cap\overline{F^q_s}\).
  • A flat bilinear form \(Q\) satisfying the Hodge-Riemann signs.

What Changes And What Does Not

topology: fixed \(H_{\mathbb{Z}}\)

complex geometry: moving \(F^\bullet_s\)

compatibility: \(Q(F^p,F^{k-p+1})=0\)

The VHS is the mechanism that lets topology remember complex deformation.

Emphasize that the definition deliberately separates flat and holomorphic structures. The local system knows how to identify nearby cohomology groups, while the filtration knows how the complex structure decomposes cohomology. The polarization is not decoration; it cuts the target down and supplies the positivity needed for Hodge theory.

The Period Domain Is A Flag Domain With Equations

Period Domain \(D\)

The target consists of filtrations with prescribed Hodge numbers and polarization constraints.

\[ D=\{F^\bullet:\dim F^p=f^p,\; Q(F^p,F^{k-p+1})=0,\; \text{positivity}\} \]

Forgetting \(Q\) gives a larger flag variety; Hodge theory lives in the open constrained locus.

The important correction here is that the period domain is not the whole Grassmannian or flag variety. Dimension data gives the flag shape, orthogonality imposes algebraic equations, and positivity selects an open region. This is why later differential statements can be tested against a concrete tangent subspace.

The Period Map Needs A Flat Trivialization

Local Picture On A Simply Connected Patch \(U\)

  • Choose a flat frame for \(H_{\mathbb{Z}}\) over \(U\).
  • Express each \(F^\bullet_s\) inside one fixed vector space \(H_{\mathbb{C}}\).
  • Define \(\Phi:U\to D\), \(s\mapsto F^\bullet_s\).

Leaving \(U\) introduces monodromy, so the global target is usually a quotient by an arithmetic group.

Pause on the phrase local period map. Without a flat frame, the filtrations live in different fibers and cannot be compared as points in one flag variety. On a simply connected patch the Gauss-Manin connection supplies the comparison; around loops, monodromy records the obstruction to making that choice globally.

Griffiths Transversality: The Derivative Lowers By One

Horizontal Condition

For every tangent vector \(v\in T_sS\), the Gauss-Manin derivative satisfies

\[ \nabla_v(F^p_s)\subseteq F^{p-1}_s . \]

Equivalently, the infinitesimal period map lands in the horizontal tangent bundle.

This is the central theorem-level constraint for the chapter. The derivative of the filtration is not arbitrary; it can move a class from level p only as far as level p minus one. The block diagram makes the condition falsifiable: any two-step lowering block is a visible failure of horizontality.

Hodge Bundles Turn The Filtration Into Geometry On \(S\)

Bundle View

The subspaces \(F^p_s\) assemble into holomorphic subbundles \(F^p\subset H\).

Their graded pieces \(\operatorname{Gr}^p_F H=F^p/F^{p+1}\) are the Hodge bundles that carry curvature and positivity information.

This slide shifts from pointwise linear algebra to vector bundles. Once the filtrations vary holomorphically, their subspaces become subbundles, and the graded pieces become the Hodge bundles. This language is what lets the local differential condition interact with curvature, positivity, and later moduli questions.

Polarization Keeps The Moving Flag In A Smaller Target

Two Roles Of \(Q\)

  • Orthogonality: \(Q(F^p,F^{k-p+1})=0\).
  • Positivity: the Hodge-Riemann signs distinguish the correct open region.
  • Flatness: monodromy and parallel transport preserve \(Q\).
The polarization is both algebraic and metric. Algebraically it tells which flags are isotropic in the right way; analytically it supplies the positivity signs. In a family, flatness means the parallel transport and monodromy transformations must preserve this form, which is the finite-dimensional check represented here.

Monodromy Is The Price Of Leaving A Local Patch

Common Misreading

The period map is easiest to draw after choosing a local flat frame. Globally, analytic continuation around a loop may return the filtration in a transformed lattice frame.

\[ \rho:\pi_1(S,s_0)\to \operatorname{Aut}(H_{\mathbb{Z}},Q) \]

Make monodromy concrete: a flat section continued around a loop may come back as a different integral class. The local period map is still the right object for differential questions, but global statements must remember the monodromy representation and often pass to a quotient of the period domain.

Calabi-Yau Periods: One Line Generates A Rich Variation

Weight \(n\) Calabi-Yau Pattern

  • The line \(F^n_s=H^{n,0}(X_s)\) is generated by a holomorphic volume form \(\Omega_s\).
  • Periods \(\int_{\gamma_i}\Omega_s\) give local coordinates for the top piece of the period map.
  • Differentiating \(\Omega_s\) lands in \(F^{n-1}\), not in an arbitrary cohomology direction.
Use this example to show why variations of Hodge structure are computationally useful. For a Calabi-Yau family, the top Hodge line has a distinguished generator, and period integrals become functions of the parameter. The same transversality rule controls their derivatives and leads toward Picard-Fuchs equations.

A Finite Check For Horizontality

Block Test

Model the derivative by a block matrix relative to the Hodge filtration. Horizontal means only the first lower diagonal may be nonzero.

allowed: A[p, p-1]

reject: A[p, p-2], A[p, p-3], ...

Notebook Diagnostics

  • Nested filtration: true.
  • Hodge symmetry: true.
  • Boundary square norm: \(0.0\).
  • Symplectic monodromy residual: \(0\).

These checks are small models, not replacements for the general theorem.

Connect the lecture back to the notebook lab. The toy matrix check is useful because it makes Griffiths transversality editable: turn on a forbidden block and the horizontal condition fails immediately. The other diagnostics keep the surrounding Hodge bookkeeping honest, especially nested filtrations, pairing preservation, and symmetry.

What The Chapter Adds To The Course

Local System

The cohomology groups are compared by flat transport, so topology is held fixed while complex structure varies.

Period Domain

Flags satisfying Hodge numbers, orthogonality, and positivity form the target for period data.

Horizontal Map

The derivative of the period map is constrained by \(\nabla F^p\subset F^{p-1}\).

A variation of Hodge structure is the precise interface between deformation theory, topology, and complex analytic period maps.

Close by asking students to say which part of the construction is topological, which part is holomorphic, and which part is metric. That classification is the quickest way to avoid the chapter's common errors. The next topics can then use period maps as structured invariants rather than mysterious coordinate functions.
