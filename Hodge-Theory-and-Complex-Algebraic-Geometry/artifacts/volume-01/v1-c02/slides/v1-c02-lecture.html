Guiding question. When does a pointwise complex structure on tangent spaces actually come from holomorphic coordinates?

Notebook route: atlas, splitting, integrability, Dolbeault2 / 11
Use this route slide to set the cognitive load for the lecture. Students should hear that there are three layers: coordinate data, tangent-bundle bookkeeping, and a cohomological payoff. The warning question is the through-line: an almost complex structure gives a pointwise algebraic operation, while a complex manifold requires compatible holomorphic coordinates.
Definition

Complex Manifolds Are Atlases With Holomorphic Glue

Complex Manifold

A real \(2n\)-manifold \(X\) is complex of dimension \(n\) when it has charts \(\phi_i:U_i\to V_i\subset \mathbb C^n\) whose transition maps are holomorphic wherever defined.

\(\phi_j\circ\phi_i^{-1}: \phi_i(U_i\cap U_j)\to\phi_j(U_i\cap U_j)\)
Why This Matters

The holomorphic condition is preserved under composition, so local analytic computations can be transported around the manifold without changing type.

Local analytic structure3 / 11
Here the key point is that the definition is global because the overlap maps are analytic. Invite students to inspect the overlap region in the diagram: that is where the condition lives. If the transition maps were merely smooth, later expressions such as holomorphic sections and type decompositions would not patch coherently across charts.
Holomorphic Vector Bundles

Bundle Glue Must Also Be Holomorphic

Definition

A holomorphic vector bundle \(E\to X\) is locally \(U_i\times\mathbb C^r\), with changes of frame given by holomorphic matrices \(g_{ij}:U_i\cap U_j\to GL_r(\mathbb C)\).

Cocycle Ledger

On triple overlaps the matrices satisfy \(g_{ij}g_{jk}=g_{ik}\). This is the finite check that local frames describe one global object.

\(\mathcal O(E)(U)=\{s:U\to E\;|\;s_i=g_{ij}s_j\}\)
Holomorphic frames and cocycles4 / 11
Treat vector bundles as a second gluing problem layered above the atlas. The base charts say how points are compared; bundle transition matrices say how fibers and sections are compared. This prepares the audience for later sheaf and cohomology language, where sections become objects tracked by compatibility rules rather than by a single global formula.
Pointwise Linear Algebra

An Almost Complex Structure Is Not Yet A Complex Manifold

Almost Complex Structure

An endomorphism \(J:TX\to TX\) with \(J^2=-I\) turns each tangent space into a complex vector space.

\(P^{1,0}=\frac12(I-iJ),\quad P^{0,1}=\frac12(I+iJ)\)
Trap

The equation \(J^2=-I\) is algebraic at each point. It does not by itself provide holomorphic coordinates.

Almost complex versus integrable5 / 11
This slide should slow the room down. The projectors are a clean algebraic win: they split the complexified tangent space into eigenbundles of J. But the warning is the chapter's essential distinction. Later theorems ask whether these eigenbundles are compatible with Lie brackets, which is a differential condition rather than a pointwise matrix identity.
Type Decomposition

Forms Split Into \((p,q)\)-Pieces After Complexification

Local Basis

In holomorphic coordinates, one-forms are generated by \(dz_1,\ldots,dz_n\) and \(d\bar z_1,\ldots,d\bar z_n\). A \((p,q)\)-form has \(p\) holomorphic and \(q\) antiholomorphic factors.

\(A^k(X)_\mathbb C=\bigoplus_{p+q=k}A^{p,q}(X)\)
Bookkeeping Check

Real dimension \(2n\) becomes complex rank \(n\) in each tangent type. Mixing these ranks is a common source of false counts.

Differential forms by type6 / 11
Connect this slide back to ordinary differential forms: nothing mystical is being added, but the complexified cotangent bundle is being reorganized by type. The direct sum formula is the bookkeeping device that makes Hodge theory possible. Stress the rank issue: a complex n-fold has real tangent rank 2n, while each type piece has complex rank n.
Frobenius Viewpoint

Integrability Is Bracket Closure

Criterion

The \(T^{0,1}X\) distribution must be closed under Lie bracket. Equivalently, the obstruction measured by the Nijenhuis tensor vanishes.

\([\,\Gamma(T^{0,1}),\Gamma(T^{0,1})\,]\subseteq \Gamma(T^{0,1})\)
Toy Check

The notebook's small symbolic model separates an integrable bracket table from a perturbed nonintegrable one.

Frobenius and bracket closure7 / 11
Frame integrability as the moment where calculus enters the story. A pointwise split of tangent spaces is not enough; vector fields of type (0,1) must remain of that type after taking brackets. This is the Frobenius flavor of the condition and gives students a concrete inspection target before naming the Newlander-Nirenberg theorem.
Theorem-Level Meaning

Newlander-Nirenberg Converts The Obstruction Into Coordinates

Theorem Schema

For a smooth almost complex structure \(J\), vanishing of the integrability obstruction is equivalent to the existence of local holomorphic coordinates whose induced complex structure is \(J\).

\(N_J=0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad J\text{ comes from a complex atlas}\)
Proof Roadmap For Lecture
  • start with the \(T^{0,1}\) distribution
  • use bracket closure as the involutivity hypothesis
  • produce local functions annihilated by \(T^{0,1}\)
  • read those functions as holomorphic coordinates
Newlander-Nirenberg as a conversion theorem8 / 11
Present this theorem as a conversion result rather than a black box. The hypothesis is expressed in tangent language, while the conclusion is expressed in coordinate language. That is exactly what the chapter needs: it explains when the algebraic operation J has enough differential compatibility to support holomorphic charts and hence the Dolbeault machinery.
Operators \(\partial\) And \(\bar\partial\)

The Exterior Derivative Splits By Type

On A Complex Manifold

The exterior derivative respects the type decomposition in two adjacent directions:

\(d=\partial+\bar\partial,\quad \partial:A^{p,q}\to A^{p+1,q},\quad \bar\partial:A^{p,q}\to A^{p,q+1}\)
Complex Identities

Because \(d^2=0\), the type components satisfy \(\partial^2=0\), \(\bar\partial^2=0\), and \(\partial\bar\partial+\bar\partial\partial=0\).

Dolbeault double complex9 / 11
This is the payoff slide. Once type decomposition is coordinate-independent and integrable, d has only the two displayed type components. The grid makes the algebra visual: horizontal arrows are partial, vertical arrows are dbar, and the identities come from sorting d squared equals zero by bidegree. Later Dolbeault cohomology will live on these vertical complexes.

Summary

What Must Survive The Chapter

Conceptual Chain
  • holomorphic chart transitions define complex manifolds
  • holomorphic transition matrices define holomorphic bundles
  • an almost complex structure gives tangent type splitting
  • integrability turns the splitting into local holomorphic coordinates
  • the Dolbeault complex is the operator-level output
Three Traps To Avoid
  • confusing \(J^2=-I\) with integrability
  • counting real tangent rank as complex rank
  • splitting \(d\) before the complex structure is integrable
complex atlas \(\Rightarrow\) type decomposition \(\Rightarrow\) \(\bar\partial\)-complex \(\Rightarrow\) Hodge-theoretic cohomology
