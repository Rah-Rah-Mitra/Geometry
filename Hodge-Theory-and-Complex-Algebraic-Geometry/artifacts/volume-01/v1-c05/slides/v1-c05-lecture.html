The five boxes are not a table of contents only; they are dependencies. Without the metric there is no adjoint, without the adjoint there is no Laplacian, and without elliptic theory the harmonic kernel would not yet explain finite-dimensional cohomology.

The Metric Makes Forms Measurable

Construction

On a compact oriented Riemannian manifold \(X\), the metric defines pointwise inner products on \(\Lambda^kT_X^\ast\), and the volume form promotes them to a global pairing.

\((\alpha,\beta)_{L^2}=\int_X(\alpha_x,\beta_x)_x\,\mathrm{Vol}_g\)

Hodge Star View

The Hodge operator packages the same pairing as an integral of a top-degree form:

\(\alpha\wedge \ast\beta=(\alpha,\beta)_x\,\mathrm{Vol}_g\)

Emphasize that the metric is not decorative structure. It is the data that lets one take lengths and angles of forms, and compactness is what makes the integral pairing behave cleanly. The Hodge star is useful because it converts a pointwise inner product into a top-degree form, so the same formula can be read geometrically and analytically.

Formal Adjoints Move Derivatives Across the Pairing

Adjunction Rule

For an operator \(P:C^\infty(E)\to C^\infty(F)\), the formal adjoint \(P^\ast\) is characterized by

\((Pu,v)_{L^2}=(u,P^\ast v)_{L^2}\).

Metric Dependence

The adjoint is not an abstract inverse. Change the metric or volume form, and the concrete expression for \(P^\ast\) changes.

Use this slide to separate formal adjunction from pointwise linear algebra. The equality is an equality after integration, so boundary behavior, compactness, orientation, and the chosen metrics are all part of the mechanism. This is also the first place to warn students that the star and the adjoint will change when the metric changes, even when cohomology dimensions remain stable.

The Laplacian Measures Failure to Be Closed and Coclosed

Definition

\(\Delta_d=dd^\ast+d^\ast d\)

On \(k\)-forms, \(\Delta_d\) is second order, self-adjoint, and nonnegative with respect to the \(L^2\) pairing.

Energy Identity

\((\Delta_d\alpha,\alpha)=\|d\alpha\|^2+\|d^\ast\alpha\|^2\)

Thus \(\Delta_d\alpha=0\) exactly when \(d\alpha=0\) and \(d^\ast\alpha=0\).

Generated chapter artifact: the operator identities used as a finite inspection model.

The energy identity is the quickest route from an operator equation to two first-order equations. It also explains why harmonic forms are closed: they are not just selected closed forms, they also satisfy the adjoint equation. The generated visual is included as a local inspection aid from the notebook, but the key lecture point is the identity linking the Laplacian to two squared norms.

Harmonic Means Kernel of the Correct Laplacian

de Rham

\(\mathcal H^k_d=\ker(\Delta_d:A^k\to A^k)\)

Closed and coclosed \(k\)-forms.

Dolbeault

\(\mathcal H^{p,q}_{\bar\partial}=\ker\Delta_{\bar\partial}\)

Type \((p,q)\) forms controlled by \(\bar\partial\).

Bundle-Valued

\(\mathcal H^{0,q}(E)=\ker\Delta_E\)

Forms with coefficients in a holomorphic vector bundle.

Closed alone gives a cohomology class, but not a distinguished representative.
Coclosed is the gauge-fixing condition supplied by the metric.
Harmonicity is an analytic equation with topological consequences.
The chosen Laplacian must match the cohomology theory under discussion.
Pause on the phrase correct Laplacian. The de Rham theorem uses the Laplacian built from \(d\), while Dolbeault cohomology uses the one built from \(\bar\partial\), and vector bundle cohomology uses the bundle-valued version. The word harmonic is therefore not free-floating; it always refers to the operator that matches the complex whose cohomology we want to represent.

Ellipticity Is the Finite-Dimensionality Engine

Symbol Test

The highest-order part of \(P\) defines \(\sigma_P(\xi)\). Ellipticity asks that this map be injective for every nonzero covector \(\xi\).

Laplacian Symbol

\(\sigma_{\Delta}(\xi)(\omega)=-\|\xi\|^2\omega\)

For \(\xi\ne0\), this is scalar multiplication by a nonzero number, hence elliptic.

Ellipticity is the analytic theorem's hypothesis, not a visual nicety. For the Laplacian, the symbol collapses to scalar multiplication by the squared length of the covector, so away from the zero covector it cannot lose information. This is what allows the later theorem to assert finite-dimensional kernels and closed images on compact manifolds.

Hodge Decomposition Splits Forms Into Three Orthogonal Parts

Elliptic Decomposition

The space of smooth \(k\)-forms decomposes into harmonic, exact, and coexact summands. Orthogonality comes from the \(L^2\) pairing.

Notebook Checks
  • \(\Delta\) is symmetric positive semidefinite.
  • \(\ker\Delta\) has the intended toy cohomology dimension.
  • Exact and harmonic components are orthogonal.
Present the decomposition as a projection theorem for differential forms. The harmonic summand is the part that survives both \(d\) and \(d^\ast\); exact and coexact parts are analytic noise from the point of view of cohomology. The notebook's finite checks mirror this statement by verifying symmetry, positivity, kernel dimension, and orthogonality in a small model.

Every de Rham Class Has One Harmonic Representative

Hodge Theorem

For compact oriented Riemannian \(X\), the natural map

\(\mathcal H^k_d(X)\longrightarrow H^k_{\mathrm{dR}}(X)\)

is an isomorphism.

Proof Roadmap
  • Surjectivity: decompose a closed form and discard exact error.
  • Injectivity: an exact harmonic form has zero \(L^2\)-norm contribution.
  • Finite rank: \(\mathcal H^k\) is an elliptic kernel.
This is the conceptual heart of the chapter. The harmonic representative is canonical only after the metric has been chosen, but the dimension it computes is the topological Betti number. In the proof outline, stress that surjectivity comes from decomposing a closed form, while injectivity uses the impossibility of a nonzero form being both exact and harmonic under the energy identity.

The Same Mechanism Represents Holomorphic Bundle Cohomology

Bundle-Valued Dolbeault Complex

\(\bar\partial_E:A^{0,q}(E)\to A^{0,q+1}(E)\)

Hermitian metrics on \(X\) and \(E\) define \(\bar\partial_E^\ast\) and \(\Delta_E=\bar\partial_E\bar\partial_E^\ast+\bar\partial_E^\ast\bar\partial_E\).

Harmonic Representation

\(\mathcal H^{0,q}(E)\cong H^q(X,E)\)

Thus sheaf cohomology becomes computable by smooth bundle-valued forms.

The passage from \(d\) to \(\bar\partial_E\) is not a change in philosophy. It is the same analytic package applied to the complex that computes holomorphic vector bundle cohomology. Students should notice the recurring pattern: choose metrics, build an adjoint, form a Laplacian, invoke ellipticity, and identify the harmonic kernel with the desired cohomology group.

Poincare Duality Pairs Harmonic Representatives

Perfect Pairing

For compact connected oriented \(n\)-manifolds, integration gives

\(H^p(X,\mathbb R)\otimes H^{n-p}(X,\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R\).

Harmonic representatives make nondegeneracy visible through the \(L^2\) pairing.

Analytic Reading

If \(\alpha\) is nonzero harmonic, pairing with \(\ast\alpha\) detects it by \(\int_X\alpha\wedge\ast\alpha=\|\alpha\|^2\).

The duality proof is a good moment to show why harmonic representatives are powerful rather than merely elegant. If a nonzero harmonic class is paired with its Hodge-star partner, the integral is its squared norm, so it cannot vanish. This turns a topological duality statement into an analytic positivity argument once the Hodge theorem is available.

Serre Duality Is the Holomorphic Version of the Same Strategy

Pairing

\(H^q(X,E)\otimes H^{n-q}(X,E^\ast\otimes K_X)\to\mathbb C\)

The bundle contraction \(E\otimes E^\ast\to\mathcal O_X\) and the canonical bundle \(K_X\) produce a top holomorphic form to integrate.

Harmonic Proof Move

Represent both classes by \(\bar\partial\)-harmonic forms. Pair them by wedge, contraction, and integration; nondegeneracy follows from the same metric positivity principle.

Serre duality may look sheaf-theoretic at first glance, but in this chapter it is reached with the same harmonic method. The dual bundle and canonical bundle arrange the degrees and coefficients so that the wedge-contraction product can be integrated. The analytical content is again that harmonic representatives and metric positivity make the pairing perfect.

What to Carry Forward

Main Theorem Pattern

\(\text{metric}\Rightarrow P^\ast\Rightarrow \Delta\Rightarrow\ker\Delta\Rightarrow H^\bullet\)

Hodge theory turns cohomology into the kernel of a canonical elliptic operator attached to the chosen metric.

Two Traps
  • Closed does not mean harmonic.
  • The harmonic representative depends on the metric, even when the cohomology group does not.
Applied Lab

Change the inner product in the toy model, recompute the adjoint and Laplacian, and track how the representative moves while the cohomology dimension stays fixed.

Close by asking students to distinguish metric-dependent representatives from metric-independent dimensions. The lab prompt is deliberately small: alter the inner product, recompute the formal adjoint, and watch the harmonic vector change. That experiment reinforces the chapter's central lesson: analysis selects canonical representatives, while cohomology records the invariant classes they represent.
