Hyperbolic

Hyperbola and rotated hyperbola: use asymptote frames or rotate to principal directions.

Working Principle

Coordinates are successful when a geometric invariant becomes either a constant, a sign, a zero residual, or a simple family parameter.

A coordinate system is a lens, not the geometry itselfChoose it by the question being asked
This synthesis slide is for avoiding a common misconception: coordinates are not the geometry itself. A good coordinate system preserves the object while making one feature simple, such as a tangent, an area determinant, a constant angle, a ruling family, or a conic type.
