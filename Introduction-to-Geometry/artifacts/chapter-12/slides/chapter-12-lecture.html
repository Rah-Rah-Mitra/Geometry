Betweenness Is A Predicate, Not Just A Visual Guess

True

\([AEB]\), \([BEA]\), \([ABC]\), and \([DAC]\).

False

\([ACB]\) and \([EAB]\).

One-Dimensional Test

$$b\in[\min(a,c),\max(a,c)]$$

Endpoint Symmetry

\([AEB]\) and \([BEA]\) are both true because endpoints can be reversed.

Six sampled triplesOrder statements should survive table lookup
Betweenness is deliberately shown as a truth table because order statements are directional but symmetric in the endpoints. The diagram decomposes a line into segments and rays, while the table distinguishes true triples such as AEB from false triples such as ACB.

Pasch's theorem is the crossing invariant for ordered triangles. If a line enters a triangle through one side and avoids a vertex, the order data force it to leave through another side. The computed parameters certify the crossing points lie on the intended segments.
