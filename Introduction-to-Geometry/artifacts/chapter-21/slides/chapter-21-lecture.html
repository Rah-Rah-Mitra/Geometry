Elementary Coloring BoundInduction by a removable face

Euler's Formula Forces A Face With At Most Five Neighbors

Average Face Degree

If \(2E\ge 3V\), then a positive-\(\chi\) map has some face of degree \(\le 5\).

Induction Move

Remove the low-neighbor face, color the rest, then reinsert it.

Why Six Suffice

At most five adjacent colors are forbidden, leaving one available color.

Figure: six-color-induction-step.svgCheck: six-color-theorem-checks.json
Present the six-color theorem as the elementary proof the chapter can own. Euler's formula forces a face with at most five neighbors on positive-characteristic surfaces. Remove that face, color the remaining map by induction, then return it with a color not used by its neighbors.
