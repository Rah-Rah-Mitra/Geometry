Normal Curvature Is A Quotient In A Tangent Direction

Rayleigh Quotient

$$\kappa(a)=\frac{a^TBa}{a^TGa}$$

Second Fundamental Form

\(b_{ij}=r_{ij}\cdot n\) records how the surface bends in the chosen normal direction.

Scan Target

Extrema Preview

The extrema of this quotient become the principal curvatures.

Figure: normal-curvature-principal-directions.pngFirst form measures length; second form measures bending
Frame normal curvature as the bridge from tangent measurement to bending measurement. The numerator uses the second fundamental form and the denominator uses the first fundamental form, so the quotient compares two quadratic forms on the same direction. Rotating the direction gives a full scan of sectional bending.

Common Misread

Positive \(K\) does not imply an umbilic; equality of principal curvatures is the stronger condition.

Figure: umbilic-surface-of-revolution-curvatures.pngUmbilicity is isotropic bending, not merely positive curvature
Separate the idea of positive curvature from the idea of an umbilic. A sphere is umbilic everywhere because the two principal curvatures agree, but many positively curved surfaces still have preferred principal directions. The surface-of-revolution plot makes this distinction visible by comparing the sphere with an ordinary spheroid.
