Crystallographic RestrictionForbidden rotation orders

A Lattice-Preserving Rotation Has an Integer Trace

Trace Condition

$$\operatorname{tr}(R)=2\cos(2\pi/n)\in\mathbb Z$$

Allowed Orders

2, 3, 4, 6

Order 5 Signal

Trace \((-1+\sqrt5)/2\) is not an integer.

Interactive artifact: rotation-order-restriction.htmlTrace arithmetic plus nearest-center contradiction
The crystallographic restriction is the conceptual center of the chapter. A lattice-preserving rotation must act by an integer matrix, so its trace equals two cosine of the rotation angle and must be an integer. That leaves exactly the orders two, three, four, and six.
