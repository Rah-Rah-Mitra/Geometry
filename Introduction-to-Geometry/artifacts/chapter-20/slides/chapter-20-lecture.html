Chapter Problem

A geodesic is the curve whose acceleration has no tangential component when measured by the surface metric.

Lecture Arc

First fundamental form → Christoffel symbols → geodesic ODE → curvature budgets → Euler-Poincare.

Adding Triangle Budgets Leaves Topology

Cancellation Mechanism
  • Tile a closed surface by geodesic triangles.
  • Sum each triangle identity.
  • Interior edge angle contributions cancel in pairs.
  • The remaining total is governed by chi = V - E + F.
intS K dS = 2 pi chi(S)
Check residual: maximum absolute error between total curvature and 2 pi chi is 1.78e-15.
Now widen the triangle budget from one cell to a whole closed surface. The cancellation along shared edges is the important proof idea, because it leaves only vertex deficits and the combinatorial quantity V minus E plus F. The table gives two sanity checks: a tetrahedral sphere and a flat triangular torus.

Recovering Curvature from Circle Growth

Lab Recipe
  1. Sample geodesic circle circumference C(r).
  2. Set f(r)=C(r)/(2 pi).
  3. Estimate K=-f''(r)/f(r) by finite differences.

This gives a practical reason to care about circle growth and explains why numerical checks exclude unstable boundary samples.

The Chapter as a Chain of Auditable Invariants
