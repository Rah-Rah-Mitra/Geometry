Degree

A Sphere Map Multiplies The Top Class

Source span: pp. 195-212
Definition

For \(f:S^n\to S^n\), the induced map on top homology satisfies \[ f_*([S^n]) = d\,[S^n]. \] The integer \(d\) is the degree of \(f\).

Homotopy Invariance

If \(f\simeq g\), then \(f_*=g_*\) on homology, so \(\deg f=\deg g\).

Concept: degree of \(S^n\to S^n\)Original schematic plus notebook definition
Emphasize that degree is not first a picture of winding; it is the integer multiplying the top homology generator. The winding picture is a model students can inspect. Once the class in top homology is fixed, homotopic maps cannot change the multiplier, which is why degree becomes an obstruction.

Chain-Level Action

Mod-2 Homology

When Signs Disappear, Parity Still Obstructs

Concept bridge: antipodal symmetry
Mod-2 Chain Rule

With coefficients in \(\mathbb{Z}/2\), the signs in a boundary matrix are read modulo two. Orientation reversals no longer change a coefficient by \(-1\).

Antipodal Parity

Antipodal symmetry is detected by whether the top class is hit an odd number of times. That parity is the homological obstruction behind Borsuk-Ulam.

Common Mistake

Mod-2 does not mean "no information." It discards orientation signs so that parity information can become stable.

Coefficient shift: \(\mathbb{Q}\) traces to \(\mathbb{Z}/2\) parityUsed for antipodal obstruction
Introduce mod-2 homology as the place where orientation signs disappear but parity remains meaningful. This is the correct language for antipodal arguments because paired cells cancel in twos. Students should hear that the chapter is not abandoning integral degree, but adding a parity detector for symmetry.

Borsuk-Ulam Proof Logic

Odd Maps Cannot Be Compressed Away

Mod-2 degree supplies the parity contradiction
Borsuk-Ulam

Every continuous \(f:S^n\to\mathbb{R}^n\) has a point \(x\) with \(f(x)=f(-x)\).

Contradiction Route
  1. Assume \(f(x)\ne f(-x)\) for every \(x\).
  2. Normalize \(f(x)-f(-x)\) to get an odd map \(S^n\to S^{n-1}\).
  3. Use mod-2 homology to rule out the required parity behavior.
Proof style: assume no antipodal equality, then normalizeMod-2 homology detects the impossible odd compression
Connect the gap plot back to the homological proof strategy. If every antipodal pair had different values, one normalizes the difference and obtains an odd map to a lower sphere. Mod-2 degree rules out the needed factorization, giving the obstruction behind the visible zero.
Lefschetz Number

Fixed-Point Pressure Is An Alternating Trace

Compact triangulable spaces
Definition

For a self-map \(f:X\to X\),

\[ \Lambda(f)=\sum_q(-1)^q\operatorname{tr}\!\left(f_*:H_q(X;\mathbb{Q})\to H_q(X;\mathbb{Q})\right). \]
Fixed-Point Theorem

If \(X\) is compact and triangulable and \(\Lambda(f)\ne 0\), then \(f\) has a fixed point.

Formula over rational homologyFor \(S^1\): \(\Lambda=1-\deg(f)\)
Define the Lefschetz number as an alternating trace, not as a fixed-point count. For a compact triangulable space, a nonzero value forces at least one fixed point. The formula for S1, Lambda equals one minus degree, lets students reuse the degree intuition immediately.

Hopf Trace Theorem

Chain Traces And Homology Traces Agree Alternatingly

Finite chain models make Lefschetz computable
Hopf Trace

For a chain map of a finite chain complex,

\[ \sum_q(-1)^q\operatorname{tr}(C_q) = \sum_q(-1)^q\operatorname{tr}(H_q). \]
Why It Matters

The left side is a finite matrix calculation. The right side is the homological invariant used in the Lefschetz number.

Ask What The Invariant Is Allowed To See

Chapter 9 synthesis
Degree

Top homology multiplier. It sees orientation and winding behavior and multiplies under composition.

Euler-Poincare

Alternating cell counts match alternating homology dimensions, making \(\chi\) a homological invariant.

Borsuk-Ulam

Antipodal symmetry plus mod-2 parity forces an equal-valued antipodal pair.

Lefschetz

Alternating traces on rational homology force a fixed point whenever \(\Lambda\ne 0\).

Hopf Trace

Finite chain traces and homology traces agree after alternating, allowing matrix checks to prove topological facts.

Dimension

Open-cover nerves measure dimension only after allowing refinements that remove misleading intersections.
