Objects With Signs

Orientation Turns A Simplex Into A Signed Basis Element

Chapter 8
chains before matrices
Definition
A \(q\)-simplex with ordered vertices \((v_0,\ldots,v_q)\) represents a basis element of \(C_q(K)\). Odd permutations reverse its sign.
Boundary Sign Rule
\(\partial[v_0,\ldots,v_q]=\sum_i(-1)^i[v_0,\ldots,\widehat{v_i},\ldots,v_q]\)
  • Chains are integer vectors in these oriented simplex bases.
  • The same geometric face can appear with opposite signs.
  • Those signs are the engine of cancellation.
Oriented simplexesSource orientation: printed pp. 173-176
Use the triangle to make orientation concrete before any algebra appears. An ordered simplex is not just a set of vertices; swapping two vertices changes the sign of the basis element. That sign convention is what lets neighboring faces cancel instead of merely accumulate.

Chain Complex Criterion

The Boundary Of A Boundary Vanishes

proof obligation
\(D_{q-1}D_q=0\)
\[ \cdots \longrightarrow C_2(K) \xrightarrow{\partial_2} C_1(K) \xrightarrow{\partial_1} C_0(K) \xrightarrow{\partial_0} 0 \]
Proof Pattern
Each codimension-two face appears exactly twice in \(\partial_{q-1}\partial_q\): once with sign \(+1\), once with sign \(-1\). The two appearances cancel.
Why It Matters
The inclusion \(B_q\subseteq Z_q\) is only legal because \(\partial_q\partial_{q+1}=0\). Without it, \(Z_q/B_q\) would not be defined.
Chain complex identityChecks: boundary_squared_zero = true
This is the algebraic hinge of the chapter. If the boundary of a boundary were not zero, then boundaries would not automatically be cycles and the quotient definition would fail. The tiny triangle computation is not a special trick; it models the cancellation pattern in every dimension.

Definition Stack

Homology Is Closed Chains Modulo Filled Chains

quotient group
\(H_q=Z_q/B_q\)
\(C_q(K)\)Free abelian group on oriented \(q\)-simplexes.
\(Z_q(K)\)Cycles: \(Z_q=\ker \partial_q\), chains with no exposed boundary.
\(B_q(K)\)Boundaries: \(B_q=\operatorname{im}\partial_{q+1}\), chains filled from above.
\(H_q(K)\)Homology: \(Z_q/B_q\), surviving closed chains.
\[ H_q(K)=\ker(\partial_q)\,/\,\operatorname{im}(\partial_{q+1}) \]
Definition of simplicial homologyPrinted pp. 176-181
Slow down here and name each group before computing ranks. Cq records every formal q-chain, Zq keeps the closed ones, Bq keeps the ones explained by a higher-dimensional filling, and Hq remembers the closed chains left over after those fillings are ignored.

Maps Between Complexes

A Simplicial Map Induces A Chain Map

commuting square
\(D_1S_1=S_0D_1\)
Chain-Map Condition
For a simplicial map \(f:K\to L\), the induced maps \(S_q:C_q(K)\to C_q(L)\) satisfy
\(\partial S_q = S_{q-1}\partial\).
zero\(D_1S_1-S_0D_1\)
truecycle image is a cycle
Visual Reading
The relabeling changes names and orientations, but it does not create endpoints on a closed chain.
Artifact: simplicial-map-chain-map-commutes.pngFunctoriality of homology
A simplicial relabeling should not merely send vertices to vertices; it must respect the boundary operator. The commutative square says that mapping a chain and then taking its boundary agrees with taking the boundary first and then mapping. That is the mechanism behind functoriality.
Proof Consequence

Chain Maps Descend To Homology

cycles to cycles
boundaries to boundaries
Two-Line Proof
If \(z\in Z_q(K)\), then \(\partial S_qz=S_{q-1}\partial z=0\), so \(S_qz\) is a cycle. If \(z=\partial c\), then \(S_qz=S_q\partial c=\partial S_{q+1}c\), so boundaries map to boundaries.
Induced Map
The formula \([z]\mapsto[S_qz]\) is well-defined on \(H_q(K)\), because changing \(z\) by a boundary changes \(S_qz\) by a boundary.
Invariance Strategy
Homeomorphisms, subdivision maps, and simplicial approximations become algebraic isomorphisms after passing to homology.
Chain maps preserve homology classesPDF pp. 198-202
Convert the commutative square into a proof in two lines. If a chain is a cycle, its image has zero boundary. If a chain is a boundary, its image is again a boundary. Therefore the map descends from chain groups to homology groups without depending on the representative.
