From Euclidean Moves To Auditable Data

Definition
Construction certificate. A finite record of permitted straightedge and compass operations, their dependencies, and the residual equalities or incidences that must vanish when the construction is correct.

Euclid I.1

The First Proposition As A Test Case

Claim
Given segment AB, construct a point C such that AC = AB = BC.
Proof Obligations
The circles must exist, they must meet, and the selected intersection must inherit both radius equalities.
Equilateral triangle construction4 / 17
Use Euclid I-1 as the first stress test because it is small enough to see completely. The two circles encode the two copied radii, and the chosen intersection point becomes the third vertex. Existence is the key checkpoint.

Hidden Existence

Seeing An Intersection Is Not The Same As Having One

Warning
In the rational coordinate plane, the unit equilateral triangle would require a third vertex whose height is sqrt(3) / 2, not a rational coordinate.

What The Example Teaches

A plane can contain many lines and circles yet still fail the intersection needed by a familiar construction.

Continuity and existence assumptions6 / 17
Here the lecture pauses on a subtle point: a diagram may show two curves crossing, but the formal system still needs a principle allowing the intersection point. The rational plane example is a useful warning because the unit equilateral triangle wants a height involving sqrt(3), which does not live among rational coordinates.
Dependency Graph

What Is A Proposition Allowed To Use?

Principle
A proposition is not merely a true statement; it is a statement with a permitted ancestry. The proof graph records that ancestry so circular reasoning and invisible assumptions can be noticed.
Definitions, postulates, common notions7 / 17
This is the chapter's methodological pivot. Euclid's postulates and common notions supply part of the proof engine, but practice also depends on diagrammatic assumptions such as order, continuity, and superposition. That distinction will matter later.
Two Kinds Of Equality

Constructed Equality And Proved Equality Play Different Roles

Equal By Construction
A compass circle gives every point on it the same radius from its center. In I.1, AC = AB because C lies on the circle centered at A.
Equal By Theorem
A later equality may follow from congruence, a common notion, or a previous proposition. Its certificate must cite the result that makes the equality legal.
Proof state, not picture state8 / 17
The slide contrasts two kinds of equality that students often merge too quickly. Equal by construction means the equality follows because a compass radius was copied or a line was defined by two points; equal by theorem means a previous proposition or common notion is doing the work.
Regular Pentagon

The Golden Triangle Organizes The Construction

Angle Pattern
The special isosceles triangle satisfies 36 + 72 + 72 = 180. Its base angles are twice its vertex angle.
Pentagon Signal
The same triangle appears between a vertex, two nonadjacent vertices, and a side of the regular pentagon.
Golden triangle9 / 17
Use this slide to show why the pentagon is more than a five-sided drawing. The construction is governed by a special isosceles triangle with angles 36, 72, and 72 degrees. The angle pattern drives the proof.
Exact Check

The Pentagon Certificate Has Algebra Inside It

Golden Ratio Relation
diagonal / side = phi
phi squared = phi + 1

What The Algebra Certifies

  • All five chord lengths agree.
  • All five diagonals agree.
  • The diagonal-to-side ratio is fixed, not chosen by eye.
Symbolic and numeric checks10 / 17
This is the algebraic core of the pentagon section. The diagonal-to-side ratio is phi, and phi satisfies phi squared equals phi plus one. The point is not to derive the identity line by line here; it is to show how exact algebra certifies the equality of the five chord lengths.

Euler Line

Three Centers, One Line

Euler Line
For a non-equilateral triangle, the circumcenter O, centroid G, and orthocenter H are collinear.
OG : GH = 1 : 2
Collinearity residual13 / 17
Use the Euler line as a compact example of modern Euclidean structure. In a non-equilateral triangle, the centroid, circumcenter, and orthocenter line up, and their spacing has a fixed relation. The ratio is part of the claim.
Nine-Point Circle

One Circle Certifies Nine Constructed Points

Invariant
Nine separately constructed points share one radius from one center. The certificate asks for nine equal distances, not nine visual coincidences.
Nine-point circle14 / 17
The nine-point circle is a good capstone because it collects several constructions into one invariant object. The midpoints of sides, feet of altitudes, and midpoints from vertices to the orthocenter all lie on a single circle.
Common Failure Modes

Where Euclidean Arguments Usually Leak

Intersection
A crossing drawn by eye is used before an existence principle has licensed it.
Copied Length
A segment is treated as equal without the circle or theorem that transfers the length.
Order
A point is assumed to lie between two others because the diagram places it there.

Recap

Euclid's Geometry Is A Discipline Of Evidence

Construct

Figures enter the proof through permitted straightedge and compass moves.

Assume

Existence, order, and continuity principles must be named when the diagram relies on them.

Prove

Equalities and incidences need ancestry in definitions, postulates, common notions, or prior propositions.

Check

Residuals make a construction inspectable and reveal mistakes that a polished picture can hide.
