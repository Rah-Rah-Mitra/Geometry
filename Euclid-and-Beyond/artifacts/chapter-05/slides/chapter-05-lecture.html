Definition Layer

What An Area Function Must Remember

Positivity

Nonempty bounded regions receive positive size; the empty region receives zero.

A(R) > 0
Congruence

Rigid motion changes position but not area. A translated or rotated copy keeps the same value.

R ≅ S implies A(R) = A(S)
Additivity

Disjoint finite decompositions can be measured piece by piece and then summed.

A(R union S) = A(R) + A(S)
Teaching Target

All later arguments are invariant arguments: choose the allowed moves, then track the quantity that those moves cannot change.

Area function axiomsminimum text size audited
Present area as a bookkeeping rule rather than a formula for special shapes. Positivity prevents invisible nonempty pieces, congruence invariance makes rigid motion irrelevant, and additivity is what allows diagrams to be decomposed without changing the total.

Two Languages

Equal Content Is Not Just A Number

Synthetic content

Two figures have equal content when common-notion reasoning and finite decomposition identify them as the same size.

  • Depends on permitted comparisons.
  • Feels geometric and constructional.
  • Can expose hidden axioms.
Numerical area

A measure function sends regions to an ordered number system and lets equality be checked by arithmetic.

  • Needs additivity and congruence rules.
  • Supports formulas and limits.
  • May require Archimedean hypotheses.
Content, measure, and hypothesesSections 22-23
Separate two ideas that students often blur. Equal content is inherited from Euclidean common notions and dissection-like reasoning, while a measure function assigns a number; Chapter 5 explains when these languages agree and where assumptions such as Archimedes enter.
Scissors Congruence In The Plane

Dissection Is Constructive Equality

Finite Inventory

A dissection proof must provide:

  • a finite list of pieces,
  • a rule that pieces cover the first figure without overlap,
  • a matching list of congruent moved pieces,
  • a reassembly that covers the second figure.
piece labels replace handwaving
Dissection equivalenceSection 24
Stress that dissection equivalence is more demanding than a matching number because it asks for a finite inventory of pieces and rigid motions. The visual shows the proof habit: cut a figure into named components, move each component, and check that no area is created or lost.
Local Engine

Triangle Area From A Rearranged Double

Piece Accounting

Two congruent copies of the triangle reassemble as a parallelogram with the same base and height.

2A(triangle) = b h
A(triangle) = one half b h

The formula is the last line of the argument, not the first. The first line is a dissection.

Base-height formulasvisual proof state
Use the triangle-to-parallelogram move as the local engine behind many area formulas. Duplicating or rearranging a triangle makes base and height visible, so the familiar one-half base times height formula becomes a consequence of a rigid piece accounting.
Main Planar Theorem

Bolyai-Gerwien: Equal Area Becomes Finite Dissection

Theorem Schema

For rectilinear figures in an Archimedean Euclidean plane, equal area is equivalent to equidecomposability by finitely many polygonal pieces.

Bolyai-Gerwien routeSection 24
Frame Bolyai-Gerwien as a theorem about the equivalence of two tests for rectilinear figures in an Archimedean Euclidean plane. The path through triangles, parallelograms, and rectangles matters because it is a constructive route, not a black-box comparison of real numbers.
Hypothesis Check

Archimedes Controls The Finite Part Of The Argument

What The Axiom Supplies

Repeated copies of a small segment eventually exceed a given larger segment. In area proofs, this prevents a finite construction from chasing an unreachable infinitesimal or infinite displacement.

n a > b for some whole number n

Without this control, equal numerical area can fail to produce the finite dissection promised by the planar theorem.

Archimedean dependenceboundary of dissection completeness
This slide explains why a hidden axiom appears in a chapter about area. Finite dissections cannot always overcome infinitesimal or infinitely displaced geometry, so the Archimedean condition is the bridge from numerical equality to a finite cutting procedure.
Curved Boundary

Circles Are Measured By A Squeezing Process

Exhaustion Idea

Trap the disk between polygonal areas and refine until the gap is smaller than any prescribed tolerance.

A_inner(n) ≤ A(circle) ≤ A_outer(n)

This is not a finite squaring of the circle. It is a limiting argument that imports area through polygonal bounds.

Circle quadrature by boundsSection 25
When the boundary is curved, a finite rectilinear dissection is no longer the natural first language. Inscribed and circumscribed polygons trap the circle, and doubling the number of sides makes the annular error shrink; the area statement is therefore limiting, not a squaring construction.

Three Dimensions

Volume Repeats The Pattern, Then Becomes Stranger

Prisms

Base area times height behaves like stacked additive area.

Pyramids

The factor one third is naturally justified through decomposition or exhaustion.

Scissors Question

Equal volume asks for a number; scissors congruence asks for a finite spatial dissection.

Volume and exhaustionSection 26
Move deliberately from planar area to volume without pretending nothing changes. Prisms behave like the polygonal story, while pyramids already require an exhaustion or limiting argument, so three dimensions inherit both the promise and the fragility of the planar theory.
Hilbert's Third Problem

Equal Volume Does Not Force A 3D Dissection

Negative Answer

There exist polyhedra with the same volume that are not scissors congruent.

volume agrees
Dehn invariant disagrees

The theorem changes the moral of area: a complete planar invariant becomes an incomplete spatial invariant.

Hilbert's third problemSection 27
State the shock clearly: equal volume is not enough for scissors congruence in three dimensions. A cube and a regular tetrahedron can be scaled to match volume, but a second invariant survives cutting and blocks a dissection between them.
Invariant Beyond Volume

Dehn's Invariant Tracks Edge Lengths And Angles

Ledger Form

D(P) = sum edges length(e) tensor angle(e)

The angle is recorded modulo rational multiples of pi. Right angles vanish in this quotient, but the regular tetrahedron's dihedral angle does not.

Invariance under cutting is the critical fact: subdividing an edge subdivides its length contribution, and internal angles introduced by a cut cancel in pairs.

Dehn invariantedge-angle obstruction
Explain the Dehn invariant as an edge-length and angle ledger rather than as a mysterious formula. Cutting subdivides edges and redistributes dihedral angles, but the total class remains unchanged, so a mismatch proves that no finite dissection can exist.
