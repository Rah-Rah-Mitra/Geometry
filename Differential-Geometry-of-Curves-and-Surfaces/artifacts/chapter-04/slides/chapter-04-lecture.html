Metric Coefficients Become A Connection

Levi-Civita Formula

\[ \Gamma^{k}_{ij}=\frac12 g^{k\ell} \left(\partial_i g_{j\ell}+\partial_j g_{i\ell}-\partial_\ell g_{ij}\right) \]

The metric determines the unique torsion-free connection compatible with length measurements.

Sphere symbols

\(\Gamma^\theta_{\phi\phi}=-\frac12\sin(2\theta)\)

Mixed terms

\(\Gamma^\phi_{\theta\phi}=\Gamma^\phi_{\phi\theta}=\cot\theta\)

Count

Only three nonzero entries drive the standard sphere computation in the notebook checks.

Present the Christoffel symbols as the operational bridge from a metric table to differentiation along the surface. The formula looks coordinate-heavy, but the message is simple: once the first fundamental form is known, the tangential correction terms are no longer optional choices.
Covariant Derivative
Differentiate along curves on the surface

Covariant Derivative Is Tangential Bookkeeping

Coordinate Rule

\[ \frac{D V^k}{dt}=\frac{dV^k}{dt}+\Gamma^k_{ij}\,\dot u^i V^j \]

  • \(DV/dt=0\) defines parallel transport.
  • \(D\dot\gamma/dt=0\) defines geodesics.
  • The formula uses only the first fundamental form and its derivatives.
Instructor checkpoint

A vector field can look constant in ambient space and still fail to be parallel intrinsically; parallel means its covariant derivative along the curve vanishes.

Explain the covariant derivative as ordinary differentiation followed by the correction that keeps the result meaningful inside the tangent plane. In coordinates, this correction is exactly where the Christoffel symbols enter, so parallel and geodesic conditions become systems of ordinary differential equations.
Gauss And Codazzi
Intrinsic and extrinsic data must agree

The Compatibility Equations Are A Lock

Lock Conditions

Compatibility prevents a first form and a second form from being chosen independently.

\[ K_{\mathrm{metric}} = \frac{eg-f^2}{EG-F^2} \]

Codazzi then checks how the second form changes under the same connection.

Frame the Gauss and Codazzi equations as a consistency lock between intrinsic and extrinsic data. The first form controls the connection and intrinsic curvature; the second form can describe bending only when it passes the same checks. This prepares the local theory without copying its proof.
Geodesic Equations
\(D\dot\gamma/dt=0\)

Geodesics As Metric ODEs

Coordinate Form

\[ \ddot u^k+\Gamma^k_{ij}\dot u^i\dot u^j=0 \]

For the unit sphere:

\[ \ddot\theta-\sin\theta\cos\theta\,\dot\phi^2=0,\quad \ddot\phi+2\cot\theta\,\dot\theta\dot\phi=0 \]

Interpretation

The equation says no intrinsic acceleration remains after the tangent-plane correction has been applied.

Make geodesics computational rather than mystical. A curve is geodesic when its covariant acceleration vanishes, and in coordinates this gives a coupled second-order system. On the sphere, the same equations conserve speed and keep the numerically integrated path in a great-circle plane.
Parallel Transport
Holonomy measures enclosed curvature

Parallel Transport Records Curvature

Holonomy Test

\[ \Delta\alpha \equiv -\int_{\Omega} K\,dA \pmod{2\pi} \]

The notebook transports a vector around a latitude loop and verifies the expected area-controlled rotation.

Treat parallel transport as an experiment with a vector carried around a loop while its covariant derivative is zero. On a flat surface the vector returns unchanged for small contractible loops, but on a sphere the return angle records the enclosed area through curvature.
Exponential Coordinates
Geodesic circles compare curvature signs

Exponential Coordinates Measure Spreading

Polar Metric

\[ ds^2 = dr^2 + G(r,\theta)^2\,d\theta^2 \]

\[ G_{rr}+K\,G=0,\quad G(0,\theta)=0,\quad G_r(0,\theta)=1 \]

At radius \(r=0.35\), circle length shrinks for \(K=1\) and expands for \(K=-1\).

Connect geodesic polar coordinates to curvature by watching nearby radial geodesics spread. The metric coefficient in the angular direction satisfies a Jacobi-style equation. Positive curvature squeezes circumference, zero curvature gives Euclidean spreading, and negative curvature expands it.

Gauss-Bonnet Is A Curvature Ledger

Local Triangle Test

\[ \alpha+\beta+\gamma-\pi=\int_{\Delta}K\,dA \]

For the checked spherical triangle, angle excess and area both equal approximately \(1.0471975512\).

Global mesh ledger

Sphere target total curvature: \(4\pi\). Torus target total curvature: \(0\).

Teaching use

Angle sums become a measurable curvature instrument rather than a memorized theorem.

Introduce Gauss-Bonnet as a ledger balancing angle defect with integrated curvature and topology. For a spherical triangle, the notebook check makes the local statement tangible: angle excess and area agree to numerical precision, turning a theorem into a measurable diagnostic.
Intrinsic Curvature
Gaussian curvature does not need ambient bending data

Theorema Egregium: Curvature From The Metric

Metric Curvature

The chapter's symbolic check recovers

\[ K_{\mathrm{sphere}}=R^{-2},\qquad \det(g)=R^4\sin^2\theta. \]

The cylinder can bend in ambient space while keeping the same intrinsic zero curvature as the plane.

Emphasize the surprise of Gauss's theorem. Gaussian curvature can be computed from the first fundamental form even though bending in space may look different. The cylinder-plane example and the sphere computation show both sides: isometric bending preserves zero curvature, while the sphere metric gives K equal to one over R squared.

Clairaut Relation

\[ \rho(s)\,\sin\alpha(s)=\mathrm{constant} \]

The geodesic turns as the rotational radius changes so that the product remains conserved.

Misconceptions
Warnings that protect the theorem statements

Four Common Failure Modes

Angle preservation is not isometry

A conformal map can keep right angles and still rescale every length.

Christoffel symbols are not tensors

The connection is invariant, but its coordinate coefficients transform with derivative terms.

Geodesic does not mean shortest forever

Geodesics are locally distance-minimizing until conjugate or cut behavior intervenes.

Parallel transport depends on the path

Curvature appears as holonomy around loops, so returning to the same point need not restore the vector.

Pause for the traps before the final synthesis. Orthogonal coordinates do not imply isometry, Christoffel symbols are not tensor components, a geodesic is not necessarily globally shortest, and parallel transport is path-dependent when curvature is present. Each warning blocks a common misreading of the chapter.
