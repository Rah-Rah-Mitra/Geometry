Smooth Structure

A Manifold Is An Atlas With Smooth Overlap Rules

Definition Focus

A smooth atlas gives neighborhoods that look like open sets in Euclidean space, with smooth coordinate changes wherever two charts describe the same points.

\( \psi \circ \phi^{-1}: \phi(U \cap V) \longrightarrow \psi(U \cap V) \)
Teaching Caution

No single chart is the manifold. The structure is the compatibility of all usable charts.

Source orientation: differentiable manifolds and coordinate systemsAtlas compatibility
After the roadmap, slow down and make the definition tactile. A chart is only a local measuring device, while the atlas is the agreement between measuring devices. Ask students what would fail if the overlap map were merely continuous rather than smooth: derivatives would depend on the coordinates rather than describing the manifold.

Quiet Topology With Loud Consequences

Second Countability And Partitions Of Unity Patch The Local World

Why Countability Enters

Local coordinate work becomes global only when covers can be controlled. Paracompactness and locally finite refinements make infinite patching safe.

Partition Principle

A smooth family of weights is subordinate to a cover, and the weights add to one near every point.

\( \sum_i \rho_i(m)=1,\qquad \operatorname{supp}(\rho_i)\subset U_i \)
Existence of partitions of unityLocal finite sums, global smooth objects
Treat partitions of unity as engineering infrastructure rather than a technical interruption. The theorem says the course can build something in charts, multiply by local weights, and add the pieces without losing smoothness. That is why the topological assumptions are present before tangent vectors and vector fields become serious global objects.
Intrinsic Differential Operators

Tangent Vectors Act On Functions Before They Look Like Arrows

Definition Focus

A tangent vector at \(m\) is a linear derivation on germs of smooth functions at \(m\). It knows how every smooth observable changes at the point.

\( v = \sum_i a_i\,\frac{\partial}{\partial r_i}\Big|_m \)
Coordinate Warning

The arrow is a useful diagram. The derivation rule is the invariant definition.

Tangent vectors and differentialsDerivation viewpoint
Make this slide do conceptual repair. Many students bring a physics-style picture of tangent arrows, which is useful but incomplete. Warner's derivation definition explains why tangents make sense without an ambient Euclidean space. Coordinates then give a basis of derivations, so the familiar components reappear as a representation, not as the definition.

Coordinate Computation

For a surface patch \(F(u,v)\), the differential sends coordinate basis directions to the Jacobian columns.

\( J_F(0.45,-0.30)= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0\\ 0 & 1\\ 0.21 & 0.18 \end{bmatrix} \)

Read the matrix as geometry. The two columns are not just numerical output; they are the images of the coordinate tangent directions. Since the columns have rank two, the surface patch keeps two independent directions. This is the exact style of calculation that later supports immersions, submersions, and submanifold recognition.

Notebook Anchor

Regular Values Make Level Sets Into Submanifolds

Regular Value Test

For \(f(x,y)=x^2+y^2\), a nonzero level has nonzero gradient along the level curve.

\( \nabla f(1,0)=(2,0),\quad \|\nabla f(1,0)\|=2 \)
Singular Contrast

At the origin, \(\nabla f=0\). The level \(f^{-1}(0)\) is not a one-dimensional curve there.

\( \|\nabla f(0,0)\|=0 \)
Implicit theorem diagnosticRegular versus singular level
Use the circle example because it is visually familiar and mathematically unforgiving. Positive levels have a normal direction everywhere, so the implicit theorem provides a local coordinate slice. At the origin the picture collapses to a point and the derivative loses rank, which is exactly the hypothesis failure students must learn to notice.
Immersion, Embedding, Submanifold

Submanifold Claims Combine Rank With Topology

Immersion

The differential is injective at every point, so tangent directions are not collapsed.

Embedding

An immersion is also a homeomorphism onto its image, so the parametrized object has the intended topology.

Submanifold

Locally, the subset can be described as a coordinate slice or a regular level set.

Submanifolds and diffeomorphismsRank plus topology
Make a precise distinction that prevents later confusion. An immersion is a differential statement, so it sees the tangent map but not all global topology. An embedding adds the correct topological placement. A submanifold theorem usually proves both a local coordinate description and enough compatibility to treat the subset as a manifold in its own right.
Smooth Motion From Tangent Data

A Vector Field Assigns A Derivation At Every Point

Field View

A smooth vector field \(X\) is a smooth section of the tangent bundle. It differentiates functions and generates local flow curves.

\( \gamma'(t)=X_{\gamma(t)} \)
Flow View

The existence theorem turns a vector field into local motion, unique through each initial point while the solution exists.

Vector fields and local flowsODE existence as geometry
This is where analysis becomes geometric motion. A vector field supplies a tangent derivation at each point, and the ordinary differential equation theorem turns that assignment into local integral curves. Stress local existence and uniqueness, since later global conclusions require additional hypotheses that are not automatic from a smooth field alone.
Commutator As A Geometry Detector

The Lie Bracket Measures Failure Of Two Flows To Commute

Algebraic Formula

Vector fields act on functions, so their commutator is another derivation.

\( [X,Y](f)=X(Yf)-Y(Xf) \)
Geometric Reading

If a tiny \(X\)-then-\(Y\) motion differs from \(Y\)-then-\(X\), the bracket records the first nontrivial drift.

Lie brackets of vector fieldsCommutators feed Frobenius
Present the bracket as the algebraic preparation for Frobenius rather than as a mysterious new operation. Since vector fields differentiate functions, their commutator differentiates functions too. The geometric intuition is a tiny rectangle of flows whose two paths do not meet exactly. That drift will decide whether a distribution closes on itself.
Moving Tangent Subspaces

A Distribution Chooses Allowed Directions At Each Point

Definition Focus

A \(c\)-dimensional distribution assigns a \(c\)-dimensional subspace \({\mathcal D}_m \subset T_mM\) smoothly at each point.

Integral Manifold

A submanifold \(N\) is integral when every tangent space \(T_nN\) lands exactly inside the assigned subspace.

\( T_nN = {\mathcal D}_n \quad\text{along the leaf} \)
Distributions and integral manifoldsPlane fields as allowed motion
Describe a distribution as a rule for legal directions. At each point, it tells a moving particle which tangent directions are permitted. The main question is whether those infinitesimal permissions assemble into honest submanifolds. This is not automatic: a smoothly varying set of planes can twist too much to be tangent to any family of leaves.
The Chapter's Closing Bridge

Frobenius: Bracket Closure Is The Algebra Of Leaves

Theorem Form

If a smooth distribution is involutive, then through each point there is a local integral manifold tangent to the distribution.

\( X,Y\in{\mathcal D}\quad\Rightarrow\quad [X,Y]\in{\mathcal D} \)
Interpretation

Closure under bracket says the distribution does not generate an immediate drift in a forbidden direction.

Frobenius theoremAlgebraic closure to local leaves
State Frobenius as the chapter's final conversion theorem. The input is algebraic closure under Lie brackets, and the output is geometric existence of integral manifolds. The point is not that brackets are easier than leaves in every problem, but that they are computable in coordinates and invariant enough to certify integrability.

A Contact-Style Distribution Fails The Bracket Test

Local Frame

Use the two vector fields \(X=\partial_x+y\partial_z\) and \(Y=\partial_y\). They span the kernel of \(dz-y\,dx\).

\( [X,Y] = -\partial_z \)
Diagnostic

bracket closed = false. The bracket points outside the assigned two-plane.

Frobenius bracket testContact-style nonintegrability
Use this as the main counterexample energy of the lecture. The distribution is perfectly smooth and two-dimensional at every point, so the failure is not visual roughness. The bracket creates a vertical direction outside the plane. Frobenius then says the assigned planes cannot be tangent to a two-dimensional family of local leaves.
Proof Patterns To Reuse

Chapter 1 Runs On Two Diagnostic Engines

Rank Engine

Derivative has controlled rank

  • Choose coordinates adapted to the differential.
  • Rewrite the map as projection, inclusion, or identity block.
  • Read off diffeomorphism, immersion, submersion, or level set structure.
\( d f_m \) controls local normal form
Bracket Engine

Allowed vector fields close under bracket

  • Represent the distribution by local frame fields.
  • Compute commutators and test membership.
  • Conclude local leaves exist, or find the forbidden drift.
\( [X_i,X_j]\in{\mathcal D} \) controls leaves
