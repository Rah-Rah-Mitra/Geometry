Tensor Product

Turn Bilinear Dependence Into Linear Dependence

Core Idea

A tensor product stores a universal bilinear slot. Any bilinear map b: V x W → U factors uniquely through a linear map from V ⊗ W.

b(v,w) = B(v ⊗ w)

The construction is algebraic, but the teaching intuition is practical: two inputs become one linear input.

Tensor and exterior algebrasUniversal mapping property
Emphasize that the tensor product is introduced to linearize bilinear dependence. The universal property is the important idea, not the construction by a free vector space. Once a bilinear rule factors through one linear map, tensor notation becomes a bookkeeping device rather than a mysterious new multiplication.

Homogeneous Tensor
Trs(V)

  • s covector factors carry covariant behavior.

Tensor algebra T(V)Degrees, decomposables, contractions
Present tensor type as a slot count. Contravariant slots accept covectors, covariant slots accept vectors, and a homogeneous tensor records how many of each kind are waiting. The key teaching move is to separate the coordinate array from the multilinear object it represents.
Exterior Algebra

Alternating Means Orientation-Aware

Alternating Quotient

The exterior algebra keeps tensors whose value changes by the sign of a permutation. The wedge product records ordered independent directions.

u ∧ v = - v ∧ u,   u ∧ u = 0

The quotient removes the symmetric noise and leaves oriented area, volume, and higher analogues.

Exterior algebra A(V)Alternation and wedge signs
Make exterior algebra feel like a deliberate loss of information. The quotient kills repeated directions and remembers only oriented independence. This is exactly why forms will later integrate over oriented domains: they ignore redundant inputs and change sign when orientation is reversed.
Top Forms

Volume Scaling Is Exterior Algebra in Action

Determinant Meaning

A top-degree form evaluates a full ordered basis. A linear map changes that measurement by one scalar: its determinant.

A(e1) ∧ A(e2) = det(A) · e1 ∧ e2

Orientation is the sign. Area or volume magnitude is the absolute value.

Exterior powersDeterminant as top-form scaling
Use the determinant slide to connect algebra with geometry. A top-degree form measures oriented volume, and a linear map scales that measurement by its determinant. This gives the class a visual reason for why exterior powers are the natural home of volume and orientation.
Interior Multiplication

Insert a Vector, Lower the Degree

Contraction Rule

Interior multiplication inserts u into the first available alternating slot. Moving across earlier factors creates the signs.

i(u)(a∧b) = a(u)b - b(u)a

This operator is the algebraic partner that makes Cartan-style formulas compact.

ContractionAnti-derivation of degree -1
Interior multiplication is best taught as inserting a vector into a form and lowering the degree by one. The signs in the displayed rule are not decorative; they record the cost of moving the inserted vector past earlier slots in an alternating expression.
Tensor Fields and Forms

Algebra at Every Point, Smoothly

Smooth Section

A tensor field of type (r,s) assigns an element of (T_m M)rs to each point m.

A k-form assigns an alternating covariant tensor in Ak(T_m^*M).

E0(M) = Cinfty(M)
Tensor bundles and exterior bundlesSmooth liftings
This is the bridge from algebra at one vector space to geometry over a manifold. A tensor field or differential form assigns one of the algebraic objects to every tangent or cotangent space smoothly. The diagram should help students see bundles as organized storage, not as extra abstraction.
Local Smoothness Test

Coordinates Reveal the Coefficients, Not the Object

Tensor Field in a Chart

On (U, y1, ..., yd), a field is smooth exactly when its coefficient functions are smooth.

T = sum aI,J · dyI ⊗ ∂J

Common mistake: the coordinate array is not the tensor. It is a chart-dependent description whose transition behavior preserves the invariant field.

Local coefficientsSmoothness in every chart
Coordinate formulas are useful only because they give a local smoothness test. Stress that the tensor field is not the list of functions, but the list is how smoothness is checked in a chart. On overlaps, the transformation law protects the coordinate-free meaning.
Exterior Differentiation

The One Operator That Drives Form Calculus

Characterizing Properties
  • d extends the ordinary differential of functions.
  • d raises degree by one.
  • d(d omega) = 0.
  • d(omega ∧ eta) = d omega ∧ eta + (-1)k omega ∧ d eta.
Exterior differentiation theoremDegree +1 antiderivation
Exterior differentiation is introduced by its behavior, not by a coordinate trick. It extends the ordinary differential of functions, raises degree by one, squares to zero, and obeys a signed product rule. These four facts explain why form calculus will control cohomology later.
Effect of Mappings

Forms Pull Back Cleanly

Smooth Map Rule

For phi: M → N, a form on N becomes a form on M by feeding it pushed-forward tangent vectors.

phi*(omega ∧ eta) = phi*omega ∧ phi*eta
d(phi*omega) = phi*(d omega)
Pullback of formsCompatibility with wedge and d
Pullback is one of the chapter's most useful asymmetries. Forms move backward along smooth maps without needing an inverse map, while vector fields generally do not. The two commutation rules on the slide are the computational reason pullbacks are so effective in geometry.
The Lie Derivative

Differentiate a Tensor Along a Flow

Definition Pattern

A flow identifies nearby fibers. Pull the transported tensor back to the original point, subtract the original tensor, divide by time, and let time go to zero.

LXT = d/dt at 0 of transported T
Flow comparisonLie derivative of tensors and forms
Frame the Lie derivative as a comparison after flow transport. The tensor is moved back to the original tangent space before differentiating in time, so the derivative is meaningful without choosing coordinates. Students should notice that the flow supplies the missing identification between nearby fibers.

A One-Form Along the Translation Flow

Notebook Check

pullback: (x+t) dy

Lie derivative labFlow definition meets Cartan formula
The worked example should be quick and concrete. Flowing by the vector field partial x sends x to x plus t, so the one-form x dy changes at first order by dy. Cartan's formula gives the same answer and previews how contraction, exterior derivative, and dynamics fit together.
Differential Ideals

Forms That Vanish on a Distribution

Annihilator Ideal

A form annihilates a distribution when it evaluates to zero on all vector fields tangent to that distribution.

I(D) = forms vanishing on D

It is a differential ideal when applying d never leaves the ideal.

Differential idealsDistributions through annihilating forms
Differential ideals translate a geometric distribution into forms that vanish on it. If all forms in an ideal remain in the ideal after applying d, the algebra is closed enough to support integral manifolds. This recasts Frobenius as a closure test.
Frobenius in Form Language

Involutiveness Equals Ideal Closure

The Diagnostic

For a smooth distribution D, its annihilator ideal I(D) is closed under exterior differentiation exactly when brackets of vector fields in D remain in D.

D involutive ⇔ d I(D) ⊂ I(D)
Proposition 2.30 and theorem 2.32 orientationBracket closure via d
State the Frobenius reformulation as a two-way diagnostic. Involutive distributions have annihilator ideals closed under exterior derivative, and closed annihilator ideals force brackets to stay inside the distribution. The proof idea is evaluation of d omega on vector fields in the distribution.
Contact Obstruction

The Small Test alpha ∧ d alpha

Integrable Case
alpha = dz

Kernel planes are horizontal and tangent to surfaces z = constant.

alpha ∧ d alpha = 0
Twisting Case
alpha = dz - y dx

The kernel planes rotate with y, producing a nonzero three-form obstruction.

alpha ∧ d alpha = dx ∧ dy ∧ dz
Notebook Frobenius labalpha wedge d alpha
Use the contact example as a memorable obstruction. The form dz is integrable because its kernel planes are tangent to level surfaces. The form dz minus y dx has a nonzero alpha wedge d alpha term, so its planes twist too much to come from surfaces.
Cartan's Graph Method

Find Maps by Finding Integral Manifolds

Existence Strategy

Build forms on N x M whose integral manifolds must be graphs. If the generated ideal is differential, Frobenius supplies local integral manifolds.

forms→ideal→graph→map
Cartan methodIntegral manifolds as graphs
Cartan's graph method turns existence of maps into an integral-manifold problem. Instead of solving for a map directly, one builds forms on a product space whose integral manifolds are graphs. The method will matter again when Lie group homomorphisms are built from infinitesimal data.
