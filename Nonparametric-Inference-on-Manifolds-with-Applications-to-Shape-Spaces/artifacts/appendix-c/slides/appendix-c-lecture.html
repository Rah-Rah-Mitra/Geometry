A Prior Over Measures

Parametric

Prior mass sits on a finite-dimensional vector.

Nonparametric

Prior mass sits on the space of all probability measures.

Payoff

The data choose how many components a density needs.

Random measure

$P \sim \mathrm{DP}(\alpha, G_0), \qquad P \text{ is itself random}$

Goal

An infinite-dimensional prior with tractable inference.

Printed pp. 218-224A Prior Over Measures
Parametric priors place mass on a finite-dimensional vector. The Dirichlet process instead places mass on the infinite-dimensional space of probability measures, letting the data decide how many components a density needs. This flexibility is exactly what manifold density estimation demands.

Ferguson Definition

Idea

Defined by its marginals on every finite partition.

Existence

Kolmogorov consistency makes the random measure legitimate.

Dirichlet marginal

$(P(A_1),\dots,P(A_k)) \sim \mathrm{Dir}\big(\alpha G_0(A_1),\dots,\alpha G_0(A_k)\big)$

Read it

For any partition $A_1,\dots,A_k$ of the sample space.

Printed pp. 218-224Ferguson Definition
Ferguson defines the Dirichlet process by its finite-dimensional marginals: for every measurable partition the vector of assigned masses follows a Dirichlet distribution with parameters alpha times the base measure. Kolmogorov consistency guarantees a genuine random probability measure exists with these marginals.

Base Measure and Concentration

Base measure $G_0$

The prior mean: $\mathbb{E}[P(A)] = G_0(A)$.

Concentration $\alpha$

Controls dispersion around the mean.

Printed pp. 218-224Base Measure and Concentration
Two knobs control the prior. The base measure G-naught is the prior mean, the measure you expect on average. The concentration alpha tunes dispersion: large alpha keeps draws near G-naught, small alpha yields draws concentrated on a few atoms far from the mean.

Printed pp. 218-224Stick-Breaking Construction
Sethuraman makes the process constructive. Break a unit stick by independent Beta one alpha fractions; each piece is a weight, and atoms drawn from the base measure receive those weights. The resulting random measure is almost surely discrete, a countable sum of weighted point masses.

Weights Decay Geometrically

Discreteness

A DP draw is almost surely a discrete measure.

Draw

$P = \sum_{j=1}^{\infty} w_j\, \delta_{\theta_j}$

Practical truncation

A few atoms hold most of the mass.

Printed pp. 218-224Weights Decay Geometrically
Because each weight multiplies the remaining stick by a Beta fraction, the weights decay geometrically in expectation. A handful of atoms capture most of the mass, so although infinitely many components exist, only finitely many matter for any finite dataset.

Polya Urn Predictive

Marginalize

Integrate out $P$ to get a predictive rule.

Two moves

Copy a past value, or draw a fresh one from $G_0$.

Predictive law

$\theta_{n+1} \mid \theta_{1:n} \sim \frac{\alpha}{\alpha+n}\,G_0 + \frac{1}{\alpha+n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\delta_{\theta_i}$

Effect

Repeats become more likely: rich get richer.

Printed pp. 218-224Polya Urn Predictive
Integrating out the random measure yields the Polya urn predictive rule. A new draw either copies an existing value, with probability proportional to how often it already appeared, or samples a fresh value from the base measure with probability proportional to alpha.

Chinese Restaurant Clustering

Metaphor

Customers seat at tables; tables are clusters.

Seating

$\Pr(\text{table } c) \propto n_c, \qquad \Pr(\text{new table}) \propto \alpha$

Result

Automatic, data-driven clustering.

Printed pp. 218-224Chinese Restaurant Clustering
The same scheme reads as the Chinese restaurant process: customers pick occupied tables in proportion to their crowd size, or start a new table with weight alpha. This rich-get-richer rule induces clustering automatically, the engine behind nonparametric mixture inference.

Conjugacy of the Posterior

Closed under updating

Posterior of a DP is again a DP.

Updated mean

Base measure blends prior and empirical data.

Posterior

$P \mid \theta_{1:n} \sim \mathrm{DP}\!\Big(\alpha+n,\; \tfrac{\alpha G_0 + \sum_i \delta_{\theta_i}}{\alpha+n}\Big)$

Why it helps

Posterior simulation stays tractable.

Printed pp. 218-224Conjugacy of the Posterior
The Dirichlet process is conjugate. After observing data, the posterior is again a Dirichlet process whose updated base measure blends the prior G-naught with the empirical distribution, and whose concentration grows by the sample size. Posterior simulation stays tractable.

DP Mixture Models

Problem

A raw DP draw is discrete, not smooth.

Fix

Convolve the discrete draw with a continuous kernel.

DP mixture density

$f(x) = \sum_{j} w_j\, K(x \mid \theta_j), \quad P \sim \mathrm{DP}(\alpha, G_0)$

Components

Atoms become centers; kernel smooths each.

Printed pp. 218-224DP Mixture Models
A raw Dirichlet draw is discrete, unsuitable for smooth densities. Convolving it with a continuous kernel gives a Dirichlet process mixture: the atoms become component centers and the kernel smooths them. On manifolds these are exactly the mixtures the book uses for shape densities.
