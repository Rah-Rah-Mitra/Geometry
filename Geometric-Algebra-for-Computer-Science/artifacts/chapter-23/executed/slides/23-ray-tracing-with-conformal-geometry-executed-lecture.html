Conservative Logic

No interval means no possible hit. An interval means only that a detailed local test is worth paying for.

Camera rays are generated in normalized device coordinatesShadow and reflection rays reuse the same object form
Treat the camera as a ray factory. The conformal model can describe lines richly, but closest-hit rendering benefits from an origin, a unit direction, and a parameter interval. Bounding spheres are the first conservative test: a miss proves absence, while a hit merely permits finer work.

Two Reconstructions

\(r(t)=\lambda_0v_0+\lambda_1v_1+\lambda_2v_2\)

The notebook checks that the ray point and barycentric point match.

Closest Surface Rule

A later triangle hit can be geometrically real and still invisible if a smaller positive parameter has already been found.

Triangle faces are the actual intersection primitivesNormals and materials are read from the hit record
Slow down on barycentric coordinates because they are the bridge from intersection to shading. The ray parameter locates the surface point, while the three weights decide whether the point is inside the triangle and how vertex normals or other attributes should be interpolated.

Takeaways

Takeaways for Graphics Software

Algebra Names Objects

Points, rays, planes, spheres, reflections, and transformations belong to one conformal geometric vocabulary.

Storage Answers Queries

Point-direction rays, vertex arrays, barycentric weights, and BSP nodes are chosen for repeated rendering questions.

Checks Protect Meaning

Distance identities, unit normals, positive areas, hit agreement, and artifact existence catch broken geometric assumptions.

Closest Hit Is Local

The visible surface is the nearest valid positive parameter, not every mathematically possible intersection.

Acceleration Is Conservative

Spheres and boxes discard work only when they can prove a detailed primitive cannot be involved.

Shading Reuses Geometry

Normals, finite light segments, reflection, and refraction continue the same object-based reasoning after a hit.

Design habit. Let the conformal model define the geometric contract, then let the renderer choose the representation that keeps the contract cheap to evaluate.
