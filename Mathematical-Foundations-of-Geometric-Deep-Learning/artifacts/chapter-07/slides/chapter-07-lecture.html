Walks, Paths, and Connectivity

Definitions

Walks repeat vertices; paths do not; cycles return to start. Components are maximal connected pieces.

Walk counting

$(A^k)_{ij}=\#\text{walks }i\to j\text{ of length }k$

The adjacency power links combinatorics to linear algebra.

Why it matters

It explains how information propagates over multiple hops in a graph network.

Graph neural networksChapter 07

Message Passing

Update rule

$h_v^{(l+1)}=\phi\!\left(h_v^{(l)},\bigoplus_{u\in N(v)}\psi(h_u^{(l)})\right)$

Each node aggregates transformed neighbor features and updates itself, layer by layer.

Symmetry

Permutation-equivariant aggregation respects the graph's lack of node ordering.

Unifying view

This abstraction ties together the algebraic and spectral foundations of the book.

PDF pages 65-77Message Passing
Define the message-passing framework: each node aggregates transformed features from its neighbors and updates its own representation, repeated over layers. This permutation-equivariant scheme is the unifying abstraction of graph neural networks and ties together the algebraic and spectral foundations of the book.
