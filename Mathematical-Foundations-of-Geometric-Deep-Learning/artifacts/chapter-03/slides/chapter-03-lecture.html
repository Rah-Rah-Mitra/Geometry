LinearizationChapter 03

Derivatives and the Jacobian

Best linear approximation

$f(x+h)=f(x)+J_f(x)\,h+o(\|h\|)$

Jacobian

$(J_f)_{ij}=\dfrac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_j}$

Backpropagation

Reverse-mode differentiation multiplies layer Jacobians from output to input, giving gradients for every parameter efficiently.

Differentiability means the linear map captures the function to first order near a point.

PDF pages 26-36Derivatives & Jacobian
Define the derivative of a multivariate map as the best linear approximation, encoded by the Jacobian matrix of partial derivatives. Differentiability means the linear map approximates the function to first order. Backpropagation is exactly repeated application of the Jacobian through composed layers, so this object is foundational to learning.
Scalar fieldsChapter 03

Gradient and Directional Derivative

Gradient

$\nabla f=\left(\tfrac{\partial f}{\partial x_1},\dots,\tfrac{\partial f}{\partial x_n}\right)$

Directional derivative

$D_v f=\nabla f\cdot v$

Steepest ascent

The gradient points where $f$ increases fastest; its negative is the descent direction.

Gradient descent

$x_{t+1}=x_t-\eta\,\nabla f(x_t)$ is the optimization engine of every neural network.

PDF pages 26-36Gradient
Specialize to scalar fields. The gradient is the vector of partials pointing in the direction of steepest ascent; the directional derivative projects it onto a chosen direction. Gradient descent follows the negative gradient, making this the engine of optimization in every neural network.
Vector fieldsChapter 03

Divergence and Curl

Divergence

$\nabla\cdot F=\sum_i \tfrac{\partial F_i}{\partial x_i}$

Curl

$\nabla\times F$ measures local rotation

Laplacian

$\Delta f=\nabla\cdot\nabla f$ is divergence of the gradient and governs diffusion.

Forward link

The Laplacian reappears in spectral theory and graph diffusion in later chapters.

PDF pages 26-36Divergence & Curl
Introduce divergence and curl on vector fields. Divergence measures net outflow at a point; curl measures local rotation. The Laplacian is divergence of the gradient and reappears throughout spectral theory and diffusion on graphs and manifolds, linking calculus to later chapters.
Composition and boundariesChapter 03

Chain Rule and Integral Theorems

Chain rule

$J_{g\circ f}(x)=J_g(f(x))\,J_f(x)$

Composition of maps is multiplication of Jacobians; this is precisely what autodiff exploits.

Integral theorems

  • Green: circulation and area
  • Stokes: curl and boundary
  • Gauss: divergence and flux

They generalize to differential forms and underpin conservation laws and aggregation.

PDF pages 26-36Chain Rule & Theorems
State the multivariate chain rule as composition of Jacobians, then the integral theorems of Green, Stokes, and Gauss that relate a field on a region to its behavior on the boundary. These theorems generalize to differential forms and underpin conservation laws and message-passing aggregation.
