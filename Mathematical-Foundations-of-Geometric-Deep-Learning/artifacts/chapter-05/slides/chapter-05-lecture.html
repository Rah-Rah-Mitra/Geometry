Function spacesChapter 05

Hilbert Spaces of Functions

L-squared

$\langle f,g\rangle=\int f\,\overline{g}\,d\mu$

Square-integrable functions form a complete, infinite-dimensional inner-product space.

Basis expansion

$f=\sum_n \langle f,e_n\rangle\,e_n$

Why it matters

Orthonormal expansions are the analytic basis of Fourier and spectral methods.

PDF pages 51-54Hilbert Spaces
Recall that a Hilbert space is a complete inner-product space and emphasize the function-space examples: square-integrable functions form an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space. Orthonormal bases let any function be expanded as a series of coefficients, the analytic basis for Fourier and spectral methods.

Linear Operators

Operator norm

$\|T\|=\sup_{\|x\|=1}\|Tx\|$

Boundedness is equivalent to continuity for linear operators.

Operators in GDL

  • Convolutions
  • Integral transforms

Why norms matter

Operator norms control stability and conditioning of learning.

PDF pages 51-54Linear Operators
Introduce bounded linear operators between Hilbert spaces and the operator norm. Boundedness is equivalent to continuity. Neural network layers, convolutions, and integral transforms are operators, so understanding their norms governs stability and the conditioning of learning.
Symmetry of operatorsChapter 05

Adjoints and Self-Adjointness

Adjoint

$\langle Tx,y\rangle=\langle x,T^*y\rangle$

Self-adjoint

$T=T^*$

Consequences

Self-adjoint operators have real spectra and orthogonal eigenfunctions.

This makes the Laplacian and graph operators diagonalizable, underlying spectral convolution.

PDF pages 51-54Adjoints
Define the adjoint operator via the inner product and the special class of self-adjoint operators. Self-adjoint operators have real spectra and orthogonal eigenfunctions, the property that makes the Laplacian and graph operators diagonalizable and underlies spectral convolution.

Toward Spectral Theory

Spectral theorem

$T=\sum_n \lambda_n\,\langle\cdot,e_n\rangle e_n$

Compact self-adjoint operators admit a complete orthonormal eigenbasis with real eigenvalues.

Payoff

Differential and graph operators become multiplication in the eigenbasis, the foundation of spectral networks.

PDF pages 51-54Toward Spectral Theory
Preview spectral theory. For compact self-adjoint operators the spectral theorem provides a complete orthonormal set of eigenfunctions with real eigenvalues. This decomposition turns differential and graph operators into simple multiplication in the eigenbasis, the foundation of spectral networks.
