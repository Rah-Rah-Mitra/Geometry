Triangles

Centers Answer Different Questions

Four Meanings Of Center
  • \(G\): average of vertices.
  • \(O\): equal distance from \(A,B,C\).
  • \(H\): common point of altitudes.
  • \(I\): weighted by side lengths.
Notebook Checks

Euler residual \(=0\), ratio error \(=0\).

Median Ceva product \(=1\).

The sample triangle has area \(9.625\), inradius \(1.3431\), and circumradius \(2.7906\).

Teaching Diagnostic

Ask which construction survives if only side lengths are known, and which needs directed lines or altitude structure.

Start with the triangle as a laboratory of different meanings of center. The centroid averages vertices, the circumcenter solves equal distances to vertices, the orthocenter records altitudes, and the incenter balances side lengths. The Euler line and Ceva product are not decorative labels; they are tests that the construction is coherent.
Minimum Principle

A Point Is Certified By A Field, Not By A Guess

Objective

\[F(P)=\|P-A\|+\|P-B\|+\|P-C\|\]

For an interior minimizing point, the outward unit directions to the vertices balance.

Misread To Avoid

The closest-looking named center is not automatically the minimizing point.

This slide changes the mood from incidence to optimization. A minimum principle is easier to trust when the scalar field is visible and a residual is attached. Have students compare the Fermat point with the centroid and incenter, then read the tiny gradient norm as the numerical certificate that the minimum has been found.
Spherical Triangles

Area Appears As Angle Surplus

Spherical Excess

\[\operatorname{area}_{\mathbb{S}^2}(\triangle ABC)=\alpha+\beta+\gamma-\pi\]

For the unit sphere, area and angular excess use the same scale.

Notebook Sample
Angle sum\(3.5386\)
Excess area\(0.3970\)
Vertex norm errors\(0,0,0\)
Side lengths\(1.8492,\ 0.6959,\ 1.2761\)
Inspection Target

Follow an arc and check that its plane passes through the sphere center, not through the Euclidean chord alone.

Emphasize that a spherical side is a great-circle arc, not the straight chord seen in space. The area formula is striking because it replaces a determinant by angular surplus. The displayed sample has angle sum about 3.5386, so the excess and the unit-sphere area are about 0.3970.

Why A 3D Artifact Helps

A flat tetrahedron sketch can hide which face a sphere is tangent to; a mesh records all faces and constraints.

Present the tetrahedron as the smallest three-dimensional mesh with genuine face structure. In the regular model, every edge length agrees, the four vertices sit on one circumsphere, and every face touches one insphere. The radius ratio of three and Euler number two are the fast checks that the model is correct.
Inversion

Radial Motion With Circle Diagnostics

Transformation

\[I_R(x)=\frac{R^2}{\|x\|^2}x,\qquad \|x\|\,\|I_R(x)\|=R^2\]

Two Circle Cases
  • A circle missing the center maps to another circle.
  • A circle through the center maps to a line.
  • The conformal Jacobian residual is about \(5.97\cdot10^{-17}\).
Numerical Evidence

Radius product error \(2.22\cdot10^{-16}\), inverse-circle residual \(2.50\cdot10^{-16}\), inverse-line residual \(1.27\cdot10^{-12}\).

Do the inversion slide as a diagnostic rather than a transformation trick. The formula sends each point along its radial line and enforces the radius product. Then separate the two circle cases carefully: a circle away from the center remains a circle, while a circle through the center becomes a line.
Circle Pencils

Linear Equations Keep The Same Base Points

Circle Equation

\[\alpha(x^2+y^2)-2\beta x-2\gamma y+\delta=0\]

Members of a pencil are linear combinations of two such equations.

Base-Point Logic

If \(C_1(P)=C_2(P)=0\), then \((\lambda C_1+\mu C_2)(P)=0\). The same argument holds at \(Q\).

Diagnostics
Base points\((0.5,\pm0.8660)\)
Max base residual\(2.22\cdot10^{-16}\)
Orthogonal pencil residual\(0\)
Use the pencil equation to make the family algebraic. A linear combination of two circle equations is another member of the pencil, and the shared base points survive because both original equations vanish there. The radical axis x equals one half is the visual ledger where equal powers are recorded.
Parataxis

Hopf Fibers Make Equidistant Circles Concrete

Model

\[\mathbb{S}^3=\{(z_1,z_2)\in\mathbb{C}^2: |z_1|^2+|z_2|^2=1\}\]

\[\gamma_u(t)=e^{it}u\]

What Projection Shows

Stereographic projection sends Hopf fibers to ordinary circles in three-space, often linked in pairs.

Invariant Check

Pairwise matched distances in \(\mathbb{S}^3\) have standard deviation at most \(2.21\cdot10^{-16}\). Projected circle-fit residual is \(2.22\cdot10^{-15}\).

Parataxis can sound mysterious if it is introduced only as a spatial picture. Anchor it in the Hopf model: points of S3 are unit vectors in C2, and phase multiplication traces a great circle. Projection makes ordinary circles, but the important invariant is the constant spherical distance between matched fibers.

Common Misreadings

The Picture Is Not The Invariant

Spherical Side

A chord is visually tempting, but the side is the great-circle arc on the sphere.

\(\text{arc length}=\arccos(a\cdot b)\)

Inversion Center

Inversion is undefined at the center. Any diagnostic that samples \(O\) has already left the theorem's domain.

Require \(\|x\|>0\).

Projected Circles

The Hopf invariant lives in \(\mathbb{S}^3\). A projected circle picture is evidence, not the distance definition.

Check distance before projection.

Better Habit

For every construction, ask which quantity should remain unchanged, which residual tests it, and which visualization makes failure visible.

Use this warning slide to collect the chapter's common confusions before the recap. Chords are not spherical sides, a Euclidean-looking picture is not automatically an intrinsic measurement, and projected Hopf circles should not be judged only by ordinary three-dimensional distance. Each warning points back to a better invariant.
