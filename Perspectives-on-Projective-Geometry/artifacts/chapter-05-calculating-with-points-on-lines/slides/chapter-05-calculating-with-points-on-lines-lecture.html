The Working Line and Homogeneous Pairs

Points of $\mathbb{RP}^1$

A homogeneous pair $(u,v)$, defined up to nonzero scale.

The one invariant

The cross-ratio is the only projective invariant of four collinear points.

Cross-ratio

$$(a,b;c,d)=\frac{\,|a\,c|\,|b\,d|\,}{\,|a\,d|\,|b\,c|\,}$$

Infinity is ordinary

$(1,0)$ is just one more point on the line.

Printed pp. 79-92The Working Line and Homogeneous Pairs
Explain that a point of the projective line is a homogeneous pair, defined up to nonzero scaling. The cross-ratio is the only projective invariant of four collinear points, and its determinant formula is the engine behind every later identity. Emphasize scale-invariance and the role of infinity.

Von Staudt Addition

Affine chart

Put $\infty$ horizontal: lines to $\infty$ are parallels.

Auxiliary points

Pick $P$, $Q$ off the base line as projection pivots.

Result

$$z=\,\mathrm{meet}\big(\ell,\ \overline{r\,q'}\big)=x+y$$

Printed pp. 79-92Von Staudt Addition
Present von Staudt addition through the affine chart where infinity is the horizontal direction, so lines toward infinity are parallel horizontals. Build the auxiliary points P and Q off the line, project x and y, and read the meeting point on the base line as x plus y. Highlight symmetry in x and y.

The Field Axioms, Realized Geometrically

Identities

$0$ is additive, $1$ is multiplicative — by anchor choice.

Inverses

Negatives and reciprocals are harmonic constructions.

Distributivity

A closing incidence diagram links $+$ and $\cdot$.

The punchline

$$\big(\,\ell,\ \oplus,\ \otimes\,\big)\;\cong\;(\mathbb{R},+,\cdot)$$

Each axiom is a figure

Associativity, commutativity, distributivity: every law closes as an incidence statement, not a calculation.

Printed pp. 79-92The Field Axioms, Realized Geometrically
Summarize that the two constructions satisfy associativity, commutativity, distributivity, identities, and inverses. Each axiom corresponds to a closing incidence diagram. The slide frames the punchline: the points of a line, with geometric plus and times, form a field isomorphic to the coordinate field.

Pappos and the Commutativity of Multiplication

Make the deep link explicit: the two recipes for x times y and y times x overlay into the Pappos configuration. Over the reals they coincide automatically, but synthetically Pappos's theorem is the incidence axiom that forces commutative multiplication. This is where geometry dictates algebra.

The Fundamental Theorem: Synthetic Meets Analytic

Bridge

$$\text{incidence coords}\;\equiv\;\text{analytic coords}$$

Harmonic maps

A harmonic bijection fixing $0,1,\infty$ is a field automorphism.

Order rigidity

On the ordered reals, that automorphism is the identity.

Printed pp. 79-92The Fundamental Theorem: Synthetic Meets Analytic
State the bridge theorem. Coordinates recovered from pure incidence agree with analytic coordinates up to projective transformation, and a harmonic map fixing the anchors is forced to be a field automorphism. Over the ordered reals this leaves only the identity, tying synthetic and analytic projective geometry together.
