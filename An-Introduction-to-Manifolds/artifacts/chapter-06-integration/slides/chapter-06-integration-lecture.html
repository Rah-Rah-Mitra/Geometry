Integration Is Signed Geometry

Chapter 6 turns differential forms into numbers. The new hazard is not size but sign: a frame, a chart, a boundary normal, or a parametrization can reverse the answer.

Lecture Thesis

Orientation is the bookkeeping system that lets integration on manifolds be invariant under chart changes and still remember direction.

Learning Roadmap

Four Places Where The Sign Can Change

1. Frame

A basis is positive only relative to an orientation class.

\(u=vA,\quad \det A>0\)

2. Chart

Coordinate overlaps must preserve determinant sign.

\(\det\left(\frac{\partial y^i}{\partial x^j}\right)>0\)

3. Boundary

The outward vector is placed before the boundary frame.

\(\iota_X\omega\)

4. Integral

Pullbacks carry the Jacobian determinant, not its absolute value.

\(F^*\eta=(\det JF)\,\omega\)
Frames, charts, boundaries, integralsRoadmap
Use this slide as the explicit contract for the lecture. Students should hear that there are four sign checkpoints, and each one has a simple local test. A frame is compared by a determinant, a chart overlap is compared by the determinant of its transition Jacobian, a boundary is oriented by placing an outward vector first, and an integral changes by the signed Jacobian under pullback. The later examples are not separate tricks; they are repeated uses of these four checks.
Vector Space Orientation

A Positive Frame Is An Equivalence Class

Definition
Two ordered bases \(u=(u_1,\ldots,u_n)\) and \(v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)\) have the same orientation when \(u=vA\) with \(\det A>0\).

Chapter Check

The notebook compares one positive frame with determinant \(14/15\) and one negative frame with determinant \(-1\).

Payload: ../checks/orientation-frame-sign.json

Positive changes of basis can stretch, shear, or rotate, but they do not cross determinant zero.
A negative determinant means exactly one orientation reversal has occurred.
Orientation starts before manifolds appearFrame sign test
Begin at the linear algebra level because every later manifold definition is pointwise. The formula u equals v times A should be read as a comparison between two ordered frames. If the determinant of A is positive, a continuous motion through invertible frames can carry one to the other without crossing singularity. If the determinant is negative, the frame has crossed to the opposite component. The chapter check gives concrete numbers, so this is not only an abstract equivalence relation.
Top-Degree Forms

A Top Covector Is An Orientation Tester

Test
A nonzero top covector \(\beta\in \Lambda^n(V^*)\) selects the positive frames by \(\beta(v_1,\ldots,v_n)>0\).

Why Top Degree?

An \(n\)-form eats exactly \(n\) tangent vectors. Its output is the signed volume of the frame they span.

Useful Warning

For integration of forms, the sign is not erased. Replacing a determinant by its absolute value would change the theory.

Top covectors turn orientation into evaluation\(\beta(v_1,\ldots,v_n)>0\)
Move from frames to forms by emphasizing that a top covector is a measuring device with a sign. In two dimensions, dx wedge dy is the familiar signed area form, but the definition is dimension independent. A positive frame is exactly one on which the chosen top covector evaluates positively. This is the bridge to integration: a top-degree form is locally a signed volume element, so its integral must remember which frames count as positive.
From Vector Spaces To Manifolds

An Orientation Is A Smooth Choice Of Positive Frames

Orientation On \(M\)
For each \(p\in M\), choose one of the two orientation classes of \(T_pM\), locally represented by a smooth positive frame.

Equivalent Signal

A nowhere-vanishing smooth \(n\)-form \(\omega\) makes a frame positive exactly when \(\omega_p(v_1,\ldots,v_n)>0\).

Lecture Habit

Whenever a formula says “positive,” ask which tangent frames are being tested and which top form or atlas has declared them positive.

Orientation is pointwise linear algebra made smoothManifold orientation
Now globalize the linear picture. An orientation on a manifold is not one basis chosen forever; tangent spaces change from point to point. The smoothness condition says the choice of positive component is locally represented by smooth frames, or equivalently by a nowhere-zero top form. This is also where students should separate orientability from a particular orientation: orientability means such a smooth choice exists, while an orientation is one of the possible choices.
Coordinate Compatibility

Oriented Atlases Keep Chart Changes Positive

Oriented Atlas
On every overlap, the transition map must satisfy \(\det\left(\partial y^i/\partial x^j\right)>0\).

What The Determinant Protects

The coordinate frame \((\partial/\partial x^1,\ldots,\partial/\partial x^n)\) and the coordinate frame in \(y\)-coordinates lie in the same orientation class.

Failure Mode

A single negative transition does not always destroy orientability, but a negative sign around a closed chart cycle does.

The atlas is a sign agreement across overlapsPositive transition Jacobians
This slide translates orientation into coordinates. A chart gives an ordered coordinate frame, and an oriented atlas is one where all overlap maps compare those frames by positive determinants. Emphasize that the determinant belongs to the transition map, not to the manifold by itself. The point is compatibility: when two charts describe the same tangent frame, they must not disagree about which side of determinant zero they occupy.
Nonorientability

The Mobius Band Forces A Negative Sign Cycle

Obstruction
If transition signs around a closed cycle multiply to \(-1\), no globally consistent positive frame can exist.

Notebook Certificate

The gluing derivative \(\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}\) has determinant \(-1\), so the Mobius cycle test fails.

Check: ../checks/mobius-orientation-obstruction.json

Interpretation

Orientability is the ability to choose signs consistently around every loop, not merely on each individual chart.

A closed sign cycle can obstruct orientationMobius band
Use the Mobius band as the first global obstruction. Locally everything looks fine, so this is not a failure of charts or tangent spaces. The problem appears after transport around a loop: the twist returns a frame with the opposite sign. The check file records transition signs plus, plus, minus, whose product is minus one. That product is the compact witness that no oriented atlas can be chosen around the cycle.
Half-Space Charts

A Boundary Point Looks Like The Edge Of \(H^n\)

Model Half-Space
\(H^n=\{(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\in\mathbb R^n: x_n\ge 0\}\), with boundary \(\partial H^n=\{x_n=0\}\).

Interior Versus Boundary

Interior points have neighborhoods modeled on open subsets of \(\mathbb R^n\). Boundary points require half-space neighborhoods.

Geometric Meaning

The coordinate \(x_n\) measures inward distance in the local model. The missing side is where an outward normal will point.

Boundary geometry is locally half-space geometry\(H^n\)
Introduce boundary through the model space H to the n. This is deliberately local: a manifold with boundary is still n dimensional, but some points have neighborhoods that include only one side of a coordinate hyperplane. The last coordinate plays a special role in the local model, and that role is what later creates the outward normal. Keep the distinction sharp: the boundary is not a lower-dimensional manifold attached from outside; it is detected by the half-space charts of the original manifold.
Well-Defined Boundary

A Boundary Point Cannot Be Recharted Away

Invariance Of Boundary
If one half-space chart sends \(p\) to \(x_n=0\), then every compatible half-space chart sends \(p\) to its boundary hyperplane.

What This Buys

\(\partial M\) is a genuine \((n-1)\)-manifold, so it can carry its own charts, tangent frames, forms, and integrals.

Instructor Cue

The proof uses invariance of domain in spirit: a homeomorphism between half-space neighborhoods cannot turn a boundary point into an interior point.

The boundary is intrinsic, not a coordinate artifact\(\partial M\)
This is the conceptual pause after the definition. Students may worry that boundary status depends on the chosen half-space coordinate. The theorem says it does not: if one chart sees the point on the edge, compatible charts must also see it on their edge. This makes the notation partial M legitimate. Once that is granted, the boundary can be treated as its own lower-dimensional manifold, which is necessary before Stokes can even be stated.
Outward-First Convention

Boundary Orientation Comes From Contraction

Contraction Rule
If \(X\) is outward along \(\partial M\), the induced boundary form is \(\iota_X\omega\).
Outward First
\((v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1})\) is positive on \(\partial M\) iff \((X,v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1})\) is positive on \(M\).

Half-Space Formula

\( \iota_{-\partial/\partial x_n}(dx^1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx^n)=(-1)^n dx^1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx^{n-1}\)

Chapter check: ../checks/boundary-outward-first.json records \(\iota_{(0,-1)}(dx\wedge dy)=dx\).

Boundary signs are not arbitrary once \(M\) is oriented\(\iota_X\omega\)
This is the sign convention students will use every time Stokes appears. The boundary orientation is created by contracting the ambient top form with an outward vector. Equivalently, a boundary frame is positive precisely when putting the outward vector in front gives a positive ambient frame. In the upper half-plane example, the outward vector is negative partial y, and contraction of dx wedge dy gives dx, so the bottom boundary points to the right.

Boundaries With Measure Zero Do Not Disturb The Integral

Integrable Domain
A bounded set with boundary of measure zero can be integrated by Darboux sums because upper and lower contributions squeeze together.

Bridge To Manifolds

Charts reduce manifold integration to Euclidean integration on such local domains.

Local Euclidean integration still mattersDarboux grid
This slide is a short analytic interlude. Before integrating forms on a manifold, the chapter needs ordinary integration over acceptable coordinate domains. The disk grid shows the reason a boundary of measure zero is harmless: the ambiguous cells live near the boundary, and their total area shrinks as the grid is refined. This prepares students for chart-based integrals, where the local domain may have a rough boundary but still supports a well-defined integral.
Parametrization And Sign

Pullbacks Carry The Signed Jacobian

Pullback Of A Top Form
For \(F:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n\), \(F^*(dy^1\wedge\cdots\wedge dy^n)=\det(JF)\,dx^1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx^n\).

Ellipse Check

With \(a=2\), \(b=0.75\), the preserving parametrization gives \(\pi ab=4.7124\), while the reversing parametrization gives \(-\pi ab=-4.7124\).

Interactive: ../html/parameterized-ellipse-orientation.html

Do Not Hide The Sign

Area as a scalar uses absolute value. Integration of a differential form uses the signed determinant.

The Jacobian determinant is signedPullback formula
Here the determinant sign directly changes an integral. The pullback formula for a top form is the cleanest way to say it: the coefficient is the determinant of the Jacobian, not its magnitude. The ellipse example is useful because the geometric region is unchanged. Only the parametrization has been reflected, and the integral flips from pi a b to negative pi a b. This reinforces the distinction between unsigned area and integration of oriented forms.
Manifold Integral

Charts And Partitions Of Unity Localize The Integral

Definition Pattern
For compactly supported \(\omega\), choose an oriented chart cover and partition of unity \(\{\rho_\alpha\}\), then sum local coordinate integrals of \(\rho_\alpha\omega\).

Compact Support

Only finitely many localized pieces matter, so the sum is controlled even when the atlas is large.

Independence

Positive transition determinants ensure overlapping chart computations agree with the chosen orientation.

\(\int_M\omega=\sum_\alpha\int_{U_\alpha}\rho_\alpha\omega\)
Global integration is assembled from oriented local integralsPartitions of unity
This slide states the manifold integral in the operational form students can use. A compactly supported top form is cut by a partition of unity, each piece is moved into coordinates by an oriented chart, and the resulting Euclidean integrals are added. The important point is independence from the auxiliary choices. Oriented chart changes preserve sign, and partition of unity refinements add to one, so the global number is not tied to a particular cover.
The Main Theorem

Stokes Turns An Exterior Derivative Into A Boundary Integral

Stokes Theorem
If \(M\) is an oriented \(n\)-manifold with boundary and \(\omega\) is a compactly supported \((n-1)\)-form, then \(\int_M d\omega=\int_{\partial M} i^*\omega\), where \(i:\partial M\hookrightarrow M\) is inclusion and \(\partial M\) has the outward-first orientation.

Sign Dependency

The theorem uses exactly the boundary orientation defined by contraction with an outward vector.

Proof Roadmap

Reduce by partitions of unity to coordinate half-spaces, then apply the one-variable fundamental theorem in the normal direction.

Stokes is the global sign-consistent fundamental theorem\(\int_M d\omega=\int_{\partial M}i^*\omega\)
State Stokes as the climax of the chapter. The exterior derivative raises degree by one, so an n-dimensional integral of d omega is compared to an integral of omega over the n minus one dimensional boundary through the inclusion i from partial M into M. The sign convention is not optional: the boundary orientation from the previous slide is exactly what makes the local half-space calculation agree with the theorem. Present the proof roadmap rather than a full proof, highlighting partitions of unity and the normal direction.
Classical Faces Of Stokes

Line Integrals And Green's Theorem Are Sign Tests

Gradient Line Integral

The line integral of a gradient depends only on endpoints.

\( \int_C \nabla f\cdot dr=f(q)-f(p)\)

Green's Theorem

A planar circulation equals the oriented integral of scalar curl.

\( \oint_{\partial D}P\,dx+Q\,dy=\iint_D\left(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right)dx\,dy\)

Verification payload: ../checks/stokes-green-line-checks.json records zero Green residual and the endpoint line-integral check.

Classical vector calculus inherits the same orientation conventionsLine integral and Green
Connect the abstract theorem to familiar calculus without changing the sign story. For a gradient field, Stokes in one dimension is the fundamental theorem for line integrals: the oriented boundary of an interval is endpoint minus start point. In the plane, Green's theorem is Stokes for the one-form P dx plus Q dy. The counterclockwise boundary orientation and the dx dy area orientation are paired; reversing one would reverse the equality.
Final Example

The Sphere Area Form Pulls Back To \(\sin\phi\)

Sphere Parametrization
\(F(\phi,\theta)=(\sin\phi\cos\theta,\sin\phi\sin\theta,\cos\phi)\), and the area-form coefficient is \(\sin\phi\).
Area Computation
\(\displaystyle \int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^\pi \sin\phi\,d\phi\,d\theta=4\pi\).
