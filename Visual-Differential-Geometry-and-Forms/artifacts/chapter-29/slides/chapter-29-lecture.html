One Number Becomes An Operator

Surface memory

Gaussian curvature is a single area-normalized angle defect for the unique tangent plane of a surface.

Riemann's move

Every pair \(X,Y\) spans a candidate loop plane. The tensor records the returned change of any carried vector \(Z\).

Multilinear Readout
$$R:T_pM\times T_pM\times T_pM\to T_pM$$

Pedagogical rule

Keep the surface intuition, but inspect it one two-plane at a time.

source topics: angular excess and tensor curvatureFrom \(K\) to \(R(X,Y)Z\)
Use this slide to separate old surface intuition from the new tensor object. A surface point has one tangent plane, so Gaussian curvature is one sectional reading. In higher dimension, every two-plane through a tangent vector pair can be tested, and the Riemann operator records how a third vector is changed by that loop test.

Transport Has Three Ledgers

Extrinsic picture

Embed a surface, move tangent vectors, and project any off-surface drift back into the tangent plane.

Intrinsic picture

Use only the metric's Levi-Civita connection, so the rule survives without an ambient space.

Covariant ledger

Write the rule as \(\nabla_XY\). This is the form that can be commuted, checked, and made tensorial.

Lecture Choice
$$\nabla \quad\Longrightarrow\quad R(X,Y)=[\nabla_X,\nabla_Y]-\nabla_{[X,Y]}$$
topic: three constructions of parallel transportChoose the ledger that scales
The local artifact is deliberately a ledger rather than a scenic surface. It reminds students that the same transport idea can be reached from extrinsic pictures, intrinsic metric rules, or the covariant derivative. Emphasize that the lecture will use the covariant ledger because it scales cleanly to n-manifolds.

Curvature Is Failed Commutation

Definition
$$R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-\nabla_Y\nabla_XZ-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z$$

What is being compared

Differentiate \(Z\) around the two sides of an infinitesimal parallelogram.

Why subtract the bracket?

\([X,Y]\) removes failure caused by the vector fields' own noncommuting flow. Curvature is what remains.

Tensorial payoff

After the bracket correction, \(R(X,Y)Z\) depends only on the three tangent vectors at the point.

topic: covariant derivative and Riemann tensorCommutator corrected into geometry
This is the formal hinge. If one differentiates a vector field first along Y and then along X, the result need not match the reverse order. The Lie bracket subtracts the part caused only by the failure of the coordinate moves to commute. What remains is curvature: a tensorial operator rather than a coordinate artifact.

Sectional Curvature Slices

Two-Plane Reading
$$K(u,v)=\frac{\langle R(u,v)v,u\rangle}{\langle u,u\rangle\langle v,v\rangle-\langle u,v\rangle^2}$$

Why this helps

Sectional curvature asks the old surface question inside the two-plane spanned by \(u\) and \(v\).

How to read the heatmap

Dark or light entries show that different tangent two-planes can bend differently at the same point.

Important caution

The tensor is not merely a table of numbers. The table is a sampling interface for the operator \(R\).

topic: components and sectional curvaturesSurface intuition becomes a family of slices
The heatmap is a visual index of two-plane probes. Each entry should be interpreted as asking what the old two-dimensional curvature reading would be inside that tangent plane. Sectional curvature does not replace the Riemann tensor, but it gives students a way to inspect the tensor through familiar surface-like slices.

Symmetry Reduces The Ledger

Alternating Slots
$$R(X,Y,Z,W)=-R(Y,X,Z,W)$$
$$R(X,Y,Z,W)=-R(X,Y,W,Z)$$
Pair Symmetry
$$R(X,Y,Z,W)=R(Z,W,X,Y)$$
First Bianchi Identity
$$R(X,Y)Z+R(Y,Z)X+R(Z,X)Y=0$$

Meaning

The four-slot tensor looks large, but signs, swaps, and cyclic cancellation make it highly structured.

Teaching warning

Component formulas are useful only when the geometric symmetries stay visible.

topic: components and symmetriesFour slots, fewer independent facts
A raw four-index array would be unusable without structure. The symmetries say that curvature is alternating in the first two slots, alternating in the last two slots, symmetric after swapping the two pairs, and constrained by the Bianchi identity. The component map turns bookkeeping into geometry: many apparent entries are the same information with signs or cyclic cancellation.

Jacobi Fields To Ricci

Geodesic Deviation
$$\nabla_T\nabla_TJ+R(J,T)T=0$$

Jacobi field \(J\)

Tracks how a nearby geodesic separates from the reference geodesic with tangent \(T\).

Directional Trace
$$\operatorname{Ric}(v,v)=\sum_i\langle R(e_i,v)v,e_i\rangle$$

Visual consequence

Positive Ricci focuses nearby geodesics and lowers area growth. Negative Ricci spreads them and raises area growth.

topics: Jacobi equation and Ricci curvatureFrom operator to averaged focusing
Move from loop transport to families of geodesics. Jacobi fields measure how nearby geodesics separate, and the Riemann tensor supplies the acceleration term. Ricci curvature contracts those sectional effects into a direction-by-direction average, which is why the saved plot shows circumference or area growth deviating from the flat formula.
