Sign Is Measurement Data

Reverse the orientation of \(C\), and every local cell is read with the opposite sign.

$$\int_{-C}\omega=-\int_C\omega,\qquad \int_{C_1+C_2}\omega=\int_{C_1}\omega+\int_{C_2}\omega.$$

Slow down the input data before formulas appear. A k-form cannot be integrated over an arbitrary object; it needs an oriented k-dimensional domain, or a chain made from such pieces. The diagram separates the curve, surface patch, and volume cell cases. The common operation is the same: evaluate the form on oriented tangent directions and add scalar readings.
Line IntegralsChapter 37

Line Integrals Pull A 1-Form To Time

Pullback To An Interval

$$\int_\gamma \omega=\int_a^b \omega_{\gamma(t)}\bigl(\gamma'(t)\bigr)\,dt.$$

Work Interpretation

A force-like 1-form samples directed motion. Positive and negative contributions accumulate along the path.

Parameter Test

Changing speed changes \(\gamma'(t)\) and \(dt\) in opposite ways, so the oriented geometric path is what matters.

$$\gamma^*\omega = \omega_{\gamma(t)}(\gamma'(t))\,dt.$$

Diagnostic

Compare two paths with the same endpoints. A mismatch is evidence of circulation or non-exactness.

1-forms on curveswork, circulation, path dependence
Use the path picture to make pullback concrete. A curve gamma converts a one-form on the ambient space into an ordinary density on the interval of time. The vector gamma prime supplies the directed input at each sample. Reparameterizing the same oriented curve changes the speed and the time step together, so the integral remains a geometric reading.
ExactnessChapter 37

Exact Forms Collapse To Endpoints

Fundamental Theorem For A 1-Form

$$\int_\gamma df=f(\gamma(b))-f(\gamma(a)).$$

endpointthe value depends only on \(p\) and \(q\)
loop\(\int_{\partial D}df=0\) for a closed path
testnonzero loop integral rules out exactness

Potential Picture

The path integral adds small potential changes. The sum telescopes because adjacent increments share endpoints.

Local Versus Global

Closed forms look exact in small neighborhoods; holes can prevent a single global potential.

exact form \(\omega=df\)path independence as telescoping
Make exactness operational, not merely symbolic. If omega is df, each small line-integral contribution is an infinitesimal change in the potential f. Summing along the path telescopes, so only the endpoint values remain. This explains path independence, zero loop integrals for exact forms, and why potential level pictures are the right visual test.
PullbacksChapter 37

Pullback Is The Coordinate Contract

Coordinate-Ready Integral

$$\int_{\phi(U)}\omega=\int_U \phi^*\omega.$$

What \(\phi^*\omega\) Does

It feeds parameter tangent vectors through \(d\phi\), then lets \(\omega\) evaluate the resulting geometric directions.

Orientation Check

If \(\phi\) reverses orientation, the pulled-back top form carries a negative Jacobian factor.

$$\phi^*(dx\wedge dy)=\det\!\begin{pmatrix}x_u&x_v\\y_u&y_v\end{pmatrix}du\wedge dv.$$

The pullback phi star omega feeds parameter tangent vectors through d phi before omega evaluates them. Orientation is part of the contract: reversing the parameter orientation flips the integral sign.
PartitionsChapter 37

Partitions Make Boundary Terms Cancel

Boundary Cancellation Ledger

$$\sum_i \int_{\partial C_i}\alpha=\int_{\partial R}\alpha.$$

Exterior Derivative As Local Density

The integral of \(d\alpha\) over a tiny cell measures the boundary accumulation of \(\alpha\) around that cell.

$$\int_{C_i}d\alpha=\int_{\partial C_i}\alpha \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \int_R d\alpha=\int_{\partial R}\alpha.$$

Why Orientation Matters

A shared edge belongs to two adjacent cells with opposite induced orientations. Without that sign convention, Stokes cannot telescope.

partitions and induced orientationslocal boundary accumulation becomes global boundary
Present Stokes first as local cancellation. Break a region into oriented cells and apply the boundary measurement to each cell. Every shared interior edge is traversed twice with opposite orientations, so those contributions cancel. Only the outer boundary remains. The exterior derivative is the interior density whose small-cell integral records this boundary accumulation.
Generalized StokesChapter 37

Generalized Stokes Is The Accounting Identity

Stokes Theorem

$$\boxed{\int_M d\alpha=\int_{\partial M}\alpha}$$

Objects

\(M\) is an oriented \(k\)-manifold with boundary, and \(\alpha\) is a compactly supported \((k-1)\)-form when support conditions are needed.

Boundary Orientation

The boundary orientation is chosen so that outward-normal-first followed by the boundary frame agrees with the orientation of \(M\).

One Sentence Proof

Apply the small-cell identity on a partition and let all interior faces cancel by opposite signs.

\(\int_M d\alpha=\int_{\partial M}\alpha\)interior derivative equals boundary measurement
Now state the theorem as the compact form of the cancellation story. The left side integrates the derivative over the oriented manifold, while the right side integrates the original form over the induced boundary orientation. The bar chart from the notebook is used as an accounting reminder: signs and boundary conventions are structural data, not decoration.
Classical ShadowsChapter 37

Classical Theorems Are Shadows

Choose the right form, and four familiar theorems become the same boundary identity.

Green

$$d(P\,dx+Q\,dy)=(Q_x-P_y)\,dx\wedge dy$$

Planar circulation becomes area integral of scalar curl.

Vector Stokes

$$\int_S d\alpha=\int_{\partial S}\alpha$$

Surface curl flux equals circulation around the boundary curve.

Gauss / Divergence

$$d\,\iota_F(dx\wedge dy\wedge dz)=(\nabla\!\cdot F)\,dV$$

Interior source density accumulates as outward boundary flux.

Cauchy

$$\bar\partial f=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \int_{\partial D} f(z)\,dz=0$$

Holomorphicity is a closedness condition for the complex line integral.

$$\hbox{all four read as}\qquad \int_M d\alpha=\int_{\partial M}\alpha.$$

Green, Stokes, Gauss, Cauchyone theorem, many costumes
Use this as a unification slide rather than a formula dump. Green, vector-calculus Stokes, the divergence theorem, and Cauchy's theorem differ in the degree of the form and the geometric domain. Once the relevant form is chosen, each says the same thing: an interior derivative measurement equals the accumulated boundary measurement.
de Rham PeriodsChapter 37

Closed Need Not Mean Exact

Period Obstruction

$$d\omega=0,\qquad \int_\gamma\omega=2\pi n\neq 0.$$

Local Statement

Closed means the exterior derivative vanishes, so small contractible loops have no accumulated boundary defect.

Global Statement

A loop winding around missing geometry may not bound any surface in the domain, so Stokes has no interior region to apply to.

de Rham View

Cohomology records closed forms modulo exact forms; nonzero periods are the measurements that survive.

closed forms, exact forms, periodstopology detected by integration
Introduce the topological catch. A closed form has zero exterior derivative, so locally it looks like it should be a derivative of a potential. On a punctured domain, loops can wind around missing geometry. The vortex period detects that obstruction: the local derivative vanishes, but a global loop integral can remain nonzero.
