Geometric Proof of the Metric Curvature Formula

Curvature becomes visible as the circulation of a frame-rotation field around shrinking metric cells.

The Question

Why can Gaussian curvature be computed from the first fundamental form alone?

The Geometric Answer

The metric builds local rulers. Changing rulers make an orthonormal frame rotate. The circulation of that rotation field is holonomy, and holonomy per area is curvature.

Metric DataChapter 27

Metric Rulers Before Curvature

Orthogonal Metric

$$ds^2=A(u,v)^2\,du^2+B(u,v)^2\,dv^2$$

What A And B Mean

  • \(A\,du\): physical length of a small \(u\)-step.
  • \(B\,dv\): physical length of a small \(v\)-step.
  • \(AB\,du\,dv\): metric area of a coordinate cell.

Inspection Target

Curvature is not caused by coordinates changing. It is caused by the metric rulers changing in a way that cannot be globally flattened.

metric coefficients\(A=\sqrt{E},\;B=\sqrt{G}\)
Before showing the curvature formula, make the metric concrete. In orthogonal coordinates, A and B are not abstract coefficients; they are the lengths of the coordinate ruler marks. The grid image asks the audience to inspect how a unit step in the map can mean different physical lengths on the surface. Curvature will come from the way these rulers vary from point to point.
Control ExperimentChapter 27

Flat Plane: The Zero-Curvature Dry Run

Start With The Case That Must Vanish

On the Euclidean plane, carrying a vector around a rectangle produces no net rotation. The boundary contributions cancel pairwise.

Flat Holonomy

$$\oint_{\partial R}\omega_{12}=0,\qquad K=0$$

Why Keep This Slide

The curved proof keeps the same loop bookkeeping. The only new ingredient is that metric rulers now vary across the map.

dry runconstant rulers have zero circulation
The flat plane is the control experiment. If the same vector is carried around a rectangle in the Euclidean plane, the edge turns cancel and the vector returns with no net rotation. This zero case matters because the curved proof will keep the same bookkeeping but replace constant rulers by changing metric rulers.
Connection From RulersChapter 27

The Connection Field Lives in the Map

Build An Orthonormal Frame

Normalize the coordinate directions: \(e_u=X_u/A\) and \(e_v=X_v/B\). The metric alone tells us these unit rulers.

Connection One-Form

$$\omega_{12}=\frac{A_v}{B}\,du-\frac{B_u}{A}\,dv$$

Read The Coefficients

\(A_v/B\) measures how the \(u\)-ruler changes as one walks in the \(v\)-direction. \(B_u/A\) measures the matching cross-change of the \(v\)-ruler.

connection one-formframe rotation is already encoded by \(A,B\)
Now introduce the connection one-form as the map-level field that tells an orthonormal frame how to rotate. In orthogonal coordinates its two coefficients are built from cross-variation of the metric scale factors: how the u-ruler changes in the v-direction and how the v-ruler changes in the u-direction. This is the first place the proof becomes metric-only.
Boundary AccountingChapter 27

A Small Cell Converts Circulation to Holonomy

Geometric Claim

$$\Delta\theta_{\partial C}\approx\oint_{\partial C}\omega_{12}=\iint_C d\omega_{12}\approx K(p)\,AB\,\Delta u\,\Delta v$$

Boundary View

Walking around the cell adds four signed edge rotations. Opposite edges almost cancel, and their small failure to cancel is measured by derivatives.

Area View

The same number is the curvature two-form integrated over the metric area of the cell.

Stokes on a tiny cellboundary circulation becomes area density
The holonomy around a small coordinate cell is the circulation of the connection around its boundary. Walking the four sides is just adding signed edge contributions. Stokes' theorem converts this boundary sum into the exterior derivative of the connection over the cell, and for a tiny cell the result is curvature times metric area.
Limit ProcessChapter 27

Let the Cell Shrink

Curvature Is A Density

Holonomy is accumulated around a loop. Gaussian curvature is the infinitesimal holonomy per unit metric area.

Per-Area Limit

$$K(p)=\lim_{C\to p}\frac{\oint_{\partial C}\omega_{12}}{\operatorname{Area}(C)}$$

What Shrinking Removes

The limit strips away the loop's size and leaves only the local rotation defect attached to the point \(p\).

localizationholonomy divided by metric area
The shrinking-cell picture gives the limiting logic. Large loops include accumulated variation, but as the cell collapses to a point, circulation divided by metric area settles onto the local curvature. The notebook's diagnostic curve checks the same idea numerically: limit ratios approach the invariant value near zero.
Formula From The PictureChapter 27

The Metric Curvature Formula

For \(ds^2=A^2du^2+B^2dv^2\)
$$K=-\frac{1}{AB}\left[\frac{\partial}{\partial u}\left(\frac{B_u}{A}\right)+\frac{\partial}{\partial v}\left(\frac{A_v}{B}\right)\right]$$

Numerator

The two derivative terms measure how the boundary rotations fail to cancel across opposite sides of the small cell.

Denominator

\(AB\) converts coordinate area \(du\,dv\) into metric area. Curvature is the circulation density, not the raw circulation.

metric-only curvaturethe formula is a circulation density
Only after the circulation story is in place do we display the formula. The numerator is the divergence-like sum of two ruler-change rates, and the denominator converts coordinate area into metric area. The minus sign is an orientation convention tied to the chosen ordered frame. The important point is that no normal vector, embedding, or second fundamental form appears.
ChecksChapter 27

Two Sanity Checks

Changing coordinate rulers are not automatically curvature. The formula must distinguish coordinate effects from geometric rotation defects.

Plane In Polar Coordinates

$$ds^2=dr^2+r^2d\theta^2,\quad A=1,\quad B=r$$

Since \(\partial_r(B_r/A)=0\) and \(A_\theta=0\), the formula gives \(K=0\).

Unit Sphere On A Regular Patch

$$ds^2=d\phi^2+\cos^2\phi\,d\lambda^2,\quad A=1,\quad B=\cos\phi$$

Then \(\partial_\phi(B_\phi/A)=-\cos\phi\), so \(K=1\) away from the coordinate singularities.

SynthesisChapter 27

Proof Ledger And Takeaways

The Four-Line Proof Ledger

  • Metric scale factors \(A,B\) build an orthonormal frame.
  • Frame rotation is the connection one-form \(\omega_{12}\).
  • Loop holonomy is boundary circulation \(\oint\omega_{12}\).
  • Shrinking circulation per metric area is \(K\).

Why This Is Intrinsic

Every term in the final formula is read from \(ds^2\). A surface may bend through space, but Gaussian curvature has already been encoded in its internal ruler field.

Instructor Prompt

Ask students to choose another orthogonal metric, compute \(A,B\), and decide whether changing rulers create real curvature or cancel as a coordinate artifact.
