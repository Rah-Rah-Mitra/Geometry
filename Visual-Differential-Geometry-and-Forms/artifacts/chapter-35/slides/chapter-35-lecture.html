AlternationChapter 35

Alternation Locks The Dimension

Repeated Direction Test

$$\Omega(u,u,w)=-\Omega(u,u,w)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \Omega(u,u,w)=0.$$

Why \(R^2\) Has No Nonzero 3-Forms

Any ordered triple chosen from two basis directions repeats at least one direction, so every alternating component vanishes.

Common Trap

A three-slot formula can be written in two dimensions, but alternation makes its value identically zero.

alternating trilinearitynonzero 3-forms need dimension at least 3
Make the zero result feel inevitable. Alternation says a slot swap changes the sign. If the two slots contain the same vector, the input did not actually change, so the value must equal its own negative. That is why repeated directions collapse a 3-form measurement, and why a nonzero 3-form cannot live on a two-dimensional tangent space.
Volume FormChapter 35

The Coordinate Volume Form

Standard 3-Form On \(R^3\)

$$dx\wedge dy\wedge dz(u,v,w)=\det[u\ v\ w].$$

+1.107625slot order u, v, w
-1.107625slot order v, u, w

What The Determinant Remembers

The magnitude measures a parallelepiped. The sign records whether the ordered edge triple agrees with the chosen orientation.

Teaching Emphasis

Orientation is not decoration on volume; it is part of the scalar reported by the form.

\(dx\wedge dy\wedge dz\)determinant as volume measurement
Use the volume box as the main mental model. The coordinate form dx wedge dy wedge dz evaluates three input vectors by placing them as columns in a determinant. The absolute value is the parallelepiped volume and the sign records orientation. Swapping two columns keeps the same geometric box but reverses the orientation reading.
Wedge ProductChapter 35

The Wedge Product Is A Determinant Ledger

Three 1-Forms

$$\sigma_1\wedge\sigma_2\wedge\sigma_3(v_1,v_2,v_3)=\det\big(\sigma_i(v_j)\big).$$

Six-Term Expansion

Each permutation of the three vector slots contributes one product, weighted by its sign.

Notebook Check

$$\sum_{\pi\in S_3}\operatorname{sgn}(\pi)\,dx(v_{\pi 1})dy(v_{\pi 2})dz(v_{\pi 3})=1.107625.$$

Why \((2\text{-form})\wedge(1\text{-form})\) Fits

The two-form has already measured pairs of slots, so the remaining cyclic ledger has three terms instead of six.

determinant expansionwedge algebra as visible bookkeeping
This slide connects the algebraic expansion to the determinant picture. Three one-forms wedged together produce the signed six-term determinant. Building the same object as a two-form wedged with a one-form compresses the bookkeeping into three cyclic terms. The notebook checks that both ledgers give the same number for the same three edge vectors.

Calibration Rule

$$\Omega(Au,Av,Aw)=\det(A)\,\Omega(u,v,w).$$

Interpretation

These checks distinguish an oriented volume meter from an unsigned volume calculator.

Treat the 3-form as an instrument with calibration tests. A slot swap should flip the sign. A repeated slot should read zero. CoordinatesChapter 35

Pullback Creates The Integration Density

Spherical Pullback

$$\Phi^*(dx\wedge dy\wedge dz)=r^2\sin\phi\,dr\wedge d\phi\wedge d\theta.$$

Geometric Edge Box

  • radial edge length: \(dr\)
  • polar edge length: \(r\,d\phi\)
  • azimuthal edge length: \(r\sin\phi\,d\theta\)

Symbolic Check

The Jacobian determinant of \((r,\phi,\theta)\mapsto(x,y,z)\) simplifies exactly to \(r^2\sin\phi\).

Meaning For Integration

The coefficient is the local volume density that converts coordinate boxes into Euclidean volume boxes.

spherical coordinatespullback coefficient as volume density
Now make coordinate change visible. Pulling back dx wedge dy wedge dz through spherical coordinates means measuring the small coordinate box in ordinary Euclidean volume. The three local edge lengths are dr, r dphi, and r sin phi dtheta, so the volume density is r squared sin phi. The symbolic Jacobian and the geometric edge picture agree.
Basis FormsChapter 35

Basis 3-Forms Count Distinct Triples

Counting Rule

$$\dim \Lambda^3(\mathbb{R}^n)^*=\binom{n}{3}.$$

R2C(2, 3) = 0
R3C(3, 3) = 1
R4C(4, 3) = 4

Top Form In \(R^3\)

Every 3-form is a scalar multiple of \(dx_1\wedge dx_2\wedge dx_3\).

Four Slots In \(R^4\)

The independent basis forms omit one coordinate at a time, producing four possible oriented triples.

Memory Aid

The count is not arbitrary: a repeated label would make the alternating form zero.

basis ledger\(\Lambda^3\) as distinct oriented triples
A basis 3-form needs three distinct coordinate labels. In R2 there are no choices, in R3 there is the single top form, and in R4 there are four independent choices. This is the binomial coefficient C(n,3) appearing as a geometric availability statement rather than a memorized counting formula.
Graded CommutativityChapter 35

Graded Signs Explain Self-Wedges

Swap Rule

$$\alpha\wedge\beta=(-1)^{pq}\,\beta\wedge\alpha,\qquad \deg\alpha=p,\ \deg\beta=q.$$

Why The Exponent Is \(pq\)

Move each of the \(p\) one-form factors past each of the \(q\) one-form factors. That is \(pq\) slot swaps.

+12-form past 1-form
-13-form past 3-form
nonzeroeven self-wedge may survive

Self-Wedge Diagnostic

If \(p\) is odd, \(\alpha\wedge\alpha=-\alpha\wedge\alpha\), so the self-wedge is forced to vanish.

sign \((-1)^{pq}\)odd self-wedges vanish by alternation
Use the sign grid as a compact proof. Moving a p-form past a q-form requires p times q one-form swaps, so the sign is minus one to the p q. A 2-form commutes with a 1-form because the sign is positive. Odd-degree forms wedge with themselves to zero by the same sign logic, while an even 2-form can have a nonzero square.
