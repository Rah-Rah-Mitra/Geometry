Tensors Are Slot Machines For Measurement

A tensor is a multilinear device with typed input slots. Components are the readings after we choose basis probes.

slot contractwhich inputs the machine accepts
varianceupper and lower index discipline
componentsbasis-dependent response table
operationsproduct, contraction, symmetry
invariant readingsame measurement in any chart
Local model

$$T:(V^*)^r\times V^s\longrightarrow \mathbb{R}$$

A type \((r,s)\) tensor eats \(r\) covectors and \(s\) vectors.

Why multilinear?

Each slot is linear while the other slots are held fixed. The tensor records a measurement rule, not a pile of unrelated numbers.

Diagnostic question

Before writing indices, ask: what inputs go into the slots, and what measurement should survive a coordinate change?

printed pp. 360-369; PDF pp. 389-398Chapter question: how do slots organize measurement?
Open with the chapter question rather than a component definition. A tensor is a device that accepts a prescribed list of vector and covector inputs and returns a number, vector, covector, or another tensor after operations. The route is slot type, component ledger, tensor product, contraction, metric duality, symmetry, and coordinate independence. Emphasize that the slots are the object; coordinates are only labels for reading the same machine.

Valence Says What The Tensor Eats

Valence as an input contract

$$T(\alpha^1,\ldots,\alpha^r;\;v_1,\ldots,v_s)$$

Covectors and vectors occupy different kinds of slots. Mixing the slots changes the object.

1-formsone vector slot

Read the heatmap carefully

Rows and columns are basis choices. The tensor is the rule that would produce a compatible table after any allowed change of basis.

Lecture move

Ask students to identify the slot type before naming the components. This keeps the geometry ahead of the notation.

Variance Keeps The Slots Honest

Index position records slot type

$$T^{i_1\cdots i_r}{}_{j_1\cdots j_s}$$

Upper indices track covector-input slots. Lower indices track vector-input slots.

Covariant slot

\(\alpha(v)\) is a one-form measuring a vector direction. Its components carry lower indices.

Contravariant slot

A vector pairs with covectors. Its components carry upper indices.

Common mistake

An index is not a decoration. Moving it requires structure, usually a metric, and changes the type of slot being represented.

Slot reading

$$T^{i}{}_{jk}\quad\text{has one upper and two lower slots}$$

tensor-valence-slot-map.pngVariance prevents slot confusion
This slide slows down the upper-versus-lower index distinction. Lower indices correspond to vector inputs because one-forms measure directions. Upper indices correspond to covector inputs because vectors pair with covectors. The slot map is a bookkeeping discipline: when a coordinate basis changes, each slot transforms with the rule appropriate to what it accepts.

A Linear Map Is Already A Tensor

Mixed tensor model

$$A=A^{i}{}_{j}\,e_i\otimes \varepsilon^j$$

$$A(v)^i=A^{i}{}_{j}v^j$$

Two roles in the table

The lower slot reads the input vector. The upper slot records the output vector direction.

Basis dependence

The matrix entries change when the frame changes. The linear map, as an operation on vectors, remains the same.

Why this example matters

Students already know matrices. Tensors generalize the same idea to many slots and geometric input types.

linear-map-as-tensor-components.pngMatrix as type \((1,1)\) tensor
Connect tensors to familiar linear algebra. A matrix for a linear map has one output direction and one input covector measurement, so it is naturally a mixed tensor with one upper and one lower index. The table changes with the chosen basis, but the operation v maps to A v does not. Ask students to name the two slots before they read the formula.

Tensor Products Preserve Separate Slots

Product of measurements

$$(\alpha\otimes\beta)(u,v)=\alpha(u)\,\beta(v)$$

Before product

\(\alpha\) has one vector slot and \(\beta\) has one vector slot.

After product

\(\alpha\otimes\beta\) has two ordered vector slots.

No summing yet

The product expands the ledger. Contraction is a separate operation.

Component ledger

$$(S\otimes T)^{ab}{}_{ijk}=S^{a}{}_{ij}\,T^{b}{}_{k}$$

Pedagogical cue

Read the product left to right as slot concatenation. The visual grid is the bookkeeping shadow of that concatenation.

tensor-product-grid.pngTensor product makes a larger machine
Explain tensor product as slot concatenation, not as mysterious multiplication. If alpha measures one vector and beta measures another, alpha tensor beta measures an ordered pair by multiplying the two independent readings. The component grid visual is the outer-product ledger. The important point is that the product creates a larger measurement machine before any summation or symmetry is imposed.

Contraction Wires One Up Slot To One Down Slot

Trace as the model contraction

$$\operatorname{tr}(A)=A^{i}{}_{i}$$

General pattern

$$(CT)^{ik}{}_{l}=T^{ijk}{}_{jl}$$

A repeated upper-lower index means "pair and sum".

Why the answer is invariant

The paired slots transform in opposite ways, so the basis change cancels inside the summation.

Conceptual reading

Contraction turns a multilinear measurement into a simpler measurement by internally testing one matching pair of slots.

inline SVG contraction diagramProduct creates slots; contraction removes matched slots
Contraction is the first operation that intentionally removes slots. Feeding a matching vector-covector pair through a dual basis and summing is drawn as a wire between opposite variance slots. This is why trace is the model example. The summation is basis independent only because the paired slots transform oppositely.

The Metric Turns Vectors Into Covectors

Metric tensor

$$g(v,w)=g_{ij}v^i w^j,\qquad ds^2=g_{ij}dx^i dx^j$$

Musical maps

$$v^\flat=g(v,\cdot)=g_{ij}v^i dx^j$$

$$\alpha^\sharp=g^{ij}\alpha_j\,\partial_i$$

What changes

The components move between upper and lower positions because the metric supplies a translator between vectors and covectors.

What does not change

The geometric vector or covector being represented is not replaced by a table; the table is a coordinate reading of it.

metric-duality-raising-lowering.pngMetric duality raises and lowers indices
Make metric duality visual before using the sharp and flat notation. The metric tensor evaluates two vectors, but fixing one vector leaves a covector that measures every other vector by length and angle against it. This is the musical isomorphism: the metric moves information between vectors and one-forms, and its inverse moves it back.

Symmetry And Antisymmetry Are Slot Tests

Symmetric tensor

$$S(u,v)=S(v,u)$$

The measurement ignores the order of matching slots.

Antisymmetric tensor

$$\omega(u,v)=-\omega(v,u)$$

Swapping the directed inputs reverses the sign.

Forms connection

Differential forms are antisymmetric covariant tensors. Repeated directions force the measurement to vanish.

Area test

$$dx\wedge dy(u,v)=u^xv^y-u^yv^x$$

inline SVG symmetry diagramForms are antisymmetric covariant tensors
Present symmetry as a behavior under slot exchange. A symmetric tensor does not care if two matching inputs are swapped; an antisymmetric tensor flips sign. Differential forms are antisymmetric covariant tensors, so they vanish on repeated directions and measure oriented area, volume, and higher directed pieces of geometry.

Components Change; Measurements Do Not

Coordinate change law protects the reading

$$\widetilde{T}_{ab}=\frac{\partial x^i}{\partial \widetilde{x}^a}\frac{\partial x^j}{\partial \widetilde{x}^b}T_{ij}$$

Same scalar measurement

$$T(u,v)=T_{ij}u^i v^j=\widetilde{T}_{ab}\widetilde{u}^{a}\widetilde{v}^{b}$$

Interpretation

The table, basis vectors, and coordinate components can all change. The multilinear reading stays fixed.

Diagnostic habit

If a computed number changes under a pure coordinate relabeling, the slot transformation has been mishandled.

inline SVG coordinate-change diagramTensor laws preserve scalar readings
This is the coordinate-independence checkpoint. A coordinate change redraws basis vectors and therefore changes the component table. But when the transformed components are paired with transformed input components, the scalar reading is the same. The slide should make the transformation law feel like protection for the measurement, not like a separate formula to memorize.
