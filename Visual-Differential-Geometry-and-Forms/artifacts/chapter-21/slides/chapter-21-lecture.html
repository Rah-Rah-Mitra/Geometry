An Historical Puzzle

Why did a geometric account of relativity need parallel transport?

The puzzle

Einstein's 1915 theory already made gravity a statement about curvature. Levi-Civita's parallel transport appeared in 1917.

The missing verb

Curvature compares directions after motion. Before transport, the theory had tensors, but not a clean rule for carrying a direction between tangent spaces.

Lecture question

What must be added so curvature becomes an intrinsic comparison, not only a coordinate formula?

What Tensor Calculus Could Already Say

Coordinate-respectful statement

G_{\mu\nu} = 8\pi T_{\mu\nu}

At one point

A tensor equation is meaningful because both sides transform the same way when coordinates change.

The available tool

Ricci calculus could track components, contractions, and invariance without pretending that coordinates are geometry itself.

pp. 231-232Tensor Calculus
Explain tensor calculus as the available bookkeeping system: it keeps equations coordinate-respectful at a point and lets curvature appear as a tensorial object. But bookkeeping alone does not tell an arrow at p how to become an arrow at q without making an extra geometric choice.

The Missing Operation

Vectors based at different points live in different tangent spaces.

The false shortcut

Subtracting arrows drawn at two different points treats \(T_pM\) and \(T_qM\) as if they were already the same vector space.

No canonical comparison

v_q - v_p \quad \text{is undefined until a transport rule identifies the spaces.}

What a chart does

Coordinates label both tangent planes. They do not choose the geometrically preferred way to carry one direction into the other.

pp. 231-232The Missing Operation
Point to the two tangent planes as genuinely different vector spaces. A coordinate chart can label both, but labels do not create a canonical subtraction of vectors across points. This is the conceptual gap: curvature wants to record how directions fail to return, but first directions need a transport rule.

Curvature Wants A Loop Test

Why loops solve comparison

After a full loop the vector comes back to the original tangent space, so comparison is meaningful again.

Small-loop diagnostic

Levi-Civita's Rule

Parallel transport supplies the missing verb: carry a tangent vector along a curve without introducing extra twist.

Parallel along a curve

\nabla_{\dot\gamma}V = 0

Preserve

Length and angle are preserved locally by the metric connection.

Reveal

Historical role

Introduce parallel transport as the missing rule, not as a formula dropped from the sky. Along a curve, the vector is adjusted so its covariant derivative in the direction of travel vanishes. The rule preserves length and angle locally while allowing the surface itself to bend the final comparison.
