Moving FramesChapter 38

Coframes Replace Coordinate Grids

Frame And Coframe

Choose local vectors \(e_1,\ldots,e_n\) and dual 1-forms \(\theta^1,\ldots,\theta^n\):

$$\theta^i(e_j)=\delta^i_j,\qquad g=\sum_i \theta^i\otimes\theta^i.$$

Why This Helps

A coframe can be adapted to a surface, sphere, cylinder, or spacetime observer even when coordinates are awkward.

Attitude Matrix View

The columns of an attitude matrix store frame directions. Its differential records how the frame changes from point to point.

Diagnostic

Coordinates label points. Coframes measure tangent inputs. Confusing those roles hides where the metric enters.

Cartan moving framescoframes are local measurement systems
Emphasize that the coframe is not a decorative choice of notation. It is a local ruler system that may be better adapted to the geometry than coordinates. The duality theta i of e j equals delta i j says that each one-form extracts one frame component. Once the metric is written in the coframe, later structure equations can track geometry without constantly returning to coordinate partial derivatives.
Connection FormsChapter 38

Connection 1-Forms Measure Turning

Infinitesimal Frame Rotation

$$\nabla e_i=\sum_j \omega_i{}^j\,e_j,\qquad \omega_{ij}=-\omega_{ji}.$$

\(\theta^i\)measures components
\(\omega_i{}^j\)measures turning
skeworthonormal frames do not stretch

Surface Reading

On an oriented surface, one tangent connection form \(\omega^1{}_2\) records rotation inside the tangent plane, while normal forms record bending out of the plane.

Key Misconception

A connection form is not curvature yet. It is the local rule for comparing nearby frames; curvature appears when that rule fails to close around a loop.

connection 1-formsturning rates before curvature
This slide is the hinge between frames and curvature. A frame can move along a curve or across a surface, and the connection forms are the instruments that report the infinitesimal rotation of one frame vector into another. In an orthonormal frame the matrix is skew, so the diagonal does not measure stretching; the off-diagonal one-forms measure rotation between axes.
Structure EquationsChapter 38

Cartan's Structural Equations

First Structure Equation

$$T^i=d\theta^i+\sum_j\omega^i{}_j\wedge\theta^j.$$

For the Levi-Civita connection, \(T^i=0\).

Second Structure Equation

$$\Omega^i{}_j=d\omega^i{}_j+\sum_k\omega^i{}_k\wedge\omega^k{}_j.$$

What The Wedge Term Does

The term \(\omega\wedge\omega\) records two-step frame changes. It is the correction that makes curvature a geometric 2-form, not a raw derivative table.

Reading Rule

Feed the curvature form two tangent directions. The output is the infinitesimal holonomy they enclose.

Cartan I and IItorsion-free comparison, then curvature
Present the structural equations as a workflow rather than a memorized pair. The first equation says whether the chosen coframe closes up without torsion. The second equation takes the connection one-forms and differentiates them, then corrects by the connection wedged with itself. That correction term is what lets the formula remain geometric instead of merely coordinate-wise.

Surface Equations Expose Compatibility

Restrict The Ambient Frame

For a surface frame \(e_1,e_2,e_3\) with \(e_3\) normal, the surface condition is

$$\theta^3=0,\qquad d\theta^3=\theta^1\wedge\omega^3{}_1+\theta^2\wedge\omega^3{}_2=0.$$

Six Equations, Three Jobs

The surface form equations organize tangent compatibility, symmetry of the second fundamental form, and the Gauss-Codazzi conditions that make metric and bending data fit together.

Theorema Egregium In This Language

The coefficient \(K\) in the curvature form is determined by the metric coframe and its connection, so Gaussian curvature is intrinsic.

fundamental form equationsmetric, shape, Gauss-Codazzi compatibility
Here the abstract Cartan equations become the classical surface equations. Setting the normal coform to zero restricts the ambient moving frame to the surface. The six resulting equations separate three jobs: metric compatibility in the tangent coframe, symmetry of the shape operator, and the Gauss-Codazzi compatibility that allows a first and second fundamental form to come from an actual surface.
Curvature 2-FormsChapter 38

Curvature Forms Are Tensor Entries

Tensor Expansion

$$\Omega^i{}_j=\frac12 R^i{}_{jkl}\,\theta^k\wedge\theta^l.$$

Surface Specialization

In two dimensions there is one independent curvature form:

$$\Omega^1{}_2=K\,\theta^1\wedge\theta^2.$$

Second Bianchi Identity

$$D\Omega=d\Omega+\omega\wedge\Omega-\Omega\wedge\omega=0.$$

Inspection Target

The heatmap should be read as a component ledger: basis pair in, rotation plane out, scalar curvature component recorded.

Capstone Point

Exterior calculus does not erase tensors. It groups their antisymmetric slots into the natural measuring object: a 2-form.

Riemann tensor through formscurvature as holonomy density
Make the tensor map concrete. A curvature two-form is not an extra object added after the Riemann tensor; it is the exterior-calculus packaging of the same components. Expanding Omega i j against theta k wedge theta l reads the Riemann tensor entries. On a two-dimensional oriented surface, the single curvature form Omega one two is simply K times the area form.
Global CurvatureChapter 38

Gauss-Bonnet Is Curvature Accounting

Closed Surface

$$\int_M \Omega^1{}_2=\int_M K\,dA=2\pi\chi(M).$$

With Boundary And Corners

$$\int_M K\,dA+\int_{\partial M}k_g\,ds+\sum_i\alpha_i=2\pi\chi(M).$$

local\(\Omega^1{}_2\) reads curvature density
edgeboundary turning contributes
globalEuler characteristic remains

Sphere Check

For \(S^2\), \(K=1\), area \(4\pi\), and \(\chi(S^2)=2\). The identity reads \(4\pi=2\pi\cdot2\).

Gauss-Bonnet via formslocal curvature, global topology
Connect the chapter back to the visual meaning of integration. Curvature forms give local signed area measurements of bending. Gauss-Bonnet says that after adding boundary turning and corner angles, the normalized total equals the Euler characteristic. For a closed sphere the check is particularly clean: total curvature four pi equals two pi times Euler characteristic two.
Lorentz GeometryChapter 38

The Same Pipeline Reads Spacetime

Schwarzschild Coframe

A standard orthonormal coframe has the schematic form

$$\theta^0=\sqrt{1-\frac{2M}{r}}\,dt,\quad \theta^1=\left(1-\frac{2M}{r}\right)^{-1/2}dr,$$

$$\theta^2=r\,d\vartheta,\qquad \theta^3=r\sin\vartheta\,d\varphi.$$

Same Cartan Steps

  • Choose an adapted coframe for the metric.
  • Solve \(d\theta^i+\omega^i{}_j\wedge\theta^j=0\).
  • Compute \(\Omega^i{}_j=d\omega^i{}_j+\omega^i{}_k\wedge\omega^k{}_j\).

Meaning

The nonzero curvature forms describe tidal bending of spacetime; their components scale with the mass parameter and radial distance.

Why It Belongs Here

The chapter is no longer about surfaces only. Forms give a dimension-independent method for Riemannian and Lorentzian geometry.

Schwarzschild curvatureCartan forms in four-dimensional spacetime
Use Schwarzschild as the stress test for the method. Nothing in the Cartan workflow requires a surface in Euclidean three-space. Choose an orthonormal coframe for the Lorentz metric, solve the connection forms from the first structure equation, then compute curvature forms from the second. The plotted light cone and free-fall curve remind students that the forms still measure geometry, now with time in the frame.
