Regular Functions

Definition

Restrictions of polynomials to the variety, taken as functions to the base field.

When equal

Two polynomials agree as functions exactly when their difference vanishes on the variety.

Coordinate ring

$k[X]=k[x_1,\dots,x_n]/I(X)$

What it captures

This ring records every piece of polynomial data living on the variety.

Printed pp. 73-84 · PDF 71-82Regular Functions
Define regular functions on an affine variety as restrictions of polynomials. Two polynomials give the same function exactly when they differ by something vanishing on the variety, so the ring of regular functions is the coordinate ring, the polynomial ring modulo the ideal of the variety. This ring captures all polynomial data on the space.

Polynomial Maps

Morphisms

Maps whose every coordinate is given by a polynomial in the source variables.

Pullback

A morphism pulls regular functions back to regular functions.

Contravariance

$\varphi:X\to Y\ \rightsquigarrow\ \varphi^*:k[Y]\to k[X]$

Signature

The reversal of direction is the algebraic fingerprint of a geometric map.

Printed pp. 73-84 · PDF 71-82Polynomial Maps
Introduce morphisms of affine varieties: maps given in each coordinate by a polynomial. A morphism between varieties pulls regular functions back to regular functions, inducing a ring homomorphism between coordinate rings in the opposite direction. This contravariance is the algebraic signature of a genuinely geometric map.

The Coordinate Ring as Invariant

Determination

A morphism is the same data as a homomorphism of coordinate rings.

Isomorphism

Varieties are isomorphic exactly when their coordinate rings are isomorphic.

Complete invariant

Everything about an affine variety is encoded in this one algebraic object.

Correspondence

$\mathrm{Mor}(X,Y)\cong\mathrm{Hom}_k(k[Y],k[X])$

Printed pp. 73-84 · PDF 71-82The Coordinate Ring as Invariant
Show that the coordinate ring determines the variety. A morphism corresponds precisely to a homomorphism of coordinate rings, and an isomorphism of varieties matches an isomorphism of rings. Thus the coordinate ring is a complete invariant: everything about an affine variety is encoded in this single algebraic object.

The Equivalence of Categories

Statement

Affine varieties are equivalent, contravariantly, to finitely generated reduced algebras.

Reduced

No nonzero nilpotents, the algebraic echo of the Nullstellensatz radical.

Two sides, one theory

$\{\text{affine varieties}\}\ \simeq\ \{\text{f.g. reduced }k\text{-algebras}\}^{\mathrm{op}}$

Payoff

We move freely between geometric pictures and explicit computations.

Printed pp. 73-84 · PDF 71-82The Equivalence of Categories
State the punchline. The category of affine varieties is equivalent, contravariantly, to the category of finitely generated reduced algebras over the field. Geometry and algebra are not merely analogous here; they are the same theory viewed from two sides, which lets us move freely between pictures and computations.
