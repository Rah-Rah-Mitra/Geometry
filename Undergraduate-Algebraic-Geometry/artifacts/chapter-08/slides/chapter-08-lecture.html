Printed pp. 121-132 · PDF 119-130From Varieties to Schemes
Point toward schemes, the modern generalization of varieties due to Grothendieck. By allowing arbitrary commutative rings and nilpotent elements, schemes handle non-reduced structure, families, and arithmetic uniformly. The affine variety theory we developed is exactly the reduced, finite-type case over a field, the doorway into this far broader landscape.

Printed pp. 121-132 · PDF 119-130Sheaves and Cohomology
Preview sheaves and their cohomology, the tools that make local-to-global reasoning systematic. Cohomology groups measure obstructions, count sections, and power theorems like Riemann-Roch. They turn the bookkeeping of functions and forms on a variety into computable linear algebra and are indispensable in every part of modern algebraic geometry.

Reflect on the base field. Working over an algebraically closed field gave the cleanest statements, but the same equations over the rationals or finite fields carry deep arithmetic, from Diophantine problems to the Weil conjectures. Algebraic geometry over different fields is the meeting point of geometry, number theory, and arithmetic.
