11.1 Upper Half-Plane

The Metric Makes The Boundary Infinitely Far Away

Definition

For z = v + iw with w > 0, hyperbolic length is measured by

\(ds^2 = \frac{dv^2 + dw^2}{w^2}\).

  • At small height, the same Euclidean step is expensive.
  • The real axis is a boundary, not part of the space.
  • Geodesics meet the boundary at right angles.
Use the shading to make the metric tangible: darker near the boundary means higher cost per Euclidean unit. This explains why the boundary is infinitely far away even though it is visually close. Then connect the metric to the geodesic rule, where vertical rays and orthogonal semicircles are the hyperbolic straight lines.
Geodesic Recognition

Straight Means Boundary-Orthogonal

Vertical Case

Vertical rays are geodesics with ideal endpoint on the real axis and the other endpoint at infinity.

Semicircle Case

Euclidean circular arcs become hyperbolic lines exactly when the circle center lies on the boundary.

This slide gives students a quick diagnostic for drawings in the upper half-plane. It is tempting to call any smooth arc a line, but the boundary-orthogonality condition is the decisive feature. The two panels also prepare the disk model, where the same orthogonality rule remains true at the unit circle.
Model Change

The Cayley Map Moves The Same Geometry Into A Disk

Invariant

The distance computed in the half-plane agrees with the disk distance after the Cayley map.

\(d_H(a,b) = d_D(P(a),P(b))\)

The disk is conformal, so Euclidean tangent angles in the disk are genuine hyperbolic angles.

Present the Cayley map as a change of coordinates, not as a new geometry. The visible shape of a geodesic can change, but the computed distance and angle information are transported with it. This is the first major invariant habit for the chapter: when the picture moves, ask which measurement stayed fixed.
Distance Formulas

Distances Are Ratios To The Ideal Boundary

Upper Half-Plane

\(d_H(a,b)=2\,\operatorname{arctanh}\frac{|b-a|}{|b-\overline a|}\)

The denominator reflects the mirror point across the boundary.

Poincare Disk

\(d_D(a,b)=2\,\operatorname{arctanh}\frac{|b-a|}{|1-\overline a b|}\)

The denominator records how close the pair is to the unit circle.

Operational reading: hyperbolic distance increases when a Euclidean segment approaches the ideal boundary, even if the drawing looks short.

Do not rush the formulas as algebra. In both expressions, the numerator is ordinary Euclidean separation, while the denominator corrects for position relative to the boundary. This is why the same Euclidean-looking displacement can represent a small move near the center and a large move near the edge.
11.2-11.3 Isometries

Real Mobius Maps Move The Picture Without Changing Length

Isometry Criterion

If a,b,c,d are real and ad-bc > 0, then z -> (az+b)/(cz+d) maps H to itself and preserves hyperbolic distance.

Geodesic triangles bend and relocate in Euclidean coordinates, but their hyperbolic side lengths are unchanged.

Treat Mobius maps as the chapter's motion group. The before-and-after triangle is not meant to show congruence by Euclidean eye, because the Euclidean drawing visibly changes. The mathematical point is stronger: all pairwise hyperbolic distances survive the transformation, so this is the correct notion of rigid motion in the model.
Invariant Tracker

A Small Distance Table Can Certify A Motion

Disk Translation

\(T_t(z)=\frac{z+\tanh(t/2)}{\tanh(t/2)z+1}\)

For sample points z,w, the residual |d(T_tz,T_tw)-d(z,w)| stays at numerical roundoff.

  1. Choose a pair of points in the disk.
  2. Move both points by the same automorphism.
  3. Compare the two hyperbolic distances.
This slide connects the lecture to the notebook's applied lab. The point is that a finite table of residuals is not a proof by itself, but it is an excellent diagnostic for whether the formulas and implementation agree with the theorem. Encourage students to change the points and parameter, then watch the residual rather than the Euclidean picture.
Horocycles And Equidistant Curves

Euclidean Simplicity Does Not Mean Hyperbolic Straightness

Common Trap

Horizontal lines and boundary-tangent circles look elementary in Euclidean coordinates, but they fail the geodesic right-angle test.

Distance From The Imaginary Axis

\(\frac{|v|}{w}=\sinh R\)

Fixed-distance curves are Euclidean rays through the origin.

Slow down here because this is where Euclidean intuition can mislead students. Horocycles and equidistant curves are important geometric objects, but neither family should be mistaken for hyperbolic lines. The right-angle boundary test gives a fast visual way to sort geodesics from these other natural curves.
Parallel Behavior

Through One Point There Are Many Nonintersecting Lines

Limiting Parallel

It misses the line in the disk but meets it at the ideal boundary.

Ultra-Parallel

It misses the line and has two distinct ideal endpoints from it.

This slide marks the departure from Euclid's parallel postulate in a visually precise way. There is not just one line through the point avoiding the given line; there is a whole range. The limiting parallels are the boundary cases of that range, and they are the ones used to define the angle of parallelism.
11.4 Angle Of Parallelism

Distance To A Line Determines The Limiting Angle

Angle Formula

If d is the distance from a to l, the limiting angle satisfies

\(\sin \Pi = \operatorname{sech} d\).

As a moves farther from l, the limiting cone narrows.

Explain that the angle is measured in the Poincare disk because the disk is conformal. The formula says that parallel behavior is quantitative, not merely qualitative: distance from the line controls the opening angle. Students should notice the limiting parallels share ideal endpoints with the base line, which is why they form the boundary of the nonintersecting family.
11.1 And 11.3 Triangle Geometry

Hyperbolic Triangles Spend Angle As Area

Area Formula

\(\operatorname{Area}(\triangle)=\pi-(\alpha+\beta+\gamma)\)

  • Small triangles look almost Euclidean.
  • Larger triangles have visible angle defect.
  • There are no nontrivial similar-but-different-size triangles.
The conformal disk lets us trust the drawn tangent angles, but it does not let us trust Euclidean area. Hyperbolic area is encoded by the angle defect, so changing size changes the angle sum. This is a useful moment to contrast Euclidean similarity with hyperbolic rigidity.
Triangle Computation

The Cosine Rule Closes The Numeric Check

Hyperbolic Cosine Rule

\(\cosh a=\cosh b\cosh c-\sinh b\sinh c\cos\alpha\)

Geometric Meaning

A side length is controlled by the adjacent side lengths and the included angle, but with hyperbolic trigonometric functions.

Notebook Check

Sample geodesic arcs, measure conformal tangent angles, compute side distances, and verify the residual is near zero.

Use this slide to show how formulas, diagrams, and computation reinforce one another. The formula is a theorem of hyperbolic trigonometry, while the notebook turns it into a consistency check on measured angles and distances. When the residual closes, it supports the whole chain of model construction, tangent measurement, and distance formula use.
11.5 Beltrami-Klein Model

The Klein Disk Trades Angles For Straight Chords

Poincare Disk

Beltrami-Klein Disk

The Klein model is valuable because it makes incidence visually simple: geodesics are straight chords. The price is that Euclidean angles in the drawing are no longer hyperbolic angles. This tradeoff is a good theme for model-based geometry: no single picture makes every invariant equally easy.
Projective Distance

On A Klein Chord, Distance Is A Cross-Ratio

Klein Distance

\(d(a,b)=\frac12\left|\log(a,b;c,d)\right|\)

The formula is projective: Mobius invariance of cross-ratio is the algebra behind distance invariance.

Define the letters carefully: c and d are the ideal endpoints of the chord, while a and b are the interior points whose distance is being measured. The formula looks one-dimensional because the chord has straightened the geodesic. The important conceptual payoff is that hyperbolic distance can be recovered from a projective invariant.
Recap

What To Carry Forward

Boundary Controls Scale

The ideal boundary is not part of the space, but it controls both distance growth and geodesic shape.

Models Are Tools

The half-plane, Poincare disk, and Klein disk expose different invariants of the same geometry.

Isometries Are Mobius

Real Mobius maps preserve hyperbolic distance and carry geodesics to geodesics.

Defect Measures Area

Hyperbolic triangles have angle sum below pi, and the missing angle is area.

Parallels Are Quantitative

The angle of parallelism depends on distance, with sin Pi = sech d.

Projective Data Measures Length

In the Klein disk, straight chords and cross-ratios recover hyperbolic distance.

Close by returning to the invariant habit introduced at the start. Every model change was useful because it preserved the geometry while making a selected question easier. Students should leave with a practical rule: choose the model for the measurement you need, then use the invariant checks to keep the interpretation honest.
