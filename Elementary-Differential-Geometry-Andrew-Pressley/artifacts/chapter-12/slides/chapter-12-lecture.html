12.1 Plateau's Problem

Area Minimizers Must First Be Stationary

03 / 16
First Variation Test
Local minimality implies \(H=0\)

For every compactly supported normal variation that leaves the boundary fixed, the first derivative of area vanishes only when the mean curvature is zero.

\(\frac{dA}{dt}\bigg|_{t=0}=-2\int H\alpha\,dA\)

Explain that Plateau's problem motivates the variational viewpoint without requiring us to solve the global existence problem. The boundary is held fixed, so the only first-order freedom is the normal displacement inside the patch. Because the scalar function alpha is arbitrary in the interior, stationarity forces the local coefficient H to vanish.
Local Curvature Balance

Minimal Means The Average Normal Bending Cancels

04 / 16

Two principal curvatures, one stationarity scalar

\(H=\frac{1}{2}(k_1+k_2)\)

  • Positive and negative normal bending may cancel.
  • The condition is pointwise, not a global area comparison.
  • Umbilic points on a nonplanar minimal surface are severely constrained.
Common Mistake

Do not read \(H=0\) as \(K=0\). Usually \(K=k_1k_2\leq 0\), with strict negativity away from flat points.

This slide is where the geometric intuition should become precise. A saddle-like picture is useful because it makes cancellation of normal curvature visible, but remind students that the actual statement is coordinate independent. The warning matters later: the Gauss curvature of a nonflat minimal surface is typically negative, even though mean curvature is zero.

Three Surfaces Set The Baseline

Plane

All normal curvatures vanish, so \(H=K=0\). It is the flat reference case.

Catenoid

A surface of revolution whose neck balances inward and outward bending.

Helicoid

A ruled surface generated by a line rotating while translating along an axis.

Associate Family

Catenoid And Helicoid Share The Same Minimality Check

06 / 16
Exact Identities

\(E=G=\cosh^2 u,\quad F=0,\quad H=0,\quad K=-\operatorname{sech}^4 u.\)

  • The parameter \(u\) measures distance from the neck or axis in conformal coordinates.
  • Gaussian curvature is nonpositive and concentrates where the surface bends most.
  • The associate family rotates the complex differential data, not the surface as a rigid body.
Students often see the catenoid and helicoid as unrelated pictures, so focus on the invariants rather than the appearance. The symbolic identities say that both surfaces are conformally parametrized and minimal with the same curvature expression. This is the moment to hint that complex multiplication will later organize the entire family.

12.3 Gauss Maps

The Normal Field Turns Minimality Into Sphere Geometry

08 / 16
Gauss Map Fact

Where \(K\neq 0\), the normal map is locally regular. On a minimal surface, the Gauss map is conformal and its scale is controlled by \(-K\).

  • Large negative curvature means normals spread quickly on the sphere.
  • The catenoid and helicoid share a normal image but cover it differently.
  • Flat points are exactly where this diagnostic degenerates.
Present the Gauss map as a measuring device for how the surface bends. The normal image on the sphere is not merely a picture of directions; its local scaling carries curvature information. For minimal surfaces, the conformality of the Gauss map is one of the bridges between differential geometry and complex analysis.
12.4 Coordinates

Conformal Parameters Remove The Metric Cross-Talk

09 / 16
Conformal Patch

\(E=G=\lambda^2,\quad F=0,\quad ds^2=\lambda^2(du^2+dv^2).\)

Minimality Simplifies

In conformal coordinates, \(H=0\) is equivalent to \(\sigma_{uu}+\sigma_{vv}=0\).

The coordinate functions of a conformal minimal immersion are harmonic.

The key move is that conformal coordinates keep angles while allowing lengths to be scaled by a common factor. This removes the mixed term in the first fundamental form and makes the Laplacian appear naturally. Stress that the statement is local, but local is exactly where mean curvature is tested.
Harmonicity Check

Minimality Becomes Three Scalar Laplace Equations

10 / 16

Vector equation

\(\sigma_{uu}+\sigma_{vv}=0\)

Read componentwise, this says each coordinate function of \(\sigma=(x,y,z)\) is harmonic in the conformal parameters.

Notebook Check

For both catenoid and helicoid, the symbolic residual is the zero vector.

This is the analytic payoff of conformal coordinates. Instead of computing the full mean curvature expression every time, one can test whether the coordinate functions are harmonic. That reframing is what makes complex function theory available in the next part of the chapter.
Complex Notation

The Isotropic Differential Encodes Conformality

11 / 16
Holomorphic Vector

\(\phi=\sigma_u-i\sigma_v\)

If \(\sigma\) is conformal and minimal, then \(\phi\) is holomorphic and satisfies \(\phi_1^2+\phi_2^2+\phi_3^2=0\).

  • Holomorphicity comes from harmonic coordinate functions.
  • The quadratic identity comes from \(E=G\) and \(F=0\).
  • Taking real parts recovers the immersion after integration.
This slide should feel like a compression of the previous two slides. The real differential information in the surface is packaged into a complex vector phi. The isotropy identity is the algebraic shadow of conformality, and holomorphicity is the analytic shadow of minimality.
12.5 Weierstrass Representation

Holomorphic Data Generates Minimal Surfaces

12 / 16
Representation

\(\phi=\left(\frac{1}{2}f(1-g^2),\,\frac{i}{2}f(1+g^2),\,fg\right)\)

With suitable holomorphic data, \(\sigma=\Re\int \phi\,dz\) is a conformal minimal immersion.

The notebook's symbolic check verifies \(\phi_1^2+\phi_2^2+\phi_3^2=0\).

Introduce the Weierstrass representation as a construction machine, not just a theorem statement. The pair of holomorphic inputs produces a complex vector differential that automatically solves the conformality constraint. Integration and taking real parts then return to a surface in three-dimensional Euclidean space.

Checks And Pitfalls

Three Places Where Minimal-Surface Reasoning Breaks

15 / 16

Global confusion

\(H=0\) is stationarity, not automatic global area minimality.

Always ask which competitors and boundary conditions are allowed.

Coordinate confusion

The harmonic equation \(\sigma_{uu}+\sigma_{vv}=0\) needs conformal coordinates.

Outside conformal coordinates, use the full mean-curvature expression.

Regularity confusion

Weierstrass data must produce an immersion, not only a formal integral.

Check the metric factor and avoid points where the differential collapses.

This slide collects the failure modes that usually cause errors in exercises. The first is conceptual, the second is computational, and the third is about construction. Encourage students to attach a diagnostic to each: compare competitors, verify conformality, and check regularity of the Weierstrass differential.
