Configurations of Marked Points

The quotient

$n$ labelled distinct points on $\mathbb{CP}^1$, divided by the Mobius group.

Dimension

$\dim_{\mathbb{C}} \mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb{C}) = 3$, so three points can be fixed.

Open moduli space

$M_{0,n} = \{(z_1,\dots,z_n) : z_i \neq z_j\}/\mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb{C})$

Dimension count

$\dim_{\mathbb{C}} M_{0,n} = n - 3$

Base cases

$M_{0,3}$ is a point; $M_{0,4}$ has one coordinate.

Printed pp. 619-652 · PDF pp. 634-667Configurations of Marked Points
The open moduli space records n distinct labelled points on the Riemann sphere modulo the Mobius group. Because PSL(2,C) has complex dimension three and acts simply, fixing three points kills all freedom, so the quotient has complex dimension n minus three throughout genus zero.

The Cross-Ratio Coordinate

Normalize three

Send $z_1,z_2,z_3$ to $0,1,\infty$; the fourth mark becomes the coordinate.

Invariance

The cross-ratio is unchanged by every Mobius map, checked exactly.

Cross-ratio

$\lambda = \dfrac{(z_1-z_3)(z_2-z_4)}{(z_1-z_4)(z_2-z_3)}$

Printed pp. 619-652 · PDF pp. 634-667The Cross-Ratio Coordinate
Four labelled points carry exactly one essential invariant after the Mobius action. Normalizing three of them to zero, one, and infinity leaves the fourth as the cross-ratio, an explicit coordinate that is invariant under every fractional linear transformation, as the notebook verifies symbolically using an exact rational identity.

Stable Curves as Trees of Spheres

Tree of $\mathbb{CP}^1$'s

Components are spheres; nodes glue them into a connected tree.

Stability

Every component needs $\geq 3$ special points (marks + nodes).

Stability condition

$\#\{\text{marks}\} + \#\{\text{nodes}\} \;\geq\; 3 \quad \text{on each component}$

Printed pp. 619-652 · PDF pp. 634-667Stable Curves as Trees of Spheres
A degenerate domain is a tree of projective lines joined at nodes, carrying the marked points on its leaves. Stability demands every component hold at least three special points, marks plus nodes together. That valence condition eliminates continuous automorphisms and makes the moduli space a manifold rather than a stack.

The Grothendieck-Knudsen Boundary

Smooth manifold

$\overline{M}_{0,n}$ is compact, complex, of dimension $n-3$.

Boundary divisors

Codimension one strata indexed by splits $I\,|\,J$, each side $\geq 2$.

Boundary divisor

$D_{I|J}:\quad I \sqcup J = \{1,\dots,n\},\ \; |I|\geq 2,\ |J|\geq 2$

Stratification

Deeper strata = more nodes = trees with more vertices.

For $n=5$

The boundary divisors are the $2\,|\,3$ label splittings.

The full compactification is a smooth compact complex manifold of dimension n minus three, stratified by stable trees. Codimension-one boundary divisors correspond to partitions of the labels into two blocks, each of size at least two, indexing the single-node degenerations that bound the interior configurations.

Forgetful Map and Universal Curve

Forget a point

Drop the last mark, then stabilize any unstable component.

Universal curve

Its fibers are the curves themselves over $\overline{M}_{0,n}$.

Forgetful map

$\pi:\ \overline{M}_{0,n+1} \longrightarrow \overline{M}_{0,n}$

Printed pp. 619-652 · PDF pp. 634-667The Forgetful Map and Universal Curve
Dropping the last marked point defines a forgetful map from the n-plus-one space onto the n-point space. Its fibers are the curves themselves, so this map is the universal curve. Stabilization contracts any component left unstable after deletion, giving the recursive tower linking all the spaces.

Psi Classes and Dimension Counting

Psi class

$\psi_i$ is the first Chern class of the $i$-th cotangent line.

Dimension rule

Nonzero only when exponents sum to $n-3$.

Genus-zero intersection

$\displaystyle\int_{\overline{M}_{0,n}} \psi_1^{a_1}\cdots\psi_n^{a_n} = \binom{n-3}{a_1,\dots,a_n},\quad \textstyle\sum a_i = n-3$

Printed pp. 619-652 · PDF pp. 634-667Psi Classes and Dimension Counting
Each marked point carries a psi class, the first Chern class of its cotangent line. In genus zero a product of psi powers is nonzero only when the exponents sum to the dimension n minus three, where it equals the multinomial coefficient. The notebook tabulates these exact intersection numbers.
