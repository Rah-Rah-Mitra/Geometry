The Bracket Skein Rule

Two smoothings

Each crossing splits into an A-smoothing and a B-smoothing.

Weights

The two channels carry coefficients $A$ and $A^{-1}$.

Skein

$\langle L\rangle = A\,\langle L_0\rangle + A^{-1}\langle L_\infty\rangle$

States

Repeating it yields a sum over all loop diagrams.

Printed pp. 39-46The Bracket Skein Rule
State the defining rule: at each crossing the bracket splits into two smoothings, an A-smoothing and a B-smoothing, weighted by the variable A and its inverse. This local relation, applied at every crossing, expands any diagram into a sum over all states, each a disjoint union of simple loops. The rule is purely combinatorial.

Loops and Normalization

Single loop

The bracket of one bare circle is set to one.

Extra loops

Each additional disjoint circle multiplies by $\delta$.

Loop value

$\delta = -A^{2}-A^{-2}$

Result

Every diagram becomes a Laurent polynomial in $A$.

Printed pp. 39-46Loops and Normalization
Fix the value of a single loop to one and decree that an extra disjoint loop multiplies the bracket by minus A squared minus A to the minus two. Together with the skein rule these conventions determine the bracket of any diagram as a Laurent polynomial in A obtained by summing over every state of the diagram.

Regular Isotopy

Moves II and III

The bracket is unchanged by these two Reidemeister moves.

Move I

A curl multiplies the bracket by $-A^{\pm 3}$.

Consequence

It is a regular isotopy invariant, not a true knot invariant.

The fix

Track the writhe and cancel the factor next lecture.

Printed pp. 39-46Regular Isotopy
Show that the bracket is invariant under the second and third Reidemeister moves but changes by a factor under the first. Hence it is a regular isotopy invariant rather than a true knot invariant. The first move introduces a curl whose writhe must be tracked and later cancelled to repair the defect.

Worked Example

The trefoil

Three crossings expand into eight states.

Collect

Group states by their power of $A$ and the loop factor $\delta$.

Outcome

$\langle 3_1\rangle = -A^{5}-A^{-3}+A^{-7}$

Lesson

Mechanical, finite, and nontrivial for any diagram.

Printed pp. 39-46Worked Example
Compute the bracket of the trefoil by resolving its three crossings into eight states and collecting powers of A. The resulting Laurent polynomial is nontrivial, distinguishing the trefoil diagram from the unknot. The calculation illustrates how the state sum grows but remains entirely mechanical and finite for any diagram.
