From Bracket to Jones

Writhe correction

Multiply by $(-A^{3})^{-w}$ to cancel the move-one factor.

Now invariant

Stable under all three Reidemeister moves.

Substitution

$V(t)=\big((-A^{3})^{-w}\langle L\rangle\big)\big|_{A=t^{-1/4}}$

Result

An ambient isotopy invariant in $t$.

Printed pp. 47-56From Bracket to Jones
Repair the bracket by multiplying by minus A cubed raised to the negative writhe, producing the normalized bracket that is invariant under all three Reidemeister moves. Substituting A to the minus four for the variable t turns this into the Jones polynomial, a genuine ambient isotopy invariant of oriented knots and links.

The Jones Skein Relation

Oriented relation

Links the positive, negative, and smoothed crossings.

Normalization

The unknot has Jones polynomial one.

Skein

$t^{-1}V_{+}-t\,V_{-}=(t^{1/2}-t^{-1/2})V_{0}$

Recursion

Determines $V$ for every diagram, like Conway.

Printed pp. 47-56The Jones Skein Relation
Present the oriented skein relation satisfied by the Jones polynomial, linking the positive crossing, the negative crossing, and the oriented smoothing through a linear equation in t and its square root. Together with the value one on the unknot this relation alone determines the invariant recursively, paralleling the Conway construction.

Printed pp. 47-56Computing Examples
Compute the Jones polynomial of the trefoil and the Hopf link from the skein relation. The two trefoils of opposite handedness give mirror-image polynomials, so Jones detects chirality where the Conway polynomial cannot. This sensitivity to mirror images is one of the invariant's most celebrated and immediately useful features.

Power and Mystery

Strengths

Separates many knots and detects handedness.

Open question

Does it detect the unknot? Still unknown.

Hidden depth

Meaning revealed through statistical mechanics and physics.

Legacy

Launched the era of quantum invariants.

Printed pp. 47-56Power and Mystery
Discuss what Jones can and cannot do. It distinguishes vast numbers of knots and detects chirality, yet it is unknown whether it detects the unknot, and its deeper meaning emerged only later through statistical mechanics, operator algebras, and quantum field theory. Its discovery opened the entire field of quantum invariants.
