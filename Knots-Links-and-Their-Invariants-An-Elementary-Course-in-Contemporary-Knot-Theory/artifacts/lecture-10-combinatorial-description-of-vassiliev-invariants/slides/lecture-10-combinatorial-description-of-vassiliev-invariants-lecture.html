The Algebra of Diagrams

Vector space

Chord diagrams of each degree span a graded space.

Relation

Quotient by the four-term relation.

Product

Connected sum along the circle multiplies diagrams.

Duality

The dual space is exactly the weight systems.

Printed pp. 87-94The Algebra of Diagrams
Form a vector space spanned by chord diagrams of each degree and impose the four-term relation. Connected sum of diagrams along the circle makes this a graded algebra. Its dual is the space of weight systems, so understanding the algebra of diagrams is equivalent to understanding all top-order Vassiliev symbols at once.

From Chords to Trivalent Graphs

Closed diagrams

Allow internal trivalent vertices among the chords.

STU relation

The four-term relation becomes a local vertex rule.

Relations

$\mathrm{STU},\quad \mathrm{IHX},\quad \text{antisymmetry}$

Flexibility

The graph algebra reveals hidden structure.

Printed pp. 87-94From Chords to Trivalent Graphs
Enlarge chord diagrams to allow internal trivalent vertices, giving the larger space of closed diagrams. The four-term relation becomes the STU relation, and antisymmetry and the Jacobi-like IHX relation govern the internal vertices. This graph algebra is more flexible and reveals hidden structure invisible at the chord level.

The Hopf Algebra Structure

Coproduct

Split a diagram into pairs of subdiagrams.

Connected, graded

Product and coproduct make a Hopf algebra.

Structure theorem

Freely generated by primitive elements.

Payoff

Far less data describes all orders.

Printed pp. 87-94The Hopf Algebra Structure
Equip the diagram algebra with a coproduct that splits a diagram into pairs of subdiagrams. With the connected-sum product this makes a graded connected Hopf algebra. By a structure theorem it is freely generated by its primitive elements, drastically reducing the data needed to describe invariants of every order.

Lie Algebra Weight Systems

Recipe

Structure constants at vertices, the form on chords, then contract.

Consistency

IHX reflects the Jacobi identity.

Ingredients

$(\mathfrak{g},\langle\,,\,\rangle),\quad f^{abc},\ \delta^{ab}$

Outcome

Recovers coefficients of quantum invariants.

Printed pp. 87-94Lie Algebra Weight Systems
Construct concrete weight systems from a Lie algebra with an invariant bilinear form: assign structure constants to trivalent vertices and the form to chords, then contract. The IHX relation reflects the Jacobi identity, so the construction is consistent. These weight systems recover the coefficients of the classical quantum invariants.
