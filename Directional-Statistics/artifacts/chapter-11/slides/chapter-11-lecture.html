Dependence Is A Geometry Choice

1. Identify spaces linear, circular, spherical, or time-indexed directional observations
2. Embed carefully use sine-cosine columns, torus coordinates, or unit-vector blocks
3. Fit invariantly estimate offsets, covariance blocks, or rotations without privileging a chart
4. Compare nulls shuffle pairings or time order while keeping marginal directional samples
5. Inspect residuals read residuals as wrapped angles, tangent arrows, or lag increments
Lecture contract

A dependence statistic is acceptable only after its nuisance transformations and residual geometry are explicit.

Use this route slide as the organizing principle for the lecture. The same word, dependence, hides several different tasks: fit a linear height over a circle, compare two angles on a torus, rotate one sphere to another, or detect persistence in an ordered directional sequence.

Linear-Circular Dependence Lives On A Cylinder

Regression form
$$x_i=\beta_0+\beta_1\cos\theta_i+\beta_2\sin\theta_i+\epsilon_i.$$
Notebook check

The synthetic sample has \(R^2=0.9277\), while its permutation-null 95% cutoff is \(0.0755\).

Interpretation

The fitted sine wave is a ridge on a cylinder, not a straight-line slope in an arbitrary angle chart.

Pause on the cylinder before introducing the formula. The sine and cosine columns are not a trick for making angles linear; they are the coordinate representation of a circular predictor, and the fitted curve should be read as height varying around a closed loop.

Two Angles Form A Torus

Positive rotation model
$$\phi_i \approx \theta_i+\alpha \pmod{2\pi}.$$
Negative rotation model
$$\phi_i \approx -\theta_i+\alpha \pmod{2\pi}.$$
Why the torus matters

A diagonal band can wrap across both angle cuts; flattening the plot makes the strongest relation look split into pieces.

Introduce circular-circular association as a problem on the product space, not as a scatterplot with two troublesome axes. The torus picture explains why positive and negative rotational dependence are distinct alternatives and why residuals must be wrapped after fitting an offset.

Spherical-Spherical Dependence Uses Vector Blocks

Embedded correlation
$$T=\operatorname{tr}\left(S_{xx}^{-1}S_{xy}S_{yy}^{-1}S_{yx}\right).$$
Frame invariance

Separate rotations of the \(x\)-frame and \(y\)-frame should leave the scalar dependence unchanged.

Notebook evidence

The example has vector-correlation value \(2.9431\), compared with a permutation-null 95% cutoff of \(0.1560\).

Do not let the trace formula feel like a black box. The covariance blocks summarize how centered coordinates in one sphere move with centered coordinates in the other, while the inverse blocks normalize the marginal scatter so the final scalar is not tied to a chosen coordinate frame.

Regression On \(S^2\): Fit A Rotation, Then Tangent Residuals

Kabsch step
$$\hat R=\arg\min_{R\in SO(3)}\sum_i \|y_i-Rx_i\|^2.$$
Residual lives in a tangent plane
$$r_i=y_i-(y_i\cdot \hat y_i)\hat y_i,\qquad r_i\cdot \hat y_i=0.$$

Frame regression as a constrained prediction problem. If the fitted value is supposed to be a direction, the model should not predict a point inside Euclidean space; a rotation keeps predictions on the sphere, and subtracting the radial component makes the residual tangent.

Bivariate Directional Models: Match The Question To The Space

Line-circle

Ask whether a real response varies sinusoidally around a circle. Residuals are ordinary in the response coordinate, but predictors are circular.

\(x\mid\theta\)

Circle-circle

Ask whether one angle tracks another after an offset or sign choice. Residuals are wrapped angular errors on the torus.

\(\phi\mid\theta\)

Sphere-sphere

Ask whether paired unit vectors share a rotation-like relation. Residuals are angular or tangent quantities near fitted directions.

\(y\mid x,\ x,y\in S^2\)

Directional series

Ask whether order creates persistence beyond the marginal angular distribution. Residuals are lag increments rather than raw differences.

\(\theta_{t+1}-\theta_t\)
Modeling warning

A strong dependence statistic does not identify a full probability model by itself; it tells which geometric feature deserves a model.

This is the conceptual bridge from correlation coefficients to modeling. The chapter includes bivariate model families, but the useful habit is to ask what the conditional object is, where the residual lives, and which transformations leave the scientific conclusion unchanged.

The time-series example is a warning against unwrapping by habit. A persistent angular process should be detected through adjacent wrapped increments, and the null comparison should break ordering while preserving the observed sample of directions.

Null Models Should Respect The Marginal Geometry

Pairing null

Randomly permute one member of each pair, leaving the circular or spherical margins intact.

Ordering null

Randomly permute the observed time order, leaving the marginal angle distribution intact.

Why not replace with linear noise?

Fresh Euclidean noise changes the sample space and can make the null easier or harder for the wrong reason.

Emphasize that permutation is not just a convenient computation. In this chapter it is the way the analysis protects the circular or spherical margins while breaking only the relationship under study, giving a null distribution that answers the same geometric question as the statistic.

Calibration Lab: Circular Noise Weakens Dependence

Teaching prompt

Use the calibration lab to connect visual tightness with numerical dependence. As concentration increases, the torus band becomes narrower, the positive-rotation statistic increases, and the wrapped residual shrinks, so students see the statistic as a measurement of geometry rather than a magic number.

Residual Type Is The Main Diagnostic Choice

Cylinder

Inspect vertical residuals from a sinusoidal ridge. Curvature in residuals suggests missing harmonics or non-sinusoidal response.

Torus

Inspect wrapped angular residuals. Concentration around zero supports a fitted offset; antipodal or multimodal residuals call for richer models.

Sphere

Inspect tangent residual vectors near fitted directions. Radial residuals would indicate the regression left the sample space.

Series

Inspect wrapped increments over lag. Apparent jumps at the cut point should disappear if the statistic is coordinate faithful.

Diagnostic invariant

The residual plot should change its drawing coordinates under relabeling, but the conclusion about dependence should remain stable.

This slide turns the chapter into a reusable diagnostic checklist. Instead of asking whether a residual plot looks small in a flat chart, ask whether the residual was computed in the correct geometric space and whether its interpretation survives relabeling or rotation.

Recap: Dependence Means Preserved Structure

Takeaways
  • Line-circle regression uses a cylinder and sine-cosine predictors.
  • Circle-circle association lives on a torus with wrapped residuals.
  • Sphere-sphere regression should preserve unit length through rotations.
  • Directional time-series tests must respect the angle cut and marginal sample.
