Circular Distribution Functions

Circular Distribution Functions Need A Cut

\(F(x+2\pi)=F(x)+1\)
Definition In Practice

Choose an origin and orientation, then let \(F(x)\) record accumulated probability on the unwrapped line.

Coordinate Warning

The zero direction creates a discontinuity in the plot, not a special probability event on the circle.

Primitive object: arc probabilityCoordinate object: periodic lift of the CDF
Emphasize that the cut is useful but not intrinsic. Once a zero direction and orientation are chosen, the unwrapped distribution function increases by one after a full turn. The probability object is the arc mass; the plotted function is a coordinate device for computing it.

Trigonometric Moments

Characteristic Functions Become Sequences

\(\phi_p=E(e^{ip\Theta}),\qquad p\in\mathbb Z\)
Moment Decomposition

\(\phi_p=\alpha_p+i\beta_p\), where \(\alpha_p=E(\cos p\Theta)\) and \(\beta_p=E(\sin p\Theta)\).

Why Not Real Frequencies?

Only integer frequencies respect the identification \(\theta\sim\theta+2\pi\).

The ordinary characteristic function becomes a two-sided sequence indexed by harmonic number.

Moment languageFourier language for probability on the circle
Explain why the allowed frequencies are integers. The angle theta and theta plus 2pi represent the same point, so exp(i p theta) is single valued on the circle only when p is an integer. The real and imaginary parts are the cosine and sine trigonometric moments.
From Moments Back To Shape

Fourier Reconstruction Reads The Density Back

\(f_K(\theta)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\sum_{p=-K}^{K}\phi_p e^{-ip\theta}\)
Interpretation

Small \(p\) controls broad directionality; large \(p\) sharpens local peaks and troughs.

Teaching Target

Students should inspect whether a few harmonics preserve the feature that matters for the analysis.

Fourier partial sumsMoments are coordinates for density shape
Connect the characteristic sequence to an inspectable density. Low harmonics recover broad features such as mean direction and axiality; higher harmonics sharpen local peaks. The point is not to memorize a formula but to see moments as coordinates for circular shape.
Reference Model

Uniform Is The Reference Law

\(f(\theta)=\frac{1}{2\pi},\qquad \phi_p=0\ \text{for}\ p\ne 0\)
Arc Rule

For an arc of length \(\ell\), \(P(\text{arc})=\ell/(2\pi)\).

Statistical Role

Uniformity is the no-direction baseline: no preferred mean direction, no axiality, and no harmonic signal.

Baseline modelAll nonzero harmonic amplitudes vanish
Use uniformity as the zero signal in the Fourier picture. The density is constant, every nonzero integer coefficient vanishes, and arc probability is exactly proportional to arc length. This gives the baseline for later tests of concentration or multimodality.
Concentrated Unimodal Family

Von Mises: Exponential Tilt Around A Direction

\(f(\theta\mid\mu,\kappa)=\frac{\exp\{\kappa\cos(\theta-\mu)\}}{2\pi I_0(\kappa)}\)
Parameters

\(\mu\) sets the modal direction; \(\kappa\ge 0\) sets concentration.

Moment Fit

The mean resultant is \(A(\kappa)=I_1(\kappa)/I_0(\kappa)\), so \(\kappa\) can be matched to observed \(R\).

Von Mises familyExponential tilt of the uniform circle
Present von Mises as the likelihood-friendly circular analogue of a normal peak. The parameter mu selects the modal direction and kappa controls concentration. The notebook matches a target resultant length rho by solving the Bessel ratio A(kappa) equals I1(kappa) over I0(kappa).
Near-Uniform Model

Cardioid: A First Harmonic Perturbation

\(f(\theta)=\frac{1+2\rho\cos(\theta-\mu)}{2\pi},\qquad 0\le\rho\le\frac12\)
Fourier Signature

Only the first harmonic is active: \(|\phi_1|=\rho\) and \(|\phi_p|=0\) for \(|p|\ge 2\).

Use Case

Best for mild departure from uniformity; it is not a flexible multimodal family.

Cardioid modelUniform plus one cosine wave
The cardioid family is useful because it is transparent in the Fourier basis. It adds only a first harmonic to the uniform law, so it can model mild unimodal departure from uniformity without introducing a long tail of coefficients.
Modulo-One-Turn Construction

Wrapped Families Fold A Line Onto A Circle

Wrapped Density

\(f_W(\theta)=\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z} g(\theta+2\pi k)\)

Coefficient Fingerprints

Wrapped normal: \(\phi_p=\rho^{p^2}e^{ip\mu}\). Wrapped Cauchy: \(\phi_p=\rho^{|p|}e^{ip\mu}\).

Interpretation

Wrapping is appropriate when an originally linear noise variable is observed only modulo one turn.

Wrapped modelsLinear variable reduced modulo \(2\pi\)
A wrapped model begins with a real-valued variable and reduces it modulo one revolution. The density is a sum over all integer shifts, while the characteristic coefficients have simple damping patterns. Wrapped normal coefficients decay like rho to p squared; wrapped Cauchy coefficients decay like rho to the absolute value of p.
Vector-To-Angle Construction

Projection Models Discard Radius

Generator

Sample \(X\in\mathbb R^2\), then retain only \(\Theta=\operatorname{atan2}(X_2,X_1)\).

Geometry

Radial distance is discarded, so anisotropic covariance can create angular shape even with a weak location vector.

Contrast With Wrapping

Wrapping folds a scalar. Projection collapses a planar cloud onto directions.

Projected modelsDirection is observed, radius is nuisance
Projection is a different generative story from wrapping. Start with a planar vector, keep only its angle, and the radial information disappears. Anisotropic planar variation can create strong axial structure even when the projected angular mean is near zero.

Adding Independent Angles

Convolution Is Multiplication Of Moments

\(\Theta=\Theta_1+\Theta_2\pmod{2\pi}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \phi_p(\Theta)=\phi_p(\Theta_1)\phi_p(\Theta_2)\)
Wrapped Normal Check

The notebook verifies that circular convolution in density space matches coefficient multiplication to numerical precision.

Why It Matters

Independence, smoothing, and repeated angular errors are often easier to compute in moment coordinates.

Circular convolutionAdd angles modulo one turn
This is the circular version of a familiar characteristic-function rule. If independent angles are added modulo one turn, their integer coefficients multiply. The notebook's wrapped-normal check verifies that the density convolution and the coefficient product agree to numerical precision.
Modeling Judgment

Choosing A Model Family

Uniform

No preferred direction; probability proportional to arc length.

Von Mises

One dominant direction with likelihood-friendly concentration.

Cardioid

Mild first-harmonic departure from uniformity.

Wrapped

Linear error or signal observed modulo \(2\pi\).

Projected

Planar vector measurement where only direction is retained.

Ask First

  • What generated the angle?
  • Which harmonic signal is visible?
  • Which invariant can be checked?
Model choiceMechanism first, curve shape second
Frame model choice as matching the scientific mechanism rather than choosing the prettiest curve. Uniform means no preferred direction, von Mises means a single concentrated mode, cardioid means a mild first harmonic departure, wrapped models come from linear noise, and projected models come from planar vectors.

Recap

Operational Checklist

1. State The Arc Question

Choose an origin only after the probability question is geometric.

2. Choose A Representation

Use \(F\) for arc masses, \(\phi_p\) for harmonic fingerprints, and generators for mechanism.

3. Check The Invariant

Normalization, moment identities, Bessel matching, or convolution should confirm the story.
